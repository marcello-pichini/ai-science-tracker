How to contribute

  • Suggest a missing discovery, especially from outside the large AI labs or from non-English sources.
  • Report a correction: a wrong date, attribution, number, link or an overstated result.
  • Review entries in your field, or improve the site and data pipeline with a pull request. The contributing guide explains the format and the checks.

How credit works

Contributors are listed by the name or GitHub handle they choose, with what they contributed. Anyone can ask to be listed differently or removed. Credit on this page does not mean a contributor endorses every entry; each record still rests on its cited source.
