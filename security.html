What the labels mean

This is a manually selected collection of public cases, separate from the scientific-discovery count. Each record covers one identifiable finding; aggregate model claims and benchmarks do not become individual vulnerabilities.

Undisclosed at discovery (source report) preserves a source's account of a flaw unknown to defenders at that time. It provides zero-day context, not independent proof of exploitation. Pre-release finding means the cited source says it was caught before an official release. Other disclosures are not automatically classified as zero-days.

Fix documented means a cited source records a correction or fixed release. It does not show which users installed it, whether a product is currently safe, or whether an attack occurred. Disclosure, AI-attribution and source-check dates are shown separately.

A missing CVE means no identifier is established by the cited evidence here. Findings remain attributed to the named researchers and AI systems; source checking does not independently reproduce the result. This tracker is not a live vulnerability inventory.
