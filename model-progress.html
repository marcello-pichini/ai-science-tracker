What do the numbers mean?

Entry or release? An entry is an imported model group or catalog label. Aliases can overlap. Evaluation configurations are grouped, not counted as new generations.

ECI or accuracy? The Epoch Capabilities Index combines many benchmarks on an arbitrary point scale. A gain of 5 points is not “5% smarter”. A missing ECI does not mean a weak model.

Which date? Benchmark release dates take priority; catalog publication dates fill gaps. Date spacing describes these sources, not a complete launch calendar.

Read the two signals together

A shorter gap means more frequent publications. A larger ECI gain means a larger change in the composite benchmark estimate. These are different signals; neither measures a lab’s scientific productivity.
