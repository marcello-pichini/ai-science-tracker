Healthy neural priors improve BCI decoding in patients with motor-cortex gliomas

PACT used healthy neural priors to decode hand gestures from recordings of patients with gliomas. In the reported comparison, decoding accuracy improved by more than 10%, and alignment with reference motor representations increased from r=0.66 to r=0.94. The study collected intraoperative ECoG from eight participants, four with motor-cortex gliomas and four with other gliomas. This proof of concept addresses a specific clinical population; offline decoding does not establish benefit from a deployed BCI. Review covered the abstract, affiliations and selected results.

Technology: Pathology-Aware Consensus Training (PACT), a neural decoder trained with biophysical constraints
