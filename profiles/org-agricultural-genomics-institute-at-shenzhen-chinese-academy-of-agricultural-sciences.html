18 Aug 2025Agricultural Genomics Institute at Shenzhen, Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences

AlphaCD maps experimentally measured catalytic properties across cytidine deaminases and supports a high-fidelity editor

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The researchers measured catalytic efficiency, editing windows, motif preferences and off-target activity for 1,100 APOBEC-like cytidine deaminases fused to nCas9 in HEK293T cells, then trained AlphaCD to predict these properties. They applied it to 21,335 UniProt deaminases and experimentally checked a 28-protein subsample. As an application, the team used AlphaCD to identify a high-efficiency deaminase and alanine scanning to produce the E100A variant, which retained editing activity while reducing off-target activity; cell experiments tested editing at endogenous loci and a PCSK9 splice site.

Technology: AlphaCD, a machine-learning model trained on sequence, predicted structure and related protein features

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
AlphaCD learned experimentally measured activity, editing-window, motif and off-target properties across 1,100 deaminases, predicted properties for 21,335 database sequences, and was applied to select a high-efficiency candidate for subsequent alanine-scanning engineering. The authors report the model's predictions validated on 28 sampled deaminases; the E100A mutation itself was experimentally produced by alanine scanning, not generated by the model.
Test and result
The researchers measured catalytic efficiency, editing windows, motif preferences and off-target activity for 1,100 APOBEC-like cytidine deaminases fused to nCas9 in HEK293T cells, then trained AlphaCD to predict these properties.
Limitation / still unknown
The large functional dataset was measured in HEK293T reporter assays. The 21,335-protein landscape is model-predicted, with a 28-protein validation subsample. The engineered E100A editor was tested in cells; these experiments do not establish therapeutic safety, in-vivo efficacy or clinical benefit. This is distinct from the earlier AlphaFold2 structural-clustering papers: the named discovery and assay here concern the 1,100-enzyme dataset, AlphaCD predictions and A0A2R2Z4E4 E100A variant, not CD0208^P52A or the 2023 DddA-like clade findings.

3 Jun 2024Agricultural Genomics Institute at Shenzhen, Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences

AlphaFold2-guided mining identifies context-independent cytidine deaminases for efficient base editing

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Researchers used AlphaFold2 to predict structures for 1,483 cytidine deaminases, clustered the predictions and experimentally characterized representatives. The screen identified previously uncharacterized enzymes with high editing efficiency, and CD0208^P52A showed little sequence-context preference across AC/GC/TC/CC motifs, a narrow editing window and relatively low off-target activity. In mammalian cells, its base editor introduced stop codons in single- and multicopy genes without DNA double-strand breaks.

Technology: AlphaFold2 protein-structure prediction, followed by structural clustering of candidate cytidine deaminases

Family AlphaFold: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
AlphaFold2-predicted structures at scale were used to mine and cluster 1,483 putative deaminases; representatives were then selected for experimental characterization in cytosine base editors. The model enabled structure-guided candidate selection; editing outcomes were measured experimentally in mammalian cells.
Test and result
Researchers used AlphaFold2 to predict structures for 1,483 cytidine deaminases, clustered the predictions and experimentally characterized representatives.
Limitation / still unknown
The base-editing experiments were preclinical cell experiments, not animal or clinical efficacy tests. AlphaFold2 supplied predicted rather than experimentally solved structures. The paper reports multiple promising deaminases; CD0208^P52A is the specific context-independent editor highlighted here. No therapeutic safety or in-vivo delivery claim is implied.
