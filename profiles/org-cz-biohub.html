Virtual Lab: AI agents design nanobodies against SARS-CoV-2

A group of AI agents led by a principal agent designed 92 nanobodies: two bound to spike protein from recent variants in lab tests, and humans wrote about 1% of the conversation. Only 2 of 92 bound; the date is the Nature publication date given on the page.

Technology: Multiple AI agents (Virtual Lab)

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: The Virtual Lab designed 92 nanobodies; experiments (multiplexed ELISA) showed a range of functional binders. Two showed improved binding to JN.1 or KP.3 while keeping strong binding to the ancestral spike protein.
  • Still unknown: The two are called candidates for further investigation. Neutralization and clinical use not reported in the abstract. Full text is paywalled; counts of tested designs beyond 92 and binding values were not read.
  • AI role: An LLM principal investigator agent guided LLM scientist agents through meetings, with human feedback; they built a pipeline using ESM, AlphaFold-Multimer and Rosetta. Humans ran the experiments.

Evidence sources: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09442-9
