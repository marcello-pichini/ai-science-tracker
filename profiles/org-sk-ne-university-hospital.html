Prospective Swedish cohort validates multimodal AI prostate-biopsy biomarker for recurrence and adverse pathology after prostatectomy

A previously developed multimodal AI score was retrospectively applied to archived diagnostic biopsies and follow-up from a prospectively collected Swedish prostatectomy cohort. The score, derived from digitized diagnostic prostate-biopsy images plus age, PSA and clinical T-stage, was associated with post-surgical biochemical recurrence and adverse pathology. Among 143 men with evaluable data and median 8.8-year follow-up, the continuous score was associated with recurrence (subdistribution HR 2.45, 95% CI 1.77–3.38); among 88 evaluable for adverse pathology, OR was 4.85 (95% CI 2.54–10.78). Estimated 5-year recurrence was 25% in intermediate/high score versus 4% in low score.

Technology: Artera multimodal artificial-intelligence (MMAI) prostate biomarker combining deep-learning features from digitized biopsy histopathology with age, PSA and clinical T-stage
