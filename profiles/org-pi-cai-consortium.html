PI-CAI: AI matches or beats radiologists in prostate cancer detection on MRI

In a reader study with 62 radiologists on 400 cases, the AI detected about 6.8% more clinically significant cancers at the same specificity and produced about 50% fewer false positives. On 1,000 routine-practice cases, however, non-inferiority was not confirmed (specificity 68.9% vs 69.0% at 96.1% sensitivity). The authors call for prospective validation, and the system is not available for clinical use.

Technology: PI-CAI challenge super-algorithm (ensemble of top five submitted deep learning models)
