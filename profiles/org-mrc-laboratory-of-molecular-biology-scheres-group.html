ModelAngelo: automated atomic model building in cryo-EM maps

ModelAngelo combines cryo-EM density, protein sequence and local geometry to build atomic models. Beyond method benchmarks, the paper identifies six previously unassigned proteins in a photosynthetic supercomplex and four additional radial-spoke proteins plus two central-apparatus proteins in Chlamydomonas reinhardtii. These assignments use existing experimental cryo-EM maps and sequence searches; the paper leaves the newly assigned ciliary proteins' functional roles for further investigation.

Technology: ModelAngelo graph neural network with hidden Markov model sequence search
