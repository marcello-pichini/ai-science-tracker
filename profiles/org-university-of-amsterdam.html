Machine-learning screening guides thermochemical measurements of salt hydrates

Composition-based machine-learning models screened salt-hydration reactions, after which researchers synthesized and measured selected candidates by differential scanning calorimetry. The article reports volumetric energy densities of 1.74 ± 0.02, 1.73 ± 0.02 and 1.70 ± 0.02 GJ/m³ for three rubidium Tutton salt hydrates. These compounds were already known; the bounded result is AI-guided thermochemical characterization, not new compound synthesis or demonstrated heat-storage deployment. An earlier preprint contains the measurements, but its precise public date/version chronology is unresolved, so no first-public claim is made.

Technology: Stoichiometry-based machine-learning models of salt hydration properties

23 Sep 2025University of Amsterdam

ML-guided optimization improves photochemical cysteine arylation

The ChemRxiv v1 reports a two-stage optimization for nickel-catalyzed photochemical cysteine arylation: an embedding-based ranker screens categorical reagent choices, followed by Bayesian optimization of continuous conditions. The journal version reports 88% yield for both optimized conditions; the preprint reports 89% for the shorter second condition. The ranker was initially a weak yield predictor, with better separation after experimental iteration. The study expands a reaction class with earlier precedents; it does not establish invention of cysteine arylation or clinical use.

Technology: Embedding-based neural ranking; Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization
