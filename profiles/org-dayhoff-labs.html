Horizyn-1 experimentally identifies enzymes for orphan and nonnatural reactions

Researchers used the reaction–protein dual-encoder model Horizyn-1 to prioritize enzyme candidates, then experimentally validated activity in orphan-reaction deorphaning, enzyme-promiscuity tests and nonnatural transformations. For an uncharacterized Thermus aquaticus protein, LC–MS confirmed dCMP kinase activity; a selected transaminase produced noncanonical amino acids from lysine-derived donors, with isotope tracing supporting predominant alpha-amine transfer.

Technology: Horizyn-1
