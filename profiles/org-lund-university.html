MASAI: AI-assisted mammography screening in a randomized trial

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In the initial analysis of 80,033 women, AI-assisted screening detected 244 cancers versus 203 with standard double reading and reduced the reading workload by 44%. A Lund update on January 30, 2026, after more than two years and about 106,000 women, reports higher sensitivity without more false positives. The comparison is in a Swedish setting; it does not demonstrate reduced mortality.

Technology: AI for mammogram triage and marking

Publication: Publication status unspecified in annotation · Test context: prospective or independent check. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
AI flagged higher-risk mammography exams for two physicians or marked suspicious findings for a single reader.
Test and result
A prospective randomized single-site trial compared AI-supported screening (about 40,003 women) with standard double reading (about 40,030 women), measuring cancers detected, false positives and radiologist workload.
Limitation / still unknown
The Lund article states limits: single site, no mortality outcomes, and the main analysis awaits two-year follow-up; the January 2026 update could not be verified on the page read.
