AI-assisted cerebellar atlases reveal primate-specific cellular organization

Deep-learning segmentation and spatial annotation supported cerebellar atlases of macaques, marmosets and mice, revealing primate-specific cell subtypes and regional gene-expression differences. Transcriptomic gradients corresponded with awake functional-connectivity patterns. This represents the September 27, 2024 journal version; an institutional announcement gives September 26, and the observed associations do not establish causal functions.

Technology: Deep-learning cell segmentation; STAGATE; DestVI
