27 Oct 2025Zhejiang Cancer Hospital; and six collaborating clinical centers; Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Zhejiang Chinese Medical University; Wenzhou Medical University; National Cancer Center/National Clinical Research Center for Cancer, China

A six-metabolite machine-learning panel and isovalerylcarnitine findings for gastric cancer

In two multicenter cohorts totaling 597 people, LASSO feature selection and five machine-learning classifiers produced a six-metabolite plasma panel for gastric-cancer diagnosis, with AUCs of 0.947–0.982 in discovery and 0.920–0.951 in validation. The panel included isovalerylcarnitine (C5), which was lower in cancer plasma and paired tumor tissue; the paper further reported a Mendelian-randomization association and cell-assay evidence linking C5 treatment to reduced gastric-cancer cell migration and invasion through calpain-associated changes in VE-cadherin and MMP2.

Technology: LASSO regression score feature selection with neural-network, support-vector-machine, ridge-regression, logistic-regression and naive-Bayes classifiers

17 Jul 2023Zhejiang Cancer Hospital; Shanghai Jiao Tong University and six collaborating clinical centers

Machine-learning analysis of plasma metabolomic fingerprints yields a 21-metabolite gastric-cancer panel validated across multicentre cohorts

In a multicentre study, machine-learning analysis of plasma metabolic fingerprints from 1,944 retrospective participants selected a 21-metabolite panel that differentiated gastric-cancer from non-cancer cohorts; five model types achieved AUCs of 0.921–0.971 in discovery and 0.907–0.940 in external verification. Prospective testing in 264 participants using both NPELDI-MS and UPLC-MS reported diagnostic AUCs of 0.855–0.918 and 0.856–0.916, respectively. The paper also reports a four-metabolite prognostic score based on retrospective survival data.

Technology: Neural network, lasso regression, ridge regression, support-vector machine and random-forest models applied to plasma metabolic fingerprints

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: prospective or independent check
  • Tested: In a multicentre study, machine-learning analysis of plasma metabolic fingerprints from 1,944 retrospective participants selected a 21-metabolite panel that differentiated gastric-cancer from non-cancer cohorts; five model types achieved AUCs of 0.921–0.971 in discovery and 0.907–0.940 in external verification.
  • Still unknown: The panel was evaluated in selected clinical cohorts and is not established for routine screening or clinical use. Prospective validation was at one lead center, and only six panel metabolites were robustly detected by confirmatory UPLC-MS under the reported testing. Model performance does not show that the panel is causal or improves outcomes. Primary full text/abstract checked; no independent replication assessed.
  • AI role: The study used machine-learning feature selection/modeling (including logistic-regression coefficient filtering) to identify and evaluate a 21-metabolite diagnostic panel from high-throughput mass-spectrometry fingerprints; classifiers were then validated on independent retrospective and prospective cohorts.
