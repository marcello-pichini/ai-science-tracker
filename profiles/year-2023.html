Machine learning discovers D-amino-acid mixtures that boost anti-biofilm antibiotic cocktails

A machine-learning-guided high-throughput screen identified a five-component D-amino-acid mixture (“D-mix”) with about 78% anti-biofilm activity against Pseudomonas aeruginosa at 100 μM. Combining D-mix with half the lowest effective gentamicin dose retained about 91% bacterial-growth inhibition and about 93% anti-biofilm efficacy in vitro. In a small mouse lung-infection model, gentamicin/D-mix treatment reduced measured P. aeruginosa burden by about 95% in blood and 91% in lung tissue at 72 hours.

Technology: High-throughput screening coupled to machine-learning regression and iterative global optimization of D-amino-acid mixture ratios

A machine-learning-selected P4 serum panel detects hepatocellular carcinoma and signals cirrhosis-to-cancer progression

A staged serum-proteomics study across 1,002 individuals used random-forest machine learning to select a four-marker panel, P4 (HABP2, CD163, AFP and PIVKA-II). In an independent validation set, the panel distinguished hepatocellular carcinoma from cirrhosis (AUC 0.979; sensitivity 0.925; specificity 0.915) and from healthy controls (AUC 0.992). In a prospective external cohort of cirrhosis patients followed for HCC, it predicted conversion with AUC 0.890 and median lead time of 11.4 months before imaging diagnosis.

Technology: Random-forest machine-learning model for serum-protein panel selection and HCC classification

18 Dec 2023University of Washington, Department of Earth and Space Sciences

Machine-learning denoising exposes strong size scaling in deep-earthquake rupture parameters

A multitask neural denoiser recovered teleseismic P-wave pulses from deep Mw 5.0+ earthquakes that had previously been too noisy for source analysis. The study found moment–duration scaling M₀ ∼ τ⁴, stress-drop scaling Δσ ∼ M₀⁰·²¹ and radiated-energy scaling Eᵣ ∼ M₀¹·²⁴; deep events had a median radiation efficiency near 5%, weak directivity and few subevents. After accounting for rigidity, no systematic depth trend in duration, stress drop or radiated energy remained across 100–700 km.

Technology: DenoTe, a transfer-learned multitask convolutional encoder–decoder neural network
