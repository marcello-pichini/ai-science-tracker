4 Jul 2024Boston University; Stanford University; Peking Union Medical College Hospital; Columbia University Irving Medical Center; University of Nebraska Medical Center; additional clinical and research centers contributing cohorts

Multimodal AI distinguishes ten dementia etiologies across nine clinical datasets

A multimodal AI model analyzed demographics, personal and family history, medications, cognitive and functional testing, and neuroimaging across nine datasets totaling 51,269 participants. It assigned probabilities to ten primary or contributing dementia etiologies and addressed mixed cases. The paper reports microaveraged AUROC 0.96 for dementia-etiology differentiation; model probabilities aligned with etiology-specific biomarkers and postmortem pathology. In a review of 100 cases, AI-augmented neurologist assessment outperformed neurologist-only ratings.

Technology: Multimodal machine-learning model combining clinical, neuropsychological, functional and neuroimaging data

21 May 2024Boston University; U.S. Army Combat Capabilities Development Command Soldier Center

A self-driving lab finds a polymer shell with record energy-absorbing efficiency

A human-supervised self-driving laboratory selected and physically tested more than 25,000 additively manufactured polymer shell designs across an 11-dimensional parameter space. The campaign found a structure with 75.2% energy-absorbing efficiency, exceeding the paper’s previously reported maximum of 71.8% for balsa wood, and the authors derived transferable design heuristics from the resulting dataset. The result is a structural design for specified compression thresholds, not a universal material efficiency ranking.

Technology: MAMA BEAR self-driving laboratory with Bayesian optimization and automated additive manufacturing/testing

  • Tested: A human-supervised self-driving laboratory selected and physically tested more than 25,000 additively manufactured polymer shell designs across an 11-dimensional parameter space.
  • Still unknown: The result concerns generalized cylindrical shells made from a limited set of polymers and a defined nondimensional metric, whose value depends on threshold stress. The optimization was human-in-the-loop, and transfer to other geometries, materials or operational settings is not established.

31 Oct 2022Boston University

Deep-learning control links single-cell expression to growth responses

A learned predictive controller enabled high-throughput measurements linking gene-expression patterns with functional outcomes in individual engineered bacterial cells. The study exposed heterogeneous growth responses under antibiotic challenge. The original result was public in the October 2022 preprint and later appeared in a 2024 journal article. This is a laboratory model-system study, not a clinical resistance result or validated intervention.

Technology: LSTM encoder-decoder predictive control
