22 Oct 2026AI-Stroke; Nîmes University Hospital

APOLLAI, a multimodal AI system, versus EMS personnel for prehospital detection of stroke-related deficits

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The medRxiv abstract reports APOLLAI evaluation on video/audio recordings from 295 suspected-stroke patients at Nîmes University Hospital, against seven-neurologist consensus labels for five NIHSS items. Global sensitivity was 78.5% versus 59.4% for EMS personnel, specificity 87.0% versus 85.9%, and AUC 0.882. These are diagnostic comparisons on recorded patient data; the authors call for prospective validation in real EMS conditions.

Technology: APOLLAI (computer vision, audio deep learning and SVM fusion classifier)
