AI discoveries: catalog coverage and evidence

5322 active catalog entries · descriptive coverage, not a census of science

What does this catalog cover?

Of 5322 active entries, 5321 contain a structured evidence annotation and 1 do not. The tables compare the composition of the current catalog, rather than the rate of scientific progress.

Counting unit: one active discovery record. Records can overlap and one record can describe several results. Archived discoveries, model releases and security cases are excluded. Year means the stored publication year, not the year the entry was added.

Evidence annotated means a nonempty structured evidence object is present. It does not establish that every annotation field is populated, peer review, independent replication or clinical benefit. Source checked means the catalog stores a completed source check; it is not independent reproduction. These attributes overlap and must not be added together.

Interpretation: coverage and annotation effort differ by field and year. Import batches can change historical totals. Inspect field/year acquisition and source-review gaps for finer collection coverage. A larger count does not prove a more productive field, faster AI progress, or better scientific evidence. The latest year may be incomplete.

Reproduce and cite this comparison

Each grouping sums to the active catalog size; do not sum the year and field tables together.
