13 Sep 2022National Taiwan University Hospital; National Taiwan University

Deep learning detects pancreatic cancer on CT in a nationwide study

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A deep-learning tool for contrast-enhanced CT reached 89.9% sensitivity and 95.9% specificity in an internal test set (not significantly different from the original radiologist reports, 96.1%). On 1,473 real-world Taiwanese CT studies it reached 89.7% sensitivity and 92.8% specificity, but sensitivity for cancers under 2 cm was 74.7%. The study is retrospective, with no prospective reader comparison in the abstract.

Technology: Segmentation CNN with an ensemble of five CNN classifiers for pancreatic cancer on CT
