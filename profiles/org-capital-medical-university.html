6 Nov 2026Capital Medical University; Beijing Friendship Hospital

DBFG-Net identifies cognitive impairment in chronic kidney disease from MRI features

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In a discovery cohort of 342 patients with CKD (177 with cognitive impairment, 165 without) and 58 matched controls, MRI features from T1-weighted imaging, arterial spin labeling and quantitative susceptibility mapping showing both stage-related trends and cognition-related discrimination were selected to train a Dual-Branch Feature-Gated Network. It reached AUC 0.942 in the discovery cohort and 0.902 and 0.926 in two external validation cohorts. Discriminative features were structural and susceptibility measures, not cerebral blood flow. Retrospective imaging study.

Technology: Dual-Branch Feature-Gated Network (DBFG-Net) deep model on selected multimodal MRI features

30 Oct 2026Capital Medical University; Sichuan University; Nanchong Central Hospital

Ultrasound deep learning radiomics identifies non-endometrioid endometrial carcinoma

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In 663 patients from 11 centers, radiomics features (PyRadiomics) and ResNet-101 deep features from transvaginal ultrasound were combined in machine learning classifiers. On an independent external cohort of 180 patients from three centers the combined model reached AUC 0.837, accuracy 0.756, sensitivity 0.800 and specificity 0.733, outperforming the radiomics-only and deep-learning-only models. The study is retrospective and the AUC is moderate.

Technology: ResNet-101 deep features combined with PyRadiomics features in machine learning classifiers on transvaginal ultrasound

28 Apr 2025Capital Medical University; Beijing Anzhen Hospital

ML tool stratifies catheter ablation benefit in atrial fibrillation with heart failure

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Unsupervised clustering and supervised learning on 25 clinical features from the China-AF registry (3122 patients, 31 hospitals) identified three risk clusters with composite event rates per 100 person-years of 7.7, 6.8 and 3.8. External validation used 778 patients from the CABANA trial and showed a similar pattern (adjusted HR 0.19 for ablation in the benefiting group, 95% CI 0.05-0.73). The tool was derived from observational data and the authors state that interventional trials are needed to confirm its usefulness.

2 Jan 2024Capital Medical University; Westlake University, Zhejiang University and collaborating Chinese clinical institutions

Machine learning selects CSF and serum protein panels associated with early Alzheimer disease

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In a proteomic study, researchers measured paired CSF and serum samples in a discovery cohort and used random-forest machine learning to select a 19-protein CSF panel and an eight-protein serum panel to distinguish mild cognitive impairment due to Alzheimer disease from cognitively normal participants. Targeted proteomics verified candidate proteins in an independent multicenter cohort. In small held-out test groups, the CSF panel reached AUC 0.984 and the serum panel AUC 0.881; the CSF panel also distinguished Alzheimer-related MCI from frontotemporal dementia, ALS and Huntington disease in the reported analyses.

Technology: Random-forest machine-learning classifiers on CSF and serum proteomic profiles

Editorial relevance 60 medicine Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Random forests prioritized 19 CSF proteins and eight serum proteins to form disease-specific MCI classifiers after proteomic discovery and targeted validation; the selected panels were evaluated in held-out samples and against other neurodegenerative diseases.
Test and result
In a proteomic study, researchers measured paired CSF and serum samples in a discovery cohort and used random-forest machine learning to select a 19-protein CSF panel and an eight-protein serum panel to distinguish mild cognitive impairment due to Alzheimer disease from cognitively normal participants.
Limitation / still unknown
The AUC test sets were small (CSF: 9 Alzheimer-related MCI and 21 controls; serum: 8 and 21). The study used separate independent multicenter targeted-proteomics validation for candidate proteins; that does not make the small classifier test cohorts large or establish clinical utility. The serum panel was less effective at distinguishing Alzheimer-related MCI from other neurodegenerative diseases than the CSF panel. These are research panels, not routine tests.

28 Apr 2023Capital Medical University; Beijing Chao-Yang Hospital

LASSO expression score distinguishes delayed kidney-graft function in retrospective cohorts

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A three-gene LASSO score was evaluated against observed delayed graft function after kidney transplantation. The study reports AUC 0.730 in an internal test split and 0.773 in a small separate biopsy cohort. This is retrospective clinical discrimination, without demonstrated prevention, treatment benefit or prospective deployment.

Technology: Ten-fold cross-validated LASSO for a three-gene delayed-graft-function score

Editorial relevance 33 biology Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
LASSO learned nonzero expression features and coefficients for a combined score evaluated against measured clinical graft-function outcomes. Generic pathway enrichment and recovery of known inflammatory biology are not the represented finding.
Test and result
From 202 post-reperfusion samples with graft-function information, the source made a 70:30 training/internal-test split. Training-set LASSO selected ANXA1, HSP90AA1 and NFKBIA. Reported AUC was 0.730 internally and 0.773 in GSE90861, a separate cohort of 23 paired-biopsy recipients, twelve with delayed and eleven with immediate graft function.
Limitation / still unknown
Small retrospective datasets and donor-type differences limit generalization. The separate cohort also informed earlier expression confirmation of candidate genes, so it is not a wholly untouched evaluation of the entire discovery workflow. Individual-patient split independence and supplementary implementation were not reconstructed. No causal necroptosis mechanism, reduced rejection, therapeutic benefit or locked prospective prediction was established by the represented model evaluation.
