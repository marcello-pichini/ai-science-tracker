28 Aug 2024University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign; University of Toronto; Vector Institute; University of Chicago; University of California, Berkeley

Closed-loop learning links donor–acceptor molecules’ photostability to higher triplet states

A closed-loop synthesis and learning campaign found that solution photostability across a donor–bridge–acceptor molecular series correlated with the high-energy portion of the triplet excited-state manifold, beyond the commonly considered lowest triplet state. Researchers tested the model-derived hypothesis on an experimental set and changed solvent triplet energy to test the proposed Dexter triplet-energy-transfer degradation pathway. The study used Bayesian optimization and interpretable supervised learning to extract the chemical hypothesis from measured photostability data.

Technology: Closed-loop transfer (CLT), combining Gryffin Bayesian optimization with physics-feature supervised learning

4 May 2023University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign; University of Michigan

BacterAI experimentally maps amino-acid growth requirements of two oral Streptococcus species

BacterAI autonomously designed and ran growth experiments with robotic liquid handling to learn amino-acid requirements for the oral commensals Streptococcus gordonii and S. sanguinis. It learned separate growth rules for each organism and showed that the closely related species have different amino-acid auxotrophies. For S. gordonii, its neural-network growth predictions exceeded 90% accuracy after nine days and 3,024 experiments; the article also reports transfer-learning tests in larger media.

Technology: BacterAI, a reinforcement-learning agent coupled to iterative neural-network growth models and automated laboratory robots

31 Mar 2023University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign; Cornell University; Georgia Institute of Technology

CLEAN experimentally identifies SAM hydrolase activity in previously uncharacterized proteins

Researchers at the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign developed CLEAN, a contrastive-learning enzyme-function predictor, and used it to identify the SAM hydrolase activity of the previously uncharacterized protein TTHA0338. In vitro HPLC–MS and enzyme-kinetic experiments also corrected the annotation of MJ1651 and confirmed the multiple activities of SsFIA.

Technology: CLEAN (contrastive learning-based enzyme annotation model)
