Rentosertib: AI-designed drug reports Phase IIa results in pulmonary fibrosis

In the GENESIS-IPF study of 71 patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis at 22 centers (placebo versus three doses, 12 weeks), the 60 mg/day group had a mean FVC change of +98.4 mL versus −20.3 mL for placebo. Groups were small, so larger studies are needed; biomarker analysis was exploratory.

Technology: Pharma.AI (generative AI for targets and molecules)

INS018_073: AI identifies a preclinical candidate for renal fibrosis

Insilico Medicine named INS018_073 a preclinical candidate after in vitro tests and testing in a mouse model of renal fibrosis. The company reports improvements in pathology, biomarkers, and histologically observed fibrosis. The candidate was proposed for IND-enabling studies; these are company-reported preclinical results, not clinical evidence or independent confirmation.
