Machine-learning-derived seven-protein panel distinguishes remission from active ANCA-associated vasculitis

Confounder-aware machine-learning feature selection on plasma proteomics identified a seven-protein signature—AHSG, CLEC3B, COMP, F9, LRG1, MCAM and MRC1—to classify remission versus active ANCA-associated vasculitis (AAV). After targeted PRM mass-spectrometry assay development, a generalized linear model was evaluated in an independent cohort of 108 AAV patients and achieved AUC 0.94 (95% CI 0.90–0.98); combined discovery and validation performance was AUC 0.97. In the study cohorts, the panel outperformed routine CRP and ANCA titer measurements.

Technology: MetaDeconfoundR confounder-aware feature analysis, LASSO and Boruta feature selection, and a generalized linear model applied to targeted PRM mass-spectrometry data

11 Mar 2024Max Delbrück Center for Molecular Medicine; University of Luxembourg; University of California, San Francisco; Harvard Medical School; University of California, San Diego; University of Toronto; University of Cambridge; University of Antwerp and collaborators

AI-ranked SARS-CoV-2 protein interactions guide discovery of an NSP10–NSP16 inhibitor

A machine-learning classifier applied to quantitative PPI assay data and AlphaFold-Multimer predictions to prioritize SARS-CoV-2 protein interactions; 15 of 34 high-confidence interactions were not previously listed in IMEx. The team then targeted the NSP10–NSP16 methyltransferase interface in an ultra-large virtual screen. Compound 459 bound NSP10 (MST affinity about 13 μM), reduced NSP10–NSP16 interaction in cells (BRET IC50 9.2 μM), inhibited complex methyltransferase activity by about 50% at 100 μM, and reduced SARS-CoV-2 reporter replication in cells (IC50 39.5 μM).

Technology: Multi-adaptive support-vector-machine (maSVM) classifier for quantitative protein–protein interaction data, coupled with AlphaFold-Multimer complex prediction; VirtualFlow/QuickVina2 and later docking methods performed compound screening

Compound 459 was then experimentally tested for protein binding, interaction disruption, methyltransferase activity and viral replication.
