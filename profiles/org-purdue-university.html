Active learning identifies 28 new DFT-validated pressure-induced phase transitions

Across 13 active-learning cycles, a graph neural network screened phase pairs for pressure-induced transitions and selected cases for density-functional-theory (DFT) calculations. DFT confirmed 28 previously unreported transformations; 14 high-pressure phases were dynamically stable at transition pressure, including 10 also predicted dynamically stable at zero pressure. The study also identifies a previously unrecorded dynamically stable monoclinic P2₁/c phase of Cr₂O₃.

Technology: MEGNet graph neural network with five-model ensemble and iterative active learning

6 Dec 2022Purdue University; University of Hong Kong; Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen

CNN tracking reveals irradiation-driven nanovoid size fluctuations and shrinkage in copper

A convolutional neural network combined with greedy matching to detect, track and measure pre-existing nanovoids in in-situ TEM videos of irradiated copper. The analysis revealed non-monotonic, random-walk-like size fluctuations and shrinkage during irradiation; phase-field simulations were used to investigate the mechanism. Follow-up manual measurements and additional experiments confirmed the size-fluctuation observation.

Technology: Convolutional neural network for void detection, with greedy matching for tracking and migration analysis
