Satellite machine learning maps vegetation change near a highway

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Random Forest classification of fused Sentinel optical and radar observations mapped land-cover change along a section of India’s National Highway 344 from 2016 to 2021. Tall vegetation decreased from 32.73% of the mapped area in 2016 to 24.02% in 2018, with partial later recovery; urban cover increased. Feature selection yielded reported classification accuracy of 92.75%. These are retrospective land-cover estimates, not measured erosion, landslide or flood outcomes, and the study does not isolate a causal highway effect.

Technology: Random Forest learns land-cover classes from fused Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical bands; recursive feature elimination selects the inputs for temporal mapping.

Mass-constrained hydrologic networks reveal a snow-storage limitation in a Colorado watershed

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Yihan Wang and colleagues at the University of Oklahoma, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, the International Computer Science Institute and the University of Michigan present PECAN, a distributed convolutional LSTM framework with strict and relaxed mass-conservation variants. Limitation / still unknown
