29 Oct 2026Arc Institute; Stanford University

Evo 2 uses in-context learning to design de novo regulatory sequences with only tens of examples

Arc Institute reports that Evo 2 designs functional regulatory sequences from 4 to 64 in-context examples without extra training, with 30-50% yield. Of nine AI-designed enhancers delivered by AAV to mice, seven drove detectable expression and two labeled layer 5 extratelencephalic neurons with 73% and 95% specificity. Results are bioRxiv preprints; in vivo work covers only layer 5 enhancers, and other designs (proteins, CRISPR loci, antibodies, phage genomes) are computational only.

Technology: Evo 2 (genomic language model); in-context learning; reinforcement learning post-training

17 Sep 2025Arc Institute

First bacteriophage genomes designed by a genome language model

The Arc Institute group used Evo to design genomes resembling that of phage ΦX174: of 285 designs, 16 yielded functional phages (67–392 mutations from the closest natural one; 13 had never-seen mutations). They infect only two E. coli strains; a cocktail overcame resistance in three evolved strains. bioRxiv preprint; peer review is unconfirmed.

Technology: Evo (genome language model)

19 Feb 2025Arc Institute

Evo 2: the largest AI model for biology

Trained on more than 9.3 trillion nucleotides from over 128,000 genomes, it reads sequences up to 1 million nucleotides, flags harmful mutations, and designs genetic elements and genomes as long as those of simple bacteria. In BRCA1 tests it exceeds 90% accuracy in distinguishing benign from potentially pathogenic variants (a result limited to that gene). Preprint; human pathogens were excluded from training.

Technology: Evo 2 (genome language model)

14 Nov 2024Arc Institute, Stanford, and UC Berkeley

Evo: a foundation model for DNA, from molecules to genomes

Published in Science, Evo (Arc Institute, Stanford, and UC Berkeley) designed EvoCas9-1, a CRISPR system sharing only about 73% of its sequence with CRISPR-Cas9 but with comparable activity after eleven designs, and new IS200/IS605 transposons that cut and paste DNA. The team calls this the beginning and conducted an ethics and safety study.

Technology: Evo (DNA foundation model)
