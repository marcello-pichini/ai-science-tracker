MetNet-3: short-range neural forecasts in Google products

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The model forecasts precipitation, temperature, wind and dew point up to 24 hours ahead at 1–4 km resolution, learning from about 1,000 stations, and outperforms HRRR and ENS in several regions. It operates in Google Search and the app in the United States and 27 European countries, with precipitation forecasts up to 12 hours. Preprint, no numerical values in the text and one case study.

25 Mar 2020Google Research

MetNet: neural rainfall forecasting up to 8 hours ahead

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The model forecasts precipitation at 1 km resolution and 2-minute intervals up to 8 hours ahead from radar and satellite, without physical equations. According to the page, it outperforms NOAA’s operational HRRR model up to about 7–8 hours, in seconds rather than about an hour. US coverage only, author-only comparisons, a preprint, and no stated operational use.

Technology: MetNet (neural network)

Family MetNet: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 60 climate Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Deep neural network with axial self-attention forecasting precipitation probabilities from radar and satellite inputs, without physical equations.
Test and result
Evaluated on a precipitation-rate forecasting benchmark against NWP (HRRR) and an optical-flow baseline, scored by F1 at 1.0 mm/h; outperforms NWP at forecasts up to about 7 to 8 hours.
Limitation / still unknown
Demonstrated only for the continental US; the authors call it a small stepping stone; comparisons are author-run.
