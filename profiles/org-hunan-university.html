30 Aug 2024Hunan University; Cleveland Clinic; National Cancer Institute; Tel Aviv University

deepAMP designs broad-spectrum antimicrobial peptides active against resistant bacteria

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Researchers at Hunan University and collaborators used the peptide language model deepAMP to optimize antimicrobial peptides and experimentally tested 29 candidates. More than 90% inhibited Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria better than the reference peptide penetratin; three candidates showed reduced resistance emergence in serial-passage assays and activity in a mouse Pseudomonas wound-infection model.

Technology: deepAMP peptide language model (deepAMP-general, deepAMP-AOM, deepAMP-POM and deepAMP-predict)

Editorial relevance 60 medicine Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The generative and ranking models optimized candidate peptide sequences for antimicrobial potency and membrane-disrupting activity. Researchers synthesized and tested selected sequences in vitro and in a mouse wound infection experiment.
Test and result
Researchers at Hunan University and collaborators used the peptide language model deepAMP to optimize antimicrobial peptides and experimentally tested 29 candidates.
Limitation / still unknown
This is a separate discovery study from the AMPSphere microbiome-mining paper already in the tracker: deepAMP optimizes peptide sequences, whereas AMPSphere mined microbiomes. Testing involved 29 candidates and one female mouse wound model; this is preclinical evidence, not clinical efficacy. The article itself calls for more animal-model validation.

5 May 2024Hunan University; Zhejiang University; King Abdullah University of Science and Technology; Central South University; Shandong University

HydrogelFinder: A Foundation Model for Efficient Self-Assembling Peptide Discovery Guided by Non-Peptidal Small Molecules

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: HydrogelFinder used a generative model and learned prediction to prioritize 111 candidate peptides; 17 were synthesized and nine self-assembled under tested conditions. Cell assays covered two selected materials and do not establish general safety or clinical suitability.
