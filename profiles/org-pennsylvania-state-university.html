24 Feb 2026Pennsylvania State University; Georgia Institute of Technology

Deep-learning tracking of mantle simulations finds continental-margin subduction zones persist longer

A fully convolutional network segmented subduction zones in two-dimensional mantle-convection simulations using temperature, vertical velocity and fineness fields. Across the modeled runs, zones forming near continental margins were generally longer-lived and less sensitive to internal heating than oceanic zones, supporting the hypothesis that continental lithosphere can stabilize subduction during early-Earth-like conditions. This is a computational-model result rather than direct observation of ancient Earth.

Technology: Attention-based fully convolutional network with custom segmentation loss

1 May 2025Pennsylvania State University; University of Minnesota

LIMITS-guided peptide design yields selective anti-tuberculosis host-defense peptides

The Penn State/University of Minnesota team used the Lead Informed Machine Interrogation of Therapeutic Sequences (LIMITS) workflow to train random-forest and neural-network predictors on approximately 100 host-defense peptides, using lead mutational scans and biophysical sequence features. Experimentally tested neural-network-designed MAD5.1 and MAD5.3 showed reported MICs of 5 μM against M. tuberculosis H37Rv, selectivity indices >16 against an eight-organism non-mycobacterial panel, and mammalian-cell toxicity indices >100. The study identifies peptide length as a potentially underappreciated activity determinant.

Technology: LIMITS (Lead Informed Machine Interrogation of Therapeutic Sequences), using random-forest and feed-forward neural-network predictors

12 Sep 2024Pennsylvania State University; Utah FORGE

Deep learning expands the Utah FORGE DAS microearthquake catalog and resolves stimulation structure

A ResNet-based DASEventNet model applied to continuous distributed acoustic sensing from the April 2022 Utah FORGE Well 16A (78)-32 stimulation manually verified 5,749 microearthquakes absent from the Silixa STA/LTA catalog; 45 of 5,794 model-positive windows were false detections on manual review. The study reports events down to Mw −1.80 versus −1.14 for STA/LTA and a broader Stage 3 seismicity distribution consistent with fracture geometry seen in an independent deep-borehole geophone catalog. This is a local event-catalog and stimulation-geometry result, not a direct DAS-based event-location measurement.

Technology: DASEventNet (ResNet-50 convolutional neural network)

17 Jun 2021Pennsylvania State University; Syracuse University

Machine-learning unmixing links acid rain and subsurface flowpaths to watershed CO₂ drawdown

Non-negative matrix factorization (NMF) was applied to stream-chemistry time series from three watersheds to infer solute endmembers and their changing contributions without predefining endmember chemistry. Combined with geological interpretation and independent geochemical evidence, the model separated shallow acid-rain-affected flow from deeper pyrite-weathering contributions. The analysis found that acid rain can reduce a watershed’s long-timescale CO₂-sequestration capacity and that omitting sulfide oxidation/acid-rain reactions overestimates weathering-driven CO₂ drawdown.

Technology: Non-negative matrix factorization (NMF) machine-learning blind-source-separation model applied to stream chemistry

Researchers interpreted those patterns using watershed geology, sulfur isotopes, weathering profiles and calcite saturation, then estimated mineral-weathering rates and CO₂ sequestration. The ML decomposition enabled source separation where conventional endmember assumptions were uncertain; it did not directly measure a global carbon flux.
