HarfBuzz contextual-class subset traversal can amplify CPU work

HarfBuzz · AI involvement attributed to Anthropic OSS Scanner (model not identified for this discovery)

Finding and attribution

ContextFormat2/ChainContextFormat2 subset closure and dependency traversal repeatedly walked a large ClassDef for many classes sharing it, causing quadratic work. Maintainer fix computes intersecting classes once and adds a regression font.

Product: HarfBuzz. AI system: Anthropic OSS Scanner (model not identified for this discovery). Team / organization: Anthropic / HarfBuzz maintainers.

CVE identifiers: Not established in the cited evidence.

AI and human contribution (source account): The primary maintainer commit explicitly says “Discovered by Anthropic's OSS Scanner, as vulnerability ANT-2026-ZCMLEQFJ.” It separately lists Claude Opus 4.8 as a commit co-author; the source does not establish that model as the discovering system or describe its investigative steps.

Human involvement is only described where the source account establishes it; unknown involvement does not mean none. Vendor confirmation of a flaw does not confirm every claim about AI contribution.

Discovery context: The maintainer source explicitly calls this a vulnerability and describes resource exhaustion from crafted font inputs in the subset path. It does not report exploitation in the wild.

Remediation status: fix documented. The maintainer commit documents a correction that bounds retained glyph/table work and rejects malformed input beyond the source-table budget. The commit does not establish an affected or fixed release boundary.

A source-documented fix does not establish installed patches or current safety. Source checking does not independently reproduce the finding or establish observed exploitation.

Disclosed 11 Nov 2026. AI attribution dated 11 Nov 2026. Sources checked 17 Nov 2026; these are separate dates, not a live installation check.
