Real-time discovery and characterization of AT2020xnd, an AT2018cow-like fast transient

ZTF observations identified AT2020xnd (ZTF20acigmel) at redshift 0.243 as the first unambiguous AT2018cow analogue found and confirmed in real time. Follow-up showed a rapid optical rise, persistently hot blue emission, luminous radio, millimetre and X-ray output, no classical radioactive-supernova peak, and a dwarf-galaxy host. The authors argue that these properties support a distinct fast-blue-optical-transient phenomenon, while its progenitor and central engine remain uncertain.

Technology: Zwicky Transient Facility real-time alert stream with a deep-learning real-bogus classifier and a custom contextual transient filter

  • Tested: ZTF observations identified AT2020xnd (ZTF20acigmel) at redshift 0.243 as the first unambiguous AT2018cow analogue found and confirmed in real time.
  • Still unknown: This is one transient and a small class comparison, not a general population measurement. The suggested distinct phenomenon is supported by shared observed properties, while the progenitor/engine interpretation remains unresolved and model-dependent.
  • AI role: The paper’s custom search filter required the ZTF alert’s deep-learning real-bogus score to exceed 0.65, along with repeated detections, a high Galactic latitude and no coincident stellar counterpart. This AI-based alert filtering contributed to selecting AT2020xnd in real time; rapid multiband photometry and spectroscopic and multiwavelength human follow-up established its astrophysical nature and unusual properties.
