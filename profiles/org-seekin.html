Multi-centre validation tests AI-empowered OncoSeek across 15,122 participants and four assay platforms

A 2025 study added four cohorts to evaluate the seven-protein-marker OncoSeek test, which combines blood protein tumor markers and clinical variables with AI. Across seven cohorts from seven centres in China, Brazil and the United States, totaling 15,122 participants, the reported overall sensitivity was 58.4% at 92.0% specificity; the symptomatic cohort had 73.1% sensitivity at 90.6% specificity. The study also tested performance across four platforms and two sample types.
