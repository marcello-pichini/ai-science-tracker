Model of the human nuclear pore with AlphaFold2 and electron microscopy

AlphaFold2 and ColabFold, combined with cryo-tomography and cryo-electron microscopy, produced a model of the human nuclear pore complex (about 30 proteins, about 1,000 copies, 120 MDa) covering over 90% of the complex, versus 46% for previous models. Flexible parts remain unresolved; a complete dynamic model is future work.

Technology: AlphaFold2 and ColabFold

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: The integrative model (AlphaFold2 with cryo-ET and cryo-EM data) was validated against experimental data and covers more than 90% of the NPC core versus 46% for previous models.
  • Still unknown: Flexible peripheral parts are not modelled, no quantitative confidence scores are given, and a complete dynamic model is future work.
  • AI role: AlphaFold2 and ColabFold predicted protein structures that were assembled into the nuclear pore model.
