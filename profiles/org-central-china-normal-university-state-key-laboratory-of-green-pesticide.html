31 Aug 2026Central China Normal University, State Key Laboratory of Green Pesticide

ESM-FEP combines protein-language-model fitness screening and free-energy calculations to engineer herbicide-detoxifying maize dioxygenase

ESM-FEP was applied to Zea mays dioxygenase ZmHSL1B and nominated a quadruple mutant (Q140H/Y205F/L332R/K336F) with approximately sevenfold higher catalytic efficiency for mesotrione detoxification than wild type, measured by in-vitro assays and kinetic analysis. Arabidopsis thaliana expressing the engineered variant showed enhanced herbicide tolerance in the reported transgenic tests.

Technology: ESM-FEP (fine-tuned ESM-2 protein language model plus alchemical free-energy perturbation)
