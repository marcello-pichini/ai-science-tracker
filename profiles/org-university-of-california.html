27 Sep 2026University of California, Irvine; Los Alamos National Laboratory; Center for Integrated Nanotechnologies

Generative AI designs and experimentally validates an ultrathin broadband ENZ absorber

A generative deep neural network searched multilayer indium-tin-oxide/aluminum-doped-zinc-oxide designs and selected an epsilon-near-zero absorber stack. After the researchers merged two similar adjacent layers for fabrication, the approximately 159-nm device experimentally maintained more than 98% absorption over a 1,077-nm near-infrared bandwidth at 49° p-polarized illumination. The modeled design predicted 1,688 nm bandwidth; the experiment’s detector cutoff limited measurements above 2,600 nm.

Technology: GLONet generative deep neural network, trained in simulation loop with differentiable transfer-matrix solver TMM-Fast

23 Feb 2026University of California, Berkeley (Yaghi group)

LFAST: an LLM agent drives robotic synthesis of the new COF-2000 framework

A GPT-4o-based agent (LFAST) screened reaction conditions over a 96-condition matrix and refined them after each round, raising the crystallinity index of a reference covalent organic framework by 350% and yielding COF-2000, a previously unreported material with higher structural order. The reported validation is crystallinity measurement of the synthesized products; the authors do not pursue applications. Publication date is taken from secondary reports of the JACS paper and should be checked against the journal page.

Technology: LFAST (LLM For Accelerated Synthesis Technique), GPT-4o agent with robotic experimentation

25 Nov 2025University of California, Berkeley; Monash University; VinUniversity; University of Haifa; Humboldt University of Berlin

AI-guided experiments uncover new zeolitic imidazolate framework crystals

AIRES combined probabilistic machine-learning predictions with robotic synthesis, optical crystal screening and single-crystal X-ray diffraction to explore zinc zeolitic imidazolate frameworks. The campaign crystallized new structures using 10 of 48 previously untested linkers, doubling the discovery rate of random exploration (700 versus 1,400 experiments) and increasing the single-linker Zn-ZIF library by about one-third. The paper reports structure-resolved products, including eleven previously unreported crystals across its discovery campaign.

Technology: Algorithmic Iterative Reticular Synthesis (AIRES)

CNN-guided spectroscopy confirms 28 new extremely metal-poor galaxies

21 Feb 2024University of California, San Francisco; Stanford University; University of California health-system collaborators; University of Oxford

EHR machine learning and genetic colocalization link osteoporosis to female Alzheimer’s risk near MS4A6A

Random-forest models trained on UCSF records (749 expert-diagnosed Alzheimer’s disease cases and 250,545 controls) identified clinical features predictive up to seven years before the recorded onset; the seven-year model’s AUROC was 0.72 using clinical features alone. Matched-cohort follow-up in external University of California health-system records found faster Alzheimer’s diagnosis after osteoporosis in women. Genetic colocalization analyses supported a female-specific shared signal between Alzheimer’s disease, low heel bone mineral density and MS4A6A expression near the MS4A gene cluster. The paper also reports shared APOE-locus support for hyperlipidemia and Alzheimer’s disease.

Technology: Random-forest models on longitudinal electronic health records, interpreted with the SPOKE biomedical knowledge network

21 Oct 2023University of California, Davis; NOAA Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory; European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts; Institut Pierre-Simon Laplace

Physics-guided machine learning links Southern Ocean regime shifts to a northward-shifting Antarctic Circumpolar Current

Applying THOR to one eddy-permitting CM4 climate simulation, researchers detected a projected dynamical-regime shift where the Antarctic Circumpolar Current crosses the Pacific–Antarctic Ridge under the SSP5-8.5 scenario. Neural-network explanations and transport analysis attributed the modeled change to stronger wind stress shifting the current northward into less topographically constrained waters, where the flow intensifies. This is a model-based scenario result, not an observed future change.

Technology: THOR (Tracking global Heating with Ocean Regimes), using NEMI clustering, an ensemble of 50 multilayer perceptrons, SHAP and layer-wise relevance propagation

3 Jul 2023University of California, Los Angeles; Stanford University; EarlyDiagnostics; University of Southern California; University of California, San Diego

Deep learning detects elevated tissue-derived cfDNA across cancer and cirrhosis cohorts

A study built a fragment-level methylation atlas from 521 noncancer tissue samples spanning 29 human tissue types and used it with cfSort, a supervised deep neural network, to estimate tissue composition in plasma cell-free DNA. Applying cfSort to patient data, the investigators found higher cfDNA fractions from the affected tissue in liver, lung, colorectal and stomach cancer and cirrhosis cohorts than in healthy controls; the estimated fractions also increased with cancer stage. The tissue-origin signal was reproduced on an independent whole-genome-bisulfite-sequencing dataset for liver cancer. In serial samples from four non-small-cell lung cancer patients receiving anti-PD-1 therapy, estimated liver or kidney tissue fractions tracked measured biochemical markers of organ injury.

Technology: cfSort, an ensemble of two dense deep neural networks trained on simulated mixtures using a fragment-level tissue methylation atlas

The 2025 Evo paper reports distinct generated proteins EvoAcr1–5 and separate assays; shared anti-CRISPR function does not make these identical discoveries.
  • AI role: The model ranked candidate proteins from predicted prophage regions for anti-CRISPR likelihood; the researchers selected candidates from those rankings and biochemically tested them. It directly prioritized the proteins later named AcrIIA20 and AcrIIA21, whose Cas9 inhibition was measured in vitro.
