CellPaint-POSH screening links AURKAIP1 knockout with reduced mitochondrial abundance and mTORC1 signaling

Self-supervised CP-DINO analysis of pooled Cell Painting CRISPR profiles nominated AURKAIP1 among genes with mitochondrial and mTORC1-related phenotypes. Follow-up AURKAIP1 knockout in A549 cells reduced the mitochondrial marker TOMM20 and pS6, an mTORC1 activity readout, relative to control. The authors propose that mitochondrial translation/metabolism may influence mTORC1, while the causal pathway remains unresolved.

Technology: CellPaint-POSH with CP-DINO-1640 self-supervised embeddings and a vision transformer

The model enabled candidate prioritization, while the perturbation and marker measurements supplied the specific experimental evidence.
