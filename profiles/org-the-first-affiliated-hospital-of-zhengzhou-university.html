17 Nov 2025The First Affiliated Hospital of Zhengzhou University

Machine-learning metabolomics identifies DHEAS and citrate signatures in anti-MDA5 dermatomyositis

In a single-centre cohort of 183 patients with idiopathic inflammatory myopathy and 104 healthy controls, seven machine-learning classifiers and LASSO feature selection were used with plasma and PBMC metabolomics to distinguish anti-MDA5-positive dermatomyositis. DHEAS and citrate were selected across the integrated analyses; plasma assays showed lower levels in anti-MDA5-positive cases, with concordant patterns reported in a separate 33-patient/33-control validation cohort. Within cases, DHEAS correlated inversely with rapidly progressive interstitial lung disease and citrate with rash.

Technology: Seven machine-learning classifiers (including random forest and XGBoost) with LASSO feature selection

4 Jul 2022The First Affiliated Hospital of Zhengzhou University; Zhongnan Hospital of Wuhan University

Machine-learning selection identifies a six-metabolite signature for one-year cardiovascular events after PCI in type 2 diabetes

VSURF feature selection prioritized six serum metabolites—ADPR, 1-methylnicotinamide, D-ribose 5-phosphate, nicotinamide, L-tryptophan and phosphatidylcholine PC(36:2)—for one-year major adverse cardiovascular events (MACEs) after PCI in patients with type 2 diabetes. Random-forest and XGBoost models were tested in a nested case-control study and an independent prospective cohort of 301 patients; in the external cohort, reported AUCs were 0.92 and 0.94 for MACEs, respectively. The study also linked NAD+ biosynthesis perturbation to altered bioenergetics, inflammation and proliferation in high-glucose-cultured human aortic smooth-muscle cells.

Technology: VSURF random-forest variable selection; random forest and XGBoost classifiers
