3 Nov 2026Yale University; Meta Reality Labs Research

GlucoAgent: Personalized Glucose Forecasting from Multimodal Personal Context

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding / tested result: GlucoAgent uses an LLM agent to write and run code over each participant’s glucose, meal and personal-context data. On CGMacros it achieved 22.5 mg/dL average RMSE versus 23.5 for Chronos-2; the larger multimodal-context gain was in post-meal forecasts. This is forecasting evaluation, not demonstrated clinical benefit.

Technology: GlucoAgent, an LLM agent that writes and runs code for multimodal glucose forecasts

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution

Limitation / still unknown
The study evaluates the reported models and datasets only; broader generalization and real-world deployment outcomes were not established.

1 Nov 2026Yale University; Amsterdam UMC; Amsterdam Cardiovascular Sciences

Deep learning on ECG images to prioritize testing for transthyretin amyloid cardiomyopathy

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A locally deployable AI-ECG model was developed at Yale New Haven Health (28 174 ECGs, 11 291 patients, 293 with ATTR-CM) and temporally validated. Internal AUROC was 0.84 with sensitivity 0.72 and specificity 0.86; across 5 external cohorts AUROCs ranged from 0.78 to 0.89, and in 3 screening cohorts (n = 121-645) from 0.76 to 0.91. Sequential AI-ECG then AI-enabled echocardiography raised positive predictive value from 0.24 to 0.66 and lowered sensitivity from 0.84 to 0.68. The study was retrospective and diagnostic; the authors state intended use and calibration need prospective evaluation.

Technology: Deep-learning AI-ECG model applied to routine ECG images or raw 12-lead signals to flag ATTR-CM

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Model flags patients with possible ATTR-CM from ECG images for confirmatory testing.
Test and result
Temporal and external retrospective validation against cardiac amyloid radionuclide imaging or biopsy.
Limitation / still unknown
Prospective benefit, calibration and intended use not tested; screening cohorts small; full text not reviewed.

30 Jul 2025Yale University

Connectome-based modelling links trauma-predictive brain networks to acute stress response

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Connectome-based predictive modelling predicted past trauma exposure from functional connectivity in 170 adults. The trauma-predictive network was attenuated after an acute stress task in a subsample of 92 participants and under hydrocortisone versus placebo in an independent crossover study of 27. The method is a linear-regression-based approach and the samples are modest.

Technology: Connectome-based predictive modelling

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Connectome-based predictive model of trauma exposure from functional brain connectivity.
Test and result
Prediction of past trauma exposure from connectivity, and network response to acute stress (n = 92) and hydrocortisone (n = 27).
Limitation / still unknown
Modest sample sizes; causal and clinical meaning not established; full text not reviewed.

27 Mar 2024Yale University

Neural network standardizes tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte scoring in head and neck cancer

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A neural network classifier, NN3-17, trained in QuPath to identify tumor cells, lymphocytes, fibroblasts and other cells on H&E sections, produced three predefined TIL variables. These were tuned on 154 head and neck squamous cell cancer cases and validated in two independent cohorts totalling 234 cases, where they were independently prognostic in HPV-positive and HPV-negative cancers after adjustment for clinicopathologic factors. The study is retrospective and the abstract gives no hazard ratios.

Technology: NN3-17 neural network cell classifier in QuPath producing electronic TIL variables

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Test and result
Two independent validation cohorts (234 cases) with Cox regression adjusted for clinicopathologic factors.

25 Jul 2023Yale University

Deep learning detects left ventricular systolic dysfunction from ECG images

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A convolutional neural network was trained on 385,601 ECGs with paired echocardiograms from Yale New Haven Hospital to detect reduced ejection fraction from ECG images in multiple formats. Internal AUROC was 0.91, and six external sets across US hospitals and a Brazilian cohort gave AUROCs of 0.90-0.95. An ECG flagged as suggestive of dysfunction carried more than 27-fold higher odds of dysfunction. This is a retrospective screening-performance study; full text was not reviewed.

Technology: Convolutional neural network applied to 12-lead ECG images in multiple formats

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Detects a reduced left ventricular ejection fraction from photographed or plotted ECG images.
Test and result
Internal validation plus six external ECG image sets from Cedars-Sinai, Yale outpatient clinics, community hospitals and the Brazilian ELSA-Brasil cohort.
Limitation / still unknown
Prospective clinical benefit of ECG-image screening; full text not reviewed.

31 May 2023Yale University

Deep-learning B-line ratings show 2-zone lung ultrasound matches 8-zone in heart failure

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A previously published deep-learning algorithm rating B-line severity from 0 to 4 was adapted to hand-held ultrasound videos from a prospective study of 205 subjects (5728 videos). Bland-Altman analysis showed excellent agreement between 2-zone and 8-zone averages, with a mean absolute difference of 0.03 (95% CI -0.01 to 0.07) overall and 0.01 in the confirmed heart-failure subgroup (147 subjects).

Technology: Previously published deep-learning algorithm rating B-line severity on ultrasound videos

24 Feb 2020Yale University

AI reread of head CT scans reported negative finds 16 unreported intracranial hemorrhages

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An FDA-approved convolutional neural network was applied retrospectively to 5,585 head CT scans reported as negative, from 6,565 consecutive scans at eight imaging sites. It flagged 28 scans, and three neuroradiologists confirmed hemorrhage in 16 that had not been reported, a radiologist false-negative rate of 1.6%. Review was retrospective, the reference was consensus reading, and AI performance on true negatives was not assessed.

Technology: FDA-approved convolutional neural network detecting intracranial hemorrhage on non-contrast head CT

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A convolutional neural network screened reported-negative scans to surface hemorrhages the original readers missed.
Test and result
The AI flagged 28 of 5,585 negative-by-report scans, and consensus review of three neuroradiologists judged 16 to show missed hemorrhage.
Limitation / still unknown
Whether prospective AI peer review changes outcomes is not tested. Abstract-based; full text not reviewed.
