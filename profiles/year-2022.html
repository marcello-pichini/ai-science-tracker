Results were modest and did not establish cost savings, better patient outcomes or universal superiority over clinical screening.

Technology: Random forest classification with training-set undersampling

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

The learned RF combined questionnaire and demographic predictors to classify measured previously unrecognized elevated blood pressure, enabling a specific retrospective clinical-screening validation and its observed sensitivity–specificity trade-off.
Test and result
From 37,110 campaign participants in 2015–2019, the analysis retained 20,206 adults without known or treated hypertension, including 4,192 newly detected cases defined by measured blood pressure. Data were randomly split into 14,144 training and 6,062 validation records; hyperparameter search used training cross-validation and resampling was applied to training data. The selected undersampled random forest reported sensitivity 0.818, specificity 0.629 and AUC 0.816.

Technology: 3D convolutional neural network and ridge-regression connectome predictive modeling

Editorial relevance 33 medicine Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A fitted CNN compressed activation maps into learned mediators optimized through the path model; ridge-regression connectome modeling supplied the second application. Conventional path analysis and bootstrapping tested the resulting associations.
Test and result
Pain models used 209 training participants across seven studies and a 75-person eighth-study evaluation, with activation patterns related to heat intensity and pain ratings. The pain-model association was absent for aversive sound trials in that evaluation; leave-study-out mediation was significant for five of eight pain datasets. A separate HCP analysis used 558 training and 240 test participants, identifying connectivity patterns jointly related to fluid intelligence and working-memory accuracy.
Limitation / still unknown
These are statistical mediation associations, not established neural causal pathways. Training Methods explicitly evaluated iteration-specific mediation on test data and selected the most stable indirect effect; those test results are therefore not a wholly untouched final confirmation. The pain data had previously been analyzed with a different mediation method, and familiar pain regions were recovered alongside exploratory differences. SHAP localization and its arbitrary threshold do not establish independent biological mechanisms. The primary preprint API dates version1 to October 11, 2022, but its full result tables were not read; journal-specific claims are not assigned that earlier date.
