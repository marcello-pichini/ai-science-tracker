Integrated proteomics and metabolomics with LASSO nominate a four-analyte early IgA-nephropathy plasma panel

A study of biopsy-confirmed IgA nephropathy (IgAN) integrated plasma proteomics and untargeted metabolomics and used LASSO logistic regression to select PRKAR2A, IL6ST, SOS1 and palmitoleic acid as candidate discriminators from healthy controls. The panel had AUC 0.994 in the training set and 0.977 in the test split; ELISA assays in a separate 9-case/10-control sample set yielded an AUC of 0.91. Proteomic and metabolomic analyses also indicated complement/immune activation and altered energy and amino-acid metabolism in the studied cases.

Technology: LASSO logistic regression (glmnet), with tenfold cross-validation and additional random-forest/SVM model checks

A machine-learning-guided, chemist-in-the-loop screen predicted electrochemical reactivity for 38,865 potential partners with 1,4-dicyanobenzene (DCB). Unsupervised seismic clustering reveals distinct drainage signals at Gornergletscher

An unsupervised analysis of five weeks of seismic records from Gornergletscher, Switzerland, separated cryoseismic signals into clusters associated with the 2007 Gornersee lake flood and with afternoon/evening meltwater flow. One high-energy, broadband noise cluster began about two days before the reported onset of subglacial flooding; the authors suggest it captures the earlier initiation of englacial and subglacial drainage and improves the flood chronology.

Technology: SpecUFEx unsupervised feature extraction: non-negative matrix factorization, hidden Markov modeling, principal component analysis, and k-means clustering

Deep-kernel-learning-guided STEM-EELS measurements identified a low-energy edge-localized plasmon response in the layered antiferromagnet MnPS₃, distinct from the higher-energy bulk plasmon response.
