AlphaProteo: AI-designed protein binders

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A system for designing high-affinity protein binders, tested against seven targets, achieved 88% laboratory success on BHRF1 and bindings 3 to 300 times stronger than the best existing methods. It produced no valid binders for TNFα; strong binding is only a first step.
