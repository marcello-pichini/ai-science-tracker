Structure-based virtual screening identifies mammalian enzymes catalyzing the second step of carnitine biosynthesis

The OSMES workflow screened AlphaFold-modeled vitamin-B6-dependent enzymes by docking the substrate into active sites and ranking catalytically favorable conformations. It identified human SHMT1/2 as enzymes with hydroxytrimethyllysine-aldolase activity, validated in vitro; mouse Tha1 showed much higher catalytic efficiency, supporting a species difference in the pathway. The authors infer that humans may have lost a dedicated gene, with SHMT activity partly compensating.

Technology: OSMES (One Substrate–Many Enzymes Screening), automated structure-based docking/ranking workflow using AlphaFold models
