Instantaneous voice-synthesis neuroprosthesis for a man with ALS

A brain-computer interface decoding 256 microelectrodes in the ventral precentral gyrus synthesized the participant's intended voice causally, with a reported latency of about 10 ms per decoding step. Listeners understood almost 60% of synthesized words, compared with 4% when he was not using the device, and he could modulate intonation and sing short melodies. Caveat: a single participant with ALS, within the BrainGate2 trial, and the authors describe the technology as early stage.

Technology: Deep neural network speech decoder for intracortical recordings
