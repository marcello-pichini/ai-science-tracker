A retrospective prostate-cancer messaging study finds different strengths in GPT-4 and clinician replies

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A retrospective study compared clinical-team replies with a GPT-4-based bot for 158 prostate-cancer patient inquiries. Clinicians rated team replies higher on completeness, correctness and clarity, while bot replies scored higher on empathy and more often required major editing. Four LLM graders favored bot replies and showed lower inter-grader agreement than clinician graders. The reviewed publisher abstract describes an evolving rubric and response-quality evaluation, without establishing patient outcomes, prospective workflow utility or clinical safety effects; full Methods were inaccessible.

Technology: GPT-4-based patient-message drafting bot and LLM-based graders

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Simplified medical reports improve comprehension in a randomized reader study

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A Mainz study randomly assigned 576 adult participants to an original or GPT-4-simplified version of one of five German medical documents. Among 439 completed comprehension questionnaires, simplified texts generally produced more correct answers, with the pathology comparison not statistically significant. This measures immediate understanding of selected, researcher-checked texts, not improved treatment adherence or health outcomes. Single-site recruitment, incomplete questionnaires and only one text pair per document type limit generalization.

Technology: GPT-4 text simplification

4 Dec 2025University of Oxford; UK AI Security Institute; London School of Economics; Stanford University; MIT

The levers of political persuasion with conversational AI

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In three large experiments with 76,977 participants, 19 LLMs argued 707 British political positions in dialogue. Persuasiveness was driven mainly by information density, while tailoring to user traits added little, and post-training for persuasion raised effectiveness by up to 50%. The most persuasive models also produced the most inaccurate claims. Caveat: the persuasion effects are measured in survey experiments, and accuracy was tested on a subset of claims.

Technology: 19 open and closed LLMs (including GPT-4.5, GPT-4o, Grok-3)
