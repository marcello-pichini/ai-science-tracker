8 May 2024ChemCrow group (Nature Machine Intelligence)

ChemCrow: a language-model agent with chemistry tools

An agent with 18 tools and GPT-4 planned and carried out the synthesis of DEET and three thiourea organocatalysts on a cloud platform, and guided discovery of a chromophore absorbing at 336 nm against a 369 nm target; evaluated on 14 cases by four expert chemists. Hallucinations, limited tasks and dual-use risks; 12 of 18 tools were released.

Technology: ChemCrow (GPT-4 with tools)

20 Dec 2023Carnegie Mellon University and Emerald Cloud Lab

Coscientist: autonomous chemical research with language models

A GPT-4-guided system designs, plans and runs experiments using web search, documentation, code and laboratory automation, performing six tasks including optimization of palladium cross-coupling reactions. The abstract does not make clear which tasks used real hardware; only the abstract was reviewed.
