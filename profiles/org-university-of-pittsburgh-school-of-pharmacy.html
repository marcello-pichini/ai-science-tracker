AI-guided HuJ3 mutation improves HIV-1 gp120 binding and neutralization in assays

The IsAb2.0 workflow, combining AlphaFold-Multimer complex modeling with FlexddG mutation scoring, proposed five substitutions to improve the binding of humanized J3 nanobody HuJ3 to HIV-1 gp120. Of these, E44R experimentally improved binding (ELISA EC50 1.696 nM) and neutralization relative to HuJ3; the other proposed substitutions did not consistently improve binding, and T99R failed to express. The gain did not restore neutralization to the parental llama J3 level.

Technology: IsAb2.0 antibody-design protocol integrating AlphaFold-Multimer 2.3/3.0, SnugDock, alanine scanning and FlexddG
