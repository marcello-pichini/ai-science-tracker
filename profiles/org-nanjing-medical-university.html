2 Nov 2026Nanjing Medical University, School of Public Health, Department of Epidemiology and Center for Global Health; UK Biobank

LightGBM feature selection identifies a four-protein panel associated with incident chronic kidney disease

In a prospective UK Biobank cohort of 27,429 participants without baseline chronic kidney disease, the study evaluated 2,920 plasma proteins and found 205 associated with incident CKD after protein-wide Cox regression with Bonferroni correction. LASSO reduced these to 23 candidates and LightGBM feature selection identified EDA2R, GDF15, HAVCR1 and SCARB2 as a four-protein panel. The protein panel alone had an AUC of 0.796 versus 0.805 for demographic variables; adding the proteins to the demographic model raised AUC to 0.830 in the study data.

Technology: Least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO) and Light Gradient Boosting Machine (LightGBM) feature selection

6 Apr 2026Nanjing Medical University; Jiangsu Provincial Center for Disease Control and Prevention; Southeast University; China University of Mining and Technology

Stability-based machine learning selects IL-8 and CCL3 as a two-protein serum signature for early silicosis

A stability-based feature-selection workflow combining LASSO, random forest and SVM recursive feature elimination identified serum IL-8 and CCL3 as a two-protein signature distinguishing Stage I silicosis from controls. The study used a discovery cohort of 57 cases and 57 matched controls, then assessed the signature in an independent, unmatched cohort of 40 cases and 40 controls. The article reports validation AUC 0.973, specificity 95.0% and sensitivity 77.5%; both proteins were lower in silicosis cases.

Technology: Stability-based ensemble feature selection using LASSO, random forest and SVM recursive feature elimination, followed by logistic regression
