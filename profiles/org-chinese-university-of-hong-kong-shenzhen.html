27 Apr 2020Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen; Shenzhen University; Hubei University

Adaptive cloud laboratory reveals optical chirality in perovskite nanocrystals

An automated laboratory used measured feedback and fitted local optimization models to search for optically active CsPbBr3 nanocrystals. Spectroscopy measured circular dichroism and imaging informed a proposed defect-related explanation. The composition was known; the contribution is an experimentally characterized optical property and its optimization. The proposed mechanism and future device applications are not independently established.

Technology: SNOBFIT adaptive optimization with locally fitted quadratic models
