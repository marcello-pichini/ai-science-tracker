Anderson's 2014 commutative algebra problem resolved by Rethlas and Archon

An informal reasoning agent (Rethlas) and a formal verification agent (Archon) resolve an open problem in commutative algebra posed by D. D. Anderson in 2014, as a counterexample. The Lean 4 formalization has about 19,450 lines and was produced in roughly 80 agent hours, with the code public on GitHub. Caveat: arXiv preprint; the Lean check covers the formal statement as written.

Technology: Rethlas (informal agent) with Archon (formal Lean agent)

Machine-learning screening identified experimentally active pore-forming peptides

Machine-learning models and membrane-related filters were used to prioritize natural peptide sequences for testing. The study reports seven experimentally active peptides; three showed broad-spectrum activity and pore-forming evidence in the reported membrane assays. Peptide 615 had already been disclosed in a Peking University patent published in March 2025, before the related preprint and journal article. This entry represents one study campaign and makes no first-disclosure or sequence-novelty claim. Assay results do not establish clinical safety or efficacy.

Technology: ProtBERT-BFD sequence embeddings with a support-vector classifier and membrane-interaction, secondary-structure and contact-map predictions

7 Aug 2025Peking University; Peking University Third Hospital; Tsinghua–Peking University Joint Center for Life Sciences; Nanchang University

BEAUT uncovers new gut-microbial bile-acid enzymes and the novel bile acid 3-acetoDCA

The BEAUT workflow combines ESM-2 protein-language-model embeddings with a neural classifier and active-site-pocket-similarity data augmentation to search gut microbial proteins for bile-acid enzymes. The authors predicted over 600,000 candidate enzymes and experimentally tested selected predictions; assays identified new bile-acid activities including MABH and ADS. ADS produces 3-acetoDCA, a previously undescribed bile-acid carbon skeleton, reported in human feces and shown to alter microbial composition and promote Lactobacillus-associated effects in experimental studies.

Technology: BEAUT, ESM-2 protein-language-model embeddings plus dense neural-network classifier, augmented using active-site-pocket similarity

5 Jun 2025Peking University; Michigan State University

Topological learning and simulations predict lithium superionic-conductor candidates

A multiscale topological-learning workflow combined algebraic-topology descriptors, unsupervised clustering and ab initio molecular-dynamics simulations to screen lithium-containing structures. The study reports 14 candidate conductors meeting computational thresholds for diffusion barriers and electrochemical stability. The reported conductivities are simulated, with ambient values estimated from elevated-temperature trajectories. Some candidates had prior experimental reports or patents cited by the authors; those are not experimental validation produced by this AI workflow. Many candidates remain untested.

Technology: Multiscale topological learning; persistent homology; affinity propagation clustering; ab initio molecular dynamics
