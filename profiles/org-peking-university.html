7 Aug 2025Peking University; Peking University Third Hospital; Tsinghua–Peking University Joint Center for Life Sciences; Nanchang University

BEAUT uncovers new gut-microbial bile-acid enzymes and the novel bile acid 3-acetoDCA

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The BEAUT workflow combines ESM-2 protein-language-model embeddings with a neural classifier and active-site-pocket-similarity data augmentation to search gut microbial proteins for bile-acid enzymes. The authors predicted over 600,000 candidate enzymes and experimentally tested selected predictions; assays identified new bile-acid activities including MABH and ADS. ADS produces 3-acetoDCA, a previously undescribed bile-acid carbon skeleton, reported in human feces and shown to alter microbial composition and promote Lactobacillus-associated effects in experimental studies.

Technology: BEAUT, ESM-2 protein-language-model embeddings plus dense neural-network classifier, augmented using active-site-pocket similarity

5 Jun 2025Peking University; Michigan State University

Topological learning and simulations predict lithium superionic-conductor candidates

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A multiscale topological-learning workflow combined algebraic-topology descriptors, unsupervised clustering and ab initio molecular-dynamics simulations to screen lithium-containing structures. The study reports 14 candidate conductors meeting computational thresholds for diffusion barriers and electrochemical stability. The reported conductivities are simulated, with ambient values estimated from elevated-temperature trajectories. Some candidates had prior experimental reports or patents cited by the authors; those are not experimental validation produced by this AI workflow. Many candidates remain untested.

Technology: Multiscale topological learning; persistent homology; affinity propagation clustering; ab initio molecular dynamics

Editorial relevance 60 materials Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Topological feature extraction and unsupervised learning screened structures and prioritized candidate clusters; AIMD simulations evaluated specific candidate transport claims.
Test and result
The study used ab initio molecular-dynamics simulations to estimate conductivity, diffusion barriers and electrochemical stability for 45 selected candidate materials and reports 14 meeting its computational thresholds.
Limitation / still unknown
Conductivities are computational estimates, including Arrhenius extrapolation from elevated-temperature simulations; source-cited experimental reports for some materials predate or are independent of this AI workflow, and other candidates remain untested.
