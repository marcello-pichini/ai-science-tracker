14 May 2025Meta AI

OMol25 and UMA: quantum-chemistry data and a universal model for atoms

Meta released Open Molecules 2025, which it calls the largest and most varied set of high-accuracy quantum calculations for biomolecules, metal complexes, and electrolytes (about six billion compute hours), and UMA, an interatomic potential trained on over 30 billion atoms. The post gives no numerical accuracy values.

Technology: UMA (interatomic potential), OMol25

19 Nov 2024Meta AI, University of Toronto, and VSParticle

OCx24: experimental catalysts for evaluating prediction models

A dataset from Meta FAIR, the University of Toronto, and VSParticle covers over 600 mixed-metal catalysts made from 13 elements and tested under the same conditions, alongside analysis of over 19,000 materials. Hydrogen-evolution predictions flagged platinum-containing alloys as promising, although platinum was absent from training data. Fewer than 25% of produced catalysts matched the target; this was a pilot.

Technology: Catalyst prediction models (Open Catalyst)

18 Oct 2023Meta AI

Meta AI: near-real-time image decoding from brain activity

A three-part system (image encoder, brain encoder, decoder) reconstructs viewed images from magnetoencephalography signals at each time point, with signals better aligned to DINOv2 than the other models compared. Images remain imperfect; fMRI decodes better; the page gives no numerical metrics; preprint.

Technology: Magnetoencephalography decoder

1 Nov 2022Meta AI

ESM Metagenomic Atlas: over 600 million protein structures

Meta AI published predicted structures for almost the entire MGnify90 database (over 600 million), generated in about two weeks on roughly 2,000 GPUs with a 15-billion-parameter language model, up to 60 times faster than previous methods. These are predictions, and high-confidence structures are a much smaller subset.

Technology: ESMFold (15-billion-parameter language model)
