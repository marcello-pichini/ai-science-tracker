Deep learning of salivary-gland biopsies identifies acinar CD8 T-cell infiltration associated with Sjögren disease

A deep-learning model trained on digitized minor salivary-gland biopsy slides from five expert centres and externally validated at a sixth classified focus score and Sjögren disease in a retrospective cohort of 545 participants. Explainable-model analysis identified a histological pattern of CD8-positive T cells around acinar epithelial cells associated with Sjögren disease. External-validation AUROC was 0.89 for disease classification and 0.92 among patients negative for anti-SSA antibodies.

Technology: Deep-learning histopathology classifier with Shapley-value interpretation
