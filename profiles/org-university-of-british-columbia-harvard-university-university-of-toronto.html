13 May 2020University of British Columbia; Harvard University; University of Toronto

Self-driving laboratory optimizes organic hole-transport thin films

A closed-loop robotic platform autonomously varied film composition and processing to maximize the hole mobility of organic hole-transport materials for perovskite solar cells. The optimization was demonstrated experimentally on the robot, and the paper is peer-reviewed in Science Advances. Numeric mobility gains were not verified, so the size of the improvement is unconfirmed.

Technology: Closed-loop self-driving laboratory with autonomous optimization
