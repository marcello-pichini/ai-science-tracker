AlphaTensor: new algorithms for matrix multiplication

A reinforcement-learning agent found matrix-multiplication algorithms better than known ones for many sizes (for example, 76 multiplications instead of 80 for 4×5 by 5×5). Algorithms optimized for hardware were 10–20% faster on V100 GPUs and TPU v2. The improvement over Strassen applies in a finite field.

Technology: AlphaTensor (based on AlphaZero)
