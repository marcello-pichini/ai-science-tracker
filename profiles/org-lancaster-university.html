AI-guided proof search solves five open problems in Banach space theory

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A research project used ChatGPT 5.5 Pro and Codex agents to develop proof ideas and proofs for five Banach-space problems. The resulting paper gives affirmative solutions to questions on toroidal Elton–Odell separation, non-Calkin unital Banach algebras, strict cosingularity and adjoints, weakly compact basis factorization, and primariness of Lp(L1). The authors report that models generated the core mathematics; human mathematicians checked and refined the proofs. The results are presented in an unrefereed preprint, and the authors take responsibility for correctness.

Technology: ChatGPT 5.5 Pro and Codex agents using an automated open-problem search pipeline
