Adaptive learning selected an additive for a magnesium-air battery

An iterative machine-learning adaptive-design workflow selected 2,3-dihydroxynaphthalene for a lab-made (Mg-0.2Ca)-air battery. The authors report a cell voltage of 1.82 V and 3.37 kWh kg⁻¹ specific energy calculated from total Mg-anode mass loss, not whole-cell or pack mass. The publisher abstract reports 83% anodic utilization, while the full article reports 85% at a stated 0.5 mA cm⁻² condition; this discrepancy is not resolved here and the utilization figure is omitted. The authors also state that the surrogate model overestimated performance predictions for this additive.

Technology: Iterative adaptive design with SOAP descriptors and a KPCovR surrogate model
