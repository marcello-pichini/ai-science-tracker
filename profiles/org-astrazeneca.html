12 May 2025AstraZeneca; Tempus AI

Contrastive-learning biomarker discovered from phase 2 data retrospectively generalizes to phase 3 immunotherapy outcomes

The Predictive Biomarker Modeling Framework (PBMF), an ensemble neural network trained with a treatment-versus-control contrastive objective, identified a composite clinicogenomic biomarker using phase 2 POPLAR data for atezolizumab versus docetaxel. When applied retrospectively to the phase 3 OAK trial, its biomarker-positive group had a treatment-versus-control overall-survival hazard ratio of 0.59 (95% CI 0.47–0.74); an interpretable distilled decision tree produced HR 0.55 (95% CI 0.43–0.70). The tree used baseline ctDNA maximum allele frequency, lesion diameter, and mutation status in KMT2D, TSC1, ATM, PDGFRA and LRP1B.

Technology: Predictive Biomarker Modeling Framework (PBMF), an ensemble of neural networks using a contrastive treatment/control objective; knowledge-distilled decision tree

16 May 2024AstraZeneca; University of Cambridge; Google DeepMind

Machine-learning-designed peptides show dual GCGR and GLP-1R activity

An ensemble of multitask convolutional neural networks trained on 125 peptide analogues guided optimization of 15 synthesized variants. The study reports three potent dual agonists; P3 improved potency at both glucagon and GLP-1 receptors compared with the best dual agonist in the training set. These are cell-based receptor assays only. The paper reports weak performance for designing GCGR-selective peptides and establishes no clinical safety or therapeutic efficacy.

Technology: Ensemble multitask convolutional neural networks for peptide sequence optimization
