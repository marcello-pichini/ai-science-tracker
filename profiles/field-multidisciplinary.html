9 Oct 2026Dana-Farber Cancer Institute; Massachusetts Institute of Technology

AI-assisted study finds limited code sharing in clinical prediction research

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An AI-assisted empirical review found code-sharing statements in 482 of 3,967 clinical prediction studies (12.2%) and assessed 380 repositories. GPT-5.2 screened papers, extracted links and characterized repository features; human reviewers checked 500 papers and 35 repositories. The sample covered studies citing TRIPOD or TRIPOD+AI that were accessible through PubMed Central Open Access, so it does not represent all clinical research. Automated errors remain possible, and repository features do not establish executable reproducibility. The journal article was published October 9, before the November 19 MIT announcement.

Technology: GPT-5.2 (2025-12-11)

19 May 2026Google Research

Empirical Research Assistance writes expert-level scientific code across six domains

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Google's Empirical Research Assistance (ERA), a Gemini-based tree-search system, reaches expert-level performance on six benchmarks spanning genomics, public health, satellite imagery, neuroscience, time-series forecasting and mathematics. Its state-level U.S. hospital admission forecasts for flu, COVID-19 and RSV rank at or near the top of CDC leaderboards. Results are computational and checked against public benchmarks and leaderboards, with no wet-lab validation; exact benchmark scores were not visible in the sources read.

Technology: Empirical Research Assistance (ERA), Gemini-based tree search

4 Dec 2025University of Oxford; UK AI Security Institute; London School of Economics; Stanford University; MIT

The levers of political persuasion with conversational AI

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In three large experiments with 76,977 participants, 19 LLMs argued 707 British political positions in dialogue. Persuasiveness was driven mainly by information density, while tailoring to user traits added little, and post-training for persuasion raised effectiveness by up to 50%. The most persuasive models also produced the most inaccurate claims. Caveat: the persuasion effects are measured in survey experiments, and accuracy was tested on a subset of claims.

Technology: 19 open and closed LLMs (including GPT-4.5, GPT-4o, Grok-3)

5 Nov 2025Edison Scientific

Kosmos: an AI scientist for autonomous discovery

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Each run reads about 1,500 papers and executes 42,000 lines of analysis code; outside scientists judged 79.4% of its conclusions accurate. Of seven discoveries, three reproduce results already obtained by humans and four are new. Limitations include spurious correlations, a cost of about $200 per run, and a six-month work gain estimated by beta users.

Technology: Kosmos (agents with a structured world model)

Family Kosmos: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 73 multidisciplinary Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: prospective or independent check. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
An AI scientist runs about 12-hour cycles of data analysis and literature search (about 1,500 papers and 42,000 lines of code per run), linked by a structured world model, and generates traceable reports; humans set the objectives.
Test and result
Independent scientists judged 79.4% of Kosmos's conclusions accurate; three discoveries reproduced findings from preprints or unpublished manuscripts that Kosmos did not access at runtime; the four new discoveries are wet-lab validation in progress, with one checked in an independent scRNA-seq dataset.
Limitation / still unknown
The authors state that Kosmos chases statistically significant but irrelevant findings, that about one in five statements is not accurate, and that the human time-saving estimates are intrinsically suspect.

2 Jul 2025Helmholtz Munich; Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics; University of Tübingen

Centaur: a foundation model that predicts human behaviour across psychology experiments

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Fine-tuning Llama 3.1 70B on Psych-101, a dataset of about 10 million human choices from 160 psychology experiments, produced a model that predicts and simulates human behaviour in experiments expressed in natural language, including tasks it had not seen. The authors report that its internal representations align better with human neural activity after fine-tuning. Caveat: a 2025 critique argues that its language comprehension is limited and that it fails to follow altered instructions.

Technology: Centaur (Llama 3.1 70B fine-tuned on Psych-101)

15 Nov 2024Stanford University; Google DeepMind; Northwestern University; University of Washington

Generative agents built from interview transcripts simulate 1,000 people

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: LLM agents built from participants' full interview transcripts reproduced their General Social Survey answers at about 85% of the rate at which participants reproduced their own answers two weeks later, a normalized score. They also matched personality and economic-game results (normalized correlations of about 0.80 and 0.66) and replicated four of five behavioural experiments. Caveat: arXiv preprint, not peer reviewed, and the results are normalized rather than raw accuracy.

Technology: Generative agent architecture with LLM and full interview transcripts

Editorial relevance 60 multidisciplinary Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
An LLM was the engine of agents that answer questions on behalf of individuals, grounded in their self-report data.
Test and result
Agents built from interview and survey data of 1,052 Americans were scored on held-out General Social Survey items against participants' own two-week test-retest consistency, and on personality, economic-game and experiment replications.
Limitation / still unknown
Preprint, not peer reviewed; there is no dedicated limitations section, and combining interviews and surveys gave only modest gains over either source.

13 Sep 2024MIT Sloan School of Management; Cornell University; American University

Conversations with an AI chatbot durably reduce conspiracy beliefs

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Across experiments with more than 2,000 participants, three dialogues with GPT-4 reduced belief in the targeted conspiracy theory by about 20%, and the effect persisted for two months and spilled over to unrelated conspiracies. A professional fact-checker rated 99.2% of 128 sampled chatbot claims as true. Caveats: lab setting, the chatbot did not reduce belief in true conspiracies, and most participants still held the hoax belief.

Technology: GPT-4 (DebunkBot dialogue system)

25 Sep 2023Carnegie Institution for Science

Machine learning on pyrolysis GC-MS data separates biotic from abiotic samples with about 90 percent accuracy

Source check: Needs source check. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Cleaves et al. trained machine-learning models on pyrolysis-GC-MS data from living cells, fossil organic material, carbon-rich meteorites and synthetic organics (134 samples per secondary sources) to classify biogenicity of contemporary and ancient samples at about 90 percent accuracy without identifying individual compounds. Accuracy comes from held-out data within the same sample set; no blind or flight validation and no detection of extraterrestrial life is reported. Abstract-level review.

Technology: Supervised machine-learning classifiers on chromatographic and mass-spectral peak patterns

Test and result
Train and test subsets of the sample set gave about 90 percent accuracy (per abstract).
Limitation / still unknown
