Machine learning reveals incomplete concordance between DNASE1L3 genotype and cfDNA phenotype

Classifiers and UMAP analyzed 129,676 prenatal-test cfDNA samples, with genotype checks in 169 selected samples. The authors report fragmentomic profiles resembling DNASE1L3 R206C homozygotes in some non-homozygotes, while some confirmed homozygotes initially lacked the profile and acquired it in later pregnancies. This extends an already-known genotype association with phenotype discordance and within-person variation; it does not establish the mechanism or clinical utility.

Technology: Supervised classifiers; UMAP
