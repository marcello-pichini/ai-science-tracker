5 Mar 2026George Mason University; Villanova University; Emory University; Saint Joseph’s University

Generative design and screening produced experimentally tested antimicrobial QACs

A topology-aware generative workflow and activity predictor prioritized quaternary ammonium compounds (QACs) for synthesis and testing. Across two workflows the authors report 29 selected compounds characterized and 11 QACs designated as novel, with measured minimum inhibitory concentrations of 1–32 μM against bacterial pathogens. Earlier same-team conference disclosures had already described AI-generated QAC design and synthesis; this record covers the later paper’s bounded tested results and does not claim the first AI-generated QACs or universal chemical novelty. Activity and cytotoxicity remain assay-specific and do not establish clinical use.

Technology: Topology-aware QAC generative model with structure/activity prediction and filtering (Q-HERO)
