CochleaNet: deep learning analysis of cochlear connectomics and gene therapy

Source check: Needs source check. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The journal abstract describes CochleaNet, a deep-learning framework for cleared-cochlea light-sheet images, covering hair cells, spiral ganglion neurons, synapses, tonotopy and gene expression. Trained on mouse data, it was applied to gerbil and lower-resolution mouse and marmoset data. The authors say they analyzed neuron types and validated gene-therapy products but the abstract does not identify the biological or therapy-comparison findings. The date represents the 2026-10-23 journal version; an earlier preprint appeared on 2025-11-17.

Technology: CochleaNet: deep learning framework for volumetric image segmentation and analysis
