1 May 2023University of Texas at Austin

An MRI decoder turns brain activity into text

A noninvasive decoder reconstructs continuous text from fMRI that closely, and sometimes precisely, matches the original meaning in about half of trials (after dozens of hours of training with podcasts; it also works on silent videos). Published in Nature Neuroscience; it works only with cooperative participants and is not word-for-word transcription.

Technology: Semantic decoder from fMRI

27 Apr 2022University of Texas at Austin

FAST-PETase: an AI-engineered enzyme degrades PET waste

A machine-learning model suggested PETase mutations, then tested on 51 post-consumer containers, fibers and fabrics. FAST-PETase degrades PET below 50 °C, in some cases down to monomers within 24 hours; production at scale and environmental use still needed development.

Technology: MutCompute / 3D convolutional network
