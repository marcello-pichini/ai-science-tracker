6 May 2025University of Texas at Austin; University of Houston

A dense nodal array and machine learning reveal a blind fault zone beneath the San Fernando Valley

A machine-learning workflow analyzed one month of recordings from a 140-station nodal array and located 62 earthquakes, including 36 not in the regional catalog. Their distribution exposes a previously unidentified 5.3 × 4 km blind fault zone about 5 km beneath the southern San Fernando Valley; the small-event cluster is rare in the regional catalog despite a 2014 Mb 4.4 earthquake in the zone.

Technology: Previously developed machine-learning seismic phase picker, fine-tuned on similar waveforms, with two-step event association

21 Aug 2023University of Texas at Austin; Dell Medical School; Columbia University; New York Genome Center

Deep-learning knee imaging phenotypes reveal osteoarthritis genetic and fracture associations

Using knee DXA scans from the UK Biobank, a ResNet-101 model identified 1,931 more probable knee-osteoarthritis cases than health-record coding and a U-Net segmentation model estimated minimum joint-space width (mJSW). In 29,257 participants, the quantitative mJSW phenotype had substantially higher SNP heritability than the binary phenotypes and yielded 18 genome-wide-significant loci, compared with one for the image-based binary classifier and six for ICD-10 coding; only one mJSW locus was already significant in a large prior knee-OA GWAS. The AI-derived mJSW phenotype was also associated with fracture history in an epidemiological analysis, a signal not seen with the binary case labels.

Technology: ResNet-101 convolutional neural network for knee-OA classification and U-Net image segmentation for joint-space measurement

1 May 2023University of Texas at Austin

An MRI decoder turns brain activity into text

A noninvasive decoder reconstructs continuous text from fMRI that closely, and sometimes precisely, matches the original meaning in about half of trials (after dozens of hours of training with podcasts; it also works on silent videos). Published in Nature Neuroscience; it works only with cooperative participants and is not word-for-word transcription.

Technology: Semantic decoder from fMRI

29 Mar 2023University of Texas at Austin and Ames National Laboratory

Machine-learning-guided calculations predict four rare-earth-free magnetic Fe–Co–Si compounds

A crystal-graph neural network screened 357,480 hypothetical Fe–Co–Si structures; adaptive genetic search and density-functional calculations then identified four predicted ternary structures with calculated saturation polarization ≥1.0 T, easy-axis anisotropy ≥1.0 MJ/m³, and Curie temperature >840 K. Their calculated energies lie within 70 meV/atom of the ternary convex hull, supporting but not proving experimental accessibility. The paper proposes the structures for synthesis; it does not experimentally demonstrate them.

Technology: Crystal graph convolutional neural network (CGCNN) with an adaptive genetic algorithm

FAST-PETase: an AI-engineered enzyme degrades PET waste

A machine-learning model suggested PETase mutations, then tested on 51 post-consumer containers, fibers and fabrics. FAST-PETase degrades PET below 50 °C, in some cases down to monomers within 24 hours; production at scale and environmental use still needed development.
