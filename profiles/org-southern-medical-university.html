20 Nov 2026Southern Medical University; Hong Kong Baptist University; General Hospital of Southern Theater Command

Learned prognostic screening links miR-130b-3p and NR3C2 to a putative kidney-cancer relationship

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An integrative analysis of clear-cell renal cell carcinoma used learned prognostic models and co-expression/target-gene intersections to prioritize NR3C2 and examine its relationship with miR-130b-3p. In 786-O cells, inhibiting miR-130b-3p increased NR3C2 mRNA and reduced proliferation, migration and invasion. The authors present the combined relationship as an extension of earlier work on each marker. Direct binding and NR3C2-dependent mediation were not established; the assays used one cell line. Prognostic evaluation was retrospective and subject to model-selection and feature-screening optimism, and it does not establish clinical benefit.

Technology: Integrative learned survival models; StepCox forward selection and LASSO-Cox modeling

Editorial relevance 47 medicine Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Learned survival-model comparison and selection supplied a prognostic gene signature intersected with co-expression and predicted target-gene sets to prioritize NR3C2; LASSO-Cox modeling combined the two markers.
Test and result
TCGA-KIRC model development used 516 patients/171 deaths and 99 candidate model combinations; the selected model retained 41 genes. Integrating its genes with co-expression modules and predicted targets identified NR3C2. Cell experiments in 786-O cells linked miR-130b-3p inhibition to increased NR3C2 mRNA and reduced proliferation, migration and invasion.
Limitation / still unknown
Direct miRNA binding and NR3C2 mediation remain untested. The authors identify prior work on both markers. GSE40914 performance helped select the model, so it is not fully independent validation; feature screening was not nested, and the model had about 4.2 events per retained variable. Generalization and therapeutic benefit are not established.

30 Jun 2026Southern Medical University; Guangdong Provincial People’s Hospital; Hong Kong Polytechnic University; Shantou University; Chinese University of Hong Kong

AI-measured retinal vessel features show modest associations with later colorectal cancer

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Deep-learning retinal vessel measurements were associated with later colorectal-cancer diagnoses in 55,798 UK Biobank participants. Four vascular features retained associations after clinical adjustment, with modest effect sizes. This retrospective analysis of longitudinal records does not establish causal cancer mechanisms, validated screening accuracy or benefit from changing cancer-surveillance decisions.

Technology: RMHAS deep-learning retinal vascular segmentation and automated feature measurement

Editorial relevance 40 biology Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The pretrained RMHAS deep-learning pipeline segmented retinal structures at cohort scale, enabling quantitative vascular features linked to subsequent recorded cancer diagnoses. Cox regression alone and generic vessel-segmentation benchmarks are not treated as the enabling AI result.
Test and result
The source analyzed 55,798 initially colorectal-cancer-free participants with usable fundus images, documenting 652 incident cancers over median 11.32-year follow-up. RMHAS segmented arteries, veins and optic disc before quantifying vascular geometry. After adjustment, four features remained associated with incidence; greater arterial-tortuosity variability had a per-standard-deviation hazard ratio 1.03, 95% CI 1.01–1.06. Sensitivity analyses were reported, including exclusion of early diagnoses.
Limitation / still unknown
Observational associations have modest effect sizes and may reflect shared risk factors, reverse causation or residual confounding. Multiple-feature screening and selective imaging eligibility limit interpretation; 93% of participants were White. This was retrospective analysis despite prospective original cohort collection. No independent screening model, incremental clinical utility, causal angiogenesis or reduced cancer mortality was established by the represented finding. Supplementary results were not independently reconstructed.

Machine learning selects cuproptosis-related genes for keloid diagnosis

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: SVM, random forest, GLM and XGBoost models trained on two GEO datasets selected 10 cuproptosis-related genes for keloid diagnosis from 110 WGCNA candidates. Diagnostic capability was checked in three independent GEO datasets and by RT-qPCR, Western blotting and immunohistochemistry on keloid tissue. The abstract gives no AUC or sample sizes, and mechanism claims such as MAPK involvement are not verified.

Machine learning-based integrated identification of predictive combined diagnostic biomarkers for endometriosis

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Transcriptomic machine-learning analyses nominated five genes as candidate endometriosis diagnostic biomarkers. A five-gene model reached AUC 0.836 in a test dataset, with checks on additional public datasets. This is dataset-level biomarker evidence rather than a demonstrated clinical diagnostic intervention.

Technology: Methods apply multiple learned feature-selection and classification algorithms to transcriptomic data to identify endometriosis-associated diagnostic genes.

30 Apr 2022Southern Medical University; Sun Yat-sen University

Learned stromal expression score associates with colon-cancer outcomes

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A 31-gene score selected using elastic-net and penalized survival learning was associated with worse relapse-free and overall survival in retrospective colon-cancer data. A higher score also showed adverse-outcome associations in a separate TCGA cohort. The result is a clinical expression–outcome association, not proof of chemotherapy resistance or a treatment-selection benefit.

Technology: Elastic-net learning; LASSO-Cox feature selection

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Elastic-net models learn stromal-group discriminators, and repeated penalized Cox fits select the expression features that constitute the measured-outcome-associated score.
Test and result
The score was developed using five GEO datasets totalling 990 cases and examined in 382 TCGA-COAD cases. Outcome-informed resampling and expression cutoffs selected genes; the final score subtracts the mean expression of negatively signed selected genes from the positive group. The source reports adjusted survival associations.
Limitation / still unknown
Prognostic outcomes inform development, selection and cutoffs; derivation-cohort associations are not independent validation. Treatment comparisons are observational and do not identify causal benefit or intrinsic resistance. Computational drug-response and pathway predictions are not measured clinical response. Supplemental implementation and treatment-confounding details were not inspected; separate experimental mechanisms are not represented.
