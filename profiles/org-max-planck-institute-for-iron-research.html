6 Oct 2022Max Planck Institute for Iron Research; Delft University of Technology

Machine learning and DFT discover high-entropy Invar alloys

A closed-loop active-learning framework combining machine learning, density-functional theory and thermodynamic calculations screened millions of compositions. Of 17 new alloys processed and characterized, two high-entropy Invar alloys showed very low thermal expansion, about 2 x 10^-6 per kelvin at 300 K. Only the top-ranked candidates were synthesized, so the hit rate is small.

Technology: Closed-loop active learning combining machine learning, density-functional theory and thermodynamic calculations
