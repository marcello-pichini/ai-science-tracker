REAP-guided engineering improves P450 BM3 activity

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: REAP combines a protein-language-model ranking predictor with active selection and automated build-and-test cycles. The study reports a P450 BM3 quintuple variant with 57-fold higher product yield than the engineered FL#62 starting scaffold in its high-throughput assay. This is a variant-specific comparison with an already engineered scaffold, not a 57-fold gain over wild type. A separate sortase result is not included in this bounded draft.

17 Sep 2024Tianjin University

Descriptor-guided Co–Co and Co–Ir oxygen electrocatalysts

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Physically informed feature selection derived an ARSC descriptor from calculated catalyst properties to guide oxygen-reaction candidate design. The study reports synthesis and alkaline testing of two carbon-supported samples: ORR half-wave potentials of 0.941 and 0.937 V and OER overpotentials of 330 and 340 mV at 10 mA cm−2. These are laboratory measurements; structural analyses support but do not prove every proposed active-site configuration.

Technology: ARSC descriptor; feature selection

Editorial relevance 60 chemistry Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

Calculated dual-atom catalyst descriptors selected candidates followed by synthesis and electrochemical testing.
Test and result
Publisher PDF reports synthesized Co–Co and Co–Ir samples with stated ORR and OER tests.
Limitation / still unknown
Prior composition overlap and independent replication remain open; specific active-site configuration is not directly resolved for every site.
