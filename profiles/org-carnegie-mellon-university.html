26 May 2025Carnegie Mellon University; University of Toronto Structural Genomics Consortium; University of British Columbia; University of Ottawa and collaborators

Active learning discovers eight experimental binders for the LRRK2 WDR domain

A free-energy molecular-dynamics and machine-learning active-learning workflow optimized compounds against the understudied LRRK2 WDR domain. Of 35 analogs experimentally tested, eight showed measurable binding confirmed by surface plasmon resonance and, for fluorinated compounds, an orthogonal 19F-NMR assay; measured dissociation constants ranged from 18 to 230 μM.

Technology: Active-learning-guided relative-binding-free-energy (AL-RBFE) workflow using molecular-dynamics thermodynamic-integration calculations and an ML affinity-prediction model

18 Oct 2022Carnegie Mellon University; University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill; Enamine

Generative learning selects experimentally active EGFR inhibitor analogs

A recurrent generative model refined by reinforcement learning selected compounds for an EGFR enzyme assay. Four of 15 predicted-active compounds were active, including two with nanomolar inhibition; five predicted-inactive controls were inactive. The experimentally tested compounds were commercially obtainable analogs of a known privileged scaffold, not a wholly new drug scaffold or demonstrated cellular therapy.

Technology: Recurrent neural generator with reinforcement learning

27 Sep 2022Carnegie Mellon University

Robot-guided optimization identifies six non-aqueous electrolytes with improved fast-charge performance

A closed-loop platform paired Clio, an automated electrolyte-formulation and conductivity-testing robot, with Dragonfly Bayesian optimization. In 42 experiments over two workdays, it selected six non-aqueous electrolyte formulations from a LiPF₆ and EC/EMC/DMC design space. The team tested these formulations in 220 mAh graphite‖NMC pouch cells; all showed improved fast-charging capability relative to a baseline electrolyte selected in advance from the same design space. The search reached its reported target about six times faster than random search using the same automated setup.

Technology: Dragonfly Bayesian optimization coupled to the Clio robotic electrolyte experimentation platform

  • Still unknown: This is a search within a defined design space of LiPF₆ in EC/EMC/DMC solvents, not a claim that the constituent chemicals or all six electrolyte recipes were wholly unprecedented. The optimization target was bulk ionic conductivity, with fast-charge cell performance as a subsequent validation. 23 Dec 2020Carnegie Mellon University

Autonomous machine-learning experiments identify a dual-anion aqueous sodium electrolyte with a wider stability window

A robotic electrochemistry stand coupled to Bayesian optimization tested 140 aqueous electrolyte formulas over 40 hours and selected a non-intuitive sodium mixture of NaClO4 and NaNO3. On platinum, the blend had a wider electrochemical stability window than the NaClO4 baseline despite lower total salt concentration; follow-up tests reported a 24 mV increase in the OER potential at the stated leakage-current criterion and 58% lower current density at 2 V. The result is a measured electrolyte formulation under the paper’s electrochemical protocols, not evidence of a commercial battery or field-ready cell.

Technology: Dragonfly Bayesian optimization software coupled to the Otto robotic electrochemistry test stand
