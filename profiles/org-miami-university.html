Unsupervised machine learning reveals previously missed hydraulic-fracturing seismicity in Live Oak County

An unsupervised repeating-signal detector, combined with template matching, exposed small earthquake bursts in a region of the Eagle Ford shale where conventional catalogs had detected little seismicity. The authors report that the new events temporally coincided with hydraulic-fracturing activity, and their regional analysis found detectable seismicity began after injection flux exceeded about two million barrels per month despite similar cumulative injection volume; this supports, but does not prove, a causal role for injection rate and is limited by sparse station coverage.

Technology: Repeating Signal Detector (RSD), an unsupervised machine-learning waveform-family detector
