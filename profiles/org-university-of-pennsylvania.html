5 Jun 2024University of Pennsylvania and collaborators

AMPSphere: antimicrobial peptides from the global microbiome

Machine learning identified 863,498 candidate sequences in microbial genomes and metagenomes. Of 100 synthesized peptides, 63 inhibited at least one of the eleven strains tested; some showed activity in animal models. The catalog contains candidates, not hundreds of thousands of clinically validated antibiotics.

16 Aug 2023University of Pennsylvania

Neanderthal and Denisovan peptides reconstructed as antibiotic candidates

AI analysis of proteins from extinct hominins identified sequences that were then synthesized and tested against pathogens in culture and in mice. Some peptides showed antimicrobial activity; these are preclinical results, with no demonstration of safety or efficacy in humans.
