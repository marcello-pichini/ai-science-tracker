12 Jul 2025University of Pennsylvania

Deep learning mines venom proteomes for antimicrobial peptides active in vitro and in a mouse infection model

The APEX deep-learning model screened 16,123 venom proteins by generating and scoring 40,626,260 venom-encrypted peptides. After activity prediction and filtering for low similarity to known antimicrobial peptides, the authors selected 58 for experiments; 53 showed antimicrobial activity against at least one tested pathogen. Three selected peptides reduced bacterial counts after topical treatment in a mouse Acinetobacter baumannii skin-abscess model; the wolf-spider-derived Arachnoserver-5 peptide produced a reported three-log reduction versus untreated controls through day four, without significant body-weight change under the study conditions.

Technology: APEX deep-learning model for bacterial strain-specific minimum inhibitory concentration prediction

5 Jun 2024University of Pennsylvania; and collaborators

AMPSphere: antimicrobial peptides from the global microbiome

Machine learning identified 863,498 candidate sequences in microbial genomes and metagenomes. Of 100 synthesized peptides, 63 inhibited at least one of the eleven strains tested; some showed activity in animal models. The catalog contains candidates, not hundreds of thousands of clinically validated antibiotics.

Technology: Machine learning for antimicrobial peptides

16 Aug 2023University of Pennsylvania

Neanderthal and Denisovan peptides reconstructed as antibiotic candidates

AI analysis of proteins from extinct hominins identified sequences that were then synthesized and tested against pathogens in culture and in mice. Some peptides showed antimicrobial activity; these are preclinical results, with no demonstration of safety or efficacy in humans.

Technology: AI for molecular de-extinction
