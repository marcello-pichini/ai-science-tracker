Active learning selects an oxygen electrode for protonic ceramic cells

A supervised DFT-trained model and Gaussian-process experimental loop guided selection of BaCo0.8Nb0.1Zr0.1O3−δ. AI screening identifies poly-PDO for recyclable barrier packaging

Machine-learning models screened approximately 7.4 million virtual ring-opening polymerization polymers for properties relevant to recyclable food packaging. Researchers experimentally evaluated existing poly(p-dioxanone), whose barrier performance had not previously been reported in the reviewed source, and reported water-barrier properties and 95% monomer recovery. The result is a specific material characterization and packaging candidate, not a newly invented polymer or demonstrated commercial package. The arXiv v1 already reports the measured outcome; later journal publication is the same study.

29 Jul 2024Georgia Institute of Technology; University of Pittsburgh; University of California, Santa Barbara

MODIFY-guided library screening identifies a cytochrome c variant active in both carbon–boron and carbon–silicon bond formation

Researchers applied MODIFY, an ensemble model combining protein language models and sequence-density models, to design a diverse high-fitness library for Rhodothermus marinus cytochrome c. Experimental screening identified variant MLYPPT, which catalyzed both non-natural borylation and silylation: reported total turnover numbers were 2,880 and 2,740, with enantiomeric ratios of 94:6 and 97:3, respectively. The same library yielded distinct high-performing variants for each reaction.

Technology: MODIFY (ML-optimized library design with improved fitness and diversity)

  • Tested: Experimental screening identified variant MLYPPT, which catalyzed both non-natural borylation and silylation: reported total turnover numbers were 2,880 and 2,740, with enantiomeric ratios of 94:6 and 97:3, respectively.

19 Jul 2024Georgia Institute of Technology; and University of Connecticut

AI-guided polyVERSE discovery yields a high-temperature dielectric polymer with 8.3 J cm⁻³ energy density

The polyVERSE workflow used reaction-aware polymer generation and machine-learning property screens to identify PONB-2Me5Cl, which the researchers synthesized and cast into films. Experimental characterization measured a discharged energy density of 8.3 J cm⁻³ at 200 °C and a breakdown field of 750 MV m⁻¹; the paper compares this energy density with commercial polymer dielectrics at the same temperature. This is a laboratory polymer-film result, not a packaged capacitor or aerospace-ready device.

Technology: polyVERSE with four polymer graph neural-network property predictors

29 Mar 2024Georgia Institute of Technology; University of Science and Technology of China; North Carolina State University; Chinese Academy of Sciences

Deep-learning-enhanced Sparta earthquake catalog resolves a shallow multifault rupture

For the 2020 Mw 5.1 Sparta, North Carolina sequence, an EQTransformer-based catalog combined with matched-filter detections and differential-time relocations contains 1,761 high-precision events, about 5.8 times the original NEIC count. The denser locations resolve several shallow clusters and support the authors’ interpretation that the mainshock nucleated near the intersection of a blind strike-slip fault and a reverse fault associated with the surface rupture; moment-tensor inversion places its centroid at about 1.3 km depth.

Technology: Earthquake Transformer (EQTransformer) deep-learning phase/event detector, followed by matched-filter detection and differential-time relocation

13 Apr 2023Georgia Institute of Technology; Institute for Advanced Study and NSF NOIRLab

PRIMO: a sharper image of the M87 black hole

A machine-learning method trained on more than 30,000 simulated black-hole images reconstructed M87* from 2017 Event Horizon Telescope data, using the array’s full resolution: the ring is about half as wide as in the 2019 image. It fills gaps with patterns learned from simulations, without quantified uncertainty.

Technology: PRIMO (dictionary learning)

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Not reported
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: The PRIMO reconstruction, built from over 30,000 simulated black-hole images, was checked for consistency with the 2017 EHT data and the 2019 image; no held-out tests are described.
  • Still unknown: The article states that generating the image requires assuming an appropriate form of the missing information and describes no quantified uncertainties; implications for the black hole are framed as future possibilities.
  • AI role: A machine-learning method trained on simulated images filled gaps in the 2017 EHT data to reconstruct the M87* image.
