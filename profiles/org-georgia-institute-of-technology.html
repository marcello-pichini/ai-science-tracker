13 Apr 2023Georgia Institute of Technology, Institute for Advanced Study and NSF NOIRLab

PRIMO: a sharper image of the M87 black hole

A machine-learning method trained on more than 30,000 simulated black-hole images reconstructed M87* from 2017 Event Horizon Telescope data, using the array’s full resolution: the ring is about half as wide as in the 2019 image. It fills gaps with patterns learned from simulations, without quantified uncertainty.

Technology: PRIMO (dictionary learning)

26 Aug 2021Georgia Institute of Technology

Machine learning and experiments yield tolerant polymer dielectrics

An informatics screen of over 13,000 known polymers, combined with phenomenological models, selected representative dielectric candidates for experimental testing. Two of the proposed polymers were synthesized and reported as tolerant to field and temperature extremes. The validation set is very small, and the exact property values were not verified in this search.

Technology: Machine learning and informatics models for polymer dielectric screening
