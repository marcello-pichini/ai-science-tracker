LLM-guided iterative optimization yields a previously unreported Rb3BiI6 perovskite-inspired thin film

A closed-loop materials workflow combined an LLM that distilled synthesis knowledge from papers, high-throughput hyperspectral imaging, and multi-objective Bayesian optimization with experimental feedback. In a demonstration, the team synthesized previously unreported Rb3BiI6 thin films and validated optimized films with optical bandgap analysis and X-ray diffraction. LLM-assisted initialization produced more Pareto-optimal samples and higher hypervolume than matched Latin-hypercube initialization in the reported paired campaign.

Technology: LLM-assisted synthesis-knowledge extraction and initialization with multi-objective Bayesian optimization and high-throughput hyperspectral imaging

20 Mar 2026Massachusetts Institute of Technology; NSF AI Institute for Artificial Intelligence and Fundamental Interactions; CERN; ALEPH Collaboration archival data

Claude Code produces the first primary Lund jet-plane density measurement in electron–positron collisions

A Claude Code agent pipeline analyzed archived 1994 ALEPH hadronic-Z data and reported the first primary Lund jet-plane density measurement in e⁺e⁻ collisions. Its unfolded 57-bin fiducial result shows the expected triangular QCD radiation structure and a shallow low-transverse-momentum turnover; the integrated mean primary-emission count is 4.751 ± 0.224 per hemisphere. This is one new measurement, not a claim of discovering new particles or physics beyond QCD.

Technology: Claude Code agents orchestrated by the Just Furnish Context (JFC) framework

Technology: OptPDE coefficient optimization with the CQFinder conserved-quantity search

Deep learning designs abiotic miniproteins that improve antisense-oligonucleotide nuclear delivery

A generator–predictor–optimizer deep-learning workflow trained on quantitative delivery assays was used to design abiotic Mach miniproteins. Twelve selected predicted constructs were synthesized/tested; the strongest library construct improved phosphorodiamidate morpholino oligomer (PMO) delivery nearly 20-fold over unconjugated PMO in the standardized HeLa assay, while the authors describe the best designs as up to 50-fold more effective than prior polypeptide-based variants. PMO–Mach3/4 also produced dose-dependent reporter-protein restoration in skeletal muscle, diaphragm and heart in transgenic mice after a single intravenous dose.

Technology: Deep-learning generator–predictor–optimizer workflow: nested-LSTM sequence generator, CNN activity predictor on residue chemical fingerprints, and evolutionary optimization
