GPT-6 Pro proves an optimal anticoncentration bound for polynomial Littlewood–Offord inequalities

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The source presents a proof discovered autonomously by GPT-6 Pro establishing an O_d(r^{-1/2}) zero-probability bound for degree-d multilinear polynomials with r disjoint degree-d monomials, improving the prior bound by removing a polylogarithmic factor and resolving the Nguyen–Vu conjecture. The key argument also proves a total-influence estimate for bounded-degree rational functions, resolving a recent conjecture of Kothari, Kovacs-Deak, Wang and Yang; these are one connected paper-level result, not separate entries.

Technology: OpenAI GPT-6 Pro

17 Sep 2026Xinglong Wu (author affiliation not stated in the arXiv record)

GPT-5.6 constructs a counterexample to Talagrand’s operator cotype problem

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The paper gives a negative answer, already for q=2, to Talagrand’s question whether an operator’s Rademacher cotype is universally bounded by its Gaussian cotype and its (q,1)-summing norm. For every sufficiently large power-of-two dimension it constructs a Banach space and operator violating such a universal bound. The same construction also yields a negative answer to Kwapień’s decomposition problem.

Technology: ChatGPT GPT-5.6

Family GPT-5: profile · models page

The author states that the counterexample was discovered during discussions with GPT-5.6. The paper presents an explicit Walsh–Hadamard-based construction and proof; the author independently verified the arguments.
Test and result
