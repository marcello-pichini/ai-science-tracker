30 Oct 2026LHCb Collaboration; CERN

LHCb reports first single-experiment evidence for B⁰ → μ⁺μ⁻ decay

Using 18 fb⁻¹ of Run 3 data and earlier LHCb data, the collaboration reports first single-experiment evidence for the rare B⁰→μ⁺μ⁻ decay, with a combined significance of 3.2σ and measured branching fraction (1.14 +0.42/−0.39 stat. +0.06/−0.05 syst.)×10⁻¹⁰. A boosted decision tree classified candidates and suppressed combinatorial background in the fit. This is evidence below the 5σ discovery threshold; results agree with Standard Model expectations. The date is the original preprint submission; CERN announced the result on 3 November 2026.

Technology: LHCb boosted decision tree (BDT) candidate classifier and multivariate trigger classifiers

18 Sep 2026LHCb Collaboration; CERN

LHCb observes the doubly charmed Ωcc⁺ baryon with an 8.7σ global significance

Using 6.3 fb⁻¹ of 13.6 TeV proton-proton collisions recorded in 2024, LHCb observed an Ωc⁰π⁺ mass peak with 8.7σ global significance and identified it as the weakly decaying doubly charmed Ωcc⁺ baryon. The reported mass is 3725.9 ± 1.0 (stat) ± 0.2 (syst) ± 0.4 (lifetime) ± 0.6 (external) MeV/c². A boosted decision tree suppressed random-track combinatorial background; the result is a preprint submitted to Physical Review Letters.

Technology: Boosted decision tree classifier (TMVA toolkit)

29 Jul 2025LHCb Collaboration; CERN

LHCb makes the first high-significance observation of the rare Σ⁺ → pμ⁺μ⁻ decay

Using 5.4 fb⁻¹ of 13 TeV proton-proton collision data collected in 2016–2018, LHCb observed 237 ± 16 Σ⁺ → pμ⁺μ⁻ decays and measured a branching fraction of (1.08 ± 0.17) × 10⁻⁸. The dimuon spectrum showed no evidence of resonant structures, and the results are compatible with Standard Model expectations. This is the first high-significance observation; earlier HyperCP and LHCb Run 1 results were evidence only.

Technology: LHCb boosted decision tree (BDT) classifier

16 Jul 2025LHCb Collaboration; CERN

LHCb observes CP violation for the first time in baryon decays

Using 9 fb⁻¹ of LHCb Run 1 and Run 2 data, the collaboration measured a phase-space-averaged CP asymmetry of (2.45 ± 0.46 ± 0.10)% in Λb⁰ → pK⁻π⁺π⁻ and its conjugate, a 5.2σ observation. In the resonance-dominated R(pπ⁺π⁻)K⁻ region, the asymmetry was (5.4 ± 0.9 ± 0.1)%, 6.0σ from zero. Machine-learning BDT selection suppressed combinatorial background; neural-network PID variables mitigated particle misidentification.

Technology: Boosted decision tree event classifier; neural-network particle-identification variables
