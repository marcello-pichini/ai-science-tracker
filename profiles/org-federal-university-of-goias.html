2 Jul 2025Federal University of Goiás

Machine-learning screening selects compounds active against Candida

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Random-forest prediction and chemical filters helped narrow commercial compounds for experimental testing. Of 17 selected compounds, the paper reports 11 with inhibitory activity against Candida albicans at tested concentrations. Compound 17 had an IC50 of 0.071 μM and MIC of 4.5 μM, compared with fluconazole values of 0.39 μM and 0.78 μM, respectively; its lower IC50 but higher MIC does not establish superiority. The reported selectivity-index definition appears inconsistent with the tabulated values, so that index is not repeated here. Cell assays do not establish clinical efficacy or safety.

16 Dec 2024Federal University of Goiás; University of Brasília

Multitask neural screening identifies compounds active against Leishmania infantum in vitro

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Multitask neural classification and regression screened approximately 1.3 million purchasable compounds for Leishmania infantum activity. After physicochemical and human structural filtering, 20 selected compounds were tested; nine inhibited promastigotes in vitro. Three had more favorable source-reported selectivity, with LC-15 showing an IC50 of 1.05 µM and selectivity index 30.9. LC-15 was already disclosed in a 2023 antitrypanosomal study; this entry represents the Leishmania result, not a new chemical entity. Intracellular parasite activity, animal efficacy and a proposed proteasome mechanism were not established.

Technology: Multitask deep neural network classification and regression; molecular fingerprints

Editorial relevance 47 biology Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Multitask DNN classifiers and regressors transferred information across three Leishmania species to prioritize untested molecules; physicochemical filters, similarity analysis and human inspection then selected 20 for experiments.
Test and result
Twenty model-prioritized, subsequently filtered compounds were evaluated against Leishmania infantum promastigotes. Nine were active; cytotoxicity and selectivity measurements were reported for selected active hits. Three had source-described selectivity indices above 9.
Limitation / still unknown
No intracellular-amastigote efficacy, animal therapeutic efficacy or clinical benefit is established. Six active compounds had less favorable selectivity. LC-15 is the same chemical entity disclosed in the 2023 antitrypanosomal study, whose supplement also reports the same MRC-5 cytotoxicity value; independently new cytotoxicity evidence is not inferred. Its proteasome mechanism remains a hypothesis, and the observed hit rate is not a controlled comparison with conventional screening.

16 Mar 2021Federal University of Goiás

AI-assisted virtual screening identifies a dual-stage antimalarial candidate

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An AI-assisted virtual-screening cascade using shape-based and machine-learning models prioritized potential Plasmodium kinase inhibitors. Eight virtual hits were experimentally evaluated; LabMol-167 inhibited P. berghei ookinete conversion and P. falciparum blood-stage parasites at nanomolar concentrations and showed low cytotoxicity in mammalian cells. A possible additional PK5 target was proposed from computational target fishing and docking, not demonstrated experimentally. This is a preclinical hit result and does not establish therapeutic efficacy. The article appeared online on 2021-03-16.

Technology: AI-assisted virtual screening combining shape-based and machine-learning models
