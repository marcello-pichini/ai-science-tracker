10 Dec 2025University of Cape Town

PICS-assisted survey reported 12 new Large Magellanic Cloud pulsars

A MeerKAT search of 18 additional Large Magellanic Cloud pointings reported 12 new radio pulsars. Folded candidates were classified using PICS, a deep-learning image classifier; high-ranking candidates were inspected, and the reported pulsar discoveries were identified from that candidate set. The entry represents the 12 new search detections, not the separate timing solutions for seven pulsars reported in the same paper, which include earlier Paper I discoveries. A journal correction changed the reported folded signal-to-noise for PSR J0537−6957 from 26.1 to 21.7; the publisher states the conclusions were unaffected.

Technology: Pulsar Image-based Classification System (PICS), a deep-learning model for image-based candidate classification

9 Aug 2024University of Cape Town

PICS-assisted survey reported seven new Large Magellanic Cloud pulsars

A MeerKAT survey of its first four Large Magellanic Cloud pointings reported seven new radio pulsars. The search pipeline applied the Pulsar Image-based Classification System (PICS), a deep-neural image classifier, to folded candidates and retained candidates above a PICS score threshold before human visual classification. The AI narrowed the candidate set; volunteers performed the final candidate classification and follow-up observations supplied additional checks. Six pulsars were detected again in follow-up; the incoherent-beam pulsar was not. A later paper reports 12 discoveries from additional pointings and timing follow-up of some Paper I pulsars, which are not additional discoveries in this entry.

Technology: Pulsar Image-based Classification System (PICS), a deep-neural image classifier for folded candidate plots

Relevance 53 physics Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: The survey reports seven new LMC radio pulsars. Folded candidates were screened using PICS scores and then visually classified; six of the seven were detected again in follow-up, while the incoherent-beam pulsar was not confirmed in follow-up.
  • Still unknown: PICS filtered candidates but humans classified the candidates as new pulsars. The one incoherent-beam pulsar lacked a follow-up detection in this paper. Later timing work on Paper I objects is not a separate discovery result.
  • AI role: PICS applied a pulsar-like score to folded candidate plots and filtered the survey candidate set before human visual classification; the paper does not attribute final discovery confirmation to the model alone.

Virtual screening identifies antiplasmodial beta-haematin inhibitor chemotypes

Molecular docking and Bayesian/SVM classifiers screened a ChemDiv 300k library. Researchers tested prioritized commercial compounds and reported coumarin and iminodipyridinopyrimidine chemotypes active against beta-haematin and whole-cell P. falciparum, including chloroquine-resistant parasites. This is one bounded in-vitro campaign, not clinical evidence.

Technology: Bayesian and support-vector classifiers; molecular docking
