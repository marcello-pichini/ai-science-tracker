25 Nov 2021RWTH Aachen University; University of Jyväskylä

Unsupervised machine learning identifies palladium catalysts that form dinuclear complexes

A workflow built on only five experimental data points screened 348 phosphine ligands and predicted ligands that favour dinuclear Pd(I) complexes over Pd(0) and Pd(II) species. The authors experimentally verified the predictions, including ligands that had never been synthesized before. The evidence is limited to the Pd speciation system studied, not a general catalyst performance gain.

Technology: Unsupervised machine learning workflow with clustering on generalized parameter databases and in silico data
