GPT-6 Astra proves a linear coloring bound for graphs excluding clique minors

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The authors present a proof of the Linear Hadwiger conjecture: there is a constant C such that every graph with no K_t minor is colorable with at most Ct colors. GPT-6 Astra developed the proof after the authors directed it to the dense case and then asked it to bridge existing bounds; the resulting proof combines several known frameworks with a new bootstrap argument. The result is an asymptotic linear bound, not the stronger Hadwiger conjecture χ(G)≤h(G).

Technology: OpenAI GPT-6 Astra (described in the paper as ChatGPT model Astra 6)

20 Aug 2026University of British Columbia

MACE-guided screening yields three corundum high-entropy oxides and a cation-ordered oxide

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: MACE-MP-0b2 interatomic-potential calculations screened 462 four- and five-cation trivalent-oxide compositions and selected 16 for synthesis. Experiments produced three phase-pure corundum high-entropy oxides—(Al,Cr,Fe,Ga)₂O₃, (Al,Cr,Fe,Rh,Sc)₂O₃ and (Cr,Fe,Ga,Rh,Sc)₂O₃—and a separate phase-pure cation-ordered (Al,Fe,Ga,Sc)₂O₃ oxide. The ordered compound crystallized in orthorhombic Pba2; its precise cation-site occupancies were not resolved conclusively by powder diffraction.

Technology: MACE-MP-0b2 machine-learning interatomic potential (MLIP)

Editorial relevance 60 materials Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The MLIP relaxed approximately 1,000-atom disordered supercells and estimated mixing enthalpy and atom-energy-distribution descriptors for the 462-composition search. The authors combined these with a stability metric to rank 16 synthesis candidates. Experimental powder-X-ray diffraction and Rietveld refinement then assessed the predicted materials, linking model-guided selection to the new synthesized compositions.
Test and result
MACE-MP-0b2 interatomic-potential calculations screened 462 four- and five-cation trivalent-oxide compositions and selected 16 for synthesis.
Limitation / still unknown
This is an unrefereed arXiv preprint. Only three of 16 tested compositions formed phase-pure corundum high-entropy oxides; most attempts produced competing phases, and rhodium-containing samples showed redox instability. Synthesis outcomes depended strongly on route: the three corundum HEOs were phase-pure only with one of solid-state or combustion synthesis. The ordered oxide’s structure is supported by powder diffraction/Rietveld refinement, but exact site preferences could not be conclusively determined from pXRD. The screening supports candidate ranking, not reliable prediction of phase purity or synthesis conditions for arbitrary HEOs.

27 Nov 2025University of British Columbia; University of Ottawa; Mila, Université de Montréal; Simon Fraser University

TARSA prioritizes peptides active against breast cancer cells

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: TARSA combines reinforcement learning and posterior sampling to search a 36-million-sequence library of experimentally resolved helical peptides. The study reports that 15 of 105 selected candidates showed cytotoxic activity against triple-negative breast cancer cells in vitro; the three leads were further tested against other breast cancer subtypes and peripheral blood cells. The source also reports toxicity against normal breast cells, so these findings do not establish therapeutic safety or clinical efficacy.

Technology: TARSA reinforcement learning; MCMC posterior sampling; learned cytotoxicity models

Editorial relevance 60 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
TARSA reinforcement learning and posterior sampling guide exploration of a very large peptide library, with learned potency/toxicity models used to select candidates for assays.
Test and result
105 selected peptides were experimentally evaluated; 15 showed activity in the reported breast-cancer-cell assays.
Limitation / still unknown
Cell-culture results do not establish therapeutic efficacy; the source reports normal-breast-cell toxicity, and independent replication remains unknown.
