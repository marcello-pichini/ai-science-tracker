GPT-6 Astra proves a linear coloring bound for graphs excluding clique minors

The authors present a proof of the Linear Hadwiger conjecture: there is a constant C such that every graph with no K_t minor is colorable with at most Ct colors. GPT-6 Astra developed the proof after the authors directed it to the dense case and then asked it to bridge existing bounds; the resulting proof combines several known frameworks with a new bootstrap argument. The result is an asymptotic linear bound, not the stronger Hadwiger conjecture χ(G)≤h(G).

Technology: OpenAI GPT-6 Astra (described in the paper as ChatGPT model Astra 6)

20 Aug 2026University of British Columbia

MACE-guided screening yields three corundum high-entropy oxides and a cation-ordered oxide

MACE-MP-0b2 interatomic-potential calculations screened 462 four- and five-cation trivalent-oxide compositions and selected 16 for synthesis. Experiments produced three phase-pure corundum high-entropy oxides—(Al,Cr,Fe,Ga)₂O₃, (Al,Cr,Fe,Rh,Sc)₂O₃ and (Cr,Fe,Ga,Rh,Sc)₂O₃—and a separate phase-pure cation-ordered (Al,Fe,Ga,Sc)₂O₃ oxide. The ordered compound crystallized in orthorhombic Pba2; its precise cation-site occupancies were not resolved conclusively by powder diffraction.

Technology: MACE-MP-0b2 machine-learning interatomic potential (MLIP)

Synthesis outcomes depended strongly on route: the three corundum HEOs were phase-pure only with one of solid-state or combustion synthesis.   • AI role: The MLIP relaxed approximately 1,000-atom disordered supercells and estimated mixing enthalpy and atom-energy-distribution descriptors for the 462-composition search. The authors combined these with a stability metric to rank 16 synthesis candidates. Experimental powder-X-ray diffraction and Rietveld refinement then assessed the predicted materials, linking model-guided selection to the new synthesized compositions.

13 May 2020University of British Columbia; Harvard University; University of Toronto

Self-driving laboratory optimizes organic hole-transport thin films

A robotic laboratory used model-based optimization to select thin-film compositions and processing conditions, execute experiments and learn from their measured optical and electronic properties. The reported demonstration optimized hole mobility in organic hole-transport materials used in perovskite solar cells and electronics. This description is limited to the paper abstract; it does not assert a numerical mobility gain.

Technology: Closed-loop self-driving laboratory with autonomous optimization
