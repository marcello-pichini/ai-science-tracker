21 Feb 2024Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory and Princeton University

Preventing tearing-mode instabilities in a tokamak with AI

A model trained only on past experimental data predicts instabilities up to 300 ms ahead, and an AI controller adjusted plasma shape and beam power to avoid them in a real DIII-D experiment. The authors call it a proof of concept on one machine and one type of instability; paper published in Nature on February 21, 2024.

Technology: Prediction and reinforcement-learning control
