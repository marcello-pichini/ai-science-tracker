cairo: Fixed-point floor negation overflow

cairo v1.18.4 · AI involvement attributed to Revelio using Claude Haiku 4.5 and Claude Sonnet 4.6

Finding and attribution

The Revelio paper identifies a integer overflow in cairo v1.18.4: fixed-point floor negation overflow. It lists the author-assessed severity as Moderate and says the finding was manually validated with a reproducible sanitizer-backed proof of vulnerability through a reachable project test harness. This is Table V item 7 of the seven-project real-world hunt.
