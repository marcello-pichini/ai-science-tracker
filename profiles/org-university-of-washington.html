Serine hydrolases designed from scratch

The April 2025 Science version describes RFdiffusion enzyme backbones and PLACER assessment of catalytic-state ensembles. Laboratory characterization found catalytic efficiency up to 2.2 × 10^5 M^-1 s^-1, crystal structures with C-alpha RMSD below 1 angstrom, and catalysts in five folds different from natural serine hydrolases. This summary follows the abstract and figure descriptions; an earlier bioRxiv version was posted in August 2024.
