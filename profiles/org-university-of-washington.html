25 Nov 2026University of Washington; Pacific Northwest National Laboratory

LLM-agent workflow reports new proofs on positivity problems in algebraic combinatorics

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A preprint describes PatternMiner, in which an LLM agent generates mathematical datasets, trains small models (MLPs, small transformers, trees), interprets them and formulates conjectures that are tested against counterexamples in mathematical software, applied to five positivity problems from Stanley's survey. The paper gives proofs, labelled LLM-generated, that for every n>=5 no fixed number of retained flag dimensions suffices for projection tests of Schubert structure-constant vanishing, an equivariant positivity inequality that proves Conjectures A and B of Chen et al. on equivariant nonvanishing, a character-row evaluation formula, a restricted case of Kazhdan-Lusztig combinatorial invariance, and Schur positivity for a family of immanant coefficients, supported by finite checks such as 776,891 coefficients over 1,587 shape instances. Proofs received an internal adversarial review only; no proof-kernel or independent mathematician check is reported, one decisive construction needed human redirection, and the comparison with direct LLM attempts is not compute-matched.

Technology: Unnamed LLM agent that trains and interprets small models (activation patching, probes, sparse autoencoders), proposes conjectures and attempts proofs, with counterexample searches in mathematical software.

19 Nov 2026University of Washington

Crystal structures confirm an RFdiffusion3-designed phosphotriesterase, ZAPP-1

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Two crystal structures of ZAPP-1, a phosphotriesterase designed de novo with RFdiffusion3, at 1.42 and 2.28 A closely match the design model (C-alpha RMSD 0.69-0.99 A). The higher-resolution structure shows the designed dinuclear Zn(II) site with carbamylated K16. Catalytic residues are near the designed geometry but flexible, and AlphaFold3 predicted the designed conformation with substrate bound. This follows an earlier biochemical paper; the enzyme hydrolyzes organophosphates, and the abstract suggests better active-site preorganization is needed.

Technology: RFdiffusion3 de novo enzyme design; AlphaFold3 conformation prediction

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Researchers fit scaling laws to safety probes trained on smaller language models. The fits predicted the error of the next larger model to within 0.02 in 52 of 72 comparisons at the largest label budget. They tested these held-out predictions in the Qwen2.5, Qwen3.5 and Gemma 4 families.

19 Oct 2026University of Washington; The Rockefeller University; École Normale Supérieure; CNRS; PSL University; Sorbonne University; Université Paris-Cité; Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center; Yale University

Immune-reactivity clusters reveal a gut microbiota association in mice

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Researchers grouped gut bacteria in existing mouse data using a fitted K-means model of measured T-cell reactivity. Among eight reactive clusters, greater estimated immune coverage was associated with smaller relative-abundance gains from week one to week two (R² 0.66). An analogous strain-level comparison gave R² 0.02. The preprint reanalyzed a defined microbial community across five biological replicates, so the result concerns a cluster-level association in that setting.

Technology: K-means clustering

Editorial relevance 47 biology PreprintPreprint Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
K-means learned groups from measured immune-reactivity profiles. Comparing abundance and immune coverage across those groups exposed an association that was weak at the individual-strain level.
Test and result
Using existing longitudinal measurements, the study compared estimated immune coverage with changes in transformed relative abundance at cluster and strain levels. Sensitivity checks removed individual clusters or biological replicates.
Limitation / still unknown
The result concerns one defined gut community in mice, with five biological replicates. The observational association cannot separate direct immune effects from ecological interactions, and applicability to human microbiota was not assessed. Estimated cluster coverage also uses baseline abundance weights, so shared-data dependence remains.

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The Daisy framework combines SEM-image interpretation with offline reinforcement learning trained on historical Ag-Bi-I thin-film conditions and microstructure measurements. For AgBiI4, the planner proposed a condition outside the recorded combinations, and a newly fabricated film showed no visible pinholes and mean grain size 14.5% above the historical mean for defect-free samples.
