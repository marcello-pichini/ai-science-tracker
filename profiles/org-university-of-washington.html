3 Jul 2025University of Washington

Offline reinforcement learning identifies an AgBiI4 film-processing condition

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The Daisy framework combines SEM-image interpretation with offline reinforcement learning trained on historical Ag-Bi-I thin-film conditions and microstructure measurements. For AgBiI4, the planner proposed a condition outside the recorded combinations, and a newly fabricated film showed no visible pinholes and mean grain size 14.5% above the historical mean for defect-free samples. Its grain distribution was comparable to the best historical sample, so the study does not show that the new condition outperformed the previous best or improved device performance.

Technology: Daisy image-analysis models and offline reinforcement-learning synthesis planner trained on historical microstructure data

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Daisy uses ML models to interpret SEM morphology and an offline reinforcement-learning planner trained on historical synthesis conditions to propose a processing condition that was then fabricated and measured.
Test and result
A newly fabricated AgBiI4 film under the RL-proposed processing condition showed 0% visible defect coverage and mean grain size 14.5% above the historical mean for defect-free samples; its grain distribution was comparable to the best historical sample.
Limitation / still unknown
This is a thin-film morphology result; the new condition did not outperform the best historical sample and device-level optoelectronic performance was not established.

18 Apr 2025University of Washington; University of California, Los Angeles; Howard Hughes Medical Institute

Serine hydrolases designed from scratch

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The April 2025 Science version describes RFdiffusion enzyme backbones and PLACER assessment of catalytic-state ensembles. Laboratory characterization found catalytic efficiency up to 2.2 × 10^5 M^-1 s^-1, crystal structures with C-alpha RMSD below 1 angstrom, and catalysts in five folds different from natural serine hydrolases. This summary follows the abstract and figure descriptions; an earlier bioRxiv version was posted in August 2024.

Machine-learning models guide design of two vitrimers

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

The primary preprint reports two synthesized vitrimers with higher measured glass-transition temperatures than the compared bifunctional transesterification vitrimers and demonstrates healability. 7 Aug 2023University of Washington

Machine learning guides engineering of fluorescent calcium indicators

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An ensemble of three regression models trained on experimentally derived GCaMP mutation libraries screened 1,423 uncharacterized variants. The study reports eGCaMP and eGCaMP+ with faster kinetics and larger fluorescence responses than previously published fast variants, and eGCaMP2+ with greater dynamic range than the tested sixth-, seventh- and eighth-generation indicators. The preprint reports cell and neuron assays. These are laboratory sensor results, not human or clinical validation. This is one study; the journal article is a later version.

Technology: Ensemble of three regression models
