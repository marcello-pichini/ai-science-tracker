8 Oct 2026ATLAS Collaboration; CERN

ATLAS graph neural network enables first LHC observation of single-top s-channel production

Using 140 fb⁻¹ of 13 TeV proton–proton collisions, ATLAS reports the first observation of single-top-quark s-channel production at the LHC, with 5.1σ observed (5.2σ expected) significance. The measured cross-section is 10.8 ± 1.5(stat) ± 1.6(syst) pb, consistent with the Standard Model. A graph neural network discriminant separates the signal from top-pair, W+jets and t-channel single-top backgrounds; the analysis also uses broadened event acceptance, improved b tagging and refined modeling.

Technology: Event-level graph neural network; ATLAS GN2 graph-transformer b-jet tagger

4 Aug 2026ATLAS Collaboration; CERN

Boosted decision tree enables first observation of two simultaneously longitudinally polarized Z bosons

Using 164 fb⁻¹ of 13.6 TeV proton–proton data and a statistical combination with the Run 2 measurement, ATLAS reports the first observation of ZZ production with both Z bosons longitudinally polarized, at 6.5σ observed (5.6σ expected). The combined signal strength is 1.22 ± 0.21; the Run 3 measurement also reports polarization fractions across the fiducial phase space and two four-lepton invariant-mass ranges, consistent with Standard Model predictions.

Technology: Boosted decision tree (BDT) classifier

30 Dec 2024ATLAS Collaboration; CERN Large Hadron Collider

OmniFold enables an unbinned 24-dimensional Z+jets cross-section measurement by ATLAS

Using 139 fb⁻¹ of 13 TeV proton-proton data, ATLAS simultaneously unfolded 24 correlated Z+jets kinematic observables with the machine-learning method OmniFold and released the particle-level result as an unbinned event dataset. The measured total fiducial cross section is 1,808 ± 42 fb; most illustrative differential bins have 3–5% total uncertainty, rising to about 15% in tails. The measurement is more precise than the compared predictions for many distributions, especially Sherpa. The event-level release also supports derived observables, including an infrared-and-collinear-safe τ₂₁ measurement in a specified fiducial region.

Technology: OmniFold iterative machine-learning unfolding

The paper validates closure on pseudo-data and reports its uncertainty components.

12 Jun 2023ATLAS Collaboration; CERN

ATLAS graph neural network enables first observation of four-top-quark production

Using 140 fb⁻¹ of 13 TeV proton–proton collisions, ATLAS observed simultaneous production of four top quarks in same-sign dilepton and multilepton events, with 6.1σ observed (4.3σ expected) significance. The measured cross-section is 22.5⁺⁶·⁶₋₅·₅ fb, consistent with the Standard Model prediction within 1.8σ. A graph neural network separated the four-top signal from dominant backgrounds in the signal extraction.

Technology: Event-level graph neural network built with graph_nets/TensorFlow

4 Aug 2022ATLAS Collaboration; CERN

ATLAS boosted decision trees enable first observation of triple-W boson production

In 139 fb⁻¹ of 13 TeV LHC data, ATLAS observed simultaneous WWW production at 8.0σ (5.4σ expected) and measured an inclusive cross-section of 820 ± 100(stat) ± 80(syst) fb. The analysis selected same-sign dilepton-plus-jets and trilepton final states. Two boosted decision trees—one per channel—improved signal/background separation and supplied distributions for the fit. The measured rate is about 2.6σ above the Standard Model prediction, not a significant discrepancy.

Technology: Two boosted decision tree classifiers for the dilepton and trilepton channels
