Development and validation of a prognostic model and risk calculator for the estimation of bipolar-spectrum disorder risk in hospitalised adolescents with non-psychotic/non-bipolar mental disorders

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Among 105 hospitalized adolescents followed longitudinally, 18 developed bipolar-spectrum disorder; the model identified mania-like symptom variables associated with onset and reported estimated risk through four years, with discrimination peaking around month 20. Leave-one-out cross-validation (AUC 0.72 at month 20) and bootstrap out-of-bag validation (AUC up to 0.86). This is internal validation in a small cohort with 18 transitions; external and outpatient validation are absent, and attrition limits generalizability.

Technology: Lasso-Cox machine-learning analysis uses baseline clinical and prodromal symptom characteristics from a prospective adolescent cohort to estimate risk of later bipolar-spectrum disorder.
