FerroAI guides discovery of a ferroelectric composition with high dielectric response

FerroAI, trained on text-mined phase-transition data, predicted composition–temperature phase behavior and a morphotropic phase boundary in a Zr/Hf co-doped barium titanate–BCT system. Researchers synthesized and characterized compositions near the predicted boundary. The x = 0.3 sample showed dielectric constant 9,535 at 1 kHz and 11,051 at 100 Hz at the transition temperature. The frequency-specific measured values should not be interchanged.

Technology: FerroAI deep learning model; NLP-extracted phase-transition dataset

19 Nov 2024Tongji University; Zhejiang Lab; Shanghai East Hospital; collaborating Chinese research institutions

CHOOSER uses protein foundation models to discover functional Casλ homologs

The CHOOSER framework fine-tuned ESM-2 protein-language-model representations for alignment-free CRISPR-Cas discovery and pre-crRNA self-processing predictions. It nominated 11 Casλ homologs, nearly doubling the known Casλ set. Wet-lab work on selected homologs confirmed pre-crRNA processing for five of six tested proteins; EphcCasλ also cleaved target DNA and showed trans-cleavage activity, with a TTR PAM reported.

Technology: CHOOSER, a protein foundation-model framework fine-tuning ESM-2 representations for Cas homolog identification and Cas12 pre-crRNA-processing classification
