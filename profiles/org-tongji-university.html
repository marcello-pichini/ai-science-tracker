CHOOSER uses protein foundation models to discover functional Casλ homologs

The CHOOSER framework fine-tuned ESM-2 protein-language-model representations for alignment-free CRISPR-Cas discovery and pre-crRNA self-processing predictions. It nominated 11 Casλ homologs, nearly doubling the known Casλ set. Wet-lab work on selected homologs confirmed pre-crRNA processing for five of six tested proteins; EphcCasλ also cleaved target DNA and showed trans-cleavage activity, with a TTR PAM reported.

Technology: CHOOSER, a protein foundation-model framework fine-tuning ESM-2 representations for Cas homolog identification and Cas12 pre-crRNA-processing classification
