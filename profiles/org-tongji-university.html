12 Jun 2025Tongji University; Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

FerroAI guides discovery of a ferroelectric composition with high dielectric response

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: FerroAI, trained on text-mined phase-transition data, predicted composition–temperature phase behavior and a morphotropic phase boundary in a Zr/Hf co-doped barium titanate–BCT system. Researchers synthesized and characterized compositions near the predicted boundary. The x = 0.3 sample showed dielectric constant 9,535 at 1 kHz and 11,051 at 100 Hz at the transition temperature. The frequency-specific measured values should not be interchanged.

Technology: FerroAI deep learning model; NLP-extracted phase-transition dataset

19 Nov 2024Tongji University; Zhejiang Lab; Shanghai East Hospital; collaborating Chinese research institutions

CHOOSER uses protein foundation models to discover functional Casλ homologs

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The CHOOSER framework fine-tuned ESM-2 protein-language-model representations for alignment-free CRISPR-Cas discovery and pre-crRNA self-processing predictions. It nominated 11 Casλ homologs, nearly doubling the known Casλ set. Wet-lab work on selected homologs confirmed pre-crRNA processing for five of six tested proteins; EphcCasλ also cleaved target DNA and showed trans-cleavage activity, with a TTR PAM reported.

Technology: CHOOSER, a protein foundation-model framework fine-tuning ESM-2 representations for Cas homolog identification and Cas12 pre-crRNA-processing classification

Family ESM / ESMFold: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
CHOOSER mined candidate proteins adjacent to CRISPR arrays using learned sequence representations rather than alignment-only detection and prioritized Cas12 homologs by predicted pre-crRNA processing. The study reported 11 Casλ homologs and experimentally tested selected predictions; purified EphcCasλ was validated for self-processing pre-crRNA, DNA cleavage and trans-cleavage.
Test and result
Wet-lab work on selected homologs confirmed pre-crRNA processing for five of six tested proteins; EphcCasλ also cleaved target DNA and showed trans-cleavage activity, with a TTR PAM reported.
Limitation / still unknown
This is a specific functional genomics result, not evidence of a ready-to-use diagnostic or gene-editing tool. Only selected candidates were purified and tested; the paper reports pre-crRNA activity in five of six tested Casλ proteins and DNA-cleavage validation for a subset. EphcCasλ activity and PAM characterization are in-vitro findings. The article has a publisher-listed author correction dated 2025-01-09; this record relies on the version-of-record text and retains limited scope.
