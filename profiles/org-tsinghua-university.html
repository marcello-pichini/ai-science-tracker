13 Nov 2026Tsinghua University; Hunan University; Chinese PLA General Hospital; Air Force Medical Center of the Chinese PLA

X-Strata separates aneurysm sacs and overlapping vessels in two-view angiography

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: X-Strata uses learned decomposition of cine angiography and parallax between two views to infer aneurysm-sac and vessel layers and reconstruct a 3D sac surface. On 220 held-out patients from three centers, including 122 external cases, the method ranked first among 14 baselines for sac recovery in overlap regions and layer ordering. All evaluated patients had same-session 3D rotational angiography as reference; performance and clinical benefit for patients with only 2D angiography remain unestablished.

Technology: A neural network estimates angiographic layers and contrast curves; a second view supplies depth-order cues for 3D reconstruction.

22 Jun 2026Tsinghua University; Tianjin University; Inner Mongolia University; Zhejiang Institute of Tianjin University

Few-shot protein-language-model design yields high-output bicyclogermacrene synthase variants

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: After experimentally screening activity-enhancing single mutants of LdTPS5, the researchers used PIFS-PLM, a few-shot model that selects features from protein-language-model embeddings using active-site and conserved-residue information, to propose mutation combinations. All 10 tested model-predicted combinations were active; nine produced more than 100 mg/L bicyclogermacrene in yeast, and the top variant produced 213.59 mg/L versus 2.93 mg/L for wild type (about 73-fold higher yield). A related selectivity-optimized combination increased bicyclogermacrene selectivity by 15%.

Technology: PIFS-PLM (Physics-Inspired Feature Selection of Protein Language Models), using ESM-2 embeddings with a few-shot mutation-combination classifier

Ten predicted LdTPS5 variants were tested; all were active, and the top variants substantially increased measured yeast product yield. The model also guided a selectivity optimization test. Initial single-mutation nomination used MM/GBSA or sequence conservation analysis, separate from PIFS-PLM combinatorial predictions.

8 Oct 2025Tsinghua University; Chinese Academy of Sciences; National University of Singapore; University of Connecticut; Peking University

Neural-network satellite soil-moisture data expose global hotspots and pathways of rainfall coupling

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An information-flow analysis of daily observations found statistically significant local surface-soil-moisture effects on precipitation over about 15.7% of global land (60°S–60°N) in the boreal warm season, with eight regional hotspots. Sensible-heat mediation dominated most hotspots, while evapotranspiration mediation dominated two African hotspots; remote moisture availability and boundary-layer-height variability helped explain regional differences. Only four of 16 assessed CMIP6 models reproduced the ERA5-derived relationship between soil-moisture variability and causal coupling.

Technology: Neural-network-inversion satellite soil-moisture product (NNSm), analyzed with Liang–Kleeman information-flow causal inference

Editorial relevance 53 climate Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The study's long-term daily satellite soil-moisture series was generated with a neural-network inversion algorithm, extending the observed record used to map global coupling. Researchers applied information-flow analysis to this AI-derived product and precipitation observations, then cross-checked the main spatial pattern using independent ERA5 reanalysis. AI provided the satellite soil-moisture input for a concrete global climate result; the causal-inference method itself is an information-theoretic statistical technique, not a learned model.
Test and result
An information-flow analysis of daily observations found statistically significant local surface-soil-moisture effects on precipitation over about 15.7% of global land (60°S–60°N) in the boreal warm season, with eight regional hotspots.
Limitation / still unknown
The mapped effect is local surface-soil-moisture-to-precipitation information flow, statistically significant at the authors' p<0.1 threshold over about 15.7% of land; the analysis does not imply local soil moisture causes rainfall everywhere or exclude remote/upwind effects. The AI contribution is the neural-network-derived satellite soil-moisture product; the causal analysis is not itself an AI model. The spatial pattern was broadly checked with ERA5, while pathway attribution depends on ERA5-reanalysis variables and the assumptions/limitations of information-flow causal inference. Results concern boreal warm seasons and the analyzed 2003–2020 satellite period (ERA5 cross-check spans 1979–2021).
