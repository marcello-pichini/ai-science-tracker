Machine-learned potentials resolve a disorder trade-off in lithium cathode transport

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Using actively fine-tuned machine-learning interatomic potentials with experimental validation, the authors report that cation disorder improves lithium-path connectivity but broadens site energies, suppressing diffusion in disordered-rocksalt cathodes. 21 Sep 2026Tsinghua University; Peking University

GPT-4o-assisted literature screening nominates low-lithium LiMoOBr₄ conductor

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A literature-mining workflow using GPT-4o extracted values from 465 papers; a graph-neural-network-based pipeline screened 69,920 oxyhalide structures. Ab initio molecular-dynamics simulations for LiMoOBr₄ report a lithium content of 2.28 mol kg⁻¹, a 300 K extrapolated ionic conductivity of 13.53 mS cm⁻¹ and a 0.22 eV activation energy. The reported material properties are computational predictions, not experimental measurements.

Technology: GPT-4o literature extraction; diffusion-model and graph-neural-network screening

22 Jun 2026Tsinghua University; Tianjin University; Inner Mongolia University; Zhejiang Institute of Tianjin University

Few-shot protein-language-model design yields high-output bicyclogermacrene synthase variants

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: After experimentally screening activity-enhancing single mutants of LdTPS5, the researchers used PIFS-PLM, a few-shot model that selects features from protein-language-model embeddings using active-site and conserved-residue information, to propose mutation combinations. All 10 tested model-predicted combinations were active; nine produced more than 100 mg/L bicyclogermacrene in yeast, and the top variant produced 213.59 mg/L versus 2.93 mg/L for wild type (about 73-fold higher yield). A related selectivity-optimized combination increased bicyclogermacrene selectivity by 15%.

Technology: PIFS-PLM (Physics-Inspired Feature Selection of Protein Language Models), using ESM-2 embeddings with a few-shot mutation-combination classifier

Family ESM / ESMFold: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 53 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
After single substitutions were screened and activity-enhancing mutations experimentally validated, PIFS-PLM used 60–100 measured combinations to learn from selected ESM-2 features at active-site and conserved residues and predict beneficial combinations. Ten predicted LdTPS5 variants were tested; all were active, and the top variants substantially increased measured yeast product yield. The model also guided a selectivity optimization test. Initial single-mutation nomination used MM/GBSA or sequence conservation analysis, separate from PIFS-PLM combinatorial predictions.
Test and result
After experimentally screening activity-enhancing single mutants of LdTPS5, the researchers used PIFS-PLM, a few-shot model that selects features from protein-language-model embeddings using active-site and conserved-residue information, to propose mutation combinations.
Limitation / still unknown
The 73-fold result is product yield in the reported yeast system, reflecting effects of enzyme activity and expression; it is not a 73-fold increase in catalytic rate. The selectivity improvement was 15%. The model relies on a defined active site/conserved-site set and a small, enzyme-specific experimental dataset. Product profiles differed between cell-free extracts and yeast; these results do not establish universal performance across enzymes or production settings.

8 Oct 2025Tsinghua University; Chinese Academy of Sciences; National University of Singapore; University of Connecticut; Peking University

Neural-network satellite soil-moisture data expose global hotspots and pathways of rainfall coupling

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An information-flow analysis of daily observations found statistically significant local surface-soil-moisture effects on precipitation over about 15.7% of global land (60°S–60°N) in the boreal warm season, with eight regional hotspots. Sensible-heat mediation dominated most hotspots, while evapotranspiration mediation dominated two African hotspots; remote moisture availability and boundary-layer-height variability helped explain regional differences. Only four of 16 assessed CMIP6 models reproduced the ERA5-derived relationship between soil-moisture variability and causal coupling.

Technology: Neural-network-inversion satellite soil-moisture product (NNSm), analyzed with Liang–Kleeman information-flow causal inference

Editorial relevance 53 climate Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The study's long-term daily satellite soil-moisture series was generated with a neural-network inversion algorithm, extending the observed record used to map global coupling. Researchers applied information-flow analysis to this AI-derived product and precipitation observations, then cross-checked the main spatial pattern using independent ERA5 reanalysis. AI provided the satellite soil-moisture input for a concrete global climate result; the causal-inference method itself is an information-theoretic statistical technique, not a learned model.
Test and result
An information-flow analysis of daily observations found statistically significant local surface-soil-moisture effects on precipitation over about 15.7% of global land (60°S–60°N) in the boreal warm season, with eight regional hotspots.
Limitation / still unknown
The mapped effect is local surface-soil-moisture-to-precipitation information flow, statistically significant at the authors' p<0.1 threshold over about 15.7% of land; the analysis does not imply local soil moisture causes rainfall everywhere or exclude remote/upwind effects. The AI contribution is the neural-network-derived satellite soil-moisture product; the causal analysis is not itself an AI model. The spatial pattern was broadly checked with ERA5, while pathway attribution depends on ERA5-reanalysis variables and the assumptions/limitations of information-flow causal inference. Results concern boreal warm seasons and the analyzed 2003–2020 satellite period (ERA5 cross-check spans 1979–2021).

23 Feb 2024Tsinghua University; National Cancer Center/National Clinical Research Center for Cancer, China; Harbin Medical University Cancer Hospital; collaborating Chinese cancer centers

Machine learning identifies plasma metabolite panels for gastric-cancer diagnosis and prognosis

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In a multicenter study of 702 participants, targeted plasma metabolomics and machine learning produced a 10-metabolite gastric-cancer diagnostic model and separate 28-metabolite survival-risk model. The diagnostic model was evaluated in an independent 95-person cohort (AUROC 0.920; sensitivity 0.905, specificity 0.75) and detected stage IA disease with AUROC 0.909. Among 181 patients followed for a median 40 months, the prognostic model achieved test-set concordance index 0.83. Succinate, uridine and lactate were among diagnostic features.

Technology: LASSO, random forest and random survival forest

Editorial relevance 60 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
LASSO selected ten plasma metabolites for a diagnostic model evaluated in internal and independent external cohorts. Random survival forest selected a 28-metabolite prognostic panel, evaluated in a held-out patient set.
Test and result
The diagnostic model was evaluated in an independent 95-person cohort (AUROC 0.920; sensitivity 0.905, specificity 0.75) and detected stage IA disease with AUROC 0.909.
Limitation / still unknown
Case-control cohorts do not establish population screening performance. External diagnostic test n=95; prognosis cohort median follow-up 40 months with right-censored outcomes. The paper says direct clinical use is premature and requires quality-control samples. Associations are not causal and no model-guided benefit was tested.
