13 Nov 2026Tsinghua University; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences; Binzhou Institute of Technology

Machine-learned potentials resolve a disorder trade-off in lithium cathode transport

Using actively fine-tuned machine-learning interatomic potentials with experimental validation, the authors report that cation disorder improves lithium-path connectivity but broadens site energies, suppressing diffusion in disordered-rocksalt cathodes. They develop an energy-adjusted percolation descriptor and a Li–Mn–Ti–O design map. This is a composition-specific transport result; the review does not assign individual capacity figures to modeled or measured material states.

Technology: Actively fine-tuned machine-learning interatomic potentials

22 Jun 2026Tsinghua University; Tianjin University; Inner Mongolia University; Zhejiang Institute of Tianjin University

Few-shot protein-language-model design yields high-output bicyclogermacrene synthase variants

After experimentally screening activity-enhancing single mutants of LdTPS5, the researchers used PIFS-PLM, a few-shot model that selects features from protein-language-model embeddings using active-site and conserved-residue information, to propose mutation combinations. All 10 tested model-predicted combinations were active; nine produced more than 100 mg/L bicyclogermacrene in yeast, and the top variant produced 213.59 mg/L versus 2.93 mg/L for wild type (about 73-fold higher yield). A related selectivity-optimized combination increased bicyclogermacrene selectivity by 15%.

Technology: PIFS-PLM (Physics-Inspired Feature Selection of Protein Language Models), using ESM-2 embeddings with a few-shot mutation-combination classifier

8 Oct 2025Tsinghua University; Chinese Academy of Sciences; National University of Singapore; University of Connecticut; Peking University

Neural-network satellite soil-moisture data expose global hotspots and pathways of rainfall coupling

An information-flow analysis of daily observations found statistically significant local surface-soil-moisture effects on precipitation over about 15.7% of global land (60°S–60°N) in the boreal warm season, with eight regional hotspots. Sensible-heat mediation dominated most hotspots, while evapotranspiration mediation dominated two African hotspots; remote moisture availability and boundary-layer-height variability helped explain regional differences. Only four of 16 assessed CMIP6 models reproduced the ERA5-derived relationship between soil-moisture variability and causal coupling.

Technology: Neural-network-inversion satellite soil-moisture product (NNSm), analyzed with Liang–Kleeman information-flow causal inference

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: An information-flow analysis of daily observations found statistically significant local surface-soil-moisture effects on precipitation over about 15.7% of global land (60°S–60°N) in the boreal warm season, with eight regional hotspots.

  • AI role: The study's long-term daily satellite soil-moisture series was generated with a neural-network inversion algorithm, extending the observed record used to map global coupling. Researchers applied information-flow analysis to this AI-derived product and precipitation observations, then cross-checked the main spatial pattern using independent ERA5 reanalysis. AI provided the satellite soil-moisture input for a concrete global climate result; the causal-inference method itself is an information-theoretic statistical technique, not a learned model.

23 Feb 2024Tsinghua University; National Cancer Center/National Clinical Research Center for Cancer, China; Harbin Medical University Cancer Hospital; collaborating Chinese cancer centers

Machine learning identifies plasma metabolite panels for gastric-cancer diagnosis and prognosis

In a multicenter study of 702 participants, targeted plasma metabolomics and machine learning produced a 10-metabolite gastric-cancer diagnostic model and separate 28-metabolite survival-risk model. The diagnostic model was evaluated in an independent 95-person cohort (AUROC 0.920; sensitivity 0.905, specificity 0.75) and detected stage IA disease with AUROC 0.909. Among 181 patients followed for a median 40 months, the prognostic model achieved test-set concordance index 0.83. Succinate, uridine and lactate were among diagnostic features.

Technology: LASSO, random forest and random survival forest
