8 Nov 2023Max Planck Institute for Terrestrial Microbiology; Goethe University Frankfurt; Philipps University of Marburg

Deep-learning-guided cell-free screening identifies antimicrobial peptides

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Generative and predictive deep-learning models proposed and prioritized peptide sequences for cell-free synthesis and screening. The authors report 30 functional antimicrobial peptides among 500 candidates tested across five design–build–test rounds. The assay tested peptides in unpurified cell-free mixtures, a limitation for interpreting activity and potency. The reported effects do not establish clinical efficacy or antibiotic approval.

Technology: Variational autoencoders; convolutional and recurrent neural-network regressors

5 Jul 2022Max Planck Institute for Terrestrial Microbiology

Active learning improves a cell-free carbon-fixation cycle

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An ensemble learned from experimental feedback to select compositions for an engineered carbon-fixation cycle. Laboratory measurements supported improved glycolate production compared with the study's reference cycle. This is an in-vitro pathway result, not carbon capture by living organisms or a demonstrated industrial process. Other applications in the same paper are not additional catalog entries.
