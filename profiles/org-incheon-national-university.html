AJIN machine learning predicts atomic layer deposition outcomes from experimental precursor-substrate data

AJIN, a graph-neural-network framework trained on 560 experimentally observed ALD and area-selective ALD outcomes (76 precursors, 27 substrates, 20 reactants, 25-620 C), reaches 79.2% balanced accuracy for adsorption and 84.8% for deposition. Tested against the authors' own laboratory ALD experiments, it correctly predicted 83% of new-substrate cases and 71% of new precursor-substrate adsorption cases.

Technology: AJIN (ALD Joint Interpretable Network) with graph-neural-network embeddings (CHGNet-MLP best model)
