Interpretable machine learning maps wastewater treatment conditions

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An XGBoost model fitted to 1,423 experimental and literature-derived samples linked iron/carbon ratio and operating conditions to chemical oxygen demand removal in polyvinyl-alcohol wastewater treatment. SHAP analysis identified iron/carbon ratio as the strongest modeled factor and a pH-dependent interaction window. Reported test R² was 0.8045, but the paper selected its final model using that test set, limiting an unbiased generalization claim. The preprint offers model-derived process associations, not a confirmed causal mechanism or validated industrial-scale control policy.
