Interpretable machine learning maps wastewater treatment conditions

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An XGBoost model fitted to 1,423 experimental and literature-derived samples linked iron/carbon ratio and operating conditions to chemical oxygen demand removal in polyvinyl-alcohol wastewater treatment. SHAP analysis identified iron/carbon ratio as the strongest modeled factor and a pH-dependent interaction window. Reported test R² was 0.8045, but the paper selected its final model using that test set, limiting an unbiased generalization claim. The preprint offers model-derived process associations, not a confirmed causal mechanism or validated industrial-scale control policy.

Technology: XGBoost learns COD-removal response from experimental and literature-derived process data; SHAP attributes nonlinear, condition-dependent associations to operating variables.

24 Nov 2026Hunan University; Hengyang Normal University; Second Xiangya Hospital, Central South University

Generative AI designs Type I photosensitizers tested in cells and mice

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A cross-attention classifier trained on 1,338 curated photosensitizers (85.8% accuracy and ROC-AUC 0.918 on a scaffold split) and an RNN scaffold-decorating generator proposed candidates on a redesigned tetracyclic scaffold. Six were synthesised and all produced superoxide under light. The lead compound had IC50 3.3 micromolar in 4T1 cells under normoxia and 4.5 micromolar at 2% oxygen, with similar values in HepG2 and MCF-7, and markedly slowed 4T1 tumour growth in mice over 14 days with four animals per group. Validation covers one scaffold and six molecules, the lead was also phototoxic to normal HUVEC cells (IC50 3.5 micromolar under normoxia), hypoxia attenuated but did not abolish its effect, and the in vivo group size was small.

Technology: Cross-attention classifier for Type I photosensitizer activity and an RNN SMILES scaffold decorator pretrained on general chemistry data and fine-tuned on photosensitizers.
