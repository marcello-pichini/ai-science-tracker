12 May 2026Max Planck Institute for Sustainable Materials

Machine learning designs bulk metallic glasses with peak hardness near 2,450 HV

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: VIBANN used a dataset of 673 bulk metallic-glass compositions to guide the design of five alloys, synthesized as amorphous 2 mm rods. The paper's experimental results identify alloy A1, B68Nb24Fe4W4, as the hardest: 2,447 +/- 44 HV at a 0.5 N indentation load, with six replicate indentations. Hardness decreases with increasing load; the peak is not a value for every alloy or test condition. X-ray scattering and molecular dynamics support a structural interpretation within the studied composition range. The date refers to the May 2026 paper, rather than the institute's later August announcement.

Technology: VIBANN (variational information-bottleneck attention network)
