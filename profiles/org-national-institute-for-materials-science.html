LLM-guided experiments identify three phases unique to the ternary Co–Al–Ge system

Researchers used an LLM to plan iterative composition measurements and experimentally mapped the Co–Al–Ge phase diagram at 900 °C. The domain-specific aLLoyM model directed early measurements into compositionally complex interior regions, enabling the earliest discovery of all three novel phases reported to form only in the ternary system; the general-purpose LLM strategy found more phases in fewer cycles. This is an arXiv preprint and the finding is bounded to this experimental phase-diagram study.

Technology: aLLoyM domain-specific large language model and general-purpose LLM experimental planner
