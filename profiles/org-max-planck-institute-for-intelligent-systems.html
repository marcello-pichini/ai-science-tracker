14 Dec 2021Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems; and Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics

DINGO: gravitational-wave parameters in seconds

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The neural network estimates the mass, spin, position, inclination and distance of binary black holes in seconds rather than hours or months, with about ten days of training, and was tested on eight events from LIGO and Virgo’s O1 and O2 runs. Published in Physical Review Letters; extension to neutron stars is only promising; source reviewed was a Max Planck doctoral-program page.

Technology: DINGO (Bayesian inference with neural networks)

Editorial relevance 60 physics Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Neural network acted as a surrogate for Bayesian posterior distributions for gravitational-wave parameters.
Test and result
Parameter estimates for eight LIGO-Virgo events agree closely with standard inference codes, with inference times cut from days to about 20 seconds.
Limitation / still unknown
The article says extension to neutron stars and complex signals is promising but not demonstrated.
