DINGO: gravitational-wave parameters in seconds

The neural network estimates the mass, spin, position, inclination and distance of binary black holes in seconds rather than hours or months, with about ten days of training, and was tested on eight events from LIGO and Virgo’s O1 and O2 runs. Published in Physical Review Letters; extension to neutron stars is only promising; source reviewed was a Max Planck doctoral-program page.

Technology: DINGO (Bayesian inference with neural networks)

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Parameter estimates for eight LIGO-Virgo events agree closely with standard inference codes, with inference times cut from days to about 20 seconds.
  • Still unknown: The article says extension to neutron stars and complex signals is promising but not demonstrated.
  • AI role: Neural network acted as a surrogate for Bayesian posterior distributions for gravitational-wave parameters.
