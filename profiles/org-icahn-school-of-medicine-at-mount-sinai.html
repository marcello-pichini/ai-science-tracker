Prospective silent-trial validation of a fine-tuned pathology foundation model for EGFR mutation screening in lung adenocarcinoma

A fine-tuned Prov-GigaPath ViT-g model, named EAGLE, predicted EGFR mutation status from routine H&E slides plus clinical features in lung adenocarcinoma. Across an international dataset of 8,461 slides it achieved mean AUC 0.847 internally and 0.870 in external cohorts. In a four-month prospective silent trial on 197 primary lung-adenocarcinoma samples, EAGLE achieved AUC 0.890. Simulated AI-assisted triage thresholds reduced rapid molecular EGFR tests by up to 43% while maintaining the study’s performance criteria.

Technology: EAGLE, a task-specific model fine-tuned from the Prov-GigaPath ViT-g pathology foundation model, combining H&E whole-slide image features with clinical variables for EGFR mutation prediction

25 Apr 2023Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai; University of Maryland School of Medicine; Hospital for Special Surgery

Longitudinal EHR machine learning identifies patients who may need autoimmune rheumatic-disease evaluation years earlier

A random-forest model trained on longitudinal electronic health records from 161,584 people across two institutions identified records associated with high-specificity autoantibody testing for systemic autoimmune rheumatic diseases (SARDs). The model achieved AUROC 0.93 in internal validation and 0.87 on the external All of Us cohort. In a separate BioMe cohort, model probabilities tracked SARD and other autoimmune diagnoses; records could predict later autoantibody testing and rheumatology encounters up to five years before those events. The study also found higher autoantibody yield among model-prioritized individuals than under the comparison clinical-testing rate.

Technology: Random-forest machine-learning model using longitudinal electronic-health-record diagnoses, medications, laboratory results and vital signs
