A low-rank subspace of LLM representations carries most of their brain alignment

A Dartmouth preprint studied which language-model features predict human neural responses during naturalistic listening. Across three intracranial/fMRI datasets and six model conditions, reduced-rank regression recovered full linear encoding performance with about 1–3% of embedding dimensions. These directions differed from the embeddings’ principal variance axes and transferred across held-out participants, less strongly across datasets. This summary is limited to the abstract and affiliation/date metadata of the October 29, 2026 preprint. It concerns measured brain–model alignment, not proof that brains use the same representations.

Technology: Autoregressive and masked language models with ridge reduced-rank regression
