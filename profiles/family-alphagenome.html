Paper2Agent integrates perturbation datasets to prioritize GPR137 at a psoriasis risk locus

Paper2Agent-generated agents used AlphaGenome to prioritize GPR137 as the top predicted gene affected by psoriasis-associated variant rs887314 in CD4+ T cells, then integrated that prediction with existing MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq data. A cross-screen correlation of gene-expression signatures supported GPR137 as the probable causal gene under stimulated conditions (Spearman rho 0.613 at 8 h and 0.630 at 48 h; FDR < 0.05), but not at rest. The paper presents this as activation-dependent computational evidence.

Technology: Paper2Agent-generated AlphaGenome, MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq agents, coordinated through an AI co-scientist

AlphaGenome Atlas: predictive map of human DNA variation

A precomputed database of predicted molecular effects for about 9 billion possible single-letter changes in the human genome, generated by AlphaGenome. Researchers can access it free through a portal and API. The predictions are a research tool, do not replace experiments, and are not validated for clinical use.

Technology: AlphaGenome, AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI)

25 Jun 2025Google DeepMind

AlphaGenome: predicting the effects of DNA variants

The model reads sequences up to 1 million base pairs and predicts thousands of gene-regulation properties at single-letter resolution. It outperformed the best external models in 22 of 24 sequence evaluations and matched or outperformed the best in 24 of 26 variant evaluations. Stated limits: distant regulatory elements; not validated for personal genomes or clinical use.

Technology: AlphaGenome
