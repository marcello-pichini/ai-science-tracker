27 Apr 2020Xiangya Hospital, Central South University; Rhode Island Hospital; University of Pennsylvania

AI augmentation lifts radiologists in telling COVID-19 from other pneumonia

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A CT model trained on 1,186 patients (521 COVID-19, 665 other pneumonia) reached 95% sensitivity and 96% specificity internally, and AUC 0.90 on an independent test set. Radiologist accuracy rose from 85% to 90% with AI assistance. The study is retrospective and the independent-test performance is lower than the internal result.

Technology: Deep-learning CT classifier used as an assistant for radiologists

Editorial relevance 60 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The AI classified COVID-19 versus other pneumonia on CT slices and its probabilities were shown to radiologists as decision support.
Test and result
A CT model was tested on an internal split and on an independent test set from separate hospitals, and six radiologists read cases unassisted and then with the model's probabilities.
Limitation / still unknown
The abstract states no limitations section; performance on independent data was lower (87% vs 96% accuracy) and the sensitivity confidence interval is wide.
