18 Nov 2026Leipzig University

ProteinMPNN design of right-handed antiparallel four-helix coiled coils

Source check: Source checked (abstract only). Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Removing sequence-repeat constraints raised the fraction of ProteinMPNN designs meeting structural confidence and topology criteria from 0.44% to 2.80%, and heterotetramer design gave 3.04%. Five of six experimentally characterized designs were predominantly alpha-helical, with apparent melting temperatures of 55.2 to 72.5 C. The crystal structure of the most stable design, Heteromer-2, at 1.92 A matched the computational model with a backbone RMSD of 0.45 A. Only six designs were characterized; this is a preprint.

Technology: ProteinMPNN sequence design on parametric coiled-coil backbones with structure-prediction filtering

2 Nov 2026Ajinomoto (Japan); Institute of Integrated Research, Institute of Science Tokyo; Imperial College London

BindCraft and ProteinMPNN design miniprotein binders that neutralize PACAP-38

Source check: Source checked (abstract only). Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: BindCraft designed binders to PACAP-38, giving the 63-residue ICLIST-2 (KD 26.5 nM). ProteinMPNN-guided optimization produced ICLIST-11 with nine substitutions, KD 4.51 nM and a melting temperature 14 C higher. In cell-based assays ICLIST-11 neutralized PACAP-38-induced PAC1R activation with IC50 10.6 nM, outperforming ICLIST-2. The results are in vitro, from the abstract only.

Technology: BindCraft de novo binder design and ProteinMPNN sequence optimization

3 Aug 2026The University of Texas at Austin

Machine learning and directed evolution improved an L-Dopa-producing monooxygenase

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Zero-shot protein models prioritized HpaBC substitutions, which were experimentally assayed with a L-Dopa-responsive GFP biosensor. EVOLVEpro then combined experimentally validated substitutions, including directed-evolution-derived variants, and selected higher-order designs. The best reported combined variants produced up to 57–58 times the wild-type fluorescence signal under the stated assay conditions. This is a biosensor proxy for L-Dopa production, not a purified-enzyme kinetic measurement; the work also builds on prior directed-evolution mutations and does not establish clinical benefit.

Technology: MutCompute, ProteinMPNN, ESM protein language models and EVOLVEpro combinatorial design

10 Jun 2026Weizmann Institute of Science; École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL)

AI-designed bacterial anti-defense proteins enable phage infection and plasmid retention

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: RFdiffusion-based de novo binder design and experimental screening produced synthetic proteins that inhibited three bacterial defense systems: Thoeris and Avs1 in Bacillus subtilis assays, and DdmDE in Vibrio cholerae. Five designed proteins inhibited Thoeris and nine inhibited Avs1 in the reported screens. An engineered SBSphiJ phage carrying both anti-Thoeris and anti-Avs1 proteins replicated in B. subtilis cells expressing both defenses. In native V. cholerae O1 El Tor, two of four tested DdmDE binders improved plasmid resistance to clearance.

Technology: RFdiffusion de novo binder design, ProteinMPNN sequence design and AlphaFold2/3 structure-guided candidate scoring

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

AI-designed protein family binds and senses small molecules, including cortisol

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The study combines trRosetta-based scaffold hallucination and Rosetta design with ProteinMPNN/LigandMPNN sequence design and AlphaFold structural filtering to create NTF2-like binders for six small-molecule targets. An optimized cortisol binder had a measured dissociation constant of 68 nM. A purified-protein, split-luciferase cortisol sensor showed an estimated response EC50 of 72 nM; this is a sensor response measurement, not the binding-affinity value or a clinical detection limit. The sensor also responded to cortisone and 17-alpha-hydroxyprogesterone, so specificity needs improvement. The March 2026 peer-reviewed paper is the source of record.

21 Dec 2025Weill Cornell Medicine; Macao Polytechnic University; University of Gdańsk

CycleRFdiffusion-designed TP4 is a reproducible cyclic peptide binder to TREM2

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A structure-conditioned workflow combining CycleRFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN and HighFold generated and screened 1,500 cyclic peptide–TREM2 complexes and prioritized four candidates. The lead TP4 showed weak but reproducible TREM2 binding in spectral-shift, microscale-thermophoresis and surface-plasmon-resonance assays. The authors report favorable plasma stability and moderate metabolic stability, presenting the work as an initial proof of concept for TREM2-targeted cyclic binders.
