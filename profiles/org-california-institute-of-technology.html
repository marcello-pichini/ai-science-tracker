4 Aug 2026California Institute of Technology; University of California, Los Angeles; AITHYRA / Austrian Academy of Sciences

Machine learning identifies the first enzyme for phenazine–thiol conjugation

A genome-informed machine-learning workflow (ML-CITO) used contrastive learning over protein-language-model representations and genomic context, starting from 14 known phenazine-modifying sequences, to identify PTC. Biochemical characterization showed PTC catalyzes glutathione-dependent phenazine modification, a reaction previously assumed to occur non-enzymatically.

Technology: ML-CITO (genome-context-informed data augmentation plus protein-language-space contrastive learning)

16 Jan 2025California Institute of Technology; University of California, Berkeley; Elegen Corp

Active learning identifies a protoglobin variant with 99% yield and cis-selective cyclopropanation

An iterative machine-learning campaign engineered the protoglobin from Pyrobaculum arsenaticum (ParPgb) for non-native cyclopropanation of 4-vinylanisole. After two model-guided rounds following an initial library screen, variant MPFDY gave 99% total cyclopropane yield and a 14:1 cis:trans ratio in the reported assay, compared with a parent variant that produced about 40% yield and favored trans product. The result demonstrates a specific sequence combination with improved yield and stereoselectivity in this enzyme scaffold.

Technology: ALDE (Active Learning-assisted Directed Evolution), using a bootstrapped deep-neural-network ensemble and Thompson sampling

20 Jul 2022California Institute of Technology; University of Connecticut

Interpretable machine learning discovers ultrapermeable polymer membranes for gas separation

Multitask machine-learning models trained on experimental gas permeability data screened over 9 million hypothetical polymers. They identified hundreds of never-before-seen ultrapermeable membranes, with molecular dynamics simulations confirming the predicted permeabilities of the top candidates. The validation is computational rather than fabricated membranes, so the candidates remain unproven experimentally.

Technology: Multitask machine learning with model interpretation, plus molecular dynamics

19 Aug 2021California Institute of Technology

Machine learning-assisted directed evolution with in silico combinatorial screening

A path-independent machine-learning protocol screens full combinatorial protein libraries in silico, with training-set design that avoids near-zero-fitness variants. On a four-site epistatic fitness landscape the optimized protocol reached the global fitness maximum up to 81-fold more often than single-step greedy optimization. Caveat: validation is on a combinatorial fitness landscape dataset, not a new wet-lab evolution campaign.

Technology: Machine-learning-assisted directed evolution (in silico combinatorial screening)

8 Apr 2021California Institute of Technology; IPAC; Zwicky Transient Facility collaboration

Deep learning finds comet C/2020 T2 in a single ZTF image

The Tails EfficientDet model identified the extended object C/2020 T2 in one Zwicky Transient Facility Twilight Survey image on 2020 October 7. Follow-up orbital work confirmed it as a long-period comet. The study reports this as the first comet discovery assisted by deep learning; it also recovered a previously known comet, which is not counted as a new discovery here.

Technology: Tails, an EfficientDet-based deep-learning comet detector
