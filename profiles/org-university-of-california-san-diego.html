23 Apr 2025University of California San Diego; collaborating biomedical research institutions

AlphaFold structure analysis helps reveal PHGDH transcriptional activity linked to Alzheimer’s amyloid pathology

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: AlphaFold-based structural analysis highlighted a helix-helix-turn-helix subdomain in PHGDH that resembles a TALE homeodomain, prompting tests of a previously unrecognized DNA-binding and transcriptional function. Experiments supported PHGDH binding to promoters and activation of IKKα and HMGB1 in astrocytes, independently of its metabolic enzyme activity. In mouse models and human brain organoids, PHGDH expression promoted amyloid pathology; a blood-brain-barrier-permeable inhibitor of its transcriptional function reduced amyloid pathology and improved measured behavioral deficits in mice.

Technology: AlphaFold structure prediction, followed by structural comparison of the predicted PHGDH fold

Family AlphaFold: profile · models page

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The researchers inspected AlphaFold-predicted PHGDH structure and identified a helix-helix-turn-helix subdomain resembling a TALE homeodomain, which motivated the hypothesis and targeted mutation tests for DNA binding. AlphaFold did not establish PHGDH function or disease causality; the transcriptional activity and pathology effects were tested through biochemical, cellular, organoid and mouse experiments.
Test and result
AlphaFold-based structural analysis highlighted a helix-helix-turn-helix subdomain in PHGDH that resembles a TALE homeodomain, prompting tests of a previously unrecognized DNA-binding and transcriptional function.
Limitation / still unknown
The AlphaFold-derived structural resemblance generated the mechanistic hypothesis; experiments establish the reported PHGDH transcriptional and pathology effects in the tested models. The inhibitor and behavioral effects were tested in mice, not people. Human brain organoid findings are in vitro and do not establish clinical disease causation or treatment efficacy. PHGDH overexpression alone did not reproduce all Alzheimer’s disease features, and no human therapeutic outcome is reported.

9 Sep 2021University of California San Diego

COMPOSER sepsis model flags unfamiliar cases instead of guessing

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: COMPOSER, a deep-learning early sepsis predictor, reached AUCs of 0.925-0.953 in ICU and 0.938-0.945 in ED across six cohorts (515,720 patients) from two US health systems. About 20% of non-septic and 8% of septic prediction windows were marked indeterminate, and the model gave a median early warning of 12.2 hours in ICU and 2.1 hours in ED before the first antibiotics order. The abstract reports no prospective deployment or patient-outcome results.

Technology: COMPOSER deep-learning model with uncertainty-based indeterminate output

20 Apr 2021University of California San Diego

Bayesian optimization and graph learning identify two synthesized ultra-incompressible hard materials

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A Bayesian optimization workflow using symmetry-constrained crystal relaxation and the MEGNet graph neural network screened 399,960 transition-metal boride and carbide compositions. The study identified MoWC₂ (P6₃/mmc) and ReWB (Pca2₁), then synthesized both by in-situ reactive spark-plasma sintering; the measured hardness was reported to agree with model and DFT predictions. The result is limited to these two synthesized compounds and the authors’ tested synthesis conditions.

Technology: BOWSR (Bayesian Optimization With Symmetry Relaxation) with the MEGNet graph neural network energy model

Editorial relevance 60 materials Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
MEGNet supplied graph-based formation-energy predictions; BOWSR used Bayesian optimization under crystal-symmetry constraints to relax candidate structures without DFT geometry relaxation. This enabled screening of 399,960 boride/carbide compositions, narrowing candidates for experimental synthesis.
Test and result
A Bayesian optimization workflow using symmetry-constrained crystal relaxation and the MEGNet graph neural network screened 399,960 transition-metal boride and carbide compositions.
Limitation / still unknown
The arXiv abstract states that the two materials were synthesized and describes them as ultra-incompressible hard materials; the institutional project page reports experiments on eight selected candidates and confirmation of two novel hard materials. The result is from a preprint/journal study and the provided abstract does not give numeric hardness values. Do not imply commercial performance or broader synthesis robustness.

1 May 2020University of California San Diego; Duke University

Machine-learning-guided synthesis realizes four single-phase refractory carbide compositions

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A random-forest model ranked new chromium-containing multicomponent carbide compositions by predicted entropy-forming ability. After density-functional calculations and experimental synthesis, X-ray diffraction identified four previously unreported single-phase rock-salt compositions: CrMoNbVWC₅, CrMoNbTaWC₅, CrMoTaVWC₅, and CrMoTiVWC₅. The first three were high-EFA selections; CrMoTiVWC₅ was a medium-EFA composition and required extended annealing to reach chemical homogeneity.

Technology: Random-forest models using chemical attributes, with and without CALPHAD thermodynamic features

Editorial relevance 53 materials Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The random-forest models learned entropy-forming ability from 56 previously reported carbide compositions and rapidly screened 70 new chromium-containing candidates. Model rankings guided selection of seven compositions for DFT calculations and synthesis; experimental phase outcomes agreed with predicted rankings, including four single-phase materials.
Test and result
A random-forest model ranked new chromium-containing multicomponent carbide compositions by predicted entropy-forming ability.
Limitation / still unknown
Only seven of the 70 model-screened candidates were selected for DFT and synthesis, and several tested compositions formed multiple phases. Single-phase identification used XRD and was supported by microstructure, elemental mapping and EBSD; the medium-EFA CrMoTiVWC₅ sample required extended annealing. The paper establishes synthesis and phase formation, not measured application performance or engineering readiness.

22 Jan 2020University of California San Diego; and collaborators

Interpretable multi-omics machine learning reveals unexpected drug mechanisms in Huntington disease models

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An interpretable machine-learning model integrated transcriptomic, epigenomic, metabolomic and proteomic data from Huntington-disease cell models to identify previously unknown mechanisms for protective compounds. The analysis predicted autophagy activation for one compound group and mitochondrial-respiration inhibition for another; follow-up imaging, biochemical and energetics assays experimentally confirmed these effects in the cell model.

Technology: Interpretable network-optimization model integrating multi-omics over a molecular-interaction network

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The model integrated multiple omics and prioritized cellular pathways as candidate mechanisms of action; researchers tested those predictions with cellular imaging, biochemical assays and energetics measurements. It revealed unexpected mechanisms for compounds with unrelated known targets.
Test and result
The analysis predicted autophagy activation for one compound group and mitochondrial-respiration inhibition for another; follow-up imaging, biochemical and energetics assays experimentally confirmed these effects in the cell model.
Limitation / still unknown
Findings were experimentally established in STHdhQ111 murine striatal cells, with autophagy effects also checked in human SH-SY5Y and HEK293 cell lines. The paper notes that the model prioritizes pathways but does not resolve every causal step or demonstrate therapeutic efficacy in patients.
