AI-assisted proof establishes a dimension-independent KLS isoperimetric bound

Zhao Song and Xinzhi Zhang prove a universal, dimension-independent bound for the Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits constant of isotropic log-concave probability measures, resolving the KLS conjecture. Their proof repeatedly refines bounds on polynomial variance and curvature; the authors report that AI tools helped explore more than 100 approaches, obtain the earlier log-star bound, and develop and verify the final proof. Independent researchers also posted proofs, and the paper’s streamlined proof is being formalized in Lean.

Technology: GPT-6 Astra; GPT-5.6 Sol; Claude Fable 5; Fable (as named in the paper’s AI disclosure)

Quine ranks compounds that shift pancreatic cancer cells toward a basal state

Microsoft's Quine, a multimodal biology world model, prioritized thousands of compounds for shifting tumor cell states, and a handful were tested in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma cell lines. The top-ranked compounds produced the largest classical-to-basal shifts, as validated by wet-lab experiments, and some moved cells toward a distinct third phenotype. The post gives no quantitative wet-lab figures and links no paper, and it measures cell-state changes rather than clinical efficacy.

21 Sep 2026Microsoft Research

RetroChimera: predicting small-molecule synthesis

Published in Nature, the method combines a Transformer model (R-SMILES 2) and a template-based graph model (NeuralLoc) through a learned vote. In blind tests with expert chemists, 90% of complete routes were accepted, compared with 20–50% for the component models or a baseline, and nine of ten difficult targets were solved. Evaluation covered only ten targets; one wrong prediction invalidates an entire route.

Technology: RetroChimera (Transformer and template-based model)

12 May 2026Microsoft Research

MatterSim and tantalum phosphide as a good heat conductor

A candidate identified with MatterSim-v1, tetragonal tantalum phosphide, was synthesized and showed thermal conductivity of 152 W/m/K in the best samples, close to silicon. MatterSim-v1 inference is 3–5 times faster, and the new multi-task model MatterSim-MT predicts energies, forces, and properties. Battery and other property results are predictions, not experimental confirmations.

Technology: MatterSim-v1 and MatterSim-MT

18 Jun 2025Microsoft Research

Skala: a deep-learning exchange-correlation functional

It comes close to the 1 kcal/mol threshold on the W4-17 benchmark (0.85 kcal/mol on the single-reference subset) and is competitive with leading hybrid functionals on GMTKN55 at lower cost. It was trained on about 150,000 high-accuracy energy differences, roughly 100 times the previous dataset size. Accuracy is shown only within the training data's chemical space; it was not yet public at the time.

BioEmu-1: ensembles of protein structures with deep learning

An open-source model that generates thousands of protein structures per hour on a GPU and reproduces the equilibrium distribution of a molecular-dynamics simulation using 10,000 to 100,000 times fewer GPU-hours. Predicted folding free energies correlate well with experimental values. The authors call it ‘a first step.’

A diffusion model trained on 608,000 stable materials. Compound TaCr2O6, generated with a constraint of 200 GPa bulk modulus, was synthesized and measured at 169 GPa (relative error under 20%), but with Ta–Cr disorder differing from the proposed structure. One experimental example.
