4 Oct 2026Microsoft Research; independent research collaboration

AI-assisted proof establishes a dimension-independent KLS isoperimetric bound

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Zhao Song and Xinzhi Zhang prove a universal, dimension-independent bound for the Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits constant of isotropic log-concave probability measures, resolving the KLS conjecture. Their proof repeatedly refines bounds on polynomial variance and curvature; the authors report that AI tools helped explore more than 100 approaches, obtain the earlier log-star bound, and develop and verify the final proof. Independent researchers also posted proofs, and the paper’s streamlined proof is being formalized in Lean. The 1 October preprint gave a slowly dimension-dependent bound; the 4 October version established the dimension-independent theorem described here.

Technology: GPT-6 Astra; GPT-5.6 Sol; Claude Fable 5; Fable (as named in the paper’s AI disclosure)

Quine ranks compounds that shift pancreatic cancer cells toward a basal state

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Microsoft's Quine, a multimodal biology world model, prioritized thousands of compounds for shifting tumor cell states, and a handful were tested in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma cell lines. The top-ranked compounds produced the largest classical-to-basal shifts, as validated by wet-lab experiments, and some moved cells toward a distinct third phenotype. The post gives no quantitative wet-lab figures and links no paper, and it measures cell-state changes rather than clinical efficacy.

21 Sep 2026Microsoft Research

RetroChimera: predicting small-molecule synthesis

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Published in Nature, the method combines a Transformer model (R-SMILES 2) and a template-based graph model (NeuralLoc) through a learned vote. In blind tests with expert chemists, 90% of complete routes were accepted, compared with 20–50% for the component models or a baseline, and nine of ten difficult targets were solved. Evaluation covered only ten targets; one wrong prediction invalidates an entire route.

Technology: RetroChimera (Transformer and template-based model)

Family RetroChimera: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 53 chemistry Blog postPublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
An ensemble of a Transformer (R-SMILES 2) predicting precursors and a graph neural network (NeuralLoc) selecting templates, combined by a learned rank-dependent vote.
Test and result
Blind tests by PhD-level chemists rated individual steps and complete routes for ten difficult targets: 90% of RetroChimera routes were accepted, and nine of ten targets were solved, versus 20-50% for the component models or baseline.
Limitation / still unknown
The route-level evaluation covers only ten targets; one incorrect prediction invalidates a route; NeuralLoc is constrained on reactions outside its template library, and R-SMILES 2 may hallucinate.

12 May 2026Microsoft Research

MatterSim and tantalum phosphide as a good heat conductor

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A candidate identified with MatterSim-v1, tetragonal tantalum phosphide, was synthesized and showed thermal conductivity of 152 W/m/K in the best samples, close to silicon. MatterSim-v1 inference is 3–5 times faster, and the new multi-task model MatterSim-MT predicts energies, forces, and properties. Battery and other property results are predictions, not experimental confirmations.

18 Jun 2025Microsoft Research

Skala: a deep-learning exchange-correlation functional

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: It comes close to the 1 kcal/mol threshold on the W4-17 benchmark (0.85 kcal/mol on the single-reference subset) and is competitive with leading hybrid functionals on GMTKN55 at lower cost. It was trained on about 150,000 high-accuracy energy differences, roughly 100 times the previous dataset size. Accuracy is shown only within the training data's chemical space; it was not yet public at the time.
