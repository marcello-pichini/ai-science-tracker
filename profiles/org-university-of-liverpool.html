6 Nov 2024University of Liverpool

Mobile autonomous robots for exploratory synthetic chemistry

Mobile robots move samples between a synthesis platform, a UPLC-MS spectrometer, and a benchtop NMR, while a heuristic decision-maker evaluates data and chooses reactions. Demonstrated on structural diversification, supramolecular chemistry, and photochemical synthesis. The AI is heuristic and there is no speed or accuracy metric; the announcement says robots will not have a ‘Eureka’ moment.

Technology: Heuristic decision-making with mobile robots

21 Sep 2021University of Liverpool

Unsupervised machine learning guides discovery of a lithium solid electrolyte

Unsupervised machine learning of experimentally explored chemistry, combined with crystal structure prediction, selected element combinations for new inorganic solids. The approach predicted a novel quaternary lithium solid electrolyte that was then synthesized, and the group reports four new materials in total. The conductivity values of the synthesized compounds were not verified in this search.

Technology: Unsupervised machine learning for element selection with crystal structure prediction

8 Jul 2020University of Liverpool

A mobile robotic chemist

A mobile robot ran 688 experiments in eight days in a ten-variable space, guided by a batch Bayesian algorithm, to search for photocatalysts that produce hydrogen from water; the best mixtures were six times more active than the initial formulations. This optimizes a formulation, not a new structure; only the abstract was reviewed.

Technology: Batch Bayesian optimization and mobile robot
