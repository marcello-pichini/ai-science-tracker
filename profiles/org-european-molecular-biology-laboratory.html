29 Oct 2025European Molecular Biology Laboratory; and German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ), with collaborators

Machine-learning microscopy isolates micronucleated cells and reveals chromosome-abnormality formation

The MAGIC platform used an XGBoost image classifier to identify and photolabel micronucleated cells for single-cell genome sequencing. In near-diploid human cell models, the selected cells exposed new chromosome-abnormality formation patterns, including dicentric-associated breakage-fusion-bridge events; measured baseline abnormalities approximately doubled in TP53-deficient cells, and chromosome losses outnumbered gains.

Technology: MAGIC adaptive imaging platform with an XGBoost micronuclei classifier

25 Apr 2022European Molecular Biology Laboratory; University of Cambridge

Random-forest selection links a food compound to COX-1 inhibition

A random-forest model prioritized 5-methoxysalicylic acid for a COX-1 enzyme assay. It showed about 40% inhibition at 100 micromolar, whereas a higher-ranked single-fingerprint comparator did not inhibit at that concentration. This is a modest in-vitro target result, not evidence of dietary, therapeutic or clinical benefit.

Technology: Random-forest classification of drug-target similarity

  • AI role: Random-forest classification of drug-target similarity supported the stated study; experimental and interpretive work remained with researchers.
