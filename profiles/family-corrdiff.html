24 Feb 2025NVIDIA

CorrDiff: kilometer-scale weather forecasts with generative AI

A diffusion model takes atmospheric data from 25-kilometer to 2-kilometer scale and also predicts variables absent from the inputs, such as radar reflectivity. The paper appeared the same day in Communications Earth and Environment; the initial model was tuned on Taiwan data with the Central Weather Administration, and Taiwan's disaster-reduction center uses it for alerts. NVIDIA claims 500 times faster and 10,000 times more energy-efficient than high-resolution numerical forecasting on CPU: company figures, with no accuracy comparison on the page.
