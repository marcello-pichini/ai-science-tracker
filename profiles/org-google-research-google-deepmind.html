AMIE: conversational diagnostic AI rated above primary care physicians in simulated consultations

In a blinded remote OSCE with 149 case scenarios, AMIE and primary care physicians held text consultations with simulated patients. Specialist physicians rated AMIE superior on 30 of 32 axes and patient actors on 25 of 26, and non-inferior on the rest. The evidence comes from simulated scenarios, not real patients, and the authors state that further research is needed before real-world use.

Technology: AMIE (Articulate Medical Intelligence Explorer), a large language model agent for diagnostic dialogue
