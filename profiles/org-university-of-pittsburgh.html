SLIDE uncovers a keratinocyte-centered signal in systemic sclerosis

An interpretable machine-learning analysis of multi-omic systemic-sclerosis data identified an unexplored keratinocyte-centered signature, validated by protein staining, and a previously unreported interaction between myeloid-cell and fibroblast states and macrophage HLA signaling. The same study also analyzed CD4 T-cell clonal expansion, but this record treats the paper's disease-mechanism findings as one discovery.

Technology: SLIDE (Significant Latent Factor Interaction Discovery and Exploration)

19 Feb 2024University of Pittsburgh

SLIDE links islet T-cell clone expansion to immune gene programs

SLIDE, an interpretable latent-factor interaction method, was applied to paired single-cell RNA-seq and T-cell receptor data from islet-derived CD4 T cells in NOD mice. The analysis identified latent components associated with clonal expansion and grouped inhibitory-receptor and intracellular signaling/apoptosis-related genes among the associated programs. The result is an inference from existing multiomic data, not a causal perturbation; it is a distinct T1D application in the same article as the separately catalogued systemic-sclerosis analysis.

Technology: SLIDE interpretable latent-factor interaction discovery and exploration

  • Still unknown: The inferred gene programs are associations from existing data; the T1D analysis does not prospectively perturb the proposed pathways or establish causality.
  • AI role: SLIDE uses interpretable machine learning to identify interactions among latent factors in high-dimensional omics data and was applied to connect T-cell gene programs with clonal expansion.
