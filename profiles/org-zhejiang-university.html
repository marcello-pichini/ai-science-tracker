22 Nov 2024Zhejiang University; Chengde Medical University; Hunan University; Macau University of Science and Technology; CarbonSilicon AI Technology Co., Ltd.; Macau Polytechnic University

ClickGen designs PARP1 inhibitors with measured nanomolar activity

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: ClickGen combines reaction-based molecular generation, reinforcement learning and inpainting to generate synthesizable candidate structures. In a PARP1 case study, researchers selected and synthesized three generated lead molecules; two showed nanomolar PARP1 inhibition in vitro, with reported IC50 values of 19.24 ± 1.63 nM and 69.73 ± 14.57 nM. The two compounds also showed antiproliferative activity in several tested cancer cell lines and lower toxicity in the reported normal-cell assays than the most toxic lead compounds.

Technology: ClickGen deep-learning reaction-based molecular generator with reinforcement learning, Monte Carlo tree search and an inpainting generator
