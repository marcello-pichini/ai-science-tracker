29 Apr 2026Zhejiang University

Qiushi Discovery Engine proposes and validates an optical bilinear interaction

An LLM agent (Qiushi Discovery Engine) given only the topic optical computing for AI ran an open-ended study of 145.9 million tokens, 3,242 LLM calls and 1,242 tool calls, and proposed an optical bilinear interaction analogous to the attention operation in Transformers, which the authors say they experimentally validated. The abstract gives no accuracy figure or number of experimental runs. This is an arXiv preprint and the validation is reported by the authors themselves.

Technology: Qiushi Discovery Engine (dual-layer LLM agent with Meta-Trace memory)

16 Feb 2026Zhejiang University

De novo designed proteins modulate GPCR signalling in the dopamine D1 receptor

Zhejiang University designed four de novo GPCR exoframe modulators (GEMs) that target the transmembrane domain of the dopamine D1 receptor. Functional assays showed they act as positive, negative or biased allosteric modulators, and one restored activity in loss-of-function receptor mutants. Design is computational; the exact assay numbers were not checked against the full paper, so use the paper for quantitative claims.

Technology: Computational de novo protein design (AI-based protein design pipeline)
