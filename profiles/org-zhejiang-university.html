Qiushi Discovery Engine proposes and validates an optical bilinear interaction

An LLM agent (Qiushi Discovery Engine) given only the topic optical computing for AI ran an open-ended study of 145.9 million tokens, 3,242 LLM calls and 1,242 tool calls, and proposed an optical bilinear interaction analogous to the attention operation in Transformers, which the authors say they experimentally validated. The abstract gives no accuracy figure or number of experimental runs. This is an arXiv preprint and the validation is reported by the authors themselves.

Technology: Qiushi Discovery Engine (dual-layer LLM agent with Meta-Trace memory)

16 Feb 2026Zhejiang University

De novo designed proteins modulate GPCR signalling in the dopamine D1 receptor

Zhejiang University designed four de novo GPCR exoframe modulators (GEMs) that target the transmembrane domain of the dopamine D1 receptor. Functional assays showed they act as positive, negative or biased allosteric modulators, and one restored activity in loss-of-function receptor mutants. Design is computational; the exact assay numbers were not checked against the full paper, so use the paper for quantitative claims.

Technology: Computational de novo protein design (AI-based protein design pipeline)

22 Nov 2024Zhejiang University; Chengde Medical University; Hunan University; Macau University of Science and Technology; CarbonSilicon AI Technology Co., Ltd.; Macau Polytechnic University

ClickGen designs PARP1 inhibitors with measured nanomolar activity

ClickGen combines reaction-based molecular generation, reinforcement learning and inpainting to generate synthesizable candidate structures. In a PARP1 case study, researchers selected and synthesized three generated lead molecules; two showed nanomolar PARP1 inhibition in vitro, with reported IC50 values of 19.24 ± 1.63 nM and 69.73 ± 14.57 nM. The two compounds also showed antiproliferative activity in several tested cancer cell lines and lower toxicity in the reported normal-cell assays than the most toxic lead compounds.

Technology: ClickGen deep-learning reaction-based molecular generator with reinforcement learning, Monte Carlo tree search and an inpainting generator

21 Aug 2024Zhejiang University; Zhejiang Lab; Zhejiang Chinese Medical University; Harvard University; Karolinska Institute; Stanford University

MitoReID identifies epicatechin as a cyclooxygenase-2 inhibitor

A deep-learning system called MitoReID classified drug mechanisms from time-resolved mitochondrial morphology and membrane-potential images. Among its natural-compound predictions, it nominated epicatechin as a COX-2 inhibitor; the authors report in-vitro follow-up including COX-2 activity, cellular thermal-shift and surface-plasmon-resonance assays. This is a target-mechanism finding, not evidence of clinical efficacy.

Technology: MitoReID, deep-learning re-identification model using an Inflated 3D ResNet backbone on temporal mitochondrial images
