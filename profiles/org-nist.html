24 Nov 2020NIST; University of Maryland, University of Washington, SLAC

CAMEO discovers GST467, a phase-change material with double the optical contrast

CAMEO, using Bayesian active learning and closed-loop X-ray measurements, identified the Ge4Sb6Te7 alloy in 19 experimental cycles (about 10 hours), selected from 177 compositions. GST467 showed twice the optical contrast of GST225 and was demonstrated in a phase-change memory device; the result concerns this library and application, not a commercial memory.

Technology: CAMEO, closed-loop Bayesian active learning
