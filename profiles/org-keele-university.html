t-SNE-selected LMC radio source receives a spectroscopic AGN classification at z = 2.80

SALT spectroscopy of the previously unclassified source LMC tSNE b8 (R.A. 06:22:41.86, Dec. −68:59:21.9) identified it as an active galactic nucleus at redshift z = 2.80. The source was selected from the blazar-like sequence in a t-SNE map combining VISTA near-infrared and ASKAP radio survey data with multi-wavelength photometry. This is one specific spectroscopic classification from the paper’s broader follow-up sample.

Technology: t-distributed stochastic neighbour embedding (t-SNE) applied to multi-wavelength source photometry

The selected source was classified as an AGN at z=2.80 from SALT spectroscopy, but the paper’s broader 18-target blazar-sequence follow-up also contains Magellanic objects and two low-S/N unconfirmed sources; those are not counted as confirmed discoveries. The claim here is limited to LMC tSNE b8. The paper calls the selection sequence blazar-like; that label does not establish this specific source as a blazar.
  • AI role: t-SNE mapped 6,652 Large Magellanic Cloud and 1,356 Small Magellanic Cloud sources using optical-to-radio features. The unsupervised map exposed a blazar-like sequence from which LMC tSNE b8 was selected for SALT follow-up; spectroscopy then established its AGN classification and redshift.
