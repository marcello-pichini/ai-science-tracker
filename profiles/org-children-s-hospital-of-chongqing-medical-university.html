PROBY screening uncovers target-selective fluorescent probes for tau, BCL-2 and TDP-43

The PROBY deep-learning pipeline screened 26,416 target-validated molecules for useful optical properties, identified 11 candidates for testing and found target-responsive probes PE859 (tau), obatoclax (BCL-2) and B3 (TDP-43). The study reports selectivity assays, cell/tissue labeling and ex-vivo tumor imaging with obatoclax. AI-guided structural modification of PE859 yielded 859-2, which enabled in-vivo two-photon and cleared-brain imaging of tau pathology in P301S transgenic mice; its measured tau-aggregate dissociation constant was 54.8 nM.

Technology: PROBY two-stage message-passing neural network pipeline (PROBY-L1 fluorescence classification and PROBY-L2 photophysical-property prediction)
