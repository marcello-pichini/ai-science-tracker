21 Nov 2026Max Planck Institute for Chemical Ecology; University of Georgia; Max Planck Institute for Plant Breeding Research

AlphaFold 3-guided mutagenesis identifies a candidate substrate-orienting enzyme residue

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: For an enzyme studied in a plant natural-product pathway, the authors used an AlphaFold 3 structure model with substrate docking and molecular-dynamics analysis to nominate candidate pocket residues. Mutational tests found that replacing one predicted aromatic residue abolished measured activity, while a conservative aromatic substitution restored activity, supporting a role in substrate orientation. This is a bounded residue-level mechanistic result: the authors report that the mutations do not establish a comprehensive mechanism and the predicted protein and complex structures were not experimentally characterized.

Technology: AlphaFold 3 structure prediction with substrate docking and molecular-dynamics-guided residue selection

20 Nov 2026University of California, Los Angeles; National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases

AlphaFold3-aided cryo-EM resolves native Anopheles salivary protein complexes

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Biochemical and mass-spectrometry analyses identified native interactions among Anopheles mosquito salivary proteins, including Saglin and SGS4/SGS5. AlphaFold predictions helped identify subunits, and AlphaFold3 models supplied initial structures for fitting into cryo-electron-microscopy density, followed by manual model building and refinement. The resulting Saglin–SGS4 structure and related complexes reveal native protein organization and partner-dependent conformations. AI assisted structural assignment; experimental maps and biochemical observations provide the direct evidence. The proposed relevance to malaria transmission remains a hypothesis: the study does not demonstrate parasite-invasion, transmission-blocking or clinical outcomes.

Technology: AlphaFold and AlphaFold 3 protein-structure prediction, used to guide subunit identification and cryo-EM model building

20 Nov 2026University of Milan; IRCCS Istituto Auxologico Italiano

AlphaFold-assisted study links WFS1 dysfunction to GnRH-neuron developmental defects

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An integrated human-genetics, structural-modeling and experimental study identified WFS1 as a candidate modifier of congenital hypogonadotropic hypogonadism. AlphaFold 3 models supported simulations of rare WFS1 variants, including altered protein motions that were not captured by a simple benign/damaging prediction. Cell assays found impaired protein behavior and ER-stress activation; zebrafish knockdown disrupted GnRH-neuron architecture and reduced isl1a expression, with partial pharmacological rescue. These results support a developmental mechanism in model systems, not proof that WFS1 alone causes the human disorder or that a treatment benefits patients.

Technology: AlphaFold 3 structural modeling; PolyPhen-2 variant prediction

20 Nov 2026Freie Universität Berlin; Technical University of Denmark

Predicted enzyme structures support shared substrate gates in C-glycosylation

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Analysis of structural models for 56 C-active UGT enzymes found acceptor gates throughout the set and two enzymes lacking a discernible donor gate; AlphaFold3 and source-labeled RobettaFold models supported variable open/closed gate conformations. These predictions extended a gating hypothesis derived from experimental structures and molecular simulations, rather than independently measuring dynamics across all enzymes. For maize ZmUGT708A6, crystallography, biochemical/mutational tests and QM/MM calculations supported a stepwise C-glycosylation mechanism involving water-mediated stabilization of a high-energy sigma complex. The AI contribution concerns comparison of predicted gate architectures; direct mechanistic tests focused on selected enzymes and substrates.

Technology: AlphaFold 3; RobettaFold

30 Sep 2026Google DeepMind

SynthID Bio: watermarking for proteins and synthetic structures

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Methods insert a detectable signal into protein sequences and predicted structures. For binders designed against three targets (VEGF-A, SARS-CoV-2 RBD, and PD-L1), watermarked designs matched the others in success rate and affinity. This is a proof of concept that needs to be made more robust against tampering.
