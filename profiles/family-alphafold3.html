SynthID Bio: watermarking for proteins and synthetic structures

Methods insert a detectable signal into protein sequences and predicted structures. For binders designed against three targets (VEGF-A, SARS-CoV-2 RBD, and PD-L1), watermarked designs matched the others in success rate and affinity. This is a proof of concept that needs to be made more robust against tampering.

Technology: SynthID Bio, AlphaFold 3 (optimized component)

25 Nov 2025Google DeepMind; and Norwegian University of Life Sciences

Healthier bees: AlphaFold predicts vitellogenin's structure

With the Norwegian University of Life Sciences, predicted structures of honeybee vitellogenin were confirmed by electron microscopy in July 2025; AlphaFold 3 helped study its interactions with other molecules. Early evidence suggests breeding times for resilient bees could fall from months to weeks, a still preliminary claim.

Technology: AlphaFold, AlphaFold 3

8 May 2024Google DeepMind; and Isomorphic Labs

AlphaFold 3: structure and interactions of life’s molecules

It predicts joint structures of proteins, nucleic acids, small molecules and ions. DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs report at least 50% greater accuracy than existing methods for protein interactions with other molecules. Free for non-commercial research through the AlphaFold Server.
