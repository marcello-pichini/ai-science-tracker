30 Sep 2026Google DeepMind

SynthID Bio: watermarking for proteins and synthetic structures

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Methods insert a detectable signal into protein sequences and predicted structures. For binders designed against three targets (VEGF-A, SARS-CoV-2 RBD, and PD-L1), watermarked designs matched the others in success rate and affinity. This is a proof of concept that needs to be made more robust against tampering.

Technology: SynthID Bio, AlphaFold 3 (optimized component)

Family AlphaFold 3: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Announcement only · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
SynthID Bio guided amino acid choice in a ProteinMPNN variant, adjusted predicted atomic coordinates, fine-tuned part of AlphaFold 3, and was integrated into Evo 2 to watermark a designed phage genome.
Test and result
Wet-lab tests on three targets (VEGF-A, SARS-CoV-2 RBD, PD-L1), with Adaptyv Bio helping, showed watermarked designs matched unwatermarked ones in hit rate, affinity and diversity; the phage was tested only in bacterial cultures.
Limitation / still unknown
The watermark must become more robust against deliberate tampering, the bacteriophage work is early-stage, and the post says no single biosecurity measure is a silver bullet.

4 May 2026University of California, San Francisco (Fraser and DeGrado groups)

Fragment-guided AI protein redesign yields a Kemp eliminase and fluorophore binder

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Crystallographic fragment screening of a designed binding protein guided redesign into a fluorophore binder and a Kemp eliminase. LigandMPNN and structure-prediction models supported the design workflow; crystal structures and enzyme assays checked the resulting functions. The paper reports catalytic efficiency of 3.2 million M⁻¹ s⁻¹ for the eliminase. This entry tracks the 4 May 2026 journal version; a preprint preceded it.

Technology: LigandMPNN with Rosetta relaxation for sequence design; RaptorX and later AlphaFold 3 for structure checking
