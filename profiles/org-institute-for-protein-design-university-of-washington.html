Deep-learning design of zinc metalloproteases

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: RFdiffusion2 for Molecular Interfaces designs zinc-dependent proteases for peptide-bond hydrolysis. The primary paper reports cleavage at the targeted bond in 36% of 135 designs and selected designs that cleave disease-associated proteins in vitro. These biochemical results do not establish therapeutic efficacy.

22 Nov 2025Institute for Protein Design, University of Washington

RFD2-MI designs sequence-specific cysteine proteases

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: RFD2-MI designs cysteine-nucleophile proteases that cleave peptide backbones in a sequence-dependent manner. The preprint abstract reports rate enhancements up to 3 × 10^7 and crystal structures close to design models. The journal version reports additional experiments; this draft is bounded to the preprint abstract result and does not transfer later results across versions.

Technology: RFD2-MI; ProteinMPNN; AlphaFold 3
