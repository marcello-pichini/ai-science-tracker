27 Jan 2025University of Toronto and Insilico Medicine

Two molecules against KRAS from a hybrid AI and quantum process

A process combining quantum and classical computing with Chemistry42 selected 15 candidates; two showed activity against mutated KRAS variants in living cells. Published in Nature Biotechnology, this is a proof of principle, with no significant quantum advantage or demonstrated superiority over compounds obtained by classical methods.

Technology: Chemistry42 and hybrid quantum-classical models

19 Jan 2023University of Toronto, Insilico Medicine and collaborators

A liver-cancer target and molecule identified with Pharma.AI

Pharma.AI combined PandaOmics, Chemistry42 and an AlphaFold-predicted structure to identify a hepatocellular-carcinoma target and produce a hit molecule within 30 days of target selection, synthesizing seven compounds. A second round produced a more potent hit; an early Chemical Science study, with no clinical efficacy demonstrated.

Technology: Pharma.AI, PandaOmics, Chemistry42 and AlphaFold

13 May 2020University of Toronto

Cu–Al electrocatalysts for CO2 found with active learning

DFT and active machine learning identified Cu–Al catalysts that reduce CO2 to ethylene with over 80% Faradaic efficiency at 400 mA/cm² (about 66% for pure copper) and 55% half-cell energy efficiency at 150 mA/cm². Synthesized and tested in the lab; the abstract does not state how many candidates were screened.

Technology: Active machine learning and DFT
