Generative design of de novo AAV vectors with unique serological profiles

The preprint abstract reports a variational autoencoder trained on natural AAV sequences to generate capsids. More than 255 designs were synthesized and tested in vitro. Seven transduced human cells at over 50% efficiency; four of those avoided neutralization by every AAV-reactive human serum sample tested, at the reported NT50 threshold below 1:50. This establishes in-vitro capsid properties, not in-vivo delivery or clinical efficacy. Review is limited to the abstract and original-server metadata.

Technology: Variational autoencoder (generative model of AAV capsid sequences)

28 Jun 2023University of Toronto; King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST)

Machine-learning-guided experiments identify a high-power-factor PbSe:SnSb thermoelectric family

A closed-loop error-correction learning workflow guided synthesis of PbSe alloyed with SnSb and documented a previously unexplored low-temperature thermoelectric family. At 300 K, the best tested composition, 2 wt% SnSb-doped PbSe, had a measured power factor above 850 µW mK⁻² and more than twice that of PbSe. Density-functional calculations attribute the modeled transport improvement to Sn/Sb interactions that reduce hole effective mass.

Technology: Closed-loop error-correction learning with a neural-network update to a prior materials-property model; composition representations compared included Magpie, Roost and CrabNet

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: A closed-loop error-correction learning workflow guided synthesis of PbSe alloyed with SnSb and documented a previously unexplored low-temperature thermoelectric family.
  • Still unknown: The result is specific to the tested powder-processing, composition and measurement protocol, including synthesis below 300 °C; it is not evidence of device-level or commercial performance. The reported mechanism for the increased power factor is supported by DFT calculations, not a direct measurement of carrier effective mass. The paper compares the 2 wt% composition with PbSe and reports more than 2× power factor at room temperature.

Iterative model-guided experiments selected the PbSe:SnSb family and 2 wt% SnSb-doped PbSe for characterization. AI supplied composition down-selection and adaptive experiment guidance; the authors synthesized and measured the samples.

3 Oct 2022University of Toronto; RIKEN Center for Sustainable Resource Science, and collaborators

BIONIC links the natural product NP329 to yeast cell-wall biogenesis and β-1,6-glucan synthesis

The BIONIC graph-convolutional network integrated yeast interaction datasets and predicted essential-gene chemical sensitivities to the uncharacterized compound NP329. Experimental screens supported four BIONIC-predicted sensitive genes in the cell-wall biogenesis process; follow-up tests showed related steroidal alkaloid jervine reduced β-1,6-glucan and KRE6/SKN1 mutations conferred resistance, supporting a pathway-level connection for this compound family.

Technology: BIONIC (Biological Network Integration using Convolutions), a graph-convolutional deep-learning network-integration method

15 Sep 2021University of Toronto; NVIDIA; Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai; NYU Grossman School of Medicine

Federated learning model predicts oxygen needs in COVID-19 patients

The EXAM federated model, trained on data from 20 institutes without sharing patient data, achieved an average AUC above 0.92 for predicting oxygen requirements at 24 and 72 hours. At the largest independent test site it reached sensitivity 0.950 and specificity 0.882, with a 16% average AUC gain over single-site models. Caveat: retrospective data from the pandemic period and unharmonized sites.

Technology: EXAM federated learning model (EMR data and chest X-rays)

26 Aug 2021University of Toronto; Vector Institute; Griffith University; Freie Universität Berlin

THESEUS derives resource-efficient designs for high-dimensional photonic GHZ and heralded Bell states

The interpretable THESEUS design algorithm produced a three-dimensional six-photon GHZ-state blueprint that suppresses unwanted state terms toward arbitrarily high fidelity, with lower count rates, and a heralded three-dimensional Bell-state blueprint using destructive interference to cancel vacuum contributions. The same graph-based reasoning generalized the constructions to larger photon numbers or dimensions.

Technology: THESEUS

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: The interpretable THESEUS design algorithm produced a three-dimensional six-photon GHZ-state blueprint that suppresses unwanted state terms toward arbitrarily high fidelity, with lower count rates, and a heralded three-dimensional Bell-state blueprint using destructive interference to cancel vacuum contributions.
  • Still unknown: These are theoretically calculated experiment blueprints, not experimentally demonstrated states in this paper. For the six-photon GHZ design, unwanted amplitudes can be reduced arbitrarily by adjusting graph weights, at the cost of lower count rates; feasibility is argued for contemporary photonics. 1 Apr 2020University of Toronto

Machine-learning-guided synthesis yields a new blue-emitting perovskite-like single crystal

A high-throughput robotic synthesis and characterization workflow used a convolutional neural network to recognize crystal growth and a machine-learning regressor to guide synthesis conditions. The team reported the first synthesis of (3-PLA)₂PbCl₄, a new perovskite-like single crystal with a direct band gap near 4 eV and strong blue emission. The reported growth conditions were restrictive and required a carefully tuned search.

Technology: Convolutional neural network image classifier and machine-learning synthesis-condition regressor
