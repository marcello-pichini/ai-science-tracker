libgit2 bundled PCRE heap buffer overflow

libgit2 · AI involvement attributed to Claude Mythos Preview

Finding and attribution

The bundled PCRE regular-expression engine can write one byte past a heap buffer for a crafted revspec.

What to update · libgit2

Affected versions not confirmed in this summary.

Remediation note: The advisory lists no patched libgit2 version. It describes a structural fix in PCRE2 that was not backported to bundled PCRE 8.x.
