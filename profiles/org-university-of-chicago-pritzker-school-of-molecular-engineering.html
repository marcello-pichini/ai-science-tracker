ElectrolyteGPT can generate new formulations for battery development

University of Chicago researchers trained a GPT model with a new notation (fLine) to generate complete battery electrolyte formulations, including solvent ratios, salt concentrations and temperature. Several AI-proposed compositions were synthesized and, per the release, performed on par with the state of the art in lithium metal batteries. No cycling or capacity figures are given, the work is early, and generated candidates still need experimental verification.

Technology: ElectrolyteGPT (GPT with fLine notation)

18 Oct 2023University of Chicago Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering; University of Chicago Cellular Screening Center

Machine-learning-guided screening discovers potent innate immune signaling modulators

At the University of Chicago, an active-learning pipeline iteratively selected molecules from a 139,998-compound library for in-vitro assays of NF-κB and interferon regulatory factor (IRF) signaling. After screening about 2% of the library, the study reported compounds suppressing NF-κB up to 15-fold or elevating NF-κB and IRF up to 5-fold and 6-fold, respectively. Follow-up cytokine assays found PME-4007 increased IFN-β secretion about threefold when co-delivered with the STING agonist cGAMP.

Technology: Machine-learning active-learning pipeline with variational-autoencoder molecular representations, Gaussian-process regression and Bayesian optimization
