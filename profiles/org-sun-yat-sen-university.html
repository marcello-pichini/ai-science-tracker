DeltaCata guides methyltransferase engineering with improved catalytic efficiency in vitro

The accepted-paper abstract reports that DeltaCata, a geometric graph neural network predicting mutation effects on enzyme kinetics, guided engineering of methyltransferase sGmOMT2. The authors report up to a sevenfold improvement in catalytic efficiency for in vitro icaritin synthesis. This experimental application goes beyond the model benchmarks; industrial-scale performance is not demonstrated by the reviewed abstract.

Technology: DeltaCata; Siamese geometric graph neural network

23 Jun 2025Sun Yat-sen University

Machine learning identifies disordered twist-angle sequences with strongly reduced thermal conductivity in multilayer graphene

Bayesian optimization and a CNN, coupled to non-equilibrium molecular dynamics, selected two-angle disordered twisted multilayer graphene structures with substantially lower simulated cross-plane thermal conductivity than aligned and periodic stacks. The optimized 14-layer sequence reached 0.095 W m−1 K−1 versus 0.512 W m−1 K−1 in aligned graphene in the reported model; phonon participation ratios and dynamical structure factors support strong partial phonon localization.

Technology: Bayesian optimization (COMBO) and convolutional neural network coupled to molecular-dynamics simulations

8 Jul 2024Sun Yat-sen University; Huazhong Agricultural University; Wen’s Foodstuff Group Co. Ltd.; Stanford University

Multi-omics machine learning identifies lysophosphatidic acid in PRRSV infection and links it to immune suppression

Machine-learning analysis of untargeted serum metabolomic and lipidomic profiles from clinical pig cohorts identified lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) as a PRRSV-associated biomarker. Follow-up infection experiments reported increased LPA with autotaxin upregulation; in porcine alveolar macrophages, LPA manipulation altered RIG-I/type-I-interferon responses and viral replication. In infected pigs, targeting LPA was reported to protect against infection and associated disease outcomes.

Technology: Random-forest analysis of serum metabolomic and lipidomic profiles
