11 Apr 2025Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light; and LIGO Laboratory

Urania designs new gravitational-wave detectors

An algorithm designing interferometers from scratch produced 50 layouts that, in simulation, raise the potentially observable volume by up to 50-fold (best case). Designs are published as a Detector Zoo. Simulation only: nothing has been built or tested.

Technology: Urania (continuous optimization with ML-inspired search)

10 Dec 2024Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light; Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg

XLuminA computationally discovers a super-resolution microscopy blueprint combining two optical principles

The XLuminA differentiable optical-design system rediscovered established STED beam shaping and optical-vortex approaches, then identified a previously unreported experimental blueprint combining principles from both to produce sub-diffraction imaging in the study’s computational model. This is a specific theoretical/optical-design result, not a report of an independently built and experimentally validated microscope implementing the new blueprint.

Technology: XLuminA auto-differentiating optical experiment-design framework; transformer-based code-generation model used in the discovery workflow

Nonlocal quantum interference without entanglement of intrinsic properties

An AI- and graph-theory-based prediction was confirmed in two experiments: four photons exhibit nonlocal interference without requiring entanglement of their intrinsic properties. The superposition concerns their generation locations; applications to metrology remain prospective.
