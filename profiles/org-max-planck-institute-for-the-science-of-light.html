17 May 2023Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light, Nanjing University and Hefei University

Nonlocal quantum interference without entanglement of intrinsic properties

An AI- and graph-theory-based prediction was confirmed in two experiments: four photons exhibit nonlocal interference without requiring entanglement of their intrinsic properties. The superposition concerns their generation locations; applications to metrology remain prospective.
