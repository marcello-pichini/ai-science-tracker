Graph neural networks identify oxides for hydrogen production

A model of oxygen-vacancy properties selected twelve oxides to test for thermochemical hydrogen production; about 80% were active in experiments. Some were comparable to or better than the baseline only under certain reactor conditions; further study and optimization are needed.

Technology: Graph neural networks for crystal defects

9 Dec 2025Sandia National Laboratories

A self-driving laboratory discovers lens-and-grating patterns that steer spontaneous emission

A self-driving nanophotonics experiment discovered that combining lens-like refractive-index curvature with grating-like spatial gradient can steer incoherent light emission from a semiconductor metasurface beyond conventional momentum-matching designs. Across a 74° field of view, optimized patterns produced average peak directivity 2.2 times that of sawtooth-grating controls, reaching 67% peak directivity; a fitted equation learner extracted a compact relationship from the experiments.

Technology: Variational autoencoder, active-learning experimental planner and neural-network equation learner

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: A self-driving nanophotonics experiment discovered that combining lens-like refractive-index curvature with grating-like spatial gradient can steer incoherent light emission from a semiconductor metasurface beyond conventional momentum-matching designs.
  • Still unknown: The article reports local optima under a finite experiment budget, not a global optimum. Measurements were made on a specific GaAs/InAs quantum-dot metasurface; extensions to other emitters or devices remain untested in this study.
  • AI role: The VAE generated pump-pattern configurations, an active-learning agent selected closed-loop physical experiments using measured photoluminescence, and an equation-learning network distilled observed structure–property relationships. The experimental setup measured and human researchers interpreted the results.
