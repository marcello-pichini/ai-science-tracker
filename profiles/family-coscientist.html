16 Sep 2026Stanford University; collaborating institutions

Paper2Agent integrates perturbation datasets to prioritize GPR137 at a psoriasis risk locus

Paper2Agent-generated agents used AlphaGenome to prioritize GPR137 as the top predicted gene affected by psoriasis-associated variant rs887314 in CD4+ T cells, then integrated that prediction with existing MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq data. A cross-screen correlation of gene-expression signatures supported GPR137 as the probable causal gene under stimulated conditions (Spearman rho 0.613 at 8 h and 0.630 at 48 h; FDR < 0.05), but not at rest. The paper presents this as activation-dependent computational evidence.

Technology: Paper2Agent-generated AlphaGenome, MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq agents, coordinated through an AI co-scientist

Co-Scientist: repurposed drugs against liver fibrosis

In Gary Peltz's Stanford lab, two of the three existing drugs proposed by Co-Scientist blocked fibrosis and promoted regeneration in human liver cells, while Peltz's own two choices showed no benefit. The anticancer drug vorinostat blocked 91% of a damage response that can lead to scarring. Cell experiments only; published in Advanced Science.

AI co-scientist: a multi-agent system for generating hypotheses

A system based on Gemini 2.0 that generates hypotheses and research proposals. In the lab, three drugs it suggested for repurposing against acute myeloid leukemia reduced cancer-cell viability, and all suggested treatments for liver fibrosis showed activity. Expert assessments covered only 11 targets; fibrosis results awaited a Stanford report.

Technology: AI co-scientist (Gemini 2.0, multi-agent)
