Paper2Agent integrates perturbation datasets to prioritize GPR137 at a psoriasis risk locus

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Paper2Agent-generated agents used AlphaGenome to prioritize GPR137 as the top predicted gene affected by psoriasis-associated variant rs887314 in CD4+ T cells, then integrated that prediction with existing MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq data. A cross-screen correlation of gene-expression signatures supported GPR137 as the probable causal gene under stimulated conditions (Spearman rho 0.613 at 8 h and 0.630 at 48 h; FDR < 0.05), but not at rest. The paper presents this as activation-dependent computational evidence.

Technology: Paper2Agent-generated AlphaGenome, MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq agents, coordinated through an AI co-scientist

AI contribution
The AlphaGenome paper agent scored and ranked candidate genes at rs887314. Under human-in-the-loop supervision, the AI co-scientist inspected two other paper agents' datasets, proposed ten validation strategies, and the researcher selected signature-correlation analysis. The resulting cross-screen, cross-modality analysis found significant concordance for GPR137 only under stimulated conditions.

Limitation / still unknown
The analysis reuses existing published datasets and does not report new wet-lab experiments for this psoriasis case. AlphaGenome supplied a model prediction; cross-dataset signature correlation supports, but does not establish, causal biology. The GPR137 concordance was significant only after stimulation, and the authors describe it as probable/activation-dependent. A human researcher selected which AI-proposed strategy to run. Primary paper source checked; no independent replication assessed.

19 Feb 2025Google Research

AI co-scientist: a multi-agent system for generating hypotheses

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A system based on Gemini 2.0 that generates hypotheses and research proposals. In the lab, three drugs it suggested for repurposing against acute myeloid leukemia reduced cancer-cell viability, and all suggested treatments for liver fibrosis showed activity. Expert assessments covered only 11 targets; fibrosis results awaited a Stanford report.

Technology: AI co-scientist (Gemini 2.0, multi-agent)

Family Gemini: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 67 medicine Blog postPreprint Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A Gemini-based multi-agent system generated, debated and ranked hypotheses; experts selected candidates and prioritized lab experiments.
Test and result
Elo auto-evaluation over 203 research goals; 15 expert-curated goals; blinded experts rated 11 goals (preference rank 2.36). In vitro on four AML cell lines: binimetinib, pacritinib, cerivastatin reduced viability; KIRA6 IC50 10 nM in KG-1a versus 180 nM in TK6. Seven combinations tested.
Limitation / still unknown
The paper calls validation preliminary and in vitro only. KIRA6 was far less effective in MOLM-13 and HL-60. Expert evaluation was small and subjective; Elo is not ground truth. Liver fibrosis and AMR details are summarized only.
