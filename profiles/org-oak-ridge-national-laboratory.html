7 Nov 2022Oak Ridge National Laboratory; University of Tennessee, Knoxville

Autonomous microscopy discovers an edge plasmon in layered MnPS₃

Deep-kernel-learning-guided STEM-EELS measurements identified a low-energy edge-localized plasmon response in the layered antiferromagnet MnPS₃, distinct from the higher-energy bulk plasmon response. The system used local structure to predict spectra and uncertainty, then autonomously selected where to measure based on a physics-defined objective. After 10–25 spectra, about 1% of the image-space points, it localized the edge mode to the flake boundary.

Technology: Deep kernel learning (feed-forward neural-network feature embedding with a Gaussian-process kernel and physics-based acquisition/scalarizer)

4 Nov 2022Oak Ridge National Laboratory; Argonne National Laboratory; Instituto de Física de Líquidos y Sistemas Biológicos, UNLP-CONICET

Machine-learning-assisted neutron scattering shows hydrostatic pressure tunes interactions in spin ice

Machine-learning-assisted analysis of diffuse neutron scattering from Dy₂Ti₂O₇ at ambient pressure and about 1.3 GPa extracted pressure-dependent Hamiltonian parameters and found that hydrostatic pressure modifies further-neighbor magnetic interactions and the material’s nanoscale magnetic-glass ordering. The analysis supports using pressure to tune the spin-ice glass state. A transition at substantially higher pressure is a model-based prediction, not an observed transition in this experiment.

Technology: Nonlinear autoencoder, radial-basis generative surrogate and hierarchical clustering for neutron-scattering analysis

4 Apr 2022Oak Ridge National Laboratory; Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago; Nagoya Institute of Technology and collaborators

Active-learning experiments reveal distinct on-field and off-field polarization-switching behavior in ferroelectrics

An active-learning workflow paired with automated piezoresponse-force microscopy discovered relationships between local ferroelectric domain structure and polarization-switching hysteresis. The experimentally selected sampling paths and points differed substantially between on-field and off-field hysteresis loops, indicating that the two measurement modes are dominated by different mechanisms. This is a bounded result from the reported ferroelectric sample and spectroscopy workflow, not a universal mechanism established for all ferroelectrics.

Technology: Machine-learning active-learning framework for automated piezoresponse force microscopy and spectroscopy
