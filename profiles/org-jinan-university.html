29 Nov 2022Jinan University; Shenzhen People’s Hospital; collaborating institutions

Machine-learning-guided PBMC proteomics identifies SLE diagnosis and activity biomarker panels

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A random-forest and OPLS-DA prioritization pipeline applied to peripheral-blood mononuclear-cell proteomics identified candidate protein combinations for distinguishing systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) from healthy and rheumatoid-arthritis controls and for separating active from inactive SLE. ELISA in a separate 80-person cohort supported a six-protein diagnosis panel (IFIT3, MX1, TOMM40, STAT1, STAT2 and OAS3; AUC 0.723 versus healthy controls and 0.815 versus rheumatoid arthritis) and a nine-protein activity panel (AUC 0.990). Single-cell RNA-sequencing analysis mapped several associated interferon-signature proteins to specific immune-cell populations.

Technology: POC-SLE: random-forest feature ranking with 1,000 bootstrap iterations, combined with OPLS-DA variable-importance selection

Editorial relevance 60 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The random forest ranked differentially expressed proteins from discovery-cohort PBMC proteomics; its selected proteins were intersected with OPLS-DA features to construct candidate diagnosis and activity panels. Researchers evaluated those panels with ELISA in a separate cohort and used single-cell transcriptomic data to examine immune-cell sources.
Test and result
ELISA in a separate 80-person cohort supported a six-protein diagnosis panel (IFIT3, MX1, TOMM40, STAT1, STAT2 and OAS3; AUC 0.723 versus healthy controls and 0.815 versus rheumatoid arthritis) and a nine-protein activity panel (AUC 0.990).
Limitation / still unknown
The study is cross-sectional and the ELISA validation cohort contained 80 people from the same clinical setting; it does not establish longitudinal flare prediction, treatment response, causal mechanisms or clinical utility. The six-protein panel had moderate discrimination versus healthy controls (AUC 0.723) and better but still cohort-specific discrimination versus rheumatoid arthritis (AUC 0.815). The activity-panel AUC of 0.990 was reported for active versus inactive groups in this validation cohort and needs broader independent validation. Authors note limited sample size/ethnic diversity and need for mechanistic study.
