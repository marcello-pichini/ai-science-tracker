28 May 2026Institute for Basic Science (IBS) Center for Nanoparticle Research; Seoul National University

Deep learning finds multimetallic single-atom catalysts across material families

The Crossbreeding Neural Network (CBNN), trained jointly on carbon-supported single-atom catalysts and perovskite oxides, screened 8,008 candidates. Its top W-Mo-Ru-Rh design on a perovskite support outperformed the comparison catalysts in electrochemical tests, and the activity ranking of 12 tested catalysts matched the predictions. These counts come from the IBS institutional release; the paper abstract confirms the experimental result but does not quantify the activity gain. The catalog date is the Nature Materials publication date, 28 May 2026.

Technology: CBNN (Crossbreeding Neural Network)
