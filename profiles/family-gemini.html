Language models flag spiritual pain in palliative nursing notes with many false positives

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Gemini 2.5 Flash and Microsoft Copilot detected all 56 expert-labeled spiritual-pain notes in a selected 500-note palliative-care sample. Positive predictive values were only 34% and 25%, respectively. The retrospective study identifies substantial over-detection rather than validating an autonomous diagnostic or clinical screening workflow.

Technology: Gemini 2.5 Flash; Microsoft Copilot language-model service

Family Gemini: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Pretrained language models applied prompted spiritual-pain definitions to free-text nursing notes, producing classifications compared with independent expert annotations; error analysis characterized their misinterpretations.
Test and result
From 6,858 nursing records in the last three weeks of life of 109 patients, keyword filtering retained 1,808 records and 500 were sampled. Two researchers annotated spiritual pain with consensus resolution. Japanese few-shot prompts supplied definitions/examples to Gemini 2.5 Flash and Copilot. Both detected all 56 reference-positive notes; specificity was 0.75/0.61 and positive predictive value 0.34/0.25. Gemini false positives frequently confused declining physical function and delirium with spiritual pain.
Limitation / still unknown
Selected single-hospital Japanese records, multiple notes per patient and keyword enrichment limit generalization and apparent sensitivity. Reference is retrospective note interpretation, not direct patient assessment. Copilot underlying model version was not specified. No unfiltered-record screening, prospective clinical utility, improved care or autonomous diagnosis was established; human verification remains necessary.

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

This advances particular constructions; it does not resolve Lehmer’s conjecture or establish global optimality at every degree.

Technology: Internal Gemini versions including Gemini 4 Argon, coupled to Google Antigravity

Limitation / still unknown

19 Oct 2026Hong Kong University of Science and Technology; Adobe Research

Gemini flags local execution errors in successful multi-agent runs

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In 1,828 benchmark traces from nine multi-agent configurations, a Gemini 3.1 Pro judge flagged local execution errors in 54.3% of successful runs (140 of 258) and 75.2% of failed runs (1,181 of 1,570). In a 45-trace sample, two human auditors both retained 64 of 73 existing flags and found additional errors. The result describes the errors the judge flagged under the study's definition and does not estimate how many errors it missed across the full corpus.

Technology: Gemini 3.1 Pro

AI contribution
A pretrained Gemini 3.1 Pro model assigned candidate local error labels within traces produced by other agent systems.
Test and result
The authors annotated 1,828 existing benchmark traces from nine configurations. Two people audited a stratified random sample of 45 traces. Three further language models reannotated a separate sample of 90 traces.
Limitation / still unknown
Annotation completeness was uncertain. The human audit found 21 additional errors across 11 traces, including five found by both auditors. The observed percentages do not measure the full prevalence of execution errors or operational reliability.

15 Oct 2026Stanford University

AI video analysis links lower purposeful activity with documented ICU delirium

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In a single-center retrospective study of 280 ICU patients, TRACER used Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite to detect actions across about 60,000 camera-hours. Patients with any positive delirium assessment during their stay (53 patients versus 227 without) showed less purposeful activity. Behavior was pooled across whole stays, rather than aligned to delirious periods. Detection errors, intermittent assessments, sedation and length-of-stay differences limit interpretation. This preprint reports a cohort association, without establishing early prediction, causality or clinical benefit. The result was present in the October 15 preprint before the November announcement.

Technology: TRACER; Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite

Gemini derives an exact analytic solution for the cosmic-string gravitational-radiation power integral

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A Gemini Deep Think system, guided by tree search and numerical feedback, derived closed-form spectral coefficients for the integral determining gravitational radiation power from cosmic-string loops, resolving the paper’s stated open calculation. The authors compare the expressions against direct numerical integration over ranges of loop harmonic and geometry parameters, finding close agreement. This is a theoretical/computational result, not a new astronomical detection.

Technology: Google Gemini Deep Think integrated with tree search, executable Python evaluation and high-precision numerical feedback
