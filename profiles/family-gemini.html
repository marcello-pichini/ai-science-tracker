11 Feb 2026Google DeepMind

Gemini Deep Think on research problems in mathematics, physics, and computer science

The Aletheia mathematical agent scored up to 90% on IMO-ProofBench Advanced and independently solved four open Erdős questions; work with experts covered 18 research problems, including a disproof of a submodular optimization conjecture. Its score on doctoral-level problems falls to about 38–46%; no result is claimed as a breakthrough, and the papers were submitted to journals.

Technology: Gemini Deep Think, Aletheia agent

Gemini Deep Think reaches gold-medal level at the 2025 IMO

An advanced version of Gemini Deep Think solved 5 of 6 problems at the 2025 IMO, scoring 35 out of 42 and working in natural language within the 4.5-hour limit. IMO coordinators assessed and certified the score, while clarifying that they do not validate the system.

14 May 2025Google DeepMind

AlphaEvolve: an agent for designing algorithms

An evolutionary agent based on Gemini and automated evaluators. It recovered an average of 0.7% of Google's computing resources, sped up a Gemini training kernel by 23%, and found an algorithm using 48 multiplications for 4×4 complex matrices. Across more than 50 open math problems, it matched the state of the art in about 75% and improved it in about 20%.

Technology: AlphaEvolve (Gemini Flash and Pro)

19 Feb 2025Google Research

AI co-scientist: a multi-agent system for generating hypotheses

A system based on Gemini 2.0 that generates hypotheses and research proposals. In the lab, three drugs it suggested for repurposing against acute myeloid leukemia reduced cancer-cell viability, and all suggested treatments for liver fibrosis showed activity. Expert assessments covered only 11 targets; fibrosis results awaited a Stanford report.

Technology: AI co-scientist (Gemini 2.0, multi-agent)
