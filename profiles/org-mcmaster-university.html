14 Apr 2022McMaster University; University of British Columbia; University of Toronto; University of Pittsburgh

Machine-guided microscopy characterizes an atypical ataxia presentation

Unsupervised image analysis detected cellular patterns not apparent by inspection in an investigation of one family with an atypical ataxia presentation. Additional cell experiments found altered ataxin-7 behavior and mitochondrial bioenergetic deficits. The proposed combined role of ATXN7 and TOP1MT variants remains a hypothesis: unaffected parents carried individual variants, and fly models did not reproduce the patient phenotype.

8 Jan 2021McMaster University; Dalhousie University

Learned resistance models expose tested enzyme-substrate activities

Interrogating models trained on genome and resistance data nominated enzyme-substrate relationships for experimental testing. The authors report 22 previously unknown activities validated in their assay system. This is one mechanism study; it does not establish the prevalence or clinical contribution of each relationship in patients. Model performance varied substantially across organism and drug datasets.
