GlaViTU maps glaciers worldwide close to expert accuracy

A convolutional-transformer model maps glacier outlines from open satellite imagery, with intersection over union above 0.85 on unseen images in most cases, above 0.75 in debris-rich areas such as High-Mountain Asia and above 0.90 where clean ice dominates. Compared with human expert uncertainty it approaches or matches expert delineation; adding radar data improves accuracy. A benchmark covering 9% of the world's glaciers is released.

Technology: GlaViTU (Glacier-VisionTransformer-U-Net)
