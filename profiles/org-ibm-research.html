Sequence-conditioned CogMol designs SARS-CoV-2 spike and main-protease inhibitors

IBM Research and academic collaborators used the CogMol deep generative foundation model to design compounds from target protein sequence information alone for SARS-CoV-2 spike receptor-binding domain and main protease. In vitro inhibition was reported for two of four synthesized candidates for each target; the most potent spike-RBD inhibitor also showed activity against multiple variants in live-virus neutralization assays.

Technology: CogMol, a sequence-conditioned deep generative foundation model using a molecular variational autoencoder and protein–ligand binding predictor

11 Mar 2021IBM Research

IBM designs two new antimicrobial peptides with generative models

Over 48 days, 20 candidates were designed, synthesized, and tested; two showed activity against several pathogens, including multidrug-resistant Klebsiella pneumoniae. In vitro and mouse tests only, with no demonstration of safety or efficacy in humans.

Technology: Generative autoencoder and CLaSS
