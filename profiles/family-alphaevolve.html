1 May 2026Massachusetts Institute of Technology; ETH Zurich; Stanford University

New Zarankiewicz numbers and bounds from OpenEvolve search

Using the open-source OpenEvolve LLM-driven evolutionary framework with a custom reward, the authors find exact values Z(11,21,3,3)=116, Z(11,22,3,3)=121 and Z(12,22,3,3)=132, and lower bounds for 41 further parameter sets, at under $30 per case. Caveats: arXiv preprint not peer reviewed; a separate NeurIPS 2026 listing by overlapping authors reports different counts.

Technology: OpenEvolve (LLM-guided evolutionary search, AlphaEvolve-style)

AlphaEvolve raises nine Ramsey lower bounds

AlphaEvolve-generated search algorithms improved bounds such as R(3,13) from 60 to 61, R(4,16) from 170 to 174 and R(4,19) from 213 to 219. The paper does not describe independent verification; search code is public. arXiv preprint, latest version read.

AlphaEvolve on 67 math problems with Terence Tao

Tested on 67 problems in analysis, combinatorics, geometry, and number theory, AlphaEvolve matched the best known solutions in most cases and improved them in several; in some cases it generalized finite instances into formulas valid for every input. 14 May 2025Google DeepMind

AlphaEvolve: an agent for designing algorithms

An evolutionary agent based on Gemini and automated evaluators. It recovered an average of 0.7% of Google's computing resources, sped up a Gemini training kernel by 23%, and found an algorithm using 48 multiplications for 4×4 complex matrices. Across more than 50 open math problems, it matched the state of the art in about 75% and improved it in about 20%.

Technology: AlphaEvolve (Gemini Flash and Pro)
