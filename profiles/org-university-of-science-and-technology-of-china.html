Transformer-based SDSS search identifies two previously unreported optical–UV tidal disruption events

A PCA-enhanced Transformer classifier screened 298,195 SDSS DR7 spectra and selected 14 TDE-like cases for secondary review. The authors report two newly identified TDEs: SDSS J124225.39+642919.0, whose GALEX UV source was present by the spectrum date (before 11 February 2002), and SDSS J152459.70+045423.1, whose TDE-H+He spectrum coincided with a Catalina optical outburst in 2007–08. The first is reported as the earliest optical–UV TDE then known; one other model-selected source was a previously reported likely TDE.

Technology: PCA-enhanced Transformer TDE spectrum classifier

4 Apr 2026University of Science and Technology of China; Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory

Weakly supervised deep learning maps seamount morphology patterns associated with tectonic setting

A weakly supervised deep-learning analysis combining satellite-derived gravity and bathymetry produced a global seamount inventory and reported systematic morphology differences: broad edifices along fast-spreading ridges and steeper forms near intraplate hotspots. The authors interpret these differences as reflecting geodynamic controls on volcanic construction. Many detections rely on indirect gravity/bathymetry data rather than shipborne mapping, so individual feature identifications and causal interpretations require further validation.

Technology: Weakly supervised deep-learning detector with vision foundation model, iterative pseudo-label refinement, and elliptical Gaussian fitting

21 Aug 2023University of Science and Technology of China; Chinese Academy of Sciences

Active-learning search experimentally realizes a high-zT Cu₁₋ₓAgₓGaTe₂ thermoelectric

Compressed-sensing symbolic regression and active learning screened the Cu₁₋ₓAgₓGaTe₂ thermoelectric series and guided synthesis of selected compositions. The published study reports a p-type Cu₀.₄₅Ag₀.₅₅GaTe₂ sample with a measured thermoelectric figure of merit zT of about 1.90 at 770 K, using measured heat capacity in the analysis. This is one paper-level materials result from a targeted search, not a claim that every composition in the series is new.

Technology: SISSO compressed-sensing symbolic regression within an active-learning workflow

1 May 2023University of Science and Technology of China; Mengcheng National Geophysical Observatory

Machine-learning classification shows rigidity-corrected moment release is similar across earthquake depths

Random-forest classification of source-time-function characteristics for 3,675 global Mw > 5.5 earthquakes showed that depth-dependent rigidity explains much of the differences in rupture duration, length and stress drop between shallow, intermediate and deep earthquakes. Once medium rigidity is corrected, the analyzed events exhibit similar moment-release processes, supporting a constant-strain-drop interpretation across depths and host-rock types.

Technology: Random-forest machine-learning classification and feature-importance analysis

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Random-forest classification of source-time-function characteristics for 3,675 global Mw > 5.5 earthquakes showed that depth-dependent rigidity explains much of the differences in rupture duration, length and stress drop between shallow, intermediate and deep earthquakes.
  • Still unknown: This is a global observational analysis of 3,675 moderate-to-large earthquakes. Its constant-strain-drop conclusion is a supported scaling interpretation, not proof that every earthquake rupture mechanism is identical; depth class and source-time-function estimates have sampling limitations.
  • AI role: Compared source-time-function features across depth classes and identified surrounding-medium rigidity as the principal explanatory feature, enabling the rigidity-corrected cross-depth comparison.
