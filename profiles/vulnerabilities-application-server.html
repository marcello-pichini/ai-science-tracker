Application server: how many public vulnerabilities?

The dataset links 4,044 published vulnerabilities to products in this category; 65 are in CISA KEV and 571 have public exploit code. Most common weakness classes: missing or incorrect authorization, denial of service, cross-site scripting, information disclosure, authentication bypass. Counts reflect disclosure and research attention as much as quality: they are not a safety ranking.
