25 Mar 2026Sichuan University; Technische Universität Berlin

Machine learning identifies covalent organic frameworks for hydrogen peroxide photosynthesis

An ensemble machine-learning framework combined data augmentation, multilevel structural descriptors and physics-informed features to screen more than 10,000 covalent organic framework candidates. The researchers synthesized and tested eight selected COFs. The paper reports COF-343 at 12,978.7 μmol H2O2 h−1 g−1, with additional measured results for COF-2449, COF-2835 and COF-8484. This is one study-level materials result, not four discoveries. Detailed standard-test conditions in the supplement were not reviewed, and the results do not establish industrial performance. A data/code deposit may predate the journal article, but its date and result content were not checked here.

Technology: Deep neural network ensemble; random-forest band-position model

23 Mar 2024Sichuan University

Machine learning selected nucleoside derivatives for hydrogel testing

A machine-learning model trained on 71 previously reported nucleoside derivatives was applied to an external set, from which the authors selected 24 candidates for hydrogel testing. They report gel formation for 20 of the 24 tested molecules and characterize cation-independent hydrogel behavior for 8-aminoguanosine and 8-hydroxyguanosine. A 2023 Sichuan University patent by the same team had already disclosed the model, 12 selected candidates and 10 gelation positives, including these two derivatives. This entry represents one study/campaign and does not claim that the 2024 article was the first public disclosure or that the compounds were novel.

Technology: Machine-learning classifier using molecular descriptors to predict nucleoside derivative hydrogel formation
