Minerva identifies nickel-catalyzed Suzuki reaction conditions

Minerva combines Gaussian-process optimization with automated high-throughput experiments to search reaction conditions. In the nickel-catalyzed Suzuki campaign, the preprint reports a condition with 76 area-percent yield and 92% selectivity, while two chemist-designed screening plates did not find successful conditions. Area percent is a chromatographic assay measure, not isolated yield. The preprint and expanded journal report are one study; this does not establish invention of Suzuki coupling or clinical utility.

Technology: Minerva Gaussian-process surrogate; Bayesian acquisition; automated HTE
