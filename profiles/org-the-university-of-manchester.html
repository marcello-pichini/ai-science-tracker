Random-forest target selection supported nine millisecond pulsar discoveries

A random-forest classifier ranked unidentified Fermi-LAT sources, from which the team selected 79 targets for MeerKAT UHF observations. The survey reported nine new millisecond pulsars, all detected in coherent tied-array beams; seven were also confirmed as Fermi-LAT counterparts through gamma-ray pulsations. This entry covers only the nine MSP discoveries. Six slower pulsars were also reported, but five were found in a wider incoherent beam and are not included in the AI-linked result. The authors note earlier optical redback-candidate identification for PSR J1910−5320 and single-pulse reports in the J0657−4657 beam; no claim is made that these were the first observations at all wavelengths.

Technology: Random Forest classifier used to rank unidentified Fermi-LAT sources as pulsar-like candidates

PICS-assisted survey reported seven new Small Magellanic Cloud pulsars

A MeerKAT survey of the Small Magellanic Cloud reported seven new radio pulsars. Its pipeline partially classified folded candidates with PICS, a machine-learning system, and applied score and signal-to-noise thresholds before volunteer visual review and follow-up. The paper does not identify each pulsar’s PICS score, so this is an AI-assisted survey-level result rather than a claim that the model independently discovered all seven sources. The paper also reports pulsar–nebula associations; the later timing paper follows this cohort and is not counted again.

Technology: Pulsar Image-based Classification System (PICS), a machine-learning classifier for folded pulsar candidates
