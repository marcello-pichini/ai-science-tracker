10 Sep 2026Zhejiang University of Technology; Zhejiang–Egypt Joint Laboratory on Intelligent Discovery of Marine Drugs; Zhejiang Provincial People’s Hospital; University of Pittsburgh

AI-guided natural-product-inspired molecules inhibit two multidrug-resistance transporters

Combining marine natural-product fragment mining, AI-based molecular generation and structure-guided selection, the authors designed optimized analogues Ib18 and It12 targeting the ABC transporters P-glycoprotein (ABCB1) and BCRP (ABCG2). Both analogues reversed transporter-mediated resistance in overexpressing cells and outperformed reference inhibitors. Target-engagement assays supported transporter inhibition without expression downregulation. In xenografts, Ib18 increased mitoxantrone antitumor activity and tumor accumulation without a detected increase in systemic toxicity.

Technology: AI-based molecular generation integrated with marine natural-product fragment mining and structure-guided prioritization

5 Apr 2025Zhejiang University of Technology; and collaborating institutions

Deep-learning-guided mining of NRPS scaffolds yields active naturally inspired antimicrobial peptides

The team mined 216,408 bacterial genomes, identifying 335,024 NRPS clusters and 328 dereplicated peptide scaffolds. Deep-learning scoring selected five synthesized peptides; P2 showed MIC50 values of 1–2 μM against Vibrio parahaemolyticus and V. alginolyticus. Deep-learning-guided sequence optimization produced P2.2, which showed increased antibacterial activity, membrane effects and reduced hemolysis in assays.

Technology: Deep-learning peptide activity scoring and sequence-optimization algorithms
