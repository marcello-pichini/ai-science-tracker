10 Sep 2026Zhejiang University of Technology; Zhejiang–Egypt Joint Laboratory on Intelligent Discovery of Marine Drugs; Zhejiang Provincial People’s Hospital; University of Pittsburgh

AI-guided natural-product-inspired molecules inhibit two multidrug-resistance transporters

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Combining marine natural-product fragment mining, AI-based molecular generation and structure-guided selection, the authors designed optimized analogues Ib18 and It12 targeting the ABC transporters P-glycoprotein (ABCB1) and BCRP (ABCG2). Both analogues reversed transporter-mediated resistance in overexpressing cells and outperformed reference inhibitors. Target-engagement assays supported transporter inhibition without expression downregulation. In xenografts, Ib18 increased mitoxantrone antitumor activity and tumor accumulation without a detected increase in systemic toxicity.

5 Apr 2025Zhejiang University of Technology; and collaborating institutions

Deep-learning-guided mining of NRPS scaffolds yields active naturally inspired antimicrobial peptides

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The team mined 216,408 bacterial genomes, identifying 335,024 NRPS clusters and 328 dereplicated peptide scaffolds. Deep-learning scoring selected five synthesized peptides; P2 showed MIC50 values of 1–2 μM against Vibrio parahaemolyticus and V. alginolyticus. Deep-learning-guided sequence optimization produced P2.2, which showed increased antibacterial activity, membrane effects and reduced hemolysis in assays.

Technology: Deep-learning peptide activity scoring and sequence-optimization algorithms

Editorial relevance 53 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
AI-ranked peptide candidates inspired by nonribosomal peptide synthetase cluster scaffolds from bacterial genomes and guided amino-acid optimization of P2 into P2.2; the selected sequences were synthesized and tested in vitro.
Test and result
Deep-learning-guided sequence optimization produced P2.2, which showed increased antibacterial activity, membrane effects and reduced hemolysis in assays.
Limitation / still unknown
The study calls the peptides naturally inspired; it did not establish that the synthesized sequences are the native products of the mined clusters. P2's MIC50 of 1–2 μM did not mean 80% inhibition: its MIC80 exceeded 128 μM under the reported 24-hour conditions. P2.2's measured MIC80 values were 32 μM for V. parahaemolyticus and 64 μM for V. alginolyticus. These are in-vitro findings, not clinical efficacy.

Machine learning screened metal oxides for acetylene semihydrogenation

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

12 Sep 2023Zhejiang University of Technology

PhotoCat guides experimental discovery of photocatalytic reactions

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The PhotoCat Transformer was pretrained on general reaction data and fine-tuned on the authors’ photocatalysis database to predict products and reaction conditions. In the 2023 ChemRxiv version, the authors report using it to guide experimental validation of previously unreported photocatalytic reactions. The later 2026 journal version describes four novel validated reactions, whereas the preprint reports five; this entry makes no exact-count claim and treats the journal article as a revised version of the same study.

Technology: Transformer-based deep-learning reaction predictor pretrained on USPTO and fine-tuned on PhotoCatDB, with reaction-condition inputs
