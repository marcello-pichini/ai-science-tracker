17 Feb 2025Technische Universität Dresden; Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light and collaborating institutions

Machine-learning network analysis identifies a conserved cell-mechanics gene module

Researchers combined transcriptomic profiles with single-cell mechanical measurements from mouse and human systems and used machine-learning-based discriminative network analysis to infer a conserved five-gene module associated with cell mechanical properties. In perturbation experiments, changing CAV1 expression by silencing or overexpression altered measured cell stiffness, providing experimental support for a selected marker’s role.

Technology: PC-corr machine-learning-based discriminative network analysis

10 Feb 2024Technische Universität Dresden; University Hospital Dresden; collaborating clinical and pathology institutions

CAMIL regression predicts continuous homologous-recombination deficiency and tumor immune biomarkers from histology

A self-supervised, attention-based deep-learning regression model predicted continuous homologous-recombination deficiency (HRD) scores and tumor-microenvironment biomarkers from H&E pathology slides. On TCGA internal validation, HRD classification AUROC exceeded 0.70 in five of seven cancer types; external CPTAC validation reached AUROC 0.81 for lung adenocarcinoma and 0.96 for endometrial cancer. In 2,297 colorectal-cancer patients in the DACHS cohort, continuous regression scores for leukocyte fraction and lymphocyte-infiltration-related signatures were independently prognostic in multivariable survival analysis.

Technology: CAMIL regression, a self-supervised contrastive-clustering and attention-based multiple-instance deep-learning model

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: On TCGA internal validation, HRD classification AUROC exceeded 0.70 in five of seven cancer types; external CPTAC validation reached AUROC 0.81 for lung adenocarcinoma and 0.96 for endometrial cancer.
  • Still unknown: Retrospective computational pathology analysis; the model predicts molecular and immune-related biomarkers from slides and does not replace confirmatory molecular testing or establish treatment selection benefit. HRD performance varied by cancer type, with weak internal performance in some cohorts. The reported survival associations in DACHS are prognostic associations, not evidence that using model predictions improves patient outcomes. Scores and endpoints were tested in the named datasets.
  • AI role: The model analyzed whole-slide H&E images to estimate continuous HRD and tumor-microenvironment biomarker scores. The team compared regression with classification methods across cohorts, externally validated HRD predictions in CPTAC, and tested prognostic associations of predicted scores in a separate retrospective colorectal-cancer cohort.
