5 Nov 2026Google Research

Signs of hypertension detected by a smartwatch

Prospective study of 196 people at five US sites, compared with 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure measurement: 65.8% sensitivity and 90.0% specificity for screening, and 78.4% and 90.9% in the confirmatory test (48 participants). A feasibility study; it does not replace the cuff, and many cases are missed.

Technology: AI-PPG-ACC-HTN (photoplethysmography and accelerometer)

These are author-reported deployment figures, with no sensitivity or patient outcome data; WHO and FDA approvals are still in progress.

21 Oct 2026Google Research

AI-assisted ultrasound for dating pregnancy

Prospective study of 385 pregnant women in Chicago and Nairobi: the model estimates gestational age from scans taken by minimally trained operators with a mean error of 4.18 days, non-inferior to the 4.48 days of expert sonographers. This is a non-inferiority result from only two sites, both relatively urban.

AI agents in the lab: aptamers and proteins designed in a closed loop

AI agents designed DNA aptamers and protein binders, which were then tested in the lab over multiple rounds. Target success rates reached 33.5% for myoglobin and 42.1% for α-thrombin (98.5% specificity). The post summarizes three arXiv papers whose peer review is unconfirmed; it also reports initial failures.

Earth AI for public health: geospatial models across five use cases

The PDFM model increased the explained variance in MMR vaccination coverage from 16% to 22% across 146 counties on the US–Canada border, and improved weekly correct cholera-risk-area rankings in the DRC from 1.78 to 2.10. Gains for cardiovascular mortality and postpartum depression were small or not significant.

16 Oct 2025Google Research

DeepSomatic: a neural network for somatic variants in tumors

For insertions and deletions, it reaches 90% with Illumina data versus 80% for the best other tool, and over 80% with PacBio versus under 50%. In eight pediatric leukemia samples, it recovered known variants and flagged ten new ones that have not been independently validated. The tool and CASTLE dataset are open.

AI co-scientist: a multi-agent system for generating hypotheses

A system based on Gemini 2.0 that generates hypotheses and research proposals. In the lab, three drugs it suggested for repurposing against acute myeloid leukemia reduced cancer-cell viability, and all suggested treatments for liver fibrosis showed activity. Expert assessments covered only 11 targets; fibrosis results awaited a Stanford report.

11 Nov 2024Google Research

Seven-day flood forecasts with reliability similar to earlier five-day forecasts

The new model, trained on 15,980 stations (up from 5,680), gives seven-day reliability comparable to the previous model's five-day forecast. Flood Hub covers over 100 countries and about 700 million people (up from 460 million). About 250,000 virtual points are unverified and intended for experts only; flash, urban, and coastal floods remain out of scope.

22 Jul 2024Google Research

NeuralGCM: a hybrid physics-and-learning model for weather and climate

At 1.4° resolution, the model outperformed ECMWF-ENS in 95% of cases for forecasts from 2 to 15 days; at 2.8°, its mean 1980–2020 temperature error was 0.25 °C versus 0.75 °C for atmosphere-only models. It is more than 3,500 times faster than X-SHiELD on a TPU. It models only the atmosphere and is not yet a complete climate model.

Technology: NeuralGCM (differentiable dynamical core and neural networks)

29 Mar 2024Google Research

SEEDS: weather ensembles generated with a diffusion model

The model produces 256 members at 2° resolution every three minutes on a TPU and matches or exceeds the operational GEFS ensemble (31 members) on rank histograms, RMSE and CRPS. For the Lisbon heatwave (July 2022), with 16,384 members, none of GEFS’s 31 members was as warm as observed. One extreme case; depends on physics-based forecasts as seeds; published in Science Advances.

Technology: SEEDS (diffusion model)

Reliable flood forecasts even where gauges are missing

A model trained on 5,680 gauges improved average global forecast reliability from zero to five days: forecasts at days 4–5 were similar to or better than day-zero forecasts from GloFAS v4. Flood Hub covers more than 80 countries. F1 comparison across 2,092 basins for two-year return-period events; flash and urban floods remain to be covered.

1 Nov 2023Google Research and Google DeepMind

MetNet-3: short-range neural forecasts in Google products

The model forecasts precipitation, temperature, wind and dew point up to 24 hours ahead at 1–4 km resolution, learning from about 1,000 stations, and outperforms HRRR and ENS in several regions. It operates in Google Search and the app in the United States and 27 European countries, with precipitation forecasts up to 12 hours. Preprint, no numerical values in the text and one case study.

8 Aug 2023Google Research, American Airlines and Breakthrough Energy

Avoiding aircraft contrails with AI forecasts

In a trial with American Airlines and Breakthrough Energy, pilots flew 70 flights over six months using AI forecasts to avoid forming contrails, reducing them by 54% compared with flights without the forecasts. Flights that avoided contrails used 2% more fuel; across an airline’s flights, the impact could fall to 0.3% if only a small share need rerouting.

Technology: AI contrail-formation forecasts and satellite imagery

10 May 2023Google Research and Human Pangenome Reference Consortium

First draft of the human pangenome reference

A consortium of 119 scientists from 60 institutions published in Nature a pangenome combining assembled genomes from 47 people of diverse ancestries, rather than one person. Google adapted DeepVariant to identify variants and DeepConsensus to correct sequencing errors; a long-read assembler achieved over 99.999% final accuracy. This is a draft: benefits for diagnosis and treatment remain prospective.

Real-time wildfire perimeters from satellite imagery

A model using geostationary imagery tracks fire perimeters with updates every 10–15 minutes and, according to the page, is more accurate than similar products and the previous model based on NOAA GOES-16/17 data; shown in Search and Maps for large fires in the United States, Mexico, much of Canada and Australia. Resolution is 2 km; clouds can hide fires; figures appear only in an image.

1 Jun 2021Google Research and Harvard (Lichtman Lab)

H01: reconstruction of one cubic millimeter of human cerebral cortex

A sample of about 1 mm³ of human cortex was imaged at 4 nm across about 5,300 sections and automatically reconstructed: 1.4 petabytes, tens of thousands of neurons, 130 million annotated synapses, and 104 manually corrected cells. The sample is about one-millionth of a brain; reconstruction is imperfect and initial findings are preliminary.

25 Mar 2020Google Research

MetNet: neural rainfall forecasting up to 8 hours ahead

The model forecasts precipitation at 1 km resolution and 2-minute intervals up to 8 hours ahead from radar and satellite, without physical equations. According to the page, it outperforms NOAA’s operational HRRR model up to about 7–8 hours, in seconds rather than about an hour. US coverage only, author-only comparisons, a preprint, and no stated operational use.
