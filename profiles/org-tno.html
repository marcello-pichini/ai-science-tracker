29 May 2024TNO, Amsterdam UMC, Leiden University Medical Center and collaborating Dutch and Danish clinical institutions

LightGBM links a three-protein blood panel to biopsy-staged MASLD fibrosis

A translational study combined a disease-related mouse gene-expression signature, human liver-biopsy transcriptomes and serum protein measurements to select IGFBP7, SSc5D and Sema4D as a three-protein panel for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD). A LightGBM model classified biopsy-staged fibrosis, with AUCs 0.82 for F0/F1, 0.89 for F2 and 0.87 for F3/F4 in the development/testing data. In an independent Danish cohort of 156 patients, AUC was 0.84 for F0/F1 and F3/F4; performance for F2 was lower (AUC 0.62).

Technology: LightGBM classifier (TLM3) using the three serum proteins IGFBP7, SSc5D and Sema4D

Evidence context (pilot; stated by the cited sources, not an editorial judgment):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Tested: A translational study combined a disease-related mouse gene-expression signature, human liver-biopsy transcriptomes and serum protein measurements to select IGFBP7, SSc5D and Sema4D as a three-protein panel for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD).
  • Still unknown: The model is an early translational biomarker result. Its independent cohort replicated better classification at F0/F1 and F3/F4 (AUC 0.84) but was modest for F2 (AUC 0.62). The authors say mechanistic claims could not be tested in the single-biopsy cohorts and call for prospective and intervention studies before clinical utility is established. TNO disclosed a patent application related to the biomarkers.
  • AI role: The LightGBM model combined the three protein measurements selected from a translational mouse/human fibrosis signature to classify liver-biopsy fibrosis stages. The authors compared the model with Fib-4, APRI and FibroScan in held-out and independent cohorts.
