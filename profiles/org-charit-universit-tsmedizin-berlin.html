A recurrent neural network uncovers a non-canonical human GRHL1 DNA-binding site

A convolutional recurrent neural network found 46 GRHL1 ChIP-seq sites missed by a conventional position-weight matrix, including sequences lacking the previously considered obligatory CNNG motif. Isothermal titration calorimetry confirmed GRHL1 binding to a selected non-canonical sequence lacking that core motif; nucleotide mutagenesis experiments also showed that model scores tracked measured affinity across tested variants.

Technology: Convolutional recurrent neural network trained on GRHL1 HT-SELEX binding data
