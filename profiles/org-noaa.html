NOAA puts three AI-based global models into operation

AIGFS (retrained from GraphCast) uses up to 99.7% less computing than GFS (a 16-day forecast in about 40 minutes); AIGEFS has skill comparable to GEFS with 9% of the computing; and HGEFS (62 hybrid members) outperforms GEFS and AIGEFS on most metrics. Cyclone intensity worsened in AIGFS v1.0; the announcement gives no numerical scores.

Technology: AIGFS, AIGEFS, and HGEFS (GraphCast-derived)
