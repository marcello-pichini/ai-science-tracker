25 Nov 2026Johns Hopkins University; Johns Hopkins School of Medicine

Deep learning predicts DNA damage response protein levels from routine slides

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Gated attention-based multiple-instance learning on frozen pathology foundation-model tile embeddings predicted reverse-phase protein array pathway scores from 1,673 TCGA haematoxylin-and-eosin whole-slide images covering 1,527 patients with breast, endometrial, cervical and ovarian cancer and uterine carcinosarcoma. For the 11-protein DNA damage composite the out-of-fold Spearman correlation was 0.43 against 0.31 for a cancer-type-mean baseline, about 0.29-0.30 within cancer type, and at most 0.00 with shuffled targets. Cross-validation was cancer-type-stratified and site-grouped. This is a moderate retrospective association in one public cohort: there is no external validation, no clinical or treatment-response outcome, and the authors list separating the signal from proliferation as future work, noting the cell-cycle score was predicted more strongly.

Technology: Gated attention-based multiple-instance learning over frozen UNI2-h and Virchow2 tile embeddings jointly regresses six RPPA pathway scores.

25 Nov 2026European Commission, Joint Research Centre; University of Valencia; Integrated Food Security Phase Classification Global Support Unit

Machine learning forecasts acute food crises and separates predictive from causal drivers

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Missing-data-resilient machine-learning pipelines, including gradient boosting and a tabular foundation model, forecast acute food crisis occurrence 3, 6 and 9 months ahead across 80 countries using monthly subnational data for 2007-2024, exceeding no-skill baselines by 15 to 50 percentage points in PR-AUC under nested cross-validation. TreeSHAP profiles fell into three recurring archetypes. Doubly robust causal estimates indicated that acute conflict events, which carried the weakest predictive signal, are primary causal drivers, while agroclimatology was the only dimension where predictive and causal importance agreed. The authors report that skill tracks crisis prevalence, that the causal effects are modest and only moderately robust to unmeasured confounding, and that they depend on an assumed causal graph; several variables were reported as non-identifiable. The work is a preprint.

Technology: Tree-based and tabular foundation-model classifiers with TreeSHAP attribution and cross-fitted doubly robust causal estimation.

25 Nov 2026University of Washington; Pacific Northwest National Laboratory

LLM-agent workflow reports new proofs on positivity problems in algebraic combinatorics

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A preprint describes PatternMiner, in which an LLM agent generates mathematical datasets, trains small models (MLPs, small transformers, trees), interprets them and formulates conjectures that are tested against counterexamples in mathematical software, applied to five positivity problems from Stanley's survey. The paper gives proofs, labelled LLM-generated, that for every n>=5 no fixed number of retained flag dimensions suffices for projection tests of Schubert structure-constant vanishing, an equivariant positivity inequality that proves Conjectures A and B of Chen et al. on equivariant nonvanishing, a character-row evaluation formula, a restricted case of Kazhdan-Lusztig combinatorial invariance, and Schur positivity for a family of immanant coefficients, supported by finite checks such as 776,891 coefficients over 1,587 shape instances. Proofs received an internal adversarial review only; no proof-kernel or independent mathematician check is reported, one decisive construction needed human redirection, and the comparison with direct LLM attempts is not compute-matched.

Technology: Unnamed LLM agent that trains and interprets small models (activation patching, probes, sparse autoencoders), proposes conjectures and attempts proofs, with counterexample searches in mathematical software.
