14 Apr 2026City University of Hong Kong; University of Cambridge; Imperial College London; Friedrich-Alexander University Erlangen-Nürnberg; Central South University; Chinese University of Hong Kong; Hong Kong University of Science and Technology; Huazhong University of Science and Technology

ML-guided discovery of 5ANI passivator yields high-efficiency, stable perovskite solar cells

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An interpretable machine-learning and active-learning workflow screened a virtual library of 18,264 candidate passivation molecules and guided synthesis/testing of selected candidates for perovskite solar cells. The newly identified molecule 5-(aminomethyl)nicotinonitrile hydroiodide (5ANI) yielded 0.05 cm² devices at 27.22% power-conversion efficiency (27.18% certified) and 21.4 cm² mini-modules at 23.49%. Under the reported ISOS-L-1I operating test, 5ANI devices retained 98.7% of initial efficiency after 1,200 hours.

Technology: SISSO interpretable symbolic regression and NGBoost active-learning models, coupled to DFT calculations and a Bayesian-optimization/symbolic-regression automated fabrication platform

Editorial relevance 60 materials Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The team used DFT descriptors and SISSO regression trained on measurements from 20 known passivator/device sets to learn structure–performance relationships. NGBoost active learning reduced the computational cost of estimating interfacial properties across an 18,264-molecule virtual library; model-guided screening selected four candidates for synthesis and experimental validation. 5ANI emerged as a passivator for which the automated device-fabrication loop optimized deposition and processing settings. The ML models guided material choice, while the closed-loop platform refined device fabrication and generated measured feedback.
Test and result
An interpretable machine-learning and active-learning workflow screened a virtual library of 18,264 candidate passivation molecules and guided synthesis/testing of selected candidates for perovskite solar cells.
Limitation / still unknown
This is a result for a specific perovskite composition, cell architecture and passivation/fabrication protocol. The ML regression was trained on only 20 initial known passivator/device datasets; four selected candidates were experimentally synthesized/validated. The reported champion efficiency is a small-area cell result; the mini-module efficiency is lower (23.49%) and laboratory-scale. The 1,200-hour stability result follows the stated ISOS-L-1I protocol and does not establish commercial lifetime. The paper reports high reproducibility from its automated setup, not factory-scale manufacturing. This is a different study and molecule from the 2024 Bayesian-optimized hole-transport materials record (Science DOI 10.1126/science.ads0901): here AI identifies an interfacial passivator 5ANI.

3 Sep 2024City University of Hong Kong; Shenzhen University

Generative machine learning identifies and validates new dual- and tri-phase eutectic compositionally complex alloys

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A conditional variational autoencoder generated candidate alloy compositions and an artificial neural network ranked eutectic-forming likelihood. The team experimentally validated previously unreported eutectic compositionally complex alloys across quaternary to senary systems. Reported examples include Al₁₆Cr₁₈Fe₂₄Ni₄₂ with a dual-phase FCC/BCC eutectic lamellar structure and Al₁₁.₃Nb₁₃.₇Co₃₆.₆Cr₈.₂Fe₇.₉Ni₂₂.₃ with a tri-phase FCC/BCC/Laves eutectic structure, supported by XRD, SEM and DSC.

Technology: Conditional variational autoencoder (CVAE) and artificial neural network (ANN)

Editorial relevance 60 materials Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A CVAE generated candidate compositions conditioned on eutectic/non-eutectic microstructure labels; an ANN trained on composition and thermodynamics-derived descriptors estimated eutectic-forming likelihood and selected generated candidates for experimental validation. Dataset preprocessing with PCA and K-means addressed class imbalance. The ML pipeline directly proposed the compositions whose eutectic structures were examined experimentally.
Test and result
A conditional variational autoencoder generated candidate alloy compositions and an artificial neural network ranked eutectic-forming likelihood.
Limitation / still unknown
The authors report five newly identified ECCA compositions across the studied systems; this is one paper-level result, not five records. The entry names two representative experimentally examined compositions. Validation is limited to the reported arc-melted samples and XRD/SEM/DSC characterization; no long-term mechanical performance or application testing is established. The training data were sparse and imbalanced, and dataset filtering was part of the framework.

23 Aug 2021City University of Hong Kong

Hybrid machine learning guides experimental discovery of multicomponent metallic glasses

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Hybrid classification and regression models with physically motivated descriptors, trained on about 5,000 metallic-glass compositions, guided exploration of multicomponent alloy compositions. Researchers synthesized 23 selected compositions: 12 predicted glass-formers produced glass in bulk, ribbon or both, while 11 predicted negative comparisons did not form glass under the tested preparation conditions. Six of the positive designs formed bulk glass; six formed glassy ribbons only. These selected experiments are not an unbiased estimate of global predictive accuracy. Some authors disclosed inventorship on a filed patent related to the new bulk compositions; composition-level prior art and independent replication remain open.

Technology: Hybrid classification/regression models including adaptive boosting, SVM, KNN and rational-quadratic Gaussian-process regression
