26 Aug 2026Soochow University; Zhengzhou University; Fudan University; Hefei National Laboratory

Physics-informed retrieval-augmented language model guides synthesis of five previously unreported inorganic crystals

Researchers built PIRAG-LM, which combines a structured knowledge base of experimental synthesis routes, physics-based chemical/structural/thermodynamic similarity retrieval, and LLM route reasoning. In prospective tests, it guided synthesis of five compounds previously unreported in experimental literature: BaMo0.3In0.7O2.95, BaNb0.4In0.6O2.9, Hg[B(CN)4]2, CoCo(CN)6 and SrNb2Fe2(PO4)6. Powder X-ray diffraction patterns were consistent with target phases. This is one paper-level synthesis-planning result, not five separate entries.

Technology: PIRAG-LM, physics-informed retrieval-augmented language model, with DeepSeek-V4 used in the structured synthesis-knowledge-base extraction workflow

14 Aug 2024Soochow University; Xi’an Jiaotong University

Machine learning identifies predicted zero- and extreme-thermal-expansion monolayers

High-throughput calculations and symbolic regression identified mechanical descriptors that classify positive- versus negative-thermal-expansion 2D crystals, then used those descriptors to predict monolayers with near-zero thermal expansion (2D Invar) and unusually large positive or negative expansion (anti-Invar). The paper reports multiple specific predicted crystals and explains the phonon/deformation-mode basis. The materials are computational predictions rather than experimentally synthesized monolayers.

Technology: High-throughput calculations combined with symbolic regression machine learning

  • AI role: Learned interpretable relationships between in-plane tensile stiffness, out-of-plane bending stiffness and thermal-expansion class, then used them to screen 2D-crystal databases for predicted Invar and anti-Invar materials.
