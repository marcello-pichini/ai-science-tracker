Bayesian optimization finds mild solvent-free mechanochemical conditions for cyclopropanation and epoxidation

A Bayesian-optimization-guided mechanochemical study identified conditions for the established Johnson–Corey–Chaykovsky reaction using KOH in place of hazardous NaH, under air-equilibrated conditions and with no solvent or liquid-assisted grinding agent. In the chalcone benchmark, one optimized condition reached cyclopropanation completion in five minutes; the study also demonstrated cyclopropanation and epoxidation across multiple substrates, including yields of 45–75% for four additional substrates.

Technology: Bayesian optimization implemented with BoTorch/PyTorch, including multi-objective optimization of product yield and process mass intensity
