19 Dec 2025Worcester Polytechnic Institute; University of California, Riverside; University of Massachusetts Lowell

Diffusion-guided design tests bimetallic ammonia-decomposition catalysts

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A diffusion model guided by a learned nitrogen-adsorption predictor generated bimetallic candidates near the target adsorption energy. Co75Mo25 was among the generated candidates and maps exactly to the tested Co3Mo1 ratio; under the reported supported-catalyst assay it reached 18.6 μmol gmetal⁻¹ s⁻¹ at 520 °C, about twice the paper’s Ru reference under its stated test. Co–Mo catalysts are established prior art, so this entry does not claim the first Co–Mo ammonia-decomposition catalyst or global composition novelty. Ex-situ XRD showed oxidized phases after air exposure and does not establish the working active phase.

Technology: Diffusion model guided by random-forest nitrogen-adsorption predictor
