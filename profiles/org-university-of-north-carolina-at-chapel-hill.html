2 Jul 2024University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill; University of Missouri

FRASE-bot screening identifies a small-molecule ligand that selectively kills CIB1-dependent cancer cells

FRASE-bot used a neural-network nativeness score to triage protein–ligand fragments, then used the retained structural features to seed virtual screening for Calcium and Integrin Binding Protein 1 (CIB1). Of 56 purchased virtual-screen hits, 29 showed dose-dependent CIB1 displacement; compound UNC10245380 (UNC5380) had a TR-FRET displacement IC50 of 8.0 ± 2.5 μM and selectively killed CIB1-depletion-sensitive triple-negative breast cancer cells in the reported assays, with no detected killing in an insensitive line.

Technology: FRASE-bot: fragment-in-structural-environment screening with a neural-network nativeness scoring model

12 Oct 2021University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill; Carnegie Mellon University

Machine learning guides automated synthesis of 19F MRI copolymer agents

An automated continuous-flow polymer platform synthesized 397 copolymer compositions in a six-variable space, in iterative cycles guided by machine learning. Exploring under 0.9% of the compositional space, the models identified more than ten copolymers that outperformed state-of-the-art 19F MRI agents. Validation is experimental across hundreds of real syntheses, but the imaging performance claims are limited to the compositions tested.

Technology: Machine learning models guiding automated flow copolymer synthesis
