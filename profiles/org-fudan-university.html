30 May 2025Fudan University; Guangdong Provincial People's Hospital/Southern Medical University; Fuwai Hospital/Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences; collaborating hospitals in China

Proteomic profiling and machine learning identified an externally validated diagnostic panel and severity markers for infective endocarditis

The study profiled plasma and excised vegetation samples from 238 infective-endocarditis patients and 100 controls, then used an ensemble machine-learning pipeline to select a 10-protein plasma diagnostic panel. The paper reports AUCs of 0.97 in the held-out discovery test set and 0.98 and 0.94 in two external plasma cohorts. Integrating proteomic and clinical features also produced a prognostic model for severe heart-failure status (abstract AUC 0.87). LRG1 and NDUFB4 were associated with infection severity in the cohort analyses.

Technology: Ensemble machine-learning diagnostic and prognostic models; the diagnostic workflow compared six algorithms and used recursive feature elimination with cross-validation to select a 10-protein panel

2 Jul 2024Fudan University; Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University; Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine; collaborating Shanghai hospitals

Machine-learning selection and external mass-spectrometry testing identify an ARDS 28-day prognosis protein panel

A multicenter observational serum multi-omics study used LASSO and Boruta to select candidate biomarkers for 28-day outcome in acute respiratory distress syndrome. Eight proteins (VCAM1, LDHB, MSN, FLG2, TAGLN2, LMNA, MBL2 and LBP) showed consistency in an independent cohort of 183 patients and were confirmed by targeted parallel-reaction-monitoring mass spectrometry. The protein model AUC was 0.802 in validation versus 0.738 for the clinical model. The discovery analysis also reported sphingosine-1-phosphate association with ARDS prognosis and an enriched sphingolipid pathway.

Technology: LASSO and Boruta feature selection; machine-learning prognostic model; targeted PRM validation

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Eight proteins (VCAM1, LDHB, MSN, FLG2, TAGLN2, LMNA, MBL2 and LBP) showed consistency in an independent cohort of 183 patients and were confirmed by targeted parallel-reaction-monitoring mass spectrometry.
  • Still unknown: Observational prognostic associations do not show proteins cause outcomes or that biomarker-guided care improves survival. Discovery n=130 ARDS patients; validation n=183. PRM confirmation is not independent replication by another group; deployment and prospective benefit were not established.
  • AI role: LASSO, fold-change screening and Boruta prioritized candidate 28-day ARDS prognostic proteins; candidates were tested by targeted PRM in an external cohort.

9 Nov 2022Fudan University

Machine-learning screening guides ionic-liquid polymer electrolytes for lithium-metal batteries

A machine-learning and quantum-chemistry workflow screened ionic liquids for conductivity and electrochemical-window properties, then guided selection for rigid-rod polyelectrolyte membranes. The 2022 preprint reports ~50 μm membranes and lithium-cell testing, including symmetric-cell cycling up to 800 hours at room temperature and reversible operation at 6 mA cm−2 at 80°C. Its preprint abstract reports 146 mAh g−1 at 5C/80°C; the later 2023 journal abstract lists 3C, so those version-specific figures are not conflated.

Technology: Graph-convolutional neural network and ensemble SVM/random-forest/XGBoost screening with Psi4 quantum-chemistry descriptors
