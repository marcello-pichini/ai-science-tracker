Multi-view CNN predicts pediatric obstructive sleep apnea severity from facial photographs

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A multicenter retrospective study of 212 children with frontal and lateral facial photographs linked to polysomnography built a three-class model (non-OSA, mild OSA, moderate-to-severe OSA) using a multi-view convolutional neural network (MVCNN). An external cohort of 63 children was also evaluated, but its numbers were not visible in the reviewed abstract.
