30 May 2025Fudan University; Guangdong Provincial People's Hospital/Southern Medical University; Fuwai Hospital/Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences; collaborating hospitals in China

Proteomic profiling and machine learning identified an externally validated diagnostic panel and severity markers for infective endocarditis

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The study profiled plasma and excised vegetation samples from 238 infective-endocarditis patients and 100 controls, then used an ensemble machine-learning pipeline to select a 10-protein plasma diagnostic panel. The paper reports AUCs of 0.97 in the held-out discovery test set and 0.98 and 0.94 in two external plasma cohorts. Integrating proteomic and clinical features also produced a prognostic model for severe heart-failure status (abstract AUC 0.87). LRG1 and NDUFB4 were associated with infection severity in the cohort analyses.

Technology: Ensemble machine-learning diagnostic and prognostic models; the diagnostic workflow compared six algorithms and used recursive feature elimination with cross-validation to select a 10-protein panel

Editorial relevance 53 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Machine-learning feature-importance analysis and recursive feature elimination selected a 10-protein plasma combination for distinguishing infective endocarditis from non-endocarditis samples. The models were evaluated in a held-out discovery cohort and two external plasma cohorts. A separate model combined proteomic and clinical features to classify severe heart-failure status.
Test and result
The paper reports AUCs of 0.97 in the held-out discovery test set and 0.98 and 0.94 in two external plasma cohorts.
Limitation / still unknown
The study is an observational proteomic cohort analysis; it does not demonstrate that use of the panel improves patient outcomes or can replace current diagnostic criteria. The development comparison included 238 IE patients and 100 controls, so case-control separation may not reflect performance in an unselected real-world diagnostic population. External evaluation used two additional plasma cohorts (combined n=328); the prognostic endpoint and biomarkers are associations, not causal mechanisms. The paper's proposed NET-related therapeutic implications are hypothesis-generating and are not counted as demonstrated treatment benefit.

2 Jul 2024Fudan University; Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University; Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine; collaborating Shanghai hospitals

Machine-learning selection and external mass-spectrometry testing identify an ARDS 28-day prognosis protein panel

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A multicenter observational serum multi-omics study used LASSO and Boruta to select candidate biomarkers for 28-day outcome in acute respiratory distress syndrome. Eight proteins (VCAM1, LDHB, MSN, FLG2, TAGLN2, LMNA, MBL2 and LBP) showed consistency in an independent cohort of 183 patients and were confirmed by targeted parallel-reaction-monitoring mass spectrometry. The protein model AUC was 0.802 in validation versus 0.738 for the clinical model. The discovery analysis also reported sphingosine-1-phosphate association with ARDS prognosis and an enriched sphingolipid pathway.

Technology: LASSO and Boruta feature selection; machine-learning prognostic model; targeted PRM validation

Editorial relevance 60 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
LASSO, fold-change screening and Boruta prioritized candidate 28-day ARDS prognostic proteins; candidates were tested by targeted PRM in an external cohort.
Test and result
Eight proteins (VCAM1, LDHB, MSN, FLG2, TAGLN2, LMNA, MBL2 and LBP) showed consistency in an independent cohort of 183 patients and were confirmed by targeted parallel-reaction-monitoring mass spectrometry.
Limitation / still unknown
Observational prognostic associations do not show proteins cause outcomes or that biomarker-guided care improves survival. Discovery n=130 ARDS patients; validation n=183. PRM confirmation is not independent replication by another group; deployment and prospective benefit were not established.

9 Nov 2022Fudan University

Machine-learning screening guides ionic-liquid polymer electrolytes for lithium-metal batteries

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A machine-learning and quantum-chemistry workflow screened ionic liquids for conductivity and electrochemical-window properties, then guided selection for rigid-rod polyelectrolyte membranes. The 2022 preprint reports ~50 μm membranes and lithium-cell testing, including symmetric-cell cycling up to 800 hours at room temperature and reversible operation at 6 mA cm−2 at 80°C. Its preprint abstract reports 146 mAh g−1 at 5C/80°C; the later 2023 journal abstract lists 3C, so those version-specific figures are not conflated.

Technology: Graph-convolutional neural network and ensemble SVM/random-forest/XGBoost screening with Psi4 quantum-chemistry descriptors

Editorial relevance 60 materials Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Machine-learning models and quantum-chemistry calculations screened ionic-liquid properties and guided selection for experimental polymer-electrolyte membranes and cell testing.
Test and result
The 2022 preprint reports a machine-learning/quantum-chemistry workflow that screened 2,356 ion pairs and recommended 40 for testing in rigid-rod polyelectrolyte membranes. It reports symmetric-cell cycling up to 800 hours at room temperature and reversible operation at 6 mA cm−2 at 80°C; its abstract reports 146 mAh g−1 at 5C/80°C.
