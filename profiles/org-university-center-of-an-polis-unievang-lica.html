Deep learning screens LabMol compounds for antiplasmodial activity

Deep-learning QSAR models for parasite activity and cytotoxicity prioritized commercial candidates for assay. Primary Table 3 reports LabMol-152 EC50 values of 0.049 and 0.078 µM against 3D7 and W2; LabMol-149 values were 1.450 and 0.509 µM, inconsistent with the abstract’s under-500-nM claim. The study reports in-vitro results only; the model ranked existing compounds rather than generating them.

Technology: Deep-neural-network QSAR models
