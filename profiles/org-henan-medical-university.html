EnzGFM-Agent experimentally prioritizes activity-enhancing secreted alkaline phosphatase variants

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: EnzGFM-Agent scored all possible single amino-acid substitutions in secreted alkaline phosphatase (SEAP) and selected top candidates from EnzGFM-1.5B and ESM2-3B for testing in transfected Chinese hamster ovary cells. Among the five variants uniquely prioritized by EnzGFM, all five had significantly higher measured activity than wild type in the reported assay; three exceeded 1.5-fold, and A46I reached 6.52-fold. This is an experimentally measured enzyme-engineering result for the tested SEAP variants.

Technology: EnzGFM-Agent with EnzGFM-1.5B and ESM2-3B

AI contribution
The workflow scored every possible SEAP single-point substitution, ranked candidates using EnzGFM-1.5B and ESM2-3B, and selected 15 unique variants for experimental validation. The distinct EnzGFM-prioritized group comprised five variants; the paper reports significantly increased SEAP activity for all five, with A46I at 6.52-fold wild type.
Test and result
EnzGFM-Agent scored all possible single amino-acid substitutions in secreted alkaline phosphatase (SEAP) and selected top candidates from EnzGFM-1.5B and ESM2-3B for testing in transfected Chinese hamster ovary cells.
Limitation / still unknown
The evidence is limited to single substitutions in one SEAP reporter protein tested after transfection in CHO-S cells, with three independent biological replicates; it does not establish improved activity for other enzymes, industrial process performance, or a naturally occurring enzyme function. The five EnzGFM-specific variants all differed significantly from wild type, but the study also evaluated ESM2-ranked variants and five variants shared by both models. This is a paper-specific experimental validation, not independent replication.
