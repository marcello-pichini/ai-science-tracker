First draft of the human pangenome reference

A consortium of 119 scientists from 60 institutions published in Nature a pangenome combining assembled genomes from 47 people of diverse ancestries, rather than one person. Google adapted DeepVariant to identify variants and DeepConsensus to correct sequencing errors; a long-read assembler achieved over 99.999% final accuracy. This is a draft: benefits for diagnosis and treatment remain prospective.

Technology: DeepVariant and DeepConsensus
