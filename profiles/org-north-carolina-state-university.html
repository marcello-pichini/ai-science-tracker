14 Mar 2023North Carolina State University; University at Buffalo

Reinforcement-learning-guided CdS shell-growth route using AlphaFlow

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: AlphaFlow used reinforcement learning with an automated droplet reactor to select and optimize reagent sequences for CdS-shell growth on supplied CdSe quantum dots. Under the authors’ aged, diluted starting-material comparison, the selected route achieved a 26 nm larger absorption-peak shift and 450% higher photoluminescence intensity after six cycles than the conventional sequence; freshly diluted starting material showed a different comparison. This is a lab route to a known nanomaterial, not discovery of a new compound or proof of universal superiority.

Technology: Reinforcement-learning agent with neural reward regression and boosted-tree viability classification in an automated fluidic lab
