Pangu-Weather: medium-range forecasts outperform the operational ECMWF model

According to the authors, it is the first AI model to outperform ECMWF’s IFS at medium range: five-day Z500 RMSE of 296.7 versus 333.7, a global 24-hour forecast in 1.4 seconds on a GPU, and mean cyclone-track error of 120 km at three days versus 162 km. Source reviewed: Huawei Cloud blog, not the Nature paper (July 6, 2023); it underestimates extremes.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Trained on 39 years of global data; stronger deterministic results on reanalysis data than ECMWF's operational IFS in all tested variables; also evaluated on extreme weather and ensemble forecasts.
  • Still unknown: Comparisons are initialized with reanalysis data, as the abstract states; performance from operational analyses is not given there.
  • AI role: Three-dimensional deep networks with Earth-specific priors and hierarchical temporal aggregation.

Evidence sources: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06185-3
