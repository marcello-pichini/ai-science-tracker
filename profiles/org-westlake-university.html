21 Oct 2026Westlake University; and collaborating institutions

Pinal designs functional proteins from natural-language descriptions

The 16-billion-parameter Pinal model generated protein candidates from plain-language function descriptions. In four reported design tasks—fluorescent protein, PET hydrolase, alcohol dehydrogenase and metabolic H-protein—the selected designs displayed the intended function in laboratory tests; the best H-protein raised product titre 1.7-fold relative to native E. coli H-protein in a reconstituted CO2-fixation pathway.

Technology: Pinal, a 16-billion-parameter protein foundation model with text-to-structure (T2struct) and text-conditioned sequence design (SaProt-T) components

9 Apr 2025Westlake University; Zhejiang University; Harvard University

Deep transfer learning detects methane super-emitters in oil and gas fields from Sentinel-2

A two-part framework (adaptive artifact removal plus a deep subdomain adaptation network) was trained on 1,627 Sentinel-2 images from six known methane super-emitters. Applied to an unannotated oil field in Algeria, it flagged 34 anomalous emission events over one year, attributed to three super-emitters linked to production and transmission infrastructure. DSAN achieved an average macro F1 of 0.86 on transfer tasks, versus 0.78 for the EfficientNet-V2L comparator; independent field verification is not reported in the abstract.

Technology: Deep subdomain adaptation network (DSAN) with low-reflectance artifact detection (LRAD)

6 Apr 2025Westlake University; and collaborating institutions

ESM-Ezy mining identifies multicopper oxidases and asparaginases with experimentally improved catalytic properties

The ESM-1b semantic-embedding mining strategy nominated low-sequence-similarity enzyme candidates. In the reported experimental assessment, 44% of tested multicopper oxidases outperformed their query enzymes in at least one measured property (including catalytic efficiency, heat/solvent tolerance or pH stability); 51% were reported to excel in environmental-remediation applications. Among mined L-asparaginases, 40% had higher specific activity and catalytic efficiency than query enzymes.

Technology: ESM-Ezy, using ESM-1b protein-language-model embeddings and semantic-space similarity
