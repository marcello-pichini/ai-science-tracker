Cross-state representation learning enables prospective discovery of dynamic protein allosteric sites

The preprint abstract reports that AllositeUltra recovered allosteric sites among its top three predictions in 65.2% of apo structures and 70.6% of substrate-bound structures. More importantly, application to MAGI2 identified a previously unrecognized interdomain pocket, tested by small-molecule screening and X-ray crystallography. This within-study experimental finding qualifies separately from the benchmark. Review is limited to the abstract and original-server metadata.

Technology: AllositeUltra (structure-aware protein language model combined with physical dynamics; residue-guided candidate supplementation)

13 Jan 2026Shanghai Jiao Tong University; ShanghaiTech University and collaborating institutions

Self-iterative learning finds RNA-targeting prokaryotic Argonautes and an RNA-modification-sensing activity

The self-iterative hierarchical ensemble model (SIM) nominated six high-confidence prokaryotic Argonaute candidates; five were experimentally validated as RNA-targeting proteins. The study identified three uncharacterized Agos with a novel N-terminal domain, defining a previously unrecognized subclass. Biochemical and in-vivo experiments on Haloferax profundi Ago (HpAgo) established RNA cleavage and a distinctive RNA-modification-sensing capability, which the authors used to build a method for quantifying modified RNA.

Technology: Self-Iterative hierarchical ensemble Model (SIM), an ensemble-learning and self-training pipeline
