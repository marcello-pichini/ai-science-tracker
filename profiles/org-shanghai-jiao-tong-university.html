20 Nov 2026Shanghai Jiao Tong University

Learned expert skills improve shared control in a CT-guided biopsy robot

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A CT-guided biopsy robot used a variational autoencoder–hidden Markov model and dynamic motion primitives to learn procedural phases and trajectories from expert demonstrations. Its shared controller reduced interaction forces and targeting errors relative to manual, leader–follower and admittance control in phantom and rabbit experiments, with nine experiments per strategy; porcine work separately assessed robotic feasibility. These preclinical results do not establish safety or benefit in patients, and the learned skills came from a limited expert panel.

Technology: Variational autoencoder–hidden Markov model and dynamic motion primitives for expert-skill learning

Editorial relevance 53 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A variational autoencoder–hidden Markov model segmented latent procedural phases from expert skills; dynamic motion primitives reconstructed representative trajectories. These learned representations informed phase-adaptive shared control.
Test and result
Expert data were collected from a 31-doctor panel including ten experts. Phantom and rabbit control comparisons used nine experiments per strategy; porcine work assessed feasibility. Manual, leader–follower and admittance control were the comparators.
Limitation / still unknown
Clinical performance and patient benefit remain untested. Expert demonstrations and anatomical scenarios were limited, and phantom-to-living-tissue transfer remains a source-stated limitation.

10 Nov 2026Shanghai Jiao Tong University

Cross-state representation learning enables prospective discovery of dynamic protein allosteric sites

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The preprint abstract reports that AllositeUltra recovered allosteric sites among its top three predictions in 65.2% of apo structures and 70.6% of substrate-bound structures. More importantly, application to MAGI2 identified a previously unrecognized interdomain pocket, tested by small-molecule screening and X-ray crystallography. This within-study experimental finding qualifies separately from the benchmark. Review is limited to the abstract and original-server metadata.

Technology: AllositeUltra (structure-aware protein language model combined with physical dynamics; residue-guided candidate supplementation)

26 Oct 2026Shanghai Jiao Tong University

Judge-labeled invalid coding traces were longer in two related models

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Code-generation reasoning traces labeled invalid by a model-judge ensemble averaged 17,163 tokens for DeepSeek-R1-0528, compared with 7,102 for valid-labeled traces. Its distilled 8B variant showed the same direction. These labels concern reasoning coherence and are explicitly distinct from whether the final code passes functional tests.

Technology: DeepSeek-R1-0528; DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B

Family DeepSeek: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 40 computer science PreprintPreprint Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
DeepSeek R1 models generated traces; DeepSeek-V3.2, Gemini-3-Flash and GLM-5.1 judged their validity, while learned semantic annotators classified reasoning actions.
Test and result
Researchers collected traces under matched prompting and decoding. Three model judges each rated trace validity three times; confidence-weighted votes labeled high-confidence cases and two human annotators adjudicated the remainder. The reported length contrast groups these trace-validity labels, not final code correctness.
Limitation / still unknown
Validity judgments include coherence and unnecessary detours, which may themselves relate to trace length; the contrast does not establish a causal effect of long reasoning or a functional-code failure rate. The models share a lineage, selected complete traces exclude incomplete or unparsable responses, and verbal traces need not faithfully reflect internal reasoning.

23 Oct 2026Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Max Planck Institute for Sustainable Materials

Two model-screened magnesium alloy routes passed tensile tests

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A model-screened magnesium alloy containing gadolinium and yttrium reached 296 MPa yield strength and 398 MPa tensile strength in lab tests. A separate route using recycled feedstock produced AZ31 with similar mechanical properties to its primary-feedstock comparison. The study estimated 40.4% lower operating costs and 88.9% lower emissions for that route.

Technology: Physics-informed ensemble of random forests and XGBoost

Editorial relevance 60 materials PreprintPreprint Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The fitted ensemble predicted strength and linked candidate microstructures to processing routes, supporting selection of the two experimentally tested alloys.
Test and result
The study screened composition and microstructure candidates, selected two routes and tested prepared materials with tensile measurements and microstructural characterization.
Limitation / still unknown
The cost and emissions figures are modeled for the study’s feedstock and process assumptions. The two lab-scale cases do not establish production-scale performance or savings.

13 Jan 2026Shanghai Jiao Tong University; ShanghaiTech University and collaborating institutions

Self-iterative learning finds RNA-targeting prokaryotic Argonautes and an RNA-modification-sensing activity

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The self-iterative hierarchical ensemble model (SIM) nominated six high-confidence prokaryotic Argonaute candidates; five were experimentally validated as RNA-targeting proteins. The study identified three uncharacterized Agos with a novel N-terminal domain, defining a previously unrecognized subclass. Biochemical and in-vivo experiments on Haloferax profundi Ago (HpAgo) established RNA cleavage and a distinctive RNA-modification-sensing capability, which the authors used to build a method for quantifying modified RNA.

Technology: Self-Iterative hierarchical ensemble Model (SIM), an ensemble-learning and self-training pipeline

Editorial relevance 60 biology Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
SIM iteratively expanded its training labels to rank prokaryotic Argonautes for RNA targeting from sparse initial data; it prioritized candidates and identified a previously unrecognized domain-containing subclass. The researchers validated RNA cleavage and RNA-modification sensing biochemically and in vivo.
Test and result
The self-iterative hierarchical ensemble model (SIM) nominated six high-confidence prokaryotic Argonaute candidates; five were experimentally validated as RNA-targeting proteins.
Limitation / still unknown
Five of six selected candidates were validated; the broader predicted sequence space was not experimentally tested. The specific RNA modification-sensing mechanism and assay performance are supported for HpAgo and tested conditions, not all Argonautes. No therapeutic or universal detection claim is made. Primary abstract/citation record checked; no independent replication assessed.

5 Jul 2025Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory

VenusMine identifies PET hydrolase KbPETase

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: VenusMine combines structure-based search with protein-language-model representations and learned property filters to prioritize candidate PET hydrolases from large sequence collections. The authors tested 34 selected proteins and report PET-hydrolyzing activity for 14; KbPETase had an optimum activity temperature 32 °C above the IsPETase comparator. This is laboratory enzyme evidence, not demonstrated waste-processing performance. A 2024 preprint reportedly contains an earlier version; its exact version-to-result chronology was not freshly resolved, so this draft uses the journal version date and does not claim first discovery priority.

Technology: VenusMine; ProstT5; ESM2

28 Sep 2021Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Massachusetts General Hospital

Structural MRI learning identifies Alzheimer-like patterns associated with cognition

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A support-vector classifier learned Alzheimer-like structural patterns after age adjustment and identified 8.8% of an additional Human Connectome Project Aging sample as structurally Alzheimer-like. Those patterns were associated with cognitive differences, with additional biomarker analyses in mild cognitive impairment. These are cross-sectional exploratory associations; they do not establish future Alzheimer conversion, a clinical diagnosis in asymptomatic people or screening benefit.

Technology: Support vector machine; radial-basis kernel

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
An RBF support-vector machine learns weighted, age-adjusted cortical/subcortical patterns and assigns Alzheimer-like structural scores.
Test and result
Age-detrended structural MRI classification, additional-cohort cognitive associations and MCI biomarker comparisons.
Limitation / still unknown
Longitudinal disease conversion, independent clinical screening performance and patient benefit remain unestablished; development-stage feature weighting can inflate internal classification.
