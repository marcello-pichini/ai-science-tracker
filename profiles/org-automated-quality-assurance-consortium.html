Density clustering links unusual radiotherapy QA signals to maintenance records

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: DBSCAN identified unusual changes in longitudinal imaging-detector QA measurements from nine radiotherapy accelerators. Several flagged output, beam-profile and apparent jaw-position changes coincided with documented maintenance or calibration. These are retrospective measurement associations, not proof that maintenance caused a physical fault or that the system detects clinically dangerous failures.

Technology: DBSCAN unsupervised density-based clustering

Editorial relevance 33 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
DBSCAN learned density structure in longitudinal QA changes and flagged sparse observations, enabling targeted linkage of unusual measured signals to service documentation rather than a fixed list of expected calibration effects.
Test and result
Retrospective 2018–2024 EPID QA measurements and service histories from nine TrueBeam accelerators across six institutions. DBSCAN fit dense/noise groups separately to one-dimensional changes in each machine/metric, with a dataset-specific neighborhood rule. In one machine, 15 jaw-position outliers included a 1.3 mm apparent shift near a documented calibration. Independently paired ion-chamber measurements supported selected output-change comparisons on only two machines/12 events, not all DBSCAN flags.
Limitation / still unknown
Temporal associations do not establish service causation or independently confirmed physical faults. Apparent jaw shifts after steering may be measurement artifacts. Acquisition timing and detector recalibration records were incomplete, and two machines lacked complete service histories. Clustering parameters were not optimized against prospective truth; noise-versus-cluster statistical comparisons are circular. No sensitivity/specificity, superiority to existing QA, safer treatment or patient benefit was established.
