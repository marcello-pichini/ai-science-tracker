15 Aug 2024The First Affiliated Hospital of Wenzhou Medical University; Oujiang Laboratory; Wenzhou Medical University; Macao Polytechnic University

Machine-learning screening identified three compounds with measured ULK1 inhibition

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A Gaussian naive-Bayes classifier using ECFP6 fingerprints ranked commercial compounds from a 13-million-compound library, followed by docking, property filtering and manual selection. Researchers purchased and assayed 25 candidates; three showed micromolar ULK1 inhibition, with reported IC50 values of 37.11 ± 4.41, 7.67 ± 2.82 and 3.15 ± 0.77 μM. The molecules were purchased rather than newly synthesized, and this entry does not claim absolute chemical novelty. The study’s comparator MRT68921 was more potent; the results do not establish cellular activity or clinical utility.

Technology: Gaussian naive-Bayes classifier on ECFP6 molecular fingerprints (NB-ECFP6)
