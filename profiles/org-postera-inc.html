COVID Moonshot uses synthesis-directed de novo design for SARS-CoV-2 main protease inhibitors

A machine-learning de novo design model was coupled with synthesis route prediction in the COVID Moonshot campaign against the SARS-CoV-2 main protease. The authors report novel chemical scaffolds that are active in biochemical and live-virus assays and were synthesized along model-generated routes. The abstract gives no potency figures, so the strength of the result should be checked in the full text.
