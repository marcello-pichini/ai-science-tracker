13 Oct 2026Istituto Nazionale di Oceanografia e di Geofisica Sperimentale (OGS); CNR-ISMAR; University of Urbino; CONICET–University of Buenos Aires

Machine-learning phase picks constrain the 2025 Drake Passage earthquake depth and activated fault geometry

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: For the Mw 7.4 Drake Passage earthquake of 2 May 2025, a trained classifier identified depth-sensitive pP–sP seismic phases used to refine focal depths under sparse station coverage. The authors infer a mainshock depth of about 25 km, consistent with the small tsunami, and report aftershocks on west-dipping faults southeast of the epicenter near the Shackleton Fracture Zone; the paper integrates this with seismic-reflection and previously collected imaging data.

Technology: Trained machine-learning classifier for depth-sensitive seismic phase identification

Identified pP–sP phases in regional records; these phases were incorporated into event relocation and focal-depth determination, helping constrain the mainshock depth where station coverage is limited.

For the Mw 7.4 Drake Passage earthquake of 2 May 2025, a trained classifier identified depth-sensitive pP–sP seismic phases used to refine focal depths under sparse station coverage.

The machine-learning component contributes phase identification rather than discovering the earthquake itself.

11 May 2026Istituto Nazionale di Oceanografia e di Geofisica Sperimentale (OGS)

AI-assisted analysis reveals retrospective seismicity before the Dingri earthquake

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A PhaseNet-assisted workflow combined phase detection, polarisation analysis and template matching on records from one permanent station to reconstruct seismicity in the two years before the 2025 Mw 7.1 Dingri earthquake. The authors report increased event rate and clustering beginning about 45 days before the mainshock. This retrospective pattern is interpreted as compatible with late-stage nucleation; the study does not establish a prospective earthquake forecast.

Technology: PhaseNet deep neural-network seismic phase picker combined with polarisation analysis and template matching

Editorial relevance 40 physics Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
PhaseNet, a deep neural-network phase picker, supported detection and event-catalogue construction; the resulting catalogue enabled retrospective identification of the pre-mainshock activity increase.
Test and result
PhaseNet-assisted phase detection, polarisation analysis and template matching reconstructed 289 template events and 1,138 augmented events at one station before the 2025 Dingri earthquake. The authors report increased event rate and clustering beginning about 45 days before the mainshock.
Limitation / still unknown
This is a retrospective analysis from one station. The authors describe a pattern compatible with late-stage nucleation; it does not establish a prospective forecast or earthquake prediction.
