Cellpose 2.0: human-in-the-loop training for cell segmentation

Cellpose 2.0 provides pretrained cell-segmentation models that can be fine-tuned with fewer than 1,000 annotated regions of interest, reaching performance close to models trained on up to 200,000 regions. A human-in-the-loop pipeline reduces the required user annotation to about 100 to 200 regions while keeping segmentations high quality. Caveat: the validation is on the authors' benchmark datasets and annotation tests.

Technology: Cellpose 2.0 (deep learning cell segmentation, human-in-the-loop fine-tuning)
