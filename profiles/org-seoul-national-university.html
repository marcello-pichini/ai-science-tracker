7 Jan 2025Seoul National University; and Galux Inc.

A protein-language-model workflow discovers high-activity kynureninase enzymes for tumor models

A workflow combining homology search, protein-language-model embeddings and regression prioritized kynureninases by predicted catalytic activity. Researchers experimentally tested four top-ranked candidates; the most active had nearly twice the turnover number of the prior best, and enzyme treatment was associated with a 3.4-fold reduction in tumor weight in a mouse comparison.

Technology: Protein language model embeddings coupled with regression in the SeekRank enzyme-prioritization workflow

1 Aug 2022Seoul National University; Kyungpook National University; Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics, Peking University

Deep learning and spectroscopy confirm four faint quasars at redshift about 5

A deep-learning and Bayesian-information-criterion search of Subaru Hyper Suprime-Cam photometry selected four faint high-redshift quasar candidates. Palomar spectroscopy found broad Lyman-alpha emission and breaks in all four, confirming redshifts between 4.7 and 5.6; one object also received a black-hole mass estimate.

Technology: Deep-learning photometric classifier combined with Bayesian information criterion
