Unsupervised learning guides high-zT ScNiSb-based half-Heusler thermoelectrics

Iterative unsupervised clustering of half-Heusler compounds, using known thermoelectric materials as labeled reference points, guided selection of ScNiSb-based compositions for experimental testing. The team measured peak zT ≈0.5 at 925 K for p-type Sc₀.₇Y₀.₃NiSb₀.₉₇Sn₀.₀₃ and ≈0.3 at 778 K for n-type Sc₀.₆₅Y₀.₃Ti₀.₀₅NiSb. The paper reports these as compositionally optimized thermoelectric properties, not a new crystal structure or a device-level demonstration.

Technology: Iterative unsupervised clustering using prototype-based, density-based and hierarchical machine-learning algorithms

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Iterative unsupervised clustering of half-Heusler compounds, using known thermoelectric materials as labeled reference points, guided selection of ScNiSb-based compositions for experimental testing.
  • Still unknown: The measured materials are doped/alloyed variants of the known ScNiSb half-Heusler parent; the article does not claim discovery of a new crystal structure. Reported zT values are laboratory measurements at elevated temperatures, not performance from a fabricated waste-heat device or a commercial deployment. The two compositions are part of one paper-level screening and experimental result; do not split them into separate records.
  • AI role: The authors applied unsupervised clustering to Materials Project descriptors for 456 half-Heusler compounds, iteratively using clusters containing 20 known thermoelectric materials as reference groups to shortlist candidates. The resulting ScNiSb-based choices guided synthesis and transport-property measurements; the model supported candidate selection, while the researchers performed synthesis, characterization and interpretation.
