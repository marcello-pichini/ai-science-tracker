20 Mar 2025Harbin Institute of Technology; Chinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

Generative design leads to a synthesized magnesium-based thermoelectric composition

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A generative model combining a variational autoencoder, residual layers and discriminators proposed thermoelectric compositions from literature-derived data. Researchers synthesized the selected Mg 3.1 Sb 0.5 Bi 1.497 Te 0.003 composition and reported a thermoelectric figure of merit of 0.75 at 300 kelvin. This laboratory measurement is separate from the larger catalogue of predicted high-performance materials; it does not establish that all predictions were synthesized or that this room-temperature sample exceeded a figure of merit of 1.

Technology: Variational autoencoder; residual neural network; learned discriminators

Editorial relevance 47 materials Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A conditional generative architecture learns composition/property relationships using a variational encoder-decoder with residual layers, discriminators and a diversity-related loss, generating candidate compositions for experimental selection.
Test and result
The official supporting TableS 7 explicitly marks Mg 3.1 Bi 1.497 Sb 0.5 Te 0.003 as synthesized in this study and reports its transport properties and thermoelectric figure of merit of 0.75 at 300 kelvin.
Limitation / still unknown
Broad experimental success across the predicted catalogue, independent replication and practical device benefit remain unestablished. The reported 100 materials above a predicted figure of merit of 1 are computational candidates, not 100 newly tested materials.

4 Mar 2022Harbin Institute of Technology; Xi’an Jiaotong University; Inner Mongolia University of Technology

Unsupervised learning guides high-zT ScNiSb-based half-Heusler thermoelectrics

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Iterative unsupervised clustering of half-Heusler compounds, using known thermoelectric materials as labeled reference points, guided selection of ScNiSb-based compositions for experimental testing. The team measured peak zT ≈0.5 at 925 K for p-type Sc₀.₇Y₀.₃NiSb₀.₉₇Sn₀.₀₃ and ≈0.3 at 778 K for n-type Sc₀.₆₅Y₀.₃Ti₀.₀₅NiSb. The paper reports these as compositionally optimized thermoelectric properties, not a new crystal structure or a device-level demonstration.

Technology: Iterative unsupervised clustering using prototype-based, density-based and hierarchical machine-learning algorithms

Editorial relevance 47 materials Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The authors applied unsupervised clustering to Materials Project descriptors for 456 half-Heusler compounds, iteratively using clusters containing 20 known thermoelectric materials as reference groups to shortlist candidates. The resulting ScNiSb-based choices guided synthesis and transport-property measurements; the model supported candidate selection, while the researchers performed synthesis, characterization and interpretation.
Test and result
Iterative unsupervised clustering of half-Heusler compounds, using known thermoelectric materials as labeled reference points, guided selection of ScNiSb-based compositions for experimental testing.
Limitation / still unknown
The measured materials are doped/alloyed variants of the known ScNiSb half-Heusler parent; the article does not claim discovery of a new crystal structure. Reported zT values are laboratory measurements at elevated temperatures, not performance from a fabricated waste-heat device or a commercial deployment. The two compositions are part of one paper-level screening and experimental result; do not split them into separate records.
