Machine learning and ab initio screening predict 60 stable two-dimensional ferroelectric bimetal phosphates

A data-driven machine-learning search combined with high-throughput ab initio calculations screened 2,964 two-dimensional bimetal phosphates and identified 60 computationally stable structures with out-of-plane ferroelectric polarization: 16 predicted ferroelectric metals and 44 predicted ferroelectric semiconductors, including seven predicted multiferroics. The calculations analyze how bimetal displacement can support polarization alongside metallicity. These are theoretical candidates and proposed physical explanations, not experimentally synthesized or measured phases.

Technology: Data-driven machine-learning screening with orbital-based descriptors, combined with high-throughput ab initio calculations
