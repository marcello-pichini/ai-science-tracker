19 Nov 2021Tohoku University

Machine-learning-guided evolution identifies higher-activity sortase A variants

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Two machine-learning-guided directed-evolution series for sortase A were run with and without the known high-activity 5M variant in the initial training set. The authors report new variants with higher activity than 5M in a GFP/YFP cross-linking assay. This is not a 2.2–2.5-fold improvement over wild type; the known 5M comparator is already engineered. Feature selection used ability to find 5M and may bias exploration. The related GB1 analysis uses an existing dataset, not a second laboratory discovery. Sequence-level comparison with later sortase work remains open.

Technology: Gaussian-process Bayesian selection model

Editorial relevance 53 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A Gaussian-process ML model ranked variants from initial and second-round data to design subsequent SrtA mutant libraries.
Test and result
Constructed variants were assayed for SrtA-catalyzed GFP/YFP cross-linking; resulting variants exceeded the 5M comparator.
Limitation / still unknown
The assay is specific; 5M is an already high-activity engineered comparator; feature selection may bias to 5M; independent sequence-level overlap/replication open.
