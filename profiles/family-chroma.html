30 Apr 2026Washington University School of Medicine; University of Miami

CellOracle-guided pancreatic islet differentiation experiments reveal STAT1 and ZEB1 lineage roles

A digital model built from 400,603 cells across 61 single-cell datasets used CellOracle gene-regulatory-network inference and in-silico transcription-factor perturbations to nominate regulators of stem-cell-derived human islet differentiation. CRISPRi experiments supported a previously unreported role for STAT1 in exocrine specification: transient STAT1 knockdown reduced exocrine markers, and JAK–STAT inhibition preserved beta-cell markers and improved glucose-stimulated insulin secretion. CRISPRi also supported a stage-dependent role for ZEB1, whose early knockdown impaired endocrine specification and later knockdown reduced off-target serotonergic cells while increasing beta-cell proportion. The date is the first preprint posting; this entry summarizes the later version reviewed in the research record, which reports the specific STAT1 and ZEB1 experiments.

Technology: CellOracle v0.12.0 within a digital model integrating single-cell transcriptomic and chromatin-accessibility data

5 Dec 2023Weill Cornell Medicine; New York Genome Center; collaborating institutions

3D-HiChAT predicts embryonic enhancer hubs validated by CRISPR interference

A random-forest model integrating ATAC-seq, H3K27ac ChIP-seq and HiChIP data predicted enhancer effects across mouse trophoblast stem cells, embryonic stem cells and extraembryonic endoderm cells. CRISPRi tests supported a shared Enh14 hub regulating both Tfcp2l1 and Gli2 in embryonic stem cells, and an Enh4 hub linked to seven genes in extraembryonic endoderm; four of those seven showed expression reductions as predicted. Across 40 tested enhancer-promoter pairs, experiments yielded 12 true positives and 13 true negatives, with overall prediction accuracy 0.71.

Technology: 3D-HiChAT, an optimized Random Forest model combining chromatin accessibility, H3K27ac and 3D HiChIP connectivity features

12 Apr 2021Ghent University; VIB Center for Plant Systems Biology; Bioinformatics Institute Ghent

An integrated Arabidopsis regulatory network predicts 13 previously unlinked reactive-oxygen-stress regulators

Researchers integrated DNA-motif, chromatin-accessibility, TF-binding and expression evidence with supervised learning to build an Arabidopsis gene-regulatory network of 1,491 transcription factors and 1.7 million TF–target interactions. The network predicted candidate reactive-oxygen-species (ROS) regulators; loss- and gain-of-function phenotyping supported 13 regulators not previously linked to ROS or stress responses. Newly tested examples included WRKY55, WRKY47, AT2G33710 and AT5G57150. The paper also reports that 75% of its ROS-predicted TFs had ROS or physiological stress functions supported by either this study or prior evidence.

Technology: Supervised-learning integrated gene-regulatory network (iGRN) built from motif, open-chromatin, transcription-factor-binding and expression-based networks
