2 Jul 2025Helmholtz Munich; Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics; University of Tübingen

Centaur: a foundation model that predicts human behaviour across psychology experiments

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Fine-tuning Llama 3.1 70B on Psych-101, a dataset of about 10 million human choices from 160 psychology experiments, produced a model that predicts and simulates human behaviour in experiments expressed in natural language, including tasks it had not seen. The authors report that its internal representations align better with human neural activity after fine-tuning. Caveat: a 2025 critique argues that its language comprehension is limited and that it fails to follow altered instructions.

Technology: Centaur (Llama 3.1 70B fine-tuned on Psych-101)

13 Jan 2022Helmholtz Munich; Wellcome Sanger Institute

Cell2location: mapping fine-grained cell types in spatial transcriptomics

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Cell2location is a Bayesian model that resolves fine-grained cell types in spatial transcriptomics data by accounting for technical variation and sharing information across locations. Applied to three tissues, it revealed regional astrocyte subtypes in mouse brain, a rare pre-germinal center B cell population in human lymph node, and fine immune populations in human gut lymphoid follicles. Caveat: the reported results are biological findings from the method's application, with no single quantitative accuracy figure confirmed in the abstract.

Technology: Cell2location (Bayesian deep probabilistic model)

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A Bayesian model that resolves fine-grained cell types in spatial transcriptomics data.
Test and result
Cell2location was assessed in three tissues (mouse brain, human lymph node, human gut) and reported to give higher sensitivity and resolution than existing tools.
Limitation / still unknown
The abstract names no conditions under which the claimed higher sensitivity holds and no limitations.
