16 Dec 2021University of Michigan; MilliporeSigma; Entos, Inc.

Retrosynthetic software finds experimentally validated supply routes for umifenovir

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Retrosynthesis software proposed alternative chemical supply routes for the antiviral umifenovir and eleven other antiviral and anti-inflammatory drugs. The authors experimentally validated four routes to umifenovir and one route to bromhexine, one of which converts six C-H bonds. The work is a proof of route feasibility for supply-chain resilience, not a new therapeutic claim.

Technology: Computer-aided retrosynthesis software with exclusion of known starting materials

6 Jan 2020University of Michigan; Columbia University; University of Miami

Deep neural networks diagnose brain tumours in minutes from stimulated Raman histology

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: CNNs trained on over 2.5 million stimulated Raman histology images produced intraoperative brain tumour diagnoses in under 150 seconds, with 94.6% accuracy versus 93.9% for pathologists. The accuracy comparison comes from a prospective multicentre trial with 278 patients, but the abstract does not report sensitivity or specificity separately.

Technology: Deep neural networks on stimulated Raman histology images

Test and result
Multicenter prospective clinical trial (n = 278): CNN diagnosis of stimulated Raman images was non-inferior to pathologists reading conventional histology (94.6% versus 93.9%), in under 150 seconds.
