30 Jan 2020Sumitomo Dainippon Pharma and Exscientia

DSP-1181: an AI-created drug candidate enters Phase I

Sumitomo Dainippon Pharma and Exscientia announced the start in Japan of a Phase I study of DSP-1181, a 5-HT1A serotonin receptor agonist created with the Centaur Chemist platform, with obsessive-compulsive disorder as the first indication. The companies said exploratory research took under 12 months versus a typical 4.5 years. The announcement gives no efficacy results. In the January 31, 2022 investor-call transcript, Sumitomo said development was discontinued because the candidate did not meet the expected Phase I criterion.

Technology: Centaur Chemist (molecule-design platform)
