Tranylcypromine shows in-vitro activity against Leishmania major

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The study experimentally tested the previously model-predicted drug tranylcypromine against L. major promastigotes and intracellular amastigotes. It reports IC50 values of 83.6 and 31.6 µg/mL, respectively, and macrophage CC50 of 282.3 µg/mL. These relatively high in-vitro concentrations do not establish achievable exposure, patient safety or clinical efficacy; human monoamine oxidase mechanism is not shown as the parasite target.

Technology: Prior machine-learning drug-repurposing pipeline (the present study is experimental validation)

26 Sep 2024Institut Pasteur de Tunis; Université de Tunis El Manar

Machine-learning-predicted approved drugs show in-vitro Leishmania activity

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Two classifiers previously prioritized approved drugs from a screened set; ten candidates were purchased for assays against L. major and L. infantum. Five showed dose-dependent effects; intracellular testing and macrophage cytotoxicity were also reported. Domperidone and dibucaine had prior antiparasitic evidence, so the bounded result concerns the study’s remaining tested drugs and assays, not ten novel therapies.

Technology: Random forest and support-vector machine classifiers
