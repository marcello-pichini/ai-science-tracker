13 May 2025German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ); European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL); Wellcome Sanger Institute

Deep learning identifies MYT1L as a regulator that restricts glioblastoma plasticity

scDORI inferred enhancer-linked regulatory networks from single-cell multiome data and prioritized regulators for perturbation. MYT1L overexpression restricted alternative cell states and reduced tumor growth and invasion in mice; loss of MYT1L restored malignant features. This is preclinical evidence, not an established treatment. The result was public in a May 13, 2025 preprint; the peer-reviewed paper appeared November 4, 2026.

Technology: scDORI; deep learning for enhancer-linked gene-regulatory network inference

27 Apr 2023German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ); Heidelberg University; University of Applied Sciences Bingen

Single-cell imaging and machine learning reveal a Cdk2–COP9 interaction that induces a senescence-like immune state in Drosophila cells

A synthetic genetic-interaction and cell-morphology map covering more than 6,800 Drosophila genes supported machine-learning-based gene-function assignments across 47 modules. In follow-up, Cytoclass single-cell classification revealed a state-specific interaction between Cdk2 and COP9 signalosome knockdown: co-depletion arrested cell-cycle progression and produced a senescence-like phenotype with increased antimicrobial-peptide expression in cultured cells and hemocytes, including without bacterial infection.

Technology: Single-cell morphology classifier ensemble and machine-learning analysis of genetic-interaction profiles (Cytoclass; support-vector machines and random forest)
