LinearDesign: more stable mRNA sequences designed by an algorithm

The algorithm, based on a parsing technique from natural-language processing, finds the most stable mRNA sequence for a given protein in about eleven minutes. According to Baidu’s press release, whose study appeared in Nature, sequences produced this way showed greater stability, protein expression and antibody response than comparators in animal experiments. Figures are company-reported; the Baidu release copy on EurekAlert was reviewed, not a baidu.com page.
