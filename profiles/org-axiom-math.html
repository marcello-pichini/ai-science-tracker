Fel's conjecture proved and verified in Lean

Axiom Prover generated a proof of Fel's conjecture on syzygies of numerical semigroups, verified in Lean 4 with Mathlib, without hints, lemmas, or manual corrections after the run began. This is a case study and an unreviewed preprint, with no data on attempts or time taken.

Technology: Axiom Prover, Lean
