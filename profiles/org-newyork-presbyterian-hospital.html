EchoNext: detecting structural heart disease from ECGs with AI

EchoNext was trained on more than 1 million paired heart-rhythm and imaging records from a large health system and outperformed cardiologists in a controlled evaluation. In a prospective clinical trial of patients without prior cardiac imaging, it identified previously undiagnosed heart disease. Exact performance figures were not verified in this search, and model weights and an annotated dataset were released publicly.

Technology: EchoNext (deep learning model on 12-lead ECG)
