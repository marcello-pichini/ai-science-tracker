AI-discovered construction improves the lower bound for the complex Grothendieck constant

The authors prove the classical complex Grothendieck constant exceeds 1.35584631827168, closing more than one quarter of the gap between Davie’s lower bound and Haagerup’s upper bound reported in the paper. The numerical search used Hermite projections and a radial weight; interval arithmetic on exact rational inputs rigorously verifies the stated bound. The authors credit the Odin Automatic AI Research Agent with discovering both the lower bound and its proof.

Technology: Odin Automatic AI Research Agent
