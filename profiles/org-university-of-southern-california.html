CNN-augmented focal mechanisms reveal stress-linked faulting changes around major southern California earthquakes

CNN phase and first-motion pickers supplied additional waveform polarities and arrivals for a 297,478-event southern California focal-mechanism catalog, about 40% larger than the prior catalog. The authors used the expanded catalog to observe systematic post-mainshock faulting-style changes spatially correlated with estimated static stress perturbations from the 2010 El Mayor–Cucapah and 2019 Ridgecrest earthquakes, including changes more than 100 km from the epicentres.

Technology: Convolutional neural networks for seismic phase arrival and first-motion polarity picking
