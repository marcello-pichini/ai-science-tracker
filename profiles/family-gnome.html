GNoME: 2.2 million new predicted crystals

A graph neural network predicted 2.2 million new crystals, including 380,000 of the most stable, raising the number of known stable materials from about 48,000 to 421,000. External laboratories synthesized 736 predicted structures. Stability is calculated and does not guarantee a material can be produced or will be useful.

Technology: GNoME (Graph Networks for Materials Exploration)

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: prospective or independent check
  • Tested: Predicted stability was checked computationally with DFT, and external laboratories independently synthesized 736 of the predicted structures.
  • Still unknown: The blog states that most of the 380,000 stable predictions had not been made in a lab and that stability is judged by current scientific standards; manufacturing and usefulness remain open.
  • AI role: A graph neural network trained on about 48,000 stable crystals predicted 2.2 million new crystals, including 380,000 stable ones.
