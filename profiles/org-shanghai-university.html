13 Apr 2026Shanghai University

LLM-guided alloy design yields self-limiting oxidation behavior at 900 °C

A knowledge-enhanced AI workflow led to Co22Cr24Fe20Ni25Mo5Mn4, a non-equiatomic high-entropy alloy with experimentally observed single-phase FCC structure and self-limiting oxidation kinetics at 900 °C. After about 50 hours, the study reports a steady-state oxidation-rate constant of 1.41 × 10⁻⁸ mgⁿ cm⁻²ⁿ h⁻¹ (reaction order n = 45.35) and a Cr₂O₃/(Mn,Fe)Cr₂O₄ bilayer oxide scale. The proposed barrier–buffer explanation is the authors’ mechanistic interpretation.

Technology: Physics-guided ML phase classifiers and LLM-assisted alloy-design workflow

10 Jan 2025Shanghai University; Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

Active learning guides experimental design of lead-free solder alloys

Uncertainty-aware Gaussian-process models and multi-objective Bayesian acquisition guided seven design–experiment iterations. The study reports two selected alloys, Si-4 and Se-5, tested for tensile strength and fracture elongation. Reported averages follow the authors’ stated one-sigma selection of repeated tensile tests. Performance was mixed: the tested samples had poorer wettability and solder-joint shear strength than the commercial comparator. This is one laboratory alloy-design campaign, not evidence of industrial deployment or across-the-board superiority.

Technology: Gaussian-process models; multi-objective Bayesian active learning

3 Oct 2024Shanghai University; Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Xi’an Jiaotong University

Noise-aware active learning selects a high-latent-heat Ti–Ni–Hf shape-memory alloy

A noise-aware Kriging model with active-learning acquisition selected candidate compositions from a million-composition NiTi-based alloy design space. After four experiments, researchers reported Ti₀.₂₆₆Ni₀.₄₈₄Hf₀.₂₅ with a measured latent heat of −36.08 J g⁻¹, 9.2% greater in magnitude than the best value in the initial training data (−33.04 J g⁻¹). The alloy also had an austenite-finish temperature of 481.71 °C and relatively small transformation hysteresis, making it a candidate for high-temperature thermal energy storage.

Technology: Noise-aware Kriging surrogate model with an active-learning acquisition strategy incorporating experimental-data noise

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: A noise-aware Kriging model with active-learning acquisition selected candidate compositions from a million-composition NiTi-based alloy design space.
  • Still unknown: The reported latent heat retains the source’s negative sign; the 9.2% improvement is a comparison of absolute magnitudes against the initial dataset’s best value. This is one material result from an active-learning search, not exhaustive exploration of the million-composition space. The reported temperature/hysteresis properties are laboratory characterizations; the paper does not demonstrate a deployed thermal-storage module or independent reproduction.
  • AI role: The model estimated the measurement-noise level from noisy experimental data, incorporated that level into Kriging predictions, and used a noise-augmented acquisition strategy to choose the next NiTi-based compositions. Four experiments in the closed loop identified Ti0.266Ni0.484Hf0.25 as a promising high-latent-heat alloy; researchers synthesized and measured the material properties.
