26 Aug 2025Argonne National Laboratory

New combinations of additives for LNMO batteries found with machine learning

A model trained on data for 28 additives predicted the performance of 125 new combinations and identified formulations that outperformed the initial ones in tests. The study concerns high-voltage LNMO batteries; the Argonne announcement, accessed via Newswise, does not quantify the improvement or describe industrial-scale validation.

Technology: Machine learning on molecular features of additives

5 Oct 2022Argonne National Laboratory

Machine learning and synthesis clarify metastable phases of carbon

An algorithm constructed metastable phase diagrams of carbon and identified structural features of n-diamond that were then verified experimentally. The group also synthesized predicted phases and compared structures under a microscope; the work concerns carbon only and does not establish useful properties of the new materials.

Technology: Machine learning and atomistic simulations
