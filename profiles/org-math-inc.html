Gauss completes the Lean formalization of the dimension-8 sphere packing proof

Math Inc's autoformalization model Gauss completed the final stages of the Lean verification of Viazovska's proof that the E8 lattice is the optimal sphere packing in dimension 8, working with the human formalization team. The paper describes the human-AI collaboration and the verification in the Lean kernel, with the milestone dated February 2026 in the abstract. Formalization of an existing 2016 result, not a new theorem, and the paper is a progress report.

11 Sep 2025Math Inc

Gauss formalizes the Strong Prime Number Theorem in Lean

Math Inc reports that Gauss produced most statements and proofs for a Lean formalization of the strong Prime Number Theorem, with about 25,000 lines and 1,100 theorems/definitions in three weeks. Humans supplied mathematical scaffolding and reviewed strategies, and some files adapt prior work. The date follows the original project blueprint dated 11 September 2025, rather than an inferred announcement timestamp. This verifies a formalization of an existing theorem, not a new theorem.
