UniKP prioritizes a sulfotransferase for sulfotyrosine production

UniKP was used to rank sulfotransferase homologues by predicted kinetics. The study reports experimental testing of five prioritized candidates and identifies EfST1A1, with about 6.5-fold higher in-vitro activity than the initial hit under the reported assay. Later directed evolution is not attributed to the model. This is a bounded enzyme-discovery result, not proof of industrial production or broad enzyme generality.

21 Apr 2023Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences; Brigham and Women's Hospital; Harvard Medical School

Learned community models prioritize a tested bacterial colonization inhibitor

Models trained on community composition helped identify Enterococcus faecalis as an inhibitor of Enterococcus faecium colonization in human stool-derived laboratory communities. Follow-up supported the predicted interaction under the tested conditions. The result was public in an April 2023 preprint before the March 2024 journal article. It does not demonstrate prevention of infection or beneficial intervention in humans.

Technology: Random-forest and neural-ODE community models
