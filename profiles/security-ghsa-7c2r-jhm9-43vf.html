DTLS handshake signature scheme confusion allows peer impersonation

Pion DTLS · AI involvement attributed to Anthropic's OSS Scanner

Finding and attribution

The verifier selected its primitive from the certificate key type but its digest from the peer-controlled handshake scheme, allowing a forged signature to bypass proof of private-key possession. An attacker able to redirect a DTLS handshake and access the peer public certificate may impersonate that peer. The advisory says WebRTC SDP fingerprint checking does not stop this attack.

Product: Pion DTLS. AI system: Anthropic's OSS Scanner. Team / organization: Anthropic.
