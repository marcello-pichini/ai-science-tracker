Multi-omics machine learning prioritizes a plasma lipid signature for active tuberculosis differentiation

Machine-learning integration of plasma functional-omics data in two discovery cohorts and a validation cohort identified biosignatures that distinguished active tuberculosis from nontuberculous mycobacterial infection, latent TB infection and other lung diseases. Cohorts included 76 people in the TB–NTM discovery set, 72 in the TB–LTBI–other-lung-disease set, and 68 TB plus 30 LTBI participants in the validation set. PC(14:0_22:6) was the most important predictor and was lower in active TB; additional external datasets yielded reported AUCs of 0.77–1.00.

Technology: Multi-omics integration and machine-learning predictive models for feature selection and classification
