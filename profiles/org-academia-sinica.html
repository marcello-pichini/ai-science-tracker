Deep-learning seismic catalog reveals a potentially reactivated blind fault in Taiwan’s 2025 Dapu earthquake sequence

A RED-PAN deep-learning phase picker enabled a catalog of 6,805 relocated events for the 11-day sequence following the M6.4 Dapu earthquake. Analysis of those events, focal mechanisms and velocity structure identified an east-dipping plane at about 5–10 km and a west-dipping plane at about 10–15 km; the authors infer the mainshock involved a previously unrecognized blind west-dipping fault and interactions with the known Muchiliao–Liuchia system. This is a localized structural interpretation, and the authors describe some fault associations as potential.

Technology: RED-PAN deep-learning seismic phase picker

1 Jul 2021Academia Sinica, Taiwan

iTARGEX analysis identifies ELG1 as a regulator of yeast transcription buffering and four protein-turnover regulators

iTARGEX applied a mixture-regression model to gene-expression profiles from 1,484 single-gene deletion yeast mutants, associating transcriptomic changes with S-phase transcriptional buffering and global protein turnover. Experiments supported ELG1 as a previously unrecognized regulator of transcriptional buffering during S phase; the phenotype was reproduced in a second genetic background and rescued by a PCNA-D150E mutation. Follow-up protein-turnover measurements showed altered turnover in MRN1, UPF3, RAM1 and SSN3 deletion strains.

Technology: iTARGEX, a finite-mixture regression framework fitted by expectation maximization to single-gene-deletion transcriptomic data
