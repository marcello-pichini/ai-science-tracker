Machine learning and experiments yield tolerant polymer dielectrics

Machine-learning property models and phenomenological screening rules narrowed a list of more than 13,000 previously synthesized polymers to candidates for high-temperature dielectric applications. The authors report experimental testing of selected candidates and derive design rules for electric-field and temperature tolerance. Review is limited to the abstract and publication metadata.
