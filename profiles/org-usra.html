22 May 2023USRA, NASA and SETI Institute

ExoMiner and multiplicity validate 69 new exoplanets

ExoMiner V1.2 combines deep learning with information about other signals in the same star system to validate 69 planets in Kepler data. This is statistical validation of transit signals, not new direct observation; the result is distinct from the 301 planets validated in 2021.

Technology: ExoMiner V1.2 / multiplicity boost
