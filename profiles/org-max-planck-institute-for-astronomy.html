Self-supervised imaging search identifies 16 spectroscopically confirmed quasars at z≈5.94–6.45

Contrastive learning on DESI Legacy Survey DR10 multiband images, followed by SED-fit prioritization, selected sources for spectroscopy; follow-up confirmed 16 quasars at z=5.94–6.45. Several show uncommon narrow Lyα lines, strong Lyα+N V equivalent widths or mildly red observed-frame NIR slopes, and three lie in regions traditional color cuts would miss. The 16-object reported sample includes 15 new literature discoveries and one independently confirmed DESI DR1 source; the 45% follow-up yield includes previously known quasars among 40 observed targets.

Technology: Self-supervised contrastive-learning neural network applied to DESI Legacy Survey DR10 multiband images, with SED-fit prioritization

Random-forest selection and spectroscopy identify 20 new high-redshift quasars

A supervised random-forest search of Pan-STARRS1 and WISE photometry identified follow-up targets in the difficult redshift range around z≈5.5, where quasars resemble red and brown dwarfs. Spectroscopic observations of 37 candidates confirmed 20 previously unknown high-redshift quasars (18 at z=4.6–5.5 and two around z≈5.7), including a source outside common color-cut selections.

Technology: Supervised random-forest classification and regression applied to Pan-STARRS1 and WISE data
