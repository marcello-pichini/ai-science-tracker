Operating system: how many public vulnerabilities?

The dataset links 70,496 published vulnerabilities to products in this category; 569 are in CISA KEV and 4,288 have public exploit code. Most common weakness classes: memory corruption, out-of-bounds read, denial of service, null pointer dereference, improper input validation. Counts reflect disclosure and research attention as much as quality: they are not a safety ranking.
