29 May 2025Northwestern University; California Institute of Technology; Stockholm University; Liverpool John Moores University; NSF-Simons AI Institute for the Sky; Zwicky Transient Facility collaboration

Autonomous BTSbot-nearby follow-up captures an infant supernova’s flash spectrum and constrains progenitor mass loss

The BTSbot-nearby system used the BTSbot machine-learning classifier to identify SN 2024jlf in ZTF data and autonomously trigger a spectroscopic follow-up request, enabling spectroscopy about 0.7 days after first light. The early spectra showed short-lived Hα, He II and C IV flash-ionization features; comparison with two radiative-hydrodynamics model grids constrained the red-supergiant progenitor’s enhanced pre-explosion mass-loss rate to roughly 10⁻⁴–10⁻³ solar masses per year.

Technology: BTSbot machine-learning transient classifier integrated with the BTSbot-nearby autonomous follow-up program

20 Jan 2025Northwestern University; Stanford University

Machine learning-guided cell-free engineering creates specialized amide synthetases for pharmaceutical molecules

A cell-free design-build-test-learn platform generated sequence-function data from 1,217 McbA variants across 10,953 reactions. Ridge-regression ML guided enzyme variants that produced nine small-molecule pharmaceuticals with activity 1.6- to 42-fold above the parent; initial substrate-promiscuity assays found McbA could make 11 pharmaceutical compounds plus hybrid molecules. This is experimental enzyme engineering, not evidence of therapeutic efficacy of the products.

Technology: Augmented ridge-regression ML models within a cell-free sequence-function mapping and enzyme-engineering workflow

  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: A cell-free design-build-test-learn platform generated sequence-function data from 1,217 McbA variants across 10,953 reactions.
  • Still unknown: The platform optimizes in-vitro enzyme production of molecules and does not establish drug efficacy, manufacturing readiness, or broad transferability beyond tested reactions. The nine-product gains are relative to the parent enzyme under the reported assay conditions.
  • AI role: Used measured variant fitness data to predict and select higher-activity McbA variants for distinct amide-forming reactions; selected variants were synthesized and assayed.

11 Oct 2023Northwestern University; California Institute of Technology

BTSbot workflow automatically confirms and classifies SN 2023tyk

BTSbot selected SN 2023tyk from ZTF alerts and requested robotic spectroscopy; SNIascore supplied its Type Ia classification. The integrated workflow publicly reported the result without human intervention in those steps. This is one transient result, not a new supernova class or a claim that BTSbot alone performed the observation. It preceded the expanded 2024 study.

Technology: BTSbot convolutional classifier and SNIascore

Bayesian optimization guides complex nanoparticle heterostructures

Learning from earlier synthesis and microscopy data guided a human-supervised nanoparticle campaign. Researchers reported the desired structures for 18 of 19 selected candidates, including complex multielement particles. This success rate concerns a curated experimental set, not the full predicted composition space. Structural synthesis was demonstrated; catalytic and device performance were not.

Technology: Bayesian optimization of composition and morphology
