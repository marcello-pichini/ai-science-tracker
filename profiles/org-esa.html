27 Jan 2026ESA; and ESA-Hubble

AnomalyMatch finds over 1,300 unusual objects in the Hubble archive

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The neural network scanned about 100 million cutouts from the Hubble Legacy Archive in roughly two and a half days: nearly 1,400 anomalies were flagged and over 1,300 confirmed, including more than 800 not previously documented in the literature (mergers, lenses, and jellyfish galaxies). AI only ranks candidates; the two counts are not explained, and no false-positive rate is given.

Technology: AnomalyMatch (neural network)

The neural network screened about 100 million Hubble Legacy Archive cutouts, and the authors personally inspected the top-ranked sources; more than 1,300 were confirmed as true anomalies, of which more than 800 were not previously documented; no false-positive rate is given.

19 Mar 2025ESA; and Euclid Consortium

Euclid: 500 candidate strong gravitational lenses

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The first catalog of 500 candidate galaxy–galaxy lenses, almost all previously unknown, was selected with AI models and then reviewed by citizen scientists and experts. These are candidates, not confirmed lenses; 7,000 by the late-2026 release and about 100,000 by mission end are projections.

Technology: Image-classification models

Editorial relevance 40 physics Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: Test context unspecified in annotation. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Deep learning models performed the initial sweep for candidate lenses.
Test and result
AI candidates were inspected by citizen scientists and then vetted and modelled by experts, system by system, to produce 497 candidate lenses in the first catalogue.
Limitation / still unknown
The page calls these candidates, not confirmed lenses, gives no error or false-positive rates, and the future counts are projections.
