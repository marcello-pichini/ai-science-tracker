24 Nov 2025OpenAI and University of California, Los Angeles

GPT-5 helps solve a 40-year-old problem about Nesterov's accelerated gradient

Ernest Ryu (UCLA) proved why Nesterov's accelerated gradient method, introduced in 1983, remains stable despite the extra momentum that makes it faster. GPT-5's turning point was a rewrite of the equations: not correct as given, but with a structure Ryu then developed and proved himself, over about twelve hours across three days and after a dozen approaches. OpenAI's account says the model created no new mathematical tools and often produced plausible arguments that failed verification.

Technology: GPT-5
