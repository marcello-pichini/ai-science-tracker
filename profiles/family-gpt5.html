AI-assisted proof establishes a dimension-independent KLS isoperimetric bound

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Zhao Song and Xinzhi Zhang prove a universal, dimension-independent bound for the Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits constant of isotropic log-concave probability measures, resolving the KLS conjecture. Their proof repeatedly refines bounds on polynomial variance and curvature; the authors report that AI tools helped explore more than 100 approaches, obtain the earlier log-star bound, and develop and verify the final proof. Independent researchers also posted proofs, and the paper’s streamlined proof is being formalized in Lean. The 1 October preprint gave a slowly dimension-dependent bound; the 4 October version established the dimension-independent theorem described here.

GPT-assisted proof reduces Zieve’s Hurwitz realizability conjecture to the three-branch-point case

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The authors prove that, assuming Zieve-admissible branch data are realizable for three branch points, all such data with four or more branch points are realizable. GPT-5.6 constructs a counterexample to Talagrand’s operator cotype problem

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The paper gives a negative answer, already for q=2, to Talagrand’s question whether an operator’s Rademacher cotype is universally bounded by its Gaussian cotype and its (q,1)-summing norm. For every sufficiently large power-of-two dimension it constructs a Banach space and operator violating such a universal bound. The same construction also yields a negative answer to Kwapień’s decomposition problem.

31 Aug 2026Brookhaven National Laboratory

GPT-5.6 helps prove an exact mapping between frustrated and field-driven spin chains

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A single-author preprint proves that every finite one-dimensional open O(n) spin chain with arbitrary inhomogeneous nearest- and next-nearest-neighbor interactions has an exact, temperature-independent Hamiltonian mapping to a simpler nearest-neighbor chain with axial single-spin potentials. A parallel result for q-state Potts chains yields a closed-form solution for every q ≥ 2 and finite chain length. The theorem generalizes a 1990 Heisenberg-chain challenge and connects spontaneous collective behavior from competing interactions to field-induced behavior.

Technology: OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol; GPT-5.6 Sol Pro for final adversarial manuscript review
