Model-scored concrete examples tracked successful replies in online debates

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Across 800 held-out pairs of Reddit ChangeMyView replies to the same post, the reply that earned the original poster’s change-of-view award scored higher for concrete examples in 60.4% of pairs and for qualified rebuttals in 59.6%. The language model scores describe associations with human-awarded outcomes.

Technology: GPT-5.4 mini; GPT-5.4; statistical choice model

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
GPT-5.4 mini compared and scored Reddit discussion replies. GPT-5.4 grouped rationales into features, and the fitted choice model and reliability filter assessed which features to retain.
Test and result
Among 800 held-out same-topic pairs, the user-awarded successful reply had a higher concrete-illustration score in 60.4% of pairs (95% CI 57.0–63.8), qualified rebuttal score in 59.6%, and evidentiary support score in 57.5%.
Limitation / still unknown
Model ratings were not independently human-annotated for each feature. The comparisons use filtered pairs in one forum, and the associations do not show that adding a feature would change a reader’s mind.

People adopted false facts from confident AI tutors

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In a study with 32 volunteers, participants selected an assistant’s planted false answer for about 60% of quiz items assigned false information, compared with 11% of items assigned correct information. Mean scores fell from 7.4 to 2.9 out of 10 between fully accurate and fully falsified GPT-5-mini briefings.

Technology: GPT-5-mini

Test and result
The counterbalanced study used five briefing reliability levels and 104 completed quizzes, with independently randomized topic order and corrective debriefing.

This was a small study with one underlying model, deliberately confident responses, and immediate quizzes. Retention over longer periods and responses to other assistants were not assessed. 20 Oct 2026Eindhoven University of Technology

AI-guided simulations rank two zeolites differently as CO₂ pressure changes

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: RaspAssistant compared three literature-based adsorption models against existing MFI zeolite measurements, selected the one with the lowest aggregate error and used it to simulate CO₂ adsorption in 197 pure-silica structures. JBW had the highest simulated uptake at 1 kPa, while GIS led at 10 and 100 kPa and had the highest calculated working capacity from 1 to 100 kPa. These are model-dependent rankings of known structures. The reference measurements were for MFI.
