11 May 2020King's College London; Massachusetts General Hospital

Self-reported symptom app predicts likely COVID-19 infection in real time

A COVID Symptom Study app with 2,618,862 participants and 18,401 tested users found loss of smell or taste in 65.03% of positives versus 21.71% of negatives (OR 6.74). A machine-learning model applied to 805,753 users predicted 140,312 (17.42%) as likely infected. The inputs are self-reported and the test-verified subset is small relative to the user base, so the prediction is not a clinical diagnosis.

Technology: Machine-learning symptom model on self-reported app data
