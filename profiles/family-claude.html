First 3D structure of a prokaryotic vault particle, with Claude

Claude searched protein databases, designed DNA constructs, reduced purification from about three days to about 90 minutes, and processed cryo-EM data into an initial model in 34 hours. The result is a particle made of 96 copies of a bacteriophage protein, with about 25% more internal volume than the mammalian version. Its biological function is unknown; this is a preprint, and Claude made errors that humans corrected.

27 Oct 2026KU Leuven

AI agent maps proton-gated water loss in perrhenate-catalysed deoxydehydration

The paper reports a computational mechanism for perrhenate-catalysed deoxydehydration: proton-assisted water loss enables ring closure and alcohol reduction. Under human-defined hypotheses, a Claude Code agent located transition states, which the authors validated. Coupled-cluster-anchored microkinetic modeling predicted a rate increase of about eight orders of magnitude over the best proton-free pathway. At matched conversion conditions, modeled barriers were consistent with the 31.5–34.0 kcal/mol window inferred from previously reported experimental turnover; this was not a new wet-lab demonstration.

Technology: Claude Code LLM agent for human-directed reaction-network and transition-state search, combined with electronic-structure calculations and microkinetic modeling

5 Oct 2026Anthropic; Columbia University; Massachusetts Institute of Technology

Claude discovers algorithms that refute the 3SUM and APSP time-complexity hypotheses

Alman and Vassilevska Williams present deterministic algorithms for 3SUM on polynomial-size integers in O(n^1.9992) time and all-pairs shortest paths on directed graphs with polynomially bounded integer weights in O(n^2.9995) time, the first polynomial improvements over the textbook exponents. The single underlying technique computes selected entries of thin matrix products more efficiently, and known reductions yield the main algorithmic consequences. The paper says an internal Anthropic Claude research model discovered the algorithm autonomously; the human authors then understood, simplified, strengthened and extended it. One paper-level theoretical-computer-science result is counted.

4 Oct 2026Microsoft Research; independent research collaboration

AI-assisted proof establishes a dimension-independent KLS isoperimetric bound

Zhao Song and Xinzhi Zhang prove a universal, dimension-independent bound for the Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits constant of isotropic log-concave probability measures, resolving the KLS conjecture. Their proof repeatedly refines bounds on polynomial variance and curvature; the authors report that AI tools helped explore more than 100 approaches, obtain the earlier log-star bound, and develop and verify the final proof. Independent researchers also posted proofs, and the paper’s streamlined proof is being formalized in Lean. The 1 October preprint gave a slowly dimension-dependent bound; the 4 October version established the dimension-independent theorem described here.

Technology: GPT-6 Astra; GPT-5.6 Sol; Claude Fable 5; Fable (as named in the paper’s AI disclosure)

1 Jul 2026Sapienza University of Rome; CNR-Nanotec Rome; INFN Sezione di Roma 1; Beihang University

Claude-assisted analytic proof establishes a+b=1 for mean-field jamming exponents

Giorgio Parisi and Francesco Zamponi provide an analytic proof that the critical exponents a and b in the full replica-symmetry-breaking scaling solution for infinite-dimensional dense hard spheres obey a+b=1. The identity links gap, contact-force and overlap scaling relations and had previously been known numerically but not analytically. The authors report that Claude Opus 4.7 derived the core proof idea with minimal supervision, with Sonnet 4.6 helping refine it; the authors found and corrected errors in an early draft, then verified and edited the final proof.

20 Mar 2026Massachusetts Institute of Technology; NSF AI Institute for Artificial Intelligence and Fundamental Interactions; CERN; ALEPH Collaboration archival data

Claude Code produces the first primary Lund jet-plane density measurement in electron–positron collisions

A Claude Code agent pipeline analyzed archived 1994 ALEPH hadronic-Z data and reported the first primary Lund jet-plane density measurement in e⁺e⁻ collisions. Its unfolded 57-bin fiducial result shows the expected triangular QCD radiation structure and a shallow low-transverse-momentum turnover; the integrated mean primary-emission count is 4.751 ± 0.224 per hemisphere. This is one new measurement, not a claim of discovering new particles or physics beyond QCD.

Technology: Claude Code agents orchestrated by the Just Furnish Context (JFC) framework

9 Mar 2026Cornell University; TU Wien

AI-assisted work proves a Latin-square imbalance bound, with optimal examples through order 52

A collaborative LLM-plus-symbolic-computation study proved that every n×n Latin square with n ≡ 1 (mod 3) has imbalance at least 4n(n−1)/9. It introduced near-perfect permutations that attain the bound and computationally found independently verified matching examples for every such order from 4 through 52. The universal lower-bound theorem was formally checked in Lean 4.

Technology: Claude Opus 4.5 LLM agent with SageMath, Rust exhaustive solver, simulated annealing and Lean 4 verification tools
