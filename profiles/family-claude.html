Mathematician and Claude release a Lean proof of the Riemann hypothesis

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, released a Lean library of about 17 million lines that he states proves the Riemann hypothesis and the generalized Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions, developed with Claude. His accompanying note says he guided the argument from a toy setting while Claude supplied much of the technical work, including the √X length of a chain of theta lifts; the note's timeline runs from 6 October to 16 November 2026. A day after release, the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative certified that the library proves Mathlib's statement of the Riemann hypothesis and passes several independent proof checkers. No human-readable proof has been published, and Alpöge writes that he has not yet mastered parts of the argument.
