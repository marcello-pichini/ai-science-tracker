17 Jul 2026Henan Medical University; Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Shanghai AI Laboratory

EnzGFM-Agent experimentally prioritizes activity-enhancing secreted alkaline phosphatase variants

EnzGFM-Agent scored all possible single amino-acid substitutions in secreted alkaline phosphatase (SEAP) and selected top candidates from EnzGFM-1.5B and ESM2-3B for testing in transfected Chinese hamster ovary cells. Among the five variants uniquely prioritized by EnzGFM, all five had significantly higher measured activity than wild type in the reported assay; three exceeded 1.5-fold, and A46I reached 6.52-fold. This is an experimentally measured enzyme-engineering result for the tested SEAP variants.

Technology: EnzGFM-Agent with EnzGFM-1.5B and ESM2-3B

22 Jun 2026Tsinghua University; Tianjin University; Inner Mongolia University; Zhejiang Institute of Tianjin University

Few-shot protein-language-model design yields high-output bicyclogermacrene synthase variants

After experimentally screening activity-enhancing single mutants of LdTPS5, the researchers used PIFS-PLM, a few-shot model that selects features from protein-language-model embeddings using active-site and conserved-residue information, to propose mutation combinations. All 10 tested model-predicted combinations were active; nine produced more than 100 mg/L bicyclogermacrene in yeast, and the top variant produced 213.59 mg/L versus 2.93 mg/L for wild type (about 73-fold higher yield). A related selectivity-optimized combination increased bicyclogermacrene selectivity by 15%.

Technology: PIFS-PLM (Physics-Inspired Feature Selection of Protein Language Models), using ESM-2 embeddings with a few-shot mutation-combination classifier

30 Apr 2026Columbia University; Massachusetts Institute of Technology and Harvard University

AI-guided design enables E. coli ribosomal proteins without isoleucine

Researchers used computational protein design and generative AI to recode ribosomal proteins in E. coli so they function without isoleucine, then assembled an engineered strain that remained viable and genomically stable over more than 450 laboratory generations. The result shows a reduced amino-acid alphabet can support core translation machinery in this bacterium; the organism still uses isoleucine elsewhere.

Technology: Protein design models including ESM2, MSATr, ProteinMPNN and AfDesign

Machine-learning-guided mining identifies 91 previously unknown PET hydrolases, including acid-active enzymes

Researchers iteratively combined sequence mining, machine-learning activity/property predictors and high-throughput assays to screen natural enzyme homologs. Technology: Three-round iterative machine-learning pipeline (including VAE/rank model, supervised activity predictors, ESM embeddings and property predictors)

23 Aug 2025Zhejiang Lab; Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Nanjing Medical University; Tongji University; National University of Singapore; ShanghaiTech University; Zhejiang University; Chinese Academy of Sciences

ESM-guided metagenome mining identifies seven new Cas12a subtypes with distinct activities

AIL-Scan fine-tuned an evolutionary-scale protein language model (ESM) on Cas proteins to search metagenomic sequences and identify previously undocumented Cas12a lineages. The study classified seven previously unreported Cas12a subtypes with distinct CRISPR-locus organization, then biochemically characterized 16 Cas12a proteins from the candidate clades, documenting different DNA-cleavage and PAM-recognition preferences. The authors also demonstrated a specific assay strategy for distinguishing the KRAS G12C variant using a Cas12a variant with broader PAM recognition.

Technology: AIL-Scan, using fine-tuned ESM-2 protein language-model embeddings and a machine-learning classifier to identify Cas proteins and predict Cas12a trans-cleavage activity

PepMLM designs experimentally binding target-conditioned peptide ligands, including peptides used for targeted protein degradation

PepMLM fine-tunes ESM-2 using masked-language modeling conditioned on a target protein sequence to generate de novo linear peptide binders without structural input. In the reported experiments, selected peptides showed sequence-specific binding to targets including NCAM1 and AMHR2; incorporation into ubiquitin-based antibody constructs enabled targeted degradation of disease-relevant proteins, including Huntington’s disease drivers and viral phosphoproteins.

Technology: PepMLM, ESM-2 fine-tuned with target-sequence-conditioned masked language modeling

7 Aug 2025Peking University; Peking University Third Hospital; Tsinghua–Peking University Joint Center for Life Sciences; Nanchang University

BEAUT uncovers new gut-microbial bile-acid enzymes and the novel bile acid 3-acetoDCA

The BEAUT workflow combines ESM-2 protein-language-model embeddings with a neural classifier and active-site-pocket-similarity data augmentation to search gut microbial proteins for bile-acid enzymes. The authors predicted over 600,000 candidate enzymes and experimentally tested selected predictions; assays identified new bile-acid activities including MABH and ADS. ADS produces 3-acetoDCA, a previously undescribed bile-acid carbon skeleton, reported in human feces and shown to alter microbial composition and promote Lactobacillus-associated effects in experimental studies.

Technology: BEAUT, ESM-2 protein-language-model embeddings plus dense neural-network classifier, augmented using active-site-pocket similarity

21 Feb 2025Stanford University; Oklahoma Medical Research Foundation, Duke University, University of Pennsylvania and collaborating institutions

Mal-ID maps disease-associated immune-receptor patterns across infections and autoimmune conditions

Mal-ID analyzed B-cell and T-cell receptor repertoires from 593 people, including patients with COVID-19, HIV, systemic lupus erythematosus and type-1 diabetes, influenza-vaccination recipients and healthy controls. Its combined models classified held-out immune states and highlighted disease-associated receptor patterns; interpretation identified distinct autoreactivity features in lupus and type-1 diabetes and antigen-associated receptor sequences in COVID-19. The authors also evaluated classification on independent COVID-19 cohorts.

Technology: Mal-ID (MAchine Learning for Immunological Diagnosis), an ensemble using repertoire feature models and ESM-2 protein-language-model embeddings

CHOOSER uses protein foundation models to discover functional Casλ homologs

The CHOOSER framework fine-tuned ESM-2 protein-language-model representations for alignment-free CRISPR-Cas discovery and pre-crRNA self-processing predictions. It nominated 11 Casλ homologs, nearly doubling the known Casλ set. Wet-lab work on selected homologs confirmed pre-crRNA processing for five of six tested proteins; EphcCasλ also cleaved target DNA and showed trans-cleavage activity, with a TTR PAM reported.

Technology: CHOOSER, a protein foundation-model framework fine-tuning ESM-2 representations for Cas homolog identification and Cas12 pre-crRNA-processing classification
