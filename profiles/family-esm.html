ESMC-MonoYield predicts monomeric protein yield in E. coli from sequence

Source check: Source checked (abstract only). Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A pooled assay combining peptide barcodes, size-exclusion chromatography and mass spectrometry measured monomeric yield for over 39,000 protein domain variants, de novo proteins and nanobodies in E. coli. The data were used to benchmark inverse-folding methods and structure-confidence metrics, and to fine-tune the protein language model ESMC into ESMC-MonoYield. The abstract states that it prospectively enriched high-yield candidates among BindCraft-designed binders, but gives no effect size.

Technology: ESMC protein language model fine-tuned to predict monomeric yield from sequence

28 Oct 2026Sichuan Academy of Traditional Chinese Medicine

Pretrained model screening nominates Astragalus-ginseng for ovarian aging

Source check: Source checked (abstract only). Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A multi-strategy framework combining single-cell analysis, literature mining and a 712-molecule compound library used the ESM-T5 pretrained protein-molecule model (best of six combinations) to predict compound-target affinity, pointing to the Astragalus-ginseng pair. SPR and molecular dynamics supported binding to CYP19A1, SERPINE1 and MDK targets, and the pair ameliorated granulosa cell senescence and diminished ovarian function in cyclophosphamide-treated rats. It is a preprint at abstract level; the model is one of several strategies, and only cell and rat models were used.

Technology: ESM-T5 pretrained protein-molecule representation model for compound-target affinity prediction

22 Oct 2026Jilin University

ESM-2-based screening finds yak collagen peptides that inhibit MMP-1

Source check: Source checked (abstract only). Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: AgeAttnMLP, an ESM-2-based screening model, reached AUC 0.891 under five-fold cross-validation and 0.879 under a cluster-aware grouped evaluation. Screening yak collagen sequences with in silico Alcalase hydrolysis identified TGPPGPSGI, GEPGAPGE and GPSGEPGTA as lead peptides. GEPGAPGE showed the strongest MMP-1 inhibition (IC50 126 ug/mL), and all three peptides extended Caenorhabditis elegans lifespan, most for GPSGEPGTA at 7.5 mg/mL. Potency is modest and in vivo evidence is limited to worms.

22 Jun 2026Tsinghua University; Tianjin University; Inner Mongolia University; Zhejiang Institute of Tianjin University

Few-shot protein-language-model design yields high-output bicyclogermacrene synthase variants

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: After experimentally screening activity-enhancing single mutants of LdTPS5, the researchers used PIFS-PLM, a few-shot model that selects features from protein-language-model embeddings using active-site and conserved-residue information, to propose mutation combinations. All 10 tested model-predicted combinations were active; nine produced more than 100 mg/L bicyclogermacrene in yeast, and the top variant produced 213.59 mg/L versus 2.93 mg/L for wild type (about 73-fold higher yield). A related selectivity-optimized combination increased bicyclogermacrene selectivity by 15%.

Technology: PIFS-PLM (Physics-Inspired Feature Selection of Protein Language Models), using ESM-2 embeddings with a few-shot mutation-combination classifier

After single substitutions were screened and activity-enhancing mutations experimentally validated, PIFS-PLM used 60–100 measured combinations to learn from selected ESM-2 features at active-site and conserved residues and predict beneficial combinations. Ten predicted LdTPS5 variants were tested; all were active, and the top variants substantially increased measured yeast product yield. The model also guided a selectivity optimization test. Initial single-mutation nomination used MM/GBSA or sequence conservation analysis, separate from PIFS-PLM combinatorial predictions.

Product profiles differed between cell-free extracts and yeast; these results do not establish universal performance across enzymes or production settings.
