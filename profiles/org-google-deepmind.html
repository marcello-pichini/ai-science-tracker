New families of unstable singularities in fluid equations

Physics-informed neural networks trained to near machine precision revealed a new family of unstable singularities (blow-ups) in three fluid-dynamics equations, with a clear pattern between blow-up speed and instability order for the incompressible porous-medium and Boussinesq equations. Work with Brown, NYU, and Stanford: a step toward computer-assisted proofs, not a proof; the Navier–Stokes problem remains open.

Ithaca: restoring and dating ancient Greek inscriptions

A neural network reconstructs missing text in damaged Greek inscriptions, identifies their provenance and dates them: 62% accuracy in restoration, 71% in location, and dating within 30 years of reference ranges. With Ithaca, historians improved from 25% to 72% correct restorations. It is intended to assist historians.

DM21: a neural network as a density-functional theory functional

A neural network represents the functional used in DFT and addresses two longstanding errors of traditional functionals: excessive electron delocalization and spin-symmetry breaking during bond breaking. Published in Science with open code; the post gives no benchmark figures and DFT remains an approximation.

AI-guided pure mathematics: knots and representation theory

With mathematicians Williamson (Sydney) and Lackenby and Juhász (Oxford), supervised-learning techniques revealed patterns leading to an unexpected link between a knot’s algebraic signature and its geometry (the ‘natural slope’), and to a new algorithm for the combinatorial invariance conjecture. The conjectures and proofs are the mathematicians’; the algorithm was checked on over 3 million examples, not proved in general.

Enformer: gene expression predicted from DNA sequence

A Transformer-based model predicts gene expression from 200,000 base pairs of input DNA, over five times more than earlier methods, and is much more accurate at predicting variant effects. Enhancer attribution scores are comparable to methods using experimental data. The post gives no numerical gain and acknowledges that linking enhancers to the genes they regulate remains an open problem.

FermiNet: quantum physics of molecules from first principles

A neural network for electron wavefunctions: for the smallest molecules it captures 99.8% of the correlation energy relative to a Slater determinant, at least 97% for bicyclobutane (30 electrons), and a mean error of 4 meV for excited states of the carbon dimer versus 20 meV for earlier methods. The page is dated 2024-08-22 but gives the original date as 2020-10-19; the method is approximate.

An AI breast-cancer screening system evaluated in two countries

Among 25,856 women in the UK and 3,097 in the US, the system reduced false positives by 5.7% (US) and 1.2% (UK), and false negatives by 9.4% and 2.7% in absolute terms compared with radiologists.
