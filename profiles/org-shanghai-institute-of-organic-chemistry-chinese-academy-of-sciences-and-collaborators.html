MetGenX annotates two previously uncharacterized metabolites in mouse liver

MetGenX, a structure-informed neural-network workflow for generating structures from tandem mass spectra, was applied to untargeted mouse-liver metabolomics and reported two previously uncharacterized metabolites, adenosyl-cysteine and adenosine 5′-diphosphate 5′-inosine (Ap2I). The paper reports validation against chemical standards using retention time and MS2 spectral matches.

Technology: MetGenX structure-informed encoder-decoder neural network with LightGBM reranking
