13 May 2026University of Edinburgh; and Cancer Research UK Scotland Centre

Machine learning prioritizes compounds active against heterogeneous glioblastoma stem-cell models

Researchers trained cell-survival predictors on image-based screening data from six patient-derived glioblastoma stem-cell lines and screened more than 12,000 compounds. Experimental follow-up identified three compounds with activity across the GBM phenotype; HSP90 inhibitor XL-888 eliminated all six tested lines at nanomolar IC50 values, with analyses consistent with HSP90 inhibition and downstream client-protein disruption.

Technology: Machine-learning cell-survival predictors trained on high-throughput phenotypic screening

25 Nov 2025University of Edinburgh; British Geological Survey and University of Padua

Deep learning forecasts aftershock rates in seconds, comparable to ETAS

Neural networks trained on earthquake catalogues from California, New Zealand, Italy, Japan and Greece forecast the number of aftershocks in the following 24 hours after magnitude 4+ earthquakes. Their quality is reported as comparable to the operational ETAS model, while producing results in seconds rather than hours or days. The abstract reports no numeric skill scores, so the comparison rests on the full paper.

Technology: Deep neural networks for spatiotemporal seismicity rate forecasting, benchmarked against ETAS

2 Jan 2025University of Edinburgh; University of Lille; Heriot-Watt University; University of Southern Denmark; University of Dundee; University of Southern Denmark and collaborating UK Biobank institutions

Deep learning enables multi-site bone marrow adiposity genetics in UK Biobank

Deep learning measured bone marrow fat fraction at four skeletal sites in UK Biobank MRI data; after quality control, 48,608 participants contributed to genome-wide analyses. In the white-participant meta-GWAS, the study reported 67, 147, 134 and 174 independent significant variants for femoral head, total hip, femoral diaphysis and spine, mapping to 54, 90, 43 and 100 genes, respectively. Analyses also identified site- and sex-specific genetic associations and added signals in multi-ancestry analyses.

Technology: Previously validated deep-learning models for MRI segmentation and bone marrow fat-fraction measurement
