Machine learning guides discovery of selective submicromolar CK2 inhibitors

The accepted-paper abstract reports that docking, CDK-liability filtering, machine learning and error prediction guided more selective CK2 inhibitor leads. The authors report submicromolar hits and a 90% pipeline hit rate; kinase-assay figures support biochemical inhibition. These are discovery-stage compounds, with no demonstrated clinical benefit. The hit rate describes the authors' selected pipeline, not a general drug-discovery success rate.

Technology: Machine learning; prediction-error modeling; molecular docking

11 Sep 2024University of Helsinki; KTH Royal Institute of Technology

Machine learning identifies alkaline laccases from Lepista nuda

Machine-learning pH-optimum predictors screened metagenomic sequences, and two predicted Lepista nuda laccases were produced and profiled. The authors report LnLccA and LnLccB optimum activity at pH 10 and 9 for substrate 2,6-DMP, respectively, while both had pH 4 optima for ABTS. The substrate-dependent contrast is retained; it is not a general alkaline optimum or industrial demonstration.

Technology: Supervised, semi-supervised and unsupervised pH-optimum prediction models

  • Still unknown: Prior characterization and novelty of sequence/activity remain open; industrial applicability not demonstrated.
  • AI role: Models trained on curated laccase pH data predicted candidates from metagenomic sequences that were expressed and profiled.
