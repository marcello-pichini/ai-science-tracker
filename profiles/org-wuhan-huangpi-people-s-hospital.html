19 Mar 2020Wuhan Huangpi People's Hospital; Wuhan Pulmonary Hospital; Liaocheng People's Hospital

COVNet deep learning separates COVID-19 from other pneumonia on chest CT

The COVNet 3D deep-learning model, trained on 4,352 CT scans from 3,322 patients, reached per-scan sensitivity 90% and specificity 96% (AUC 0.96) for COVID-19, and AUC 0.95 for community-acquired pneumonia. The study is retrospective and evaluated on Chinese cohorts, so performance in other populations is not established.

Technology: COVNet, a 3D convolutional neural network on chest CT
