MatterGen: materials design with a generative model

A diffusion model trained on 608,000 stable materials. Compound TaCr2O6, generated with a constraint of 200 GPa bulk modulus, was synthesized and measured at 169 GPa (relative error under 20%), but with Ta–Cr disorder differing from the proposed structure. One experimental example.

7 Dec 2023Microsoft Research

MatterGen: stable materials generated from desired properties

A diffusion model generates stable, novel materials directly from desired properties: 2.9 times more stable, novel and unique structures than CDVAE, and over 250 materials with bulk modulus above 400 GPa versus 2 in the reference dataset. Results were checked with DFT calculations; experimental verification remains the ultimate test.
