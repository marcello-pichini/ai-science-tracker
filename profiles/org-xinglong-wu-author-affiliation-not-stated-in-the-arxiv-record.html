GPT-5.6 constructs a counterexample to Talagrand’s operator cotype problem

The paper gives a negative answer, already for q=2, to Talagrand’s question whether an operator’s Rademacher cotype is universally bounded by its Gaussian cotype and its (q,1)-summing norm. For every sufficiently large power-of-two dimension it constructs a Banach space and operator violating such a universal bound. The same construction also yields a negative answer to Kwapień’s decomposition problem.

Technology: ChatGPT GPT-5.6
