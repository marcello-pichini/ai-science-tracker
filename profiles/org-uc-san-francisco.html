23 Aug 2023UC San Francisco and UC Berkeley

Woman with paralysis communicates through a brain-controlled avatar

For a participant with stroke-related paralysis, an electrode array on the cortex and AI algorithms translated attempted speech into text, synthetic voice and avatar movements. Text reached nearly 80 words per minute after individual training. Experimental study in one person; the system requires a physical connection to computers.

Technology: Neural speech decoding and AI facial animation

14 Jul 2021UC San Francisco

A neuroprosthesis restores words for a man with paralysis

Neural networks decoded the attempted speech of a man with stroke-related paralysis, displaying words and sentences on a screen. With a 50-word vocabulary, the system reached up to 18 words per minute and a median accuracy of 75%. Proof of principle in one person, using an implant on the brain surface and a limited vocabulary.

Technology: Neural networks for speech and language model

12 Oct 2020UC San Francisco; Gladstone Institutes

Akita: predicting 3D genome folding from DNA sequence

Akita, a convolutional network, predicts chromatin contact maps for about 1 Mb of DNA sequence. On held-out data it reaches a Pearson R of 0.61 and Spearman R of 0.56, and it learns an orientation-specific grammar for CTCF binding sites. Caveat: the predictions are computational; the paper applies them to in silico mutagenesis, eQTL interpretation and structural-variant prediction.

Technology: Akita (convolutional neural network, Basenji-based trunk)
