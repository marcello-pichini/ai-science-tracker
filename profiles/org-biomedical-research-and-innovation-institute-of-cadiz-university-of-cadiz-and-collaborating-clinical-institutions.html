Machine-learning selection of a six-protein panel for atherosclerosis severity and patient stratification

Five machine-learning classifiers integrated clinical and serum-proteomic data from carotid atherosclerotic stenosis (60 participants), dyslipidemia (55) and healthy controls (66). A consensus approach identified a six-protein panel (B2M, GPV, MMP9, PLF4, TSP1 and fibrinogen/FB isoforms) that distinguished the atherosclerosis group from the comparison groups, with reported ROC-AUC above 0.9 across the tested classifiers. Protein levels were evaluated by flow cytometry and ELISA in an independent external cohort that included patients with acute atherothrombotic stroke.

Technology: Five machine-learning classifiers: Naive Bayes, linear SVM, random forest, Extra Trees and AdaBoost
