29 Jun 2026National Astronomical Observatories, Chinese Academy of Sciences; University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

MSFA-Net and Balmer-line fitting identify 3,583 new blue horizontal-branch stars

A two-stage deep-learning pipeline searched LAMOST DR12 spectra and retained 27,853 BHB candidate spectra. After removal of previously known objects, stringent Balmer-line-profile fitting, and deduplication, the authors report 3,583 unique newly identified blue horizontal-branch stars. Gaia photometry provides an additional consistency check.

Technology: MSFA-Net, a multi-scale and frequency-attention deep neural network, followed by a binary refinement classifier
