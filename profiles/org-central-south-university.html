Physics-informed generative learning yields experimentally verified single-phase B2 intermetallic alloys

A conditional variational autoencoder and artificial neural network, using physics-informed descriptors of B2 sublattice ordering, generated and ranked compositions in complex alloy systems. Experimental synthesis and XRD/TEM characterization verified previously unreported single-phase B2 alloys, including three Fe–Co–Ni–Ti–Zr compositions, Co19.9Ni30.5Ti38.1Zr11.5, and Cu4.7Co9.2Ni36.4Ti38.7Zr7.4Hf3.5. The study reports that random-sublattice descriptors separated B2 from multiphase alloys more effectively than conventional random-mixing descriptors in these datasets.

Technology: Physics-informed conditional variational autoencoder (CVAE) and artificial neural network (ANN)
