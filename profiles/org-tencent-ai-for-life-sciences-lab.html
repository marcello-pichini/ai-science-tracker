2 Sep 2026Tencent AI for Life Sciences Lab; City University of Hong Kong; The University of Hong Kong; Xijing Hospital of Digestive Diseases; Harvard Medical School, Harvard University; Monash University

ERTransformer-generated TCR candidates activate T cells in defined receptor contexts

ERTransformer generated epitope-conditioned TCR β-chain CDR3 candidates. Flow-cytometry experiments found selected artificial TCRs induced activation comparable to or greater than natural controls in defined TCR/pMHC contexts. This represents the September 2026 journal report; an ERTransformer precursor was posted October 20, 2023. The earlier report does not establish that these journal wet-lab results were already public.

Technology: ERTransformer; EpitopeBERT; ReceptorBERT

19 Mar 2026Tencent AI for Life Sciences Lab

ORI engineers active lysozymes, heat-tolerant chitinases and dual-activity enzymes

Tencent researchers used ontology-conditioned generation and experimental feedback to engineer enzymes. In the reported assays, lysozyme TX-RL15 exceeded the earlier TX-L6 design by 66-fold and hen egg-white lysozyme by over two orders of magnitude. Chitinase TX-SC2 retained activity after incubation at 85 °C; selected proteins combined lysozyme and chitinase activity. These are bounded biochemical results. The date represents initial journal publication; the version of record followed on May 7, 2026.

Technology: ORI; protein generation model (PGM); unified sequence model (USM)

10 Dec 2025Tencent AI for Life Sciences Lab

tFold designs binding antibodies against four defined antigens

Tencent researchers generated antibodies against influenza hemagglutinin, PD-1, PD-L1 and SARS-CoV-2 RBD, followed by phage-display screening and SPR characterization. Anti-SC2RBD-H330 showed neutralization IC50 of 0.42 nM. Screening remained extensive, and some confident epitope predictions failed competition tests. The date represents initial journal publication, preceding the January 19, 2026 version of record; a tFold precursor was posted February 8, 2024.

Technology: tFold System; tFold-Ab; tFold-Ag; AlphaFold-Multimer
