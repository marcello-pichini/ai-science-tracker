Biochemical-constrained generative AI yields experimentally active antimicrobial peptides against E. coli

A University of Hong Kong-led team used a conditional variational autoencoder constrained by biochemical properties and a random-forest activity classifier to generate and prioritize antimicrobial peptides. In vitro testing of 54 generated peptides found 18.5% with MIC ≤10 μg/mL and 38.9% with MIC ≤50 μg/mL against the study’s E. coli model; nine peptides were classified as both active and non-toxic in the reported assays.

Technology: CVAE-BIO: conditional variational autoencoder with biochemical constraints and a random-forest antimicrobial-activity classifier

6 Dec 2024The University of Hong Kong; Taiyuan University of Technology; Peng Cheng Laboratory

Swin-Transformer discovery of planetary nebulae with spectroscopic confirmation in VPHAS+

A Swin-Transformer search of VPHAS+ Hα images identified faint, resolved planetary-nebula candidates in crowded Galactic fields that prior point-source searches missed. Follow-up spectroscopy and HASH classification of 31 targets found 16 true, two likely and four possible planetary nebulae, alongside contaminants and a supernova-remnant fragment. This is one validated search-sample result, not 22 equally secure confirmations.

Technology: Swin-Transformer deep-learning classifier trained on known planetary nebulae
