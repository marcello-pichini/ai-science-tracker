12 May 2026ETH Zurich; Weizmann Institute of Science; University Children’s Hospital Zurich and University of Zurich

Machine-learning metabolomics identifies widespread promiscuous activities in Escherichia coli enzymes

In vitro metabolomics of 667 purified E. coli enzymes, analyzed with a machine-learning-derived multivariate classifier, indicated previously unrecognized promiscuous activities in 11% of enzymes tested. Follow-up reconstruction assigned 22 reaction stoichiometries to 11 enzymes, with four reactions experimentally validated; examples include expanded nucleotide-substrate use by DeoA, cofactor and sugar promiscuity in NanK, and FMN dephosphorylation by CobC. Follow-up assays also found that pyrimidine-specific ribonucleoside hydrolase RihB acted on thymidine, guanosine and deoxyuridine, further extending its measured substrate range.

Technology: Machine-learning-derived multivariate classifier applied to enzyme metabolomics ion-trace data

22 May 2024ETH Zurich; University of Basel and University of Regensburg

Active learning identifies a higher-activity streptavidin variant for gold-catalyzed hydroamination

An iterative active-learning campaign engineered a streptavidin artificial metalloenzyme for the known-to-the-authors gold-catalyzed hydroamination of 2-ethynylaniline to indole. Gaussian-process models guided screening across five residues in a 3.2-million-variant sequence space. The final-round library contained 38% variants more active than the prior reference, compared with 3% in the initial library. The leading variant, Sav CTNLV, showed 18-fold higher cell-specific activity than wild-type Sav and threefold higher activity than the previous reference variant in replicate testing.

Technology: Active-learning Gaussian-process regression model with optimized sequence descriptors and experimental-noise estimates

24 Apr 2024ETH Zurich; and Roche

Protein-surface-designed molecules activate PPARs

A generative model designed molecules from the three-dimensional surfaces of PPAR proteins: synthesized in the laboratory, they increased activity and were tested by Roche. They demonstrate the method and are not intended for commercial development; the tests do not establish safety or efficacy in humans.

Technology: Protein-surface-guided generative AI

2 Dec 2023ETH Zurich; Institute of Microbiology

Machine learning identifies bacterial strains and combinations that reduce pathogen colonization in Arabidopsis

Random forest and elastic-net models analyzed 136 synthetic communities, each composed of five bacterial strains, and identified strain identity as the strongest predictor of protection against Pseudomonas syringae DC3000 in Arabidopsis. Independent test communities confirmed predictive performance, and follow-up plant assays showed that Acidovorax Leaf76, Rhizobium Leaf68 and Pseudomonas Leaf15 each reduced pathogen colonization by about two orders of magnitude versus axenic controls; a Rhizobium Leaf371–Arthrobacter Leaf337 combination also reduced colonization.

Technology: Random forest and elastic-net generalized linear models (GLMNet)

27 Jun 2021ETH Zurich; Thomas Jefferson University; University of Bari; University of Innsbruck; Goethe University Frankfurt; Friedrich Schiller University Jena

Machine-intelligence design yields selective synthetic COX-1 inhibitors from a marine natural-product template

Using Marinopyrrole A as a template, an automated design workflow generated and synthesized new natural-product-inspired molecules; target-prediction software pointed to cyclooxygenases. In biochemical assays, synthetic mimetics 2 and 2a inhibited COX-1 at 0.10 and 0.16 μM, respectively, and compound 2a was crystallographically observed bound to COX-1. Human platelet assays also showed inhibition.

Technology: DOGS rule-based de novo molecule construction and CATS molecular-similarity ranking, coupled with SPiDER self-organizing-map machine-learning target prediction

11 Jun 2021ETH Zurich

Generative AI and on-chip synthesis produce new LXR agonists

A recurrent neural network generated de novo LXR agonist designs, and 25 were synthesized in a microfluidic flow system. In vitro screening found 17 hits (68%), and 12 were confirmed as potent LXR agonists after batch resynthesis. Validation is in vitro only, with no in vivo testing, and the sources disagree on the number of designs retained for synthesis.

Technology: LSTM recurrent neural network generator with transfer learning, coupled to on-chip flow synthesis
