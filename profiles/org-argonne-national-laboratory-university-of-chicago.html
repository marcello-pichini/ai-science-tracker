Polybot: an autonomous lab finds processing recipes for conductive polymer films

An AI-driven automated laboratory at Argonne used importance-guided Bayesian optimization to explore a seven-dimensional processing space for electronic polymer thin films. The campaign produced scale-up recipes for transparent conductive films with average conductivity above 4,500 S/cm, and feature-importance analysis identified key processing factors. Nature Communications abstract read; the number of experiments and the baseline comparison were not checked.

Technology: Importance-guided Bayesian optimization with automated synthesis and characterization
