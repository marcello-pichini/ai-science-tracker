Deep learning ECG model separates Takotsubo syndrome from anterior STEMI, with a randomized reader study

A self-supervised three-architecture ensemble reached AUC 0.970 and 0.914 accuracy on external validation (95 participants from two institutions). In a randomized four-phase reader study with 70 readers, AI assistance raised the probability of correct diagnosis from 0.632 to 0.864 (OR 3.71); the source notes AI-assisted accuracy stayed below model accuracy.

Technology: Self-supervised deep learning ensemble with Grad-CAM explanations
