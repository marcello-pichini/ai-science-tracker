RAI-939: an autonomous-lab alloy tested with a torch

Radical AI reports that AI-generated hypotheses and an autonomous lab produced new alloys, which were then tested by Purdue Applied Research Institute. During one minute under a torch, RAI-939 gained 0.25% mass while C103 lost 32%. This is a company announcement without peer review or replication details; scaling production remains to be developed.
