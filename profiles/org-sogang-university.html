Machine-learning screening reveals enhanced formate selectivity of Cu–Ga for CO₂ electroreduction

A machine-learning high-throughput screen of 465 binary metallic catalysts predicted previously unreported Cu–Ga selectivity for formate production in CO₂ electroreduction. Electrochemical tests of electrodeposited Cu–Ga measured a maximum formate Faradaic efficiency of about 38.4% at −1.05 V_RHE, roughly four times that on bare Cu under the reported conditions. This is a catalyst selectivity result for the tested electrode and electrolyte, not evidence of industrial-scale performance.

Technology: DSTAR active-motif machine-learning models with a CO₂-reduction selectivity map
