10 Jun 2026Weizmann Institute of Science; École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL)

AI-designed bacterial anti-defense proteins enable phage infection and plasmid retention

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: RFdiffusion-based de novo binder design and experimental screening produced synthetic proteins that inhibited three bacterial defense systems: Thoeris and Avs1 in Bacillus subtilis assays, and DdmDE in Vibrio cholerae. Five designed proteins inhibited Thoeris and nine inhibited Avs1 in the reported screens. An engineered SBSphiJ phage carrying both anti-Thoeris and anti-Avs1 proteins replicated in B. subtilis cells expressing both defenses. In native V. cholerae O1 El Tor, two of four tested DdmDE binders improved plasmid resistance to clearance.

Technology: RFdiffusion de novo binder design, ProteinMPNN sequence design and AlphaFold2/3 structure-guided candidate scoring

CycleRFdiffusion-designed TP4 is a reproducible cyclic peptide binder to TREM2

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A structure-conditioned workflow combining CycleRFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN and HighFold generated and screened 1,500 cyclic peptide–TREM2 complexes and prioritized four candidates. The lead TP4 showed weak but reproducible TREM2 binding in spectral-shift, microscale-thermophoresis and surface-plasmon-resonance assays. The authors report favorable plasma stability and moderate metabolic stability, presenting the work as an initial proof of concept for TREM2-targeted cyclic binders.

Technology: CycleRFdiffusion backbone design, ProteinMPNN sequence design and HighFold structural prediction/screening

Computational design of zinc metallohydrolases with RFdiffusion2

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: RFdiffusion2 designed zinc metallohydrolases from quantum-chemistry-derived active-site geometries. The first round of 96 designs produced an enzyme with catalytic efficiency of 16,000 M-1 s-1, and a second round of 96 yielded three more highly active enzymes, with k/K up to 53,000 M-1 s-1. Crystal structures (PDB 9PYJ, 9PYL) were determined, but the evidence covers one designed enzyme family.

Technology: RFdiffusion2 with quantum-chemistry active-site models
