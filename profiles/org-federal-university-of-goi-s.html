2 Jul 2025Federal University of Goiás

Machine-learning screening selects compounds active against Candida

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Random-forest prediction and chemical filters helped narrow commercial compounds for experimental testing. Of 17 selected compounds, the paper reports 11 with inhibitory activity against Candida albicans at tested concentrations. Compound 17 had an IC50 of 0.071 μM and MIC of 4.5 μM, compared with fluconazole values of 0.39 μM and 0.78 μM, respectively; its lower IC50 but higher MIC does not establish superiority. The reported selectivity-index definition appears inconsistent with tabulated values, so this draft omits that index. Cell assays do not establish clinical efficacy or safety.

Technology: Random-forest classifiers and regressors; chemical similarity filtering

16 Mar 2021Federal University of Goiás

AI-assisted virtual screening identifies a dual-stage antimalarial candidate

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An AI-assisted virtual-screening cascade using shape-based and machine-learning models prioritized potential Plasmodium kinase inhibitors. Eight virtual hits were experimentally evaluated; LabMol-167 inhibited P. berghei ookinete conversion and P. falciparum blood-stage parasites at nanomolar concentrations and showed low cytotoxicity in mammalian cells. A possible additional PK5 target was proposed from computational target fishing and docking, not demonstrated experimentally. This is a preclinical hit result and does not establish therapeutic efficacy. The article appeared online on 2021-03-16.

Technology: AI-assisted virtual screening combining shape-based and machine-learning models
