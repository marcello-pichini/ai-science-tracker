Machine-learning-guided optimization finds low-loading conditions for platinum-catalyzed amide reduction

A machine-learning-assisted reaction-optimization study found conditions for reducing N,N-dimethylacetamide to dimethylethylamine using the known platinum complex [Pt(DMS)₂Cl₂]. The selected condition used 0.01 mol% catalyst and reached complete conversion in two hours at 39 °C without solvent, with a reported turnover frequency of 5,002 h⁻¹. The chemical catalyst identity is not claimed as new.

Technology: Sunthetics machine-learning reaction-optimization platform

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: The selected condition used 0.01 mol% catalyst and reached complete conversion in two hours at 39 °C without solvent, with a reported turnover frequency of 5,002 h⁻¹.
  • Still unknown: This is a single-substrate reaction-condition optimization on N,N-dimethylacetamide. [Pt(DMS)₂Cl₂] is a known complex; the reported finding is the ML-assisted optimized condition and measured performance, not discovery of a new platinum compound. The best-performing condition is reported after human-guided interpretation as well as model recommendations. The paper does not establish transferability to other amides, industrial scale-up or general performance across catalyst classes.
  • AI role: The ML platform recommended reaction conditions across iterative experiments, using observed reaction outcomes to refine proposed conditions and reduce the number of trials. The team combined those recommendations with chemist interpretation and tested the predicted conditions experimentally; the source reports the optimized platinum-catalyzed reduction as a study-specific result.
