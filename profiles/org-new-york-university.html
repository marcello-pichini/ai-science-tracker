27 Oct 2026New York University; NYU Shanghai; Flatiron Institute

Learned simulations mapped a shallow free-energy landscape for acridine

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Anharmonic simulations using a fine-tuned MACE potential placed four metastable acridine forms 2.27–2.83 kJ/mol above Form II at 300 K. In the simulations, Form VIII became the most stable member of that group at higher temperature. Laboratory transitions partly agreed with the predicted changes in ordering.

Technology: MolCryst fine-tuned MACE-MH1 interatomic potential

Editorial relevance 47 materials Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A MACE-MH1 interatomic model fine-tuned on electronic-structure configurations supplied energies and forces for anharmonic thermodynamic integration.
Test and result
At 300 K the estimated Gibbs free energies of four metastable acridine forms were 2.27–2.83 kJ/mol above Form II. Form VIII was estimated to become the most stable member of that metastable group at higher temperature. Observed transitions partly agreed with the predicted crossovers.
Limitation / still unknown
The absolute free-energy differences are computational estimates. The comparison is with observed phase transformations, and Form IV was omitted from the anharmonic calculation. The main text discusses a value near 350 K, while the supporting protocol lists temperature propagation over 280–340 K; that endpoint is not promoted here. Laboratory transitions only partly match: the calculation leaves Form III more stable throughout, unlike the observed Form VIII–III crossover.

21 Oct 2026New York University; The University of Texas at Austin

Penalizing length changed an AI model’s grammatical choices

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Researchers trained Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct to turn structured events into English sentences. Penalizing longer answers increased its preference for shorter grammatical variants, including unfamiliar constructions with an optional “that.” Transfer to more distant constructions was limited.

Technology: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, spaCy en_core_web_trf

Editorial relevance 53 humanities Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A trained dependency parser and learned classifiers quantify linguistic features, and length-penalty post-training of Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct tests grammatical transfer.
Test and result
Post-training varied the length penalty and KL regularization across three seeds, then tested unseen examples of trained and untrained grammatical alternatives.
Limitation / still unknown
This controlled experiment used one small language model. It did not measure human comprehension or show that an analogous penalty caused the language patterns observed in frontier models.

23 Dec 2022New York University; Wayne State University

Learned fetal MRI patterns associate with later motor development

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A fetal MRI study combined CNN-derived movement measures with learned brain coactivation patterns. It analyzed brain networks in 120 fetuses; CNN-based movement extraction succeeded in 98. One supplementary-motor/posterior coactivation pattern was associated with seven-month motor scores, while fetal movement itself was not significantly associated with postnatal motor outcomes. These observational relationships do not establish causal development or a validated individual prognostic test.

Technology: Convolutional neural network; k-means coactivation clustering

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A CNN masks fetal brains for motion measurement, while k-means learns recurring low-motion fMRI coactivation states whose occurrence is related to development.
Test and result
CNN-derived head-motion measurements, k-means brain-state analysis and later Bayley motor-score associations.
Limitation / still unknown
Causal mechanisms, individual prognostic utility and independent replication remain unestablished; CNN failures reduced the movement sample.

24 Sep 2021New York University; Purdue University and University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

Cross-species machine learning identifies transcription factors that negatively regulate plant nitrogen-use efficiency

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Evolutionarily informed XGBoost and GENIE3 analyses of conserved nitrogen-response expression patterns prioritized transcription factors associated with nitrogen-use efficiency in Arabidopsis and maize. Loss-of-function tests found higher NUE in Arabidopsis myb33 and tcp2 mutants under both low- and high-nitrogen conditions, and in six other tested TF mutants specifically under low nitrogen; a maize nfya3 mutant also showed higher nitrogen-use efficiency under sufficient nitrogen in field tests.

Technology: Evolutionarily informed XGBoost models and GENIE3 random-forest gene-regulatory-network inference

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The models ranked conserved nitrogen-responsive genes and transcription factors for contribution or network connectivity to NUE; researchers selected the candidates and tested loss-of-function mutants in Arabidopsis and maize, including field-grown maize.
Test and result
Loss-of-function tests found higher NUE in Arabidopsis myb33 and tcp2 mutants under both low- and high-nitrogen conditions, and in six other tested TF mutants specifically under low nitrogen; a maize nfya3 mutant also showed higher nitrogen-use efficiency under sufficient nitrogen in field tests.
Limitation / still unknown
The machine-learning prediction scores are not themselves causal evidence; this paper adds functional evidence from specific mutants. Some tested maize candidate roles were assessed through Arabidopsis homologs, and the maize NFYA3 phenotype described here was under sufficient-N field conditions, not a general yield benefit across conditions.
