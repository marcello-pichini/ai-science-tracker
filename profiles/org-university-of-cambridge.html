GPT-6 Astra resolves the spectral isometry conjecture for arbitrary unital C*-algebras

The paper proves that every unital surjective complex-linear spectral isometry between unital C*-algebras is a Jordan isomorphism; without unitality, such a map is a central-unitary multiple of a Jordan isomorphism. The proof establishes that entire one-parameter groups of unital spectral isometries are algebra automorphisms, then reduces the general case to the commutative case. The authors report that the result was formally verified in Lean.

Technology: OpenAI GPT-6 Astra

10 Jul 2026University of Cambridge; and collaborators

Multiscale machine-learning microscopy identifies robust paleomagnetic carriers in meteorite WIS 91600

A multiscale microscopy study of carbonaceous chondrite WIS 91600 used a deep-learning classifier to segment magnetic remanence-carrying ensembles and quantify their volume and texture. Combined chemical mapping, 3D nanotomography, and diffraction showed fine matrix-hosted magnetite has paleomagnetic recording potential comparable to magnetite framboids but is less susceptible to strong interactions; transient heating also reduced the contribution of matrix pyrrhotite through its vacancy-ordering state. This is one meteorite case study, and the mineralogical conclusions draw on the full microscopy and diffraction workflow, not AI alone.

Technology: UNET segmentation, SIGMA interactive machine-learning mineral analysis, and few-shot image segmentation

The announcement gives no metrics; it is not operational and depends on ERA5. Published in Nature.

17 Apr 2024University of Cambridge; US National Institutes of Health, National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases; A*STAR; Indiana University School of Medicine

Iterative machine learning identifies potent inhibitors of alpha-synuclein secondary nucleation

A structure-based iterative-learning workflow combined prior α-synuclein fibril-binding hits, a junction-tree variational autoencoder, a random-forest regressor and Gaussian-process residual model to rank compounds for repeated testing. Prospective iterations produced multiple molecules inhibiting seeded α-synuclein aggregation in vitro; lead I4.05 had a reported kinetic inhibitory constant (KIC50) of 0.52 μM (95% CI 0.45–0.59 μM), compared with extrapolated values of 18.2 μM for its parent and 36.4 μM for Anle-138b in the authors’ assay. Leads also showed activity in lipid-induced and spontaneous aggregation assays.

Technology: Iterative structure-based learning workflow using a junction-tree variational autoencoder, random-forest regressor and Gaussian-process residual model

26 Mar 2024University of Cambridge; Max Planck Institute for Astronomy; Gemini Observatory/NSF NOIRLab; INAF; collaborators

Three spectroscopically confirmed z≈6 quasars found through contrastive learning

A self-supervised contrastive-learning search of Dark Energy Survey imaging isolated a group of 11 sources that included seven known z≈6 quasars. Follow-up Gemini-South and archival NTT spectroscopy confirmed three additional quasars at redshifts 5.94–6.07. One source, J0109−5424, shows evidence of a foreground object and is a lensed-quasar candidate, but its multiple-image status requires high-resolution imaging.

Technology: Self-supervised contrastive-learning neural network trained on Dark Energy Survey imaging data

24 Oct 2023University of Cambridge; and Cambridge University Hospitals; Barts Health NHS Trust; Medical University of Vienna; collaborating institutions

Integrated radiogenomic machine learning predicts neoadjuvant chemotherapy response in metastatic ovarian cancer

The IRON ensemble combined baseline clinical data, CA-125, ctDNA and CT radiomics from primary and metastatic lesions to estimate tumor-volume change during neoadjuvant chemotherapy for high-grade serous ovarian carcinoma. In an independent 42-patient Barts cohort, the integrated model achieved AUC 0.78 for RECIST 1.1 response classification versus 0.47 for a clinical-only model; radiomics-containing scores significantly correlated with volume change in internal holdout. The study also measured spatial heterogeneity, with omental lesions showing better response than pelvic/ovarian disease.

Technology: IRON ensemble combining elastic net, support-vector regression and random forest
