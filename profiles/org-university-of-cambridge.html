Machine-learned density functional theory reveals molecular control of electrolyte screening

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Machine-learned neural classical density functional theory trained on grand-canonical Monte Carlo data enabled calculations of asymptotic screening behavior across increasingly molecularly detailed electrolyte models. The authors report that solvent attraction creates a monotonic decay mode, solvent polarity produces re-entrant behavior, and ionic architecture changes screening-length exponents, challenging a single universal scaling law. These are computational predictions for modelled systems; the reviewed paper reports no experimental validation of the new predictions.

29 Oct 2026University of Cambridge; California Institute of Technology

GPT-6 Astra resolves the spectral isometry conjecture for arbitrary unital C*-algebras

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The paper proves that every unital surjective complex-linear spectral isometry between unital C*-algebras is a Jordan isomorphism; without unitality, such a map is a central-unitary multiple of a Jordan isomorphism. The proof establishes that entire one-parameter groups of unital spectral isometries are algebra automorphisms, then reduces the general case to the commutative case. The authors report that the result was formally verified in Lean.

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A multiscale microscopy study of carbonaceous chondrite WIS 91600 used a deep-learning classifier to segment magnetic remanence-carrying ensembles and quantify their volume and texture.
