Aardvark Weather: end-to-end forecasting without a physical model

A single AI model replaces the entire forecasting chain: it starts from satellite and station observations and produces global and local forecasts in minutes on a desktop using about one-tenth of the data; the page says it beats the US GFS on many variables. The announcement gives no metrics; it is not operational and depends on ERA5. Published in Nature.
