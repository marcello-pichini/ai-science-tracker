AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2: silver-medal level at the Math Olympiad

The two systems solved 4 of 6 problems at IMO 2024, scoring 28 of 42 points, one point below the gold threshold. Problems were manually translated into formal language, and some required up to three days of computation instead of the official 4.5 hours.

Technology: AlphaProof (Lean) and AlphaGeometry 2
