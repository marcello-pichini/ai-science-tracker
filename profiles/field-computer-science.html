OpenAI model claims the matrix multiplication exponent is 9/4

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: OpenAI added to its openai/math repository manuscripts produced by an unnamed internal model claiming that the exponent of square matrix multiplication is exactly 9/4 over every field, with a lower bound of n^(9/4)/2 multiplication or division gates. The same release claims separations of Valiant's classes VF, VBP, VP and VNP (subsuming a VP ≠ VNP manuscript added on 23 November), circuit lower bounds for the permanent, the Erdős–Rado sunflower conjecture with an exponential bound, a strongly polynomial algorithm for linear programming (Smale's ninth problem) and a disproof of the polynomial Hirsch conjecture. The abstract describes the 9/4 upper bound as known; this record does not assess that statement against published bounds. No referee or independent review is reported.

Technology: OpenAI internal models

20 Nov 2026Utrecht University; University of Genoa

Transformer-assisted bibliometric analysis maps changes in Italian computer science

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A study combined learned co-authorship and text representations with topic clustering to analyze a roster of 1,229 Italian computer-science scholars and publication records from 1980–2025. It identified three model-derived periods spanning foundational work, computer vision/machine learning, and later applied AI/health informatics. An ancillary Genoa pilot introduced 15 recommended researcher pairs, with four joint projects reported within six months. The topic patterns depend on metadata and analytical thresholds, 44.2% of paper abstracts were missing, and the small uncontrolled pilot does not establish a causal collaboration benefit.

Technology: Node2vec, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence transformer, BERTopic/HDBSCAN, Falcon and Gemini topic-label generation
