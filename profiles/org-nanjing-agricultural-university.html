BroadAMP-GPT generated and experimentally tested broad-spectrum AMP_S13

BroadAMP-GPT combined transformer-based peptide generation, computational screening and experimental testing. The paper reports that 57% of AI-generated candidates showed potent activity against ESKAPE pathogens. AMP_S13 showed broad-spectrum antimicrobial activity, stability under tested conditions, low cytotoxicity and hemolysis, and efficacy in Galleria mellonella and a murine MRSA wound model. These preclinical findings do not establish human safety or efficacy; the 57% abstract metric is kept distinct from the separately reported assays of 14 synthesized candidates.

Technology: Transformer-based peptide generation with deep-learning-guided screening

  • Tested: The paper reports a 57% potent-activity rate among AI-generated candidates and describes AMP_S13 broad-spectrum activity, stability, low cytotoxicity/hemolysis, Galleria mellonella infection testing and a murine MRSA wound-infection model.
