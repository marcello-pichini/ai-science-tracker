26 Jun 2025Nanjing Agricultural University; Hainan University; China Pharmaceutical University

BroadAMP-GPT generated and experimentally tested broad-spectrum AMP_S13

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: BroadAMP-GPT combined transformer-based peptide generation, computational screening and experimental testing. The paper reports that 57% of AI-generated candidates showed potent activity against ESKAPE pathogens. AMP_S13 showed broad-spectrum antimicrobial activity, stability under tested conditions, low cytotoxicity and hemolysis, and efficacy in Galleria mellonella and a murine MRSA wound model. These preclinical findings do not establish human safety or efficacy; the 57% abstract metric is kept distinct from the separately reported assays of 14 synthesized candidates.

Technology: Transformer-based peptide generation with deep-learning-guided screening

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
BroadAMP-GPT generated peptide candidates with a transformer and applied deep-learning-guided screening before experimental validation; AMP_S13 is the named lead candidate in the reported assays.
Test and result
The paper reports a 57% potent-activity rate among AI-generated candidates and describes AMP_S13 broad-spectrum activity, stability, low cytotoxicity/hemolysis, Galleria mellonella infection testing and a murine MRSA wound-infection model. A separate cohort statement reports six of 14 synthesized candidates inhibiting both E. coli and S. aureus at MIC ≤16 μg/mL; these are distinct denominators/outcomes.
Limitation / still unknown
The results are laboratory and animal preclinical findings, not human efficacy or safety. The entry makes no claim that the peptide sequence was first disclosed here or is globally novel.
