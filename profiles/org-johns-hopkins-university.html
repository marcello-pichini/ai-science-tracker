5 Jun 2025Johns Hopkins University; Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory; Gdansk University of Technology

Generative machine learning guides experimental synthesis of LiZn₂Pt and NiPt₂Ga Heusler phases

Researchers combined a generative probabilistic crystal-graph model (PGCGM) with an ALIGNN stability predictor to propose and screen ternary structures, then used the model outputs to guide synthesis. They experimentally synthesized phase-pure LiZn₂Pt and NiPt₂Ga; the measured NiPt₂Ga adopts a tetragonal Heusler structure rather than the model’s cubic proposal, with DFT comparisons supporting its lower energy. They also synthesized LiZn₂Pd as a human-selected follow-up that was not among PGCGM’s candidate outputs. This is one paper-level AI-enabled synthesis workflow and three related compounds, not three separate records.

Technology: PGCGM generative Wasserstein GAN for ternary crystal structures; ALIGNN graph neural network for decomposition-enthalpy stability screening

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Researchers combined a generative probabilistic crystal-graph model (PGCGM) with an ALIGNN stability predictor to propose and screen ternary structures, then used the model outputs to guide synthesis.

LiZn₂Pt’s composition and Fm3̄m structure were already represented in Materials Project/OQMD training data, so its novelty is experimental realization rather than structure invention. The NiPt₂Ga experimental structure is tetragonal P4/mmm, differing from the cubic PGCGM proposal; the paper gives a DFT energy comparison. No superconductivity was observed, and magnetic ordering temperature for NiPt₂Ga was unresolved.
  • AI role: PGCGM generated candidate crystal structures and ALIGNN screened their predicted stability. Researchers used these model outputs to down-select and choose Heusler-family candidates for synthesis. The authors note that expert judgment remained necessary and that LiZn₂Pt’s composition/structure appeared in the model training databases; NiPt₂Ga’s experimentally realized tetragonal structure was not the exact cubic structure proposed by PGCGM.

24 May 2023Johns Hopkins University

Active learning maps wind-tunnel terrain equivalence

A learned surrogate selected automated wind-tunnel experiments that mapped terrain configurations with similar second-order wind statistics. Different terrain shapes could meet the study's equivalence criterion. This does not imply identical full turbulence fields or equal structural safety; higher-order statistics can differ. Estimated experiment savings are not a fully executed randomized comparison with alternative sampling.

Technology: Gaussian-process sequential active learning

21 Jul 2022Johns Hopkins University; and Bayesian Health

TREWS: early sepsis alerts evaluated in five hospitals

A machine-learning system monitored about 590,000 patients in five hospitals, flagging sepsis risk from medical records. In the studied cohort, timely clinical confirmation of alerts was associated with lower mortality, organ failure and length of stay. Observational study: the association does not show that AI caused better outcomes.

Technology: TREWS (Targeted Real-time Early Warning System)

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: prospective or independent check
  • Tested: A prospective multi-site cohort of 590,736 monitored patients (five hospitals) compared outcomes of sepsis alerts confirmed by a provider within 3 hours with outcomes otherwise, adjusting for severity.
  • Still unknown: The association is observational and does not show that the AI caused better outcomes.
  • AI role: Machine-learning early warning system (TREWS) flagged sepsis risk from medical records.
