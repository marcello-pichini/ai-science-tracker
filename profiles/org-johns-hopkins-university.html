25 Nov 2026Johns Hopkins University; Johns Hopkins School of Medicine

Deep learning predicts DNA damage response protein levels from routine slides

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Gated attention-based multiple-instance learning on frozen pathology foundation-model tile embeddings predicted reverse-phase protein array pathway scores from 1,673 TCGA haematoxylin-and-eosin whole-slide images covering 1,527 patients with breast, endometrial, cervical and ovarian cancer and uterine carcinosarcoma. For the 11-protein DNA damage composite the out-of-fold Spearman correlation was 0.43 against 0.31 for a cancer-type-mean baseline, about 0.29-0.30 within cancer type, and at most 0.00 with shuffled targets. Cross-validation was cancer-type-stratified and site-grouped. This is a moderate retrospective association in one public cohort: there is no external validation, no clinical or treatment-response outcome, and the authors list separating the signal from proliferation as future work, noting the cell-cycle score was predicted more strongly.

Technology: Gated attention-based multiple-instance learning over frozen UNI2-h and Virchow2 tile embeddings jointly regresses six RPPA pathway scores.

23 Nov 2026Johns Hopkins University

MAPLE matches reference diagnoses in a small breast-pathology slide cohort

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A November 23, 2026 bioRxiv preprint reports that MAPLE, a hierarchical pathology vision-language system, matched expert reference diagnoses for 15 breast whole-slide images from Johns Hopkins at both 20x and 40x magnification. The series comprised nine invasive carcinoma, three ductal carcinoma in situ and three benign cases; the system combined learned nucleus-based region selection, a pathology CLIP encoder and a Qwen3-14B-based language model. This small, single-institution retrospective test is not evidence of prospective diagnostic benefit or readiness for routine deployment; the paper also reports separate benchmark results.

Technology: MAPLE; AgenTile vision transformer; pathology CLIP; Qwen3-14B PathLLM

23 Oct 2026Johns Hopkins University; Imperial College London

Small neuron sets shifted cognitive-impairment answers in three audio models

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Researchers screened eight audio-language models on recordings and transcripts from existing cognitive-impairment datasets. In three of five models that met the analysis criteria, disabling small neuron sets shifted the models' answers more than disabling matched random sets. Separately, fitted readouts from other neurons recovered similar classification information. The experiments distinguish influence over the model's answer from where class information is represented. They do not establish clinical diagnostic performance.

Technology: Qwen3-Omni-30B; MiniCPM-o-4.5; Kimi-Audio; activation ablation

5 Jun 2025Johns Hopkins University; Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory; Gdansk University of Technology

Generative machine learning guides experimental synthesis of LiZn₂Pt and NiPt₂Ga Heusler phases

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Researchers combined a generative probabilistic crystal-graph model (PGCGM) with an ALIGNN stability predictor to propose and screen ternary structures, then used the model outputs to guide synthesis. They experimentally synthesized phase-pure LiZn₂Pt and NiPt₂Ga; the measured NiPt₂Ga adopts a tetragonal Heusler structure rather than the model’s cubic proposal, with DFT comparisons supporting its lower energy. They also synthesized LiZn₂Pd as a human-selected follow-up that was not among PGCGM’s candidate outputs. This is one paper-level AI-enabled synthesis workflow and three related compounds, not three separate records.

Technology: PGCGM generative Wasserstein GAN for ternary crystal structures; ALIGNN graph neural network for decomposition-enthalpy stability screening

The authors note that expert judgment remained necessary and that LiZn₂Pt’s composition/structure appeared in the model training databases; NiPt₂Ga’s experimentally realized tetragonal structure was not the exact cubic structure proposed by PGCGM.
Test and result

No superconductivity was observed, and magnetic ordering temperature for NiPt₂Ga was unresolved.
