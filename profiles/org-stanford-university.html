14 Aug 2025Stanford University and collaborators

Decoding inner speech with a brain-computer interface

In four people with severe motor and speech impairments, researchers found imagined-speech signals in the motor cortex and decoded them with machine learning. They also tested filters and a trigger phrase to limit unintended decoding. This is a proof of principle with implanted electrodes: free, rapid inner speech cannot be decoded accurately, and attempted speech remains more accurate.

Technology: Brain-computer interface and machine learning

24 Apr 2023Stanford University

Language models guide improvements to seven antibodies

Two rounds of laboratory evolution, using at most 20 variants per antibody, improved the affinity of four mature antibodies by up to sevenfold and three immature ones by up to 160-fold. Biochemical and pseudovirus tests; no demonstration of therapeutic benefit in humans.

Technology: Protein language models

19 Feb 2020Stanford University, MIT, and Toyota Research Institute

Closed-loop optimization of fast-charging battery protocols

A model estimating cycle life from the first 100 cycles, paired with Bayesian optimization, assessed 224 fast-charging protocols in 16 days; the authors estimate exhaustive search without early prediction would take over 500 days. It optimizes protocols, not new materials; only the abstract was reviewed.

Technology: Bayesian optimization with early prediction
