21 Oct 2022Collaborations Pharmaceuticals; University of Arizona

Prospective machine-learning screen identifies six previously unreported OATP1B1 inhibitors

Researchers trained and compared seven machine-learning classifiers for OATP1B1 transporter inhibition. In prospective CHO-cell testing, 15 of 19 compounds predicted active by SVC models inhibited the transporter; six (abamectin, asiaticoside, berbamine, doramectin, mobocertinib and umbralisib) were described as previously unreported OATP1B1 inhibitors.

Technology: Seven molecular-fingerprint machine-learning classifiers, with prospective selection by support vector classifier (SVC)

12 Jan 2021Collaborations Pharmaceuticals

Bayesian models prioritize compounds for Schistosoma mansoni assays

Eight stage/time-specific Bayesian classifiers trained on historical phenotypic measurements guided prospective selection of predicted active and inactive commercial compounds. In-vitro assays reported 48% and 34% hit fractions for somules and adults, followed by triage of five compounds. The selected-set fractions are not unbiased head-to-head screening rates; no animal cure, clinical mechanism, safety or efficacy is established.

Technology: Bayesian classifiers
