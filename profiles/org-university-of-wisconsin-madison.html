8 May 2025University of Wisconsin-Madison

Experimental feedback guides ESM-2 to brighter CreiLOV variants

Reinforcement Learning from eXperimental Feedback (RLXF) used a learned fluorescence reward model to align ESM-2 with measured CreiLOV sequence-function data. In the peer-reviewed study, the authors expressed 60 generated variants in E. coli and measured cellular fluorescence; six of ten aligned ESM-2 five-mutant designs exceeded CreiLOV fluorescence, and three aligned designs exceeded CreiLOV-T7S. The first bioRxiv version (posted 2025-05-08) already described experimentally enhanced fluorescence from RLXF-aligned designs; detailed measurements here are from the 2026 journal version. The earlier ICLR workshop comparison was computational and did not report new wet-lab fluorescence assays.

Technology: 650-million-parameter ESM-2 protein language model aligned by reinforcement learning from experimental feedback using a learned sequence-fluorescence reward model

4 Oct 2024University of Wisconsin-Madison

Iterative Gaussian process screening discovers selective aurein-derived antifungal peptides

An uncertainty-aware Gaussian process regression workflow iteratively selected 23 synthetic α/β-peptide analogues for synthesis and measurements of fungal inhibition and human-red-blood-cell hemolysis. One lead, peptide 6-4, was a measured variant of an existing aurein-derived template and achieved a selectivity index of 57.1 (60.9 using the study’s finer MIC interval) versus 1.2 for aurein 1.2. Its high selectivity was driven by low hemolysis; the result is a specific peptide-design and assay contribution, not a new peptide scaffold or evidence of therapeutic efficacy. The ChemRxiv v1 result was posted on 2024-10-04; the peer-reviewed journal article followed in 2025.

Technology: Iterative uncertainty-aware Gaussian process regression using molecular descriptors and measured antifungal/hemolysis labels

Lead 6-4 had a selectivity index of 57.1 (60.9 using the study’s finer MIC interval), versus 1.2 for aurein 1.2; the authors attribute the selectivity to reduced hemolysis.
  • Still unknown: Peptide 6-4 modifies a known aurein-derived template. The result is a measured sequence and assay outcome, not evidence of a new scaffold or therapeutic efficacy. The version-specific first public result is the 2024 ChemRxiv preprint; the journal article followed in 2025.
  • AI role: An uncertainty-aware Gaussian process regression model selected peptide variants for synthesis and assays, and measured outcomes were fed back into subsequent selection rounds.

17 Mar 2024University of Wisconsin-Madison

A biophysics-informed protein language model designs functional GFP variants

Mutational Effect Transfer Learning (METL) combines transformer pretraining on simulated protein biophysics with fine-tuning on experimental sequence–function data. Using only 64 experimental GFP examples, the authors designed 20 previously untested variants and measured their fluorescence; 16 showed measurable fluorescence, although none was brighter than wild-type GFP. The result demonstrates functional design under a small-data setup, not improved brightness over wild type. The result was present in the bioRxiv version posted 2024-03-17; the GEM@ICLR 2024 workshop paper provides the detailed 20-variant assay report, and the later journal version is the same study.

Technology: Mutational Effect Transfer Learning (METL), a transformer protein language model pretrained on simulated biophysical features and fine-tuned on experimental data

  • AI role: METL-Local was pretrained on simulated GFP sequence/biophysical data, fine-tuned using 64 experimental examples, and used with sequence optimization to select variants for wet-lab testing.

5 Oct 2021University of Wisconsin-Madison

Machine learning guides fatty acyl-ACP reductase engineering

A machine-learning-guided iterative design–test–learn workflow engineered fatty acyl-ACP reductase variants for microbial fatty alcohol production. Across ten rounds, the study reports over twice the production of the starting natural sequences under tested conditions; the top sequence also had enhanced catalytic rate on palmitoyl-ACP. These are laboratory results, not industrial-scale production. The journal article date is used here; the earlier preprint’s exact posting and result-version differences were not checked.

Technology: Machine-learning-guided protein engineering; Gaussian-process optimization
