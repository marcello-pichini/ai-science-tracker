Catalysts without iridium or ruthenium for acidic oxygen evolution

An AI-guided, human-supervised platform assessed 2,942 catalysts and identified complex palladium oxides. InMnPdOx maintained an overpotential below 0.5 V for more than 1,000 hours in sulfuric acid at 10 mA/cm². This is a preprint and laboratory result, without validation in a PEM electrolyzer.

Technology: Sequential learning, composition–property models, and LLMs
