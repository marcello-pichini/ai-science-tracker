Machine learning identifies new CYP2C9-inhibiting drugs and drug metabolites

A structure-, dynamics- and physicochemical-feature machine-learning approach predicted CYP2C9 inhibition and experimentally identified four drugs—vatalanib, piriqualone, ticagrelor and cloperidone—as strong inhibitors, plus sertindole, asapiprant, duvelisib and dasatinib as moderate inhibitors in reported assays. Vatalanib had a reported CYP2C9 inhibition IC50 of 0.067 μM. Separate metabolism assays characterized CYP2C9-produced metabolites for abemaciclib, cloperidone, vatalanib and tarafenacin.

Technology: Machine-learning inhibitor predictors combining CYP2C9 structural/dynamics information with inhibitor physicochemical properties
