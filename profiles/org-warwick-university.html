RAVEN pipeline validates 118 new TESS planets and vets over 2,000 candidates

A pipeline combining box least squares detection with machine-learning classifiers, trained on realistic simulations, searched 2.2 million TESS main-sequence stars in full-frame images (sectors 1 to 55). It newly validates 118 planets, 31 of them newly detected, and vets over 2,000 candidates, about 1,000 of them new. The machine-learning classifier is a validation tool rather than the detection method, and the paper is a preprint accepted for MNRAS; the peer-reviewed publication date was not confirmed.

Technology: RAVEN: box least squares detection with machine-learning vetting classifiers trained on simulations
