An LLM agent in an air-free robotic lab maps lithium halide spinel conductors

An April 2026 arXiv preprint reports 352 lithium-halide spinel samples, covering72% of 171 metal pairs drawn from 19 metals. The proportion meeting conductivity above 0.05 mS/cm and high spinel purity rose from 1.33% in the first 75 agent-proposed samples to 5.33% in the final 75. LLM agents selected experiments; characterization used X-ray diffraction and impedance spectroscopy with manual handling steps. These are within-study laboratory results.

Technology: A-Lab GPSS (LLM agent integrated into a robotic synthesis platform)

13 Dec 2025Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory; Joint BioEnergy Institute; Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory; Sandia National Laboratories; University of California, Berkeley

ML-guided CRISPRi combinations improve isoprenol production

An ensemble of machine-learning regressors recommended combinations of up to four CRISPRi guide RNAs during six design–build–test–learn cycles in engineered Pseudomonas putida. The paper reports an approximately fivefold isoprenol-titer improvement over its control for highlighted strains. It reports 347 unique arrays for the highlighted optimization and 472 unique strains overall; these are different denominators. A 2024 DOE meeting abstract describes an earlier campaign but does not quantify the later result; the journal article date is used without claiming first public disclosure. The finding is specific to this strain and laboratory workflow, not industrial fuel production.

Technology: Automated Recommendation Tool ensemble of machine-learning regressors

18 Apr 2025Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory; Department of Energy Agile BioFoundry; Joint BioEnergy Institute; Sandia National Laboratories; University of California, Berkeley; BCAM, Basque Center for Applied Mathematics

Active learning identifies salt-rich media for flaviolin production

An automated recommendation tool proposed culture-medium mixtures for engineered Pseudomonas putida across three semi-automated campaigns. The paper reports direct flaviolin-titer improvements of about 70% and 60% in two campaigns and a 350% process-yield improvement in a third, each versus its campaign starting medium. The spectroscopic proxy changed by different amounts, so proxy values are not substituted for direct quantification. Results are specific to the tested strain, media and objective and do not establish industrial performance. A preprint preceded the journal article, but its exact posting date and version-specific outcome set are unresolved; the journal date is used without asserting first disclosure.

Technology: Automated Recommendation Tool for active-learning medium optimization

29 Nov 2023Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory; and UC Berkeley

A-Lab: an autonomous laboratory for synthesizing inorganic materials

In the Nature paper, the autonomous lab made compounds from computed targets over 17 days of continuous operation. The original press release reported 41 of 58 (71%), while the author’s January 19, 2026 correction reports 36 of 57: on review, 36 of 40 claimed successes were confirmed, 4 were inconclusive from diffraction alone, and ‘new’ meant new to the platform, not to science. One compound was removed because it was in the training data.

Technology: A-Lab (autonomous lab with language models and active learning)

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: The autonomous lab attempted 58 target materials in 17 days of continuous operation and produced 41 novel compounds; failed syntheses were analyzed to suggest improvements.
  • Still unknown: The abstract does not name the characterization methods and states no limitations beyond the failed-synthesis analysis; the record notes an erratum published in January 2026.
  • AI role: A robotic platform used computation, literature data, machine learning and active learning to plan and interpret solid-state powder syntheses.
