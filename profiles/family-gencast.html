GenCast: ensemble weather forecasts with a diffusion model

A 0.25° model that outperformed ECMWF's ENS ensemble on 97.2% of 1,320 variable-and-lead-time combinations (99.8% beyond 36 hours) and produces a 15-day ensemble in about 8 minutes on a TPU. Physical models remain essential because they provide training data and initial conditions.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Not reported
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Forecasts were compared with ECMWF ENS on held-out 2019 data (trained up to 2018) across 1,320 variable and lead-time combinations; GenCast was more accurate on 97.2% of targets.
  • Still unknown: Forecasts are especially uncertain beyond a few days, the wind-power analysis is only a proof of principle, and physics-based models remain needed for training data and initial conditions.
  • AI role: A diffusion model generates an ensemble of 50 or more 15-day weather trajectories from the current weather state.

Evidence sources: https://deepmind.google/blog/gencast-predicts-weather-and-the-risks-of-extreme-conditions-with-sota-accuracy/
