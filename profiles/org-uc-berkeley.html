Barbarians at the Gate: AI-driven research for systems (ADRS)

An LLM-driven generate-evaluate-refine loop built on OpenEvolve produced systems algorithms in case studies, with the abstract reporting up to 5.0x runtime improvements or 50% cost reductions over baselines. Solutions are checked with simulators or real systems, which is the validation basis. The results are on specific case studies rather than a general claim, and the work is a preprint.

Technology: OpenEvolve (LLM-driven evolutionary code search, ADRS framework)

18 Apr 2025UC Berkeley; Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory; Google DeepMind

MOFGen: agentic AI generates MOF linkers and five AI-designed MOFs are synthesized

MOFGen, a multi-agent system combining language models, diffusion models and quantum-mechanical checks, generated hundreds of thousands of novel MOF structures and synthesizable linkers. The authors report that five AI-designed MOFs were synthesized through high-throughput experiments. Evidence caveat: the abstract describes the validation only generally, the structure counts are computational, and this is an arXiv preprint not confirmed as peer-reviewed.
