30 Sep 2026Shandong University; National Astronomical Observatories, Chinese Academy of Sciences; Yunding Technology

WDNet-guided DESI follow-up confirms 75 new white dwarfs and 7 cataclysmic variables

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: WDNet detected candidates in DESI Legacy Surveys DR9 images. The researchers cross-matched 18,242 photometric candidates with DESI DR1 spectra, obtaining 719 candidate spectra; manual visual inspection of those spectra identified 75 new white dwarfs and 7 new cataclysmic variables. These spectroscopic classifications, not the much larger image-candidate list, are the reported verified sample.

Technology: WDNet, a deep-learning object-detection network based on YOLOv8 with multi-scale feature fusion

Editorial relevance 47 astronomy Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
WDNet detected and localized white-dwarf candidates in DESI Legacy Surveys photometric images, thereby prioritizing objects for DESI spectral cross-match and manual spectral inspection. Human inspection of DESI spectra supplied the classification confirmation; the neural network did not itself confirm the new stellar classes.
Test and result
WDNet detected candidates in DESI Legacy Surveys DR9 images.
Limitation / still unknown
The authors confirmed 75 new WDs and 7 new CVs among the 719 candidates with DESI spectra, and explicitly note substantial contamination among remaining candidates and selection effects in the spectroscopic subsample. No atmosphere-model fitting was done for all identifications; the authors recommend further high-S/N spectroscopic modeling. The article's abstract/results/conclusion give inconsistent counts for the full detected/candidate catalog (18,242, 61,624, and 63,214 are all reported in different sections); this entry relies only on the consistent 719 spectral matches and 75+7 inspected confirmations. The full photometric catalog is not counted as confirmed discoveries.

12 Feb 2026Shandong University; National Astronomical Observatories, Chinese Academy of Sciences

Deep learning identifies 216 previously unrecognized Green Pea galaxies in DESI Legacy Survey imaging

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A MobileMamba-ECA image classifier searched DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys data and surfaced 775 Green Pea galaxy candidates. Cross-matching with DESI DR1 spectroscopy established 216 as previously unreported Green Pea galaxies: although spectra existed, the standard DESI pipeline had classified them only as generic galaxies. The study’s remaining 511 photometric-only objects are follow-up candidates and are not included in the confirmed count.

Technology: MobileMamba-ECA deep-learning classifier (Mamba state-space backbone with efficient channel attention)

Editorial relevance 53 astronomy Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Test and result
A MobileMamba-ECA image classifier searched DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys data and surfaced 775 Green Pea galaxy candidates.
Limitation / still unknown
The 216 newly identified galaxies have archival spectra and were classified with emission-line and BPT diagnostics; they were not all newly observed. The 511 sources without archival spectral coverage remain candidates and are excluded from this finding. The result is a newly recognized population/sample, not a claim that the galaxies themselves formed recently.

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.
