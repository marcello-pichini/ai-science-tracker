WeatherNext 3: a global AI model for weather forecasting

A model that learns from real-time geostationary satellite data and produces hourly forecasts, at resolutions down to 5 km for temperature and humidity (about five times finer than WeatherNext 2). For short-term precipitation, Google reports improvements of up to 60% over NASA's IMERG. These are ‘up to’ figures from Google's evaluations, with an independent ranking by Brightband.

6 Aug 2026Google DeepMind

WeatherNext Cyclones: forecasting cyclones one day earlier

Three-day forecasts match what earlier models achieved at two days: about 100 km trajectory error and 11 knots intensity error, compared with ECMWF-ENS and HWRF. The work is with the National Hurricane Center, CIRA, and the Met Office. The comparison uses historical 2023–2024 cyclones; why results are good at 28 km remains an open question.

19 May 2026Google DeepMind

WeatherNext and Hurricane Melissa: earlier warning in Jamaica

WeatherNext forecast a Category 5 landfall in Jamaica five days ahead with 80% probability, rising to nearly 100% at three days. According to the post, it was the best single model for track and intensity in the National Hurricane Center's 2025 verification report, which used it alongside its HAFS model. The post gives no numerical comparisons; rapid intensification remains very hard to predict.
