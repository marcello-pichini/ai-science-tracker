Diffusion-guided peptide design yields antimicrobial candidate Arcinin

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: ARCADIAMP combines iterative discrete diffusion generation, ESM2-based activity classifiers and sequence novelty filtering. Eight of ten experimentally screened candidates met the paper’s MIC threshold; Arcinin showed activity against tested pathogens and reduced bacterial burden in a mouse wound infection model. These are selected preclinical findings, not evidence of human safety or treatment efficacy; formal resistance-evolution testing remains future work.

Technology: Iterative discrete diffusion model with ESM2 classifiers and novelty filter

28 Apr 2025University of California, Davis; Durham University; Isaac Newton Group of Telescopes; Southern Astrophysical Research Telescope

CNN-guided spectroscopy confirms 28 new extremely metal-poor galaxies

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A three-stage convolutional-neural-network pipeline screened more than 7.7 million SDSS DR17 imaging sources lacking SDSS spectra, then selected targets for observation. Follow-up spectra of 45 candidates confirmed all as metal-poor galaxies, including 28 newly discovered extremely metal-poor systems; 18 have weak or undetected [N II] lines and oxygen-abundance upper limits.

Technology: Three-stage CNN characterization pipeline (metal-poor galaxy classifier, XMP classifier, and N2-index predictor)

Editorial relevance 60 physics Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The CNN pipeline ranked imaging-only sources using predicted spectral line ratios to nominate telescope targets; subsequent INT and SOAR spectroscopy established their metallicity classifications and confirmed 28 as new XMP galaxies.
Test and result
A three-stage convolutional-neural-network pipeline screened more than 7.7 million SDSS DR17 imaging sources lacking SDSS spectra, then selected targets for observation.
Limitation / still unknown
The 28 new XMP classifications derive from follow-up of 45 targeted candidates, not the full 7.7-million-source imaging search. For 18 galaxies with weak or absent [N II], oxygen abundance is reported as a 2-sigma upper limit; the paper also reports four possible low-metallicity AGN candidates, which are not counted as confirmed here.

21 Oct 2023University of California, Davis; NOAA Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory; European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts; Institut Pierre-Simon Laplace

Physics-guided machine learning links Southern Ocean regime shifts to a northward-shifting Antarctic Circumpolar Current

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Applying THOR to one eddy-permitting CM4 climate simulation, researchers detected a projected dynamical-regime shift where the Antarctic Circumpolar Current crosses the Pacific–Antarctic Ridge under the SSP5-8.5 scenario. Neural-network explanations and transport analysis attributed the modeled change to stronger wind stress shifting the current northward into less topographically constrained waters, where the flow intensifies. This is a model-based scenario result, not an observed future change.

Technology: THOR (Tracking global Heating with Ocean Regimes), using NEMI clustering, an ensemble of 50 multilayer perceptrons, SHAP and layer-wise relevance propagation

Editorial relevance 47 climate Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The clustering and neural-network ensemble identified and tracked local ocean dynamical regimes across model scenarios; SHAP and layer-wise relevance propagation highlighted wind-stress curl and bathymetry for follow-up physical analysis.
Test and result
Applying THOR to one eddy-permitting CM4 climate simulation, researchers detected a projected dynamical-regime shift where the Antarctic Circumpolar Current crosses the Pacific–Antarctic Ridge under the SSP5-8.5 scenario.
Limitation / still unknown
This is an arXiv preprint and computational climate-model analysis using CM4/MOM6, one model and the SSP5-8.5 scenario. The paper reports neural-network validation accuracy around 65–70%; it describes the model output and projected shift, not an observed real-world regime transition.

31 Oct 2022University of California, Davis; University of California, Santa Cruz; University of California, Berkeley

Semi-supervised machine learning identifies two strong gravitational lenses in the Deep Lens Survey

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A ResNetV2-based semi-supervised workflow ranked images from the 20-square-degree Deep Lens Survey and surfaced two Grade-A lens systems for follow-up. Keck spectroscopy secured a source redshift of z=1.81 for DLS212072337, while DLS432021848 has only a tentative single-line redshift but supporting high-resolution Hubble morphology; the paper counts both as lensing systems, with the latter less secure. The broader visually inspected list contains additional candidates, not confirmed lenses.

Technology: ResNetV2 neural network with semi-supervised MixMatch or Pi-Model and GAN-based image augmentation

Editorial relevance 53 physics Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Ranked galaxy-survey images for strong-lensing morphology; the top-ranked systems were then human-inspected and followed up spectroscopically/imaged to validate lensing.
Test and result
A ResNetV2-based semi-supervised workflow ranked images from the 20-square-degree Deep Lens Survey and surfaced two Grade-A lens systems for follow-up.
Limitation / still unknown
This is a paper-level result about a survey search and validated lens identification, not a claim that all 22 visually graded systems are confirmed. DLS212072337 has secure spectroscopic source and deflector redshifts; DLS432021848 has an ambiguous single emission line and is judged likely from HST morphology rather than conclusive spectroscopy. arXiv version 1 is a preprint; current full text was updated in 2026.

Knowledge-graph-guided discovery of antibiotic-resistance associations

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: KIDS combined an expert-curated knowledge graph with learned link-prediction models to rank gene–antibiotic resistance hypotheses for experiments. The authors report 93 validated associations involving 83 E. coli genes and 15 antibiotics, including six genes they describe as previously unknown resistance genes; five tested Salmonella homologs also showed resistance. These are laboratory gene–drug associations, not clinical validation. The article’s second-round percentage conflicts with its counts (29/90 is 32.2%, although the text states 28.8%). Count one study.

9 Jun 2021University of California, Davis

Machine learning identifies five synthesized Chevrel-phase tellurides with unusual channel-site occupancy

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An interpretable machine-learned descriptor for decomposition enthalpy screened more than 200,000 Chevrel-phase compositions, predicting 48,501 below the study’s statistical synthesizability cutoff. Researchers selected five previously unreported tellurides for synthesis and successfully made all five; experiments also confirmed the predicted preference for intercalant cations to occupy Chevrel-phase channels, a rare motif in this family. The predicted candidate list is much larger than the experimentally attempted and confirmed subset. The date is the journal paper’s online publication; UC Davis announced it on 24 June 2021.

Technology: SIFT descriptor-generation and interpretable machine-learning model Hδ

Editorial relevance 60 materials Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The SIFT method generated more than 560,000 candidate descriptors; an interpretable ML model trained against DFT-computed decomposition enthalpies assessed synthesizability and selected Chevrel-phase compositions for experimental follow-up.
Test and result
An interpretable machine-learned descriptor for decomposition enthalpy screened more than 200,000 Chevrel-phase compositions, predicting 48,501 below the study’s statistical synthesizability cutoff.
Limitation / still unknown
The original paper abstract reports five successful syntheses among five tellurides attempted and confirms a predicted channel-site occupancy trend. The other thousands of predicted candidates were not demonstrated as synthesized in this study. The result concerns a specific molybdenum-chalcogenide family.
