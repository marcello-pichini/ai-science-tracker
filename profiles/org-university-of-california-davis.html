28 Apr 2025University of California, Davis; Durham University; Isaac Newton Group of Telescopes; Southern Astrophysical Research Telescope

CNN-guided spectroscopy confirms 28 new extremely metal-poor galaxies

A three-stage convolutional-neural-network pipeline screened more than 7.7 million SDSS DR17 imaging sources lacking SDSS spectra, then selected targets for observation. Follow-up spectra of 45 candidates confirmed all as metal-poor galaxies, including 28 newly discovered extremely metal-poor systems; 18 have weak or undetected [N II] lines and oxygen-abundance upper limits.

Technology: Three-stage CNN characterization pipeline (metal-poor galaxy classifier, XMP classifier, and N2-index predictor)

21 Oct 2023University of California, Davis; NOAA Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory; European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts; Institut Pierre-Simon Laplace

Physics-guided machine learning links Southern Ocean regime shifts to a northward-shifting Antarctic Circumpolar Current

Applying THOR to one eddy-permitting CM4 climate simulation, researchers detected a projected dynamical-regime shift where the Antarctic Circumpolar Current crosses the Pacific–Antarctic Ridge under the SSP5-8.5 scenario. Neural-network explanations and transport analysis attributed the modeled change to stronger wind stress shifting the current northward into less topographically constrained waters, where the flow intensifies. This is a model-based scenario result, not an observed future change.

Technology: THOR (Tracking global Heating with Ocean Regimes), using NEMI clustering, an ensemble of 50 multilayer perceptrons, SHAP and layer-wise relevance propagation

31 Oct 2022University of California, Davis; University of California, Santa Cruz; University of California, Berkeley

Semi-supervised machine learning identifies two strong gravitational lenses in the Deep Lens Survey

A ResNetV2-based semi-supervised workflow ranked images from the 20-square-degree Deep Lens Survey and surfaced two Grade-A lens systems for follow-up. Keck spectroscopy secured a source redshift of z=1.81 for DLS212072337, while DLS432021848 has only a tentative single-line redshift but supporting high-resolution Hubble morphology; the paper counts both as lensing systems, with the latter less secure. The broader visually inspected list contains additional candidates, not confirmed lenses.

Technology: ResNetV2 neural network with semi-supervised MixMatch or Pi-Model and GAN-based image augmentation

9 Jun 2021University of California, Davis

Machine learning identifies five synthesized Chevrel-phase tellurides with unusual channel-site occupancy

An interpretable machine-learned descriptor for decomposition enthalpy screened more than 200,000 Chevrel-phase compositions, predicting 48,501 below the study’s statistical synthesizability cutoff. Researchers selected five previously unreported tellurides for synthesis and successfully made all five; experiments also confirmed the predicted preference for intercalant cations to occupy Chevrel-phase channels, a rare motif in this family. The predicted candidate list is much larger than the experimentally attempted and confirmed subset. The date is the journal paper’s online publication; UC Davis announced it on 24 June 2021.

Technology: SIFT descriptor-generation and interpretable machine-learning model Hδ

  • Still unknown: The original paper abstract reports five successful syntheses among five tellurides attempted and confirms a predicted channel-site occupancy trend. The other thousands of predicted candidates were not demonstrated as synthesized in this study. The result concerns a specific molybdenum-chalcogenide family.
  • AI role: The SIFT method generated more than 560,000 candidate descriptors; an interpretable ML model trained against DFT-computed decomposition enthalpies assessed synthesizability and selected Chevrel-phase compositions for experimental follow-up.
