12 Nov 2026MIT; Imperial College London

Unrealistic emissions scenarios help AI climate models prepare for unfamiliar futures

MIT researchers with Imperial College London designed a small synthetic set of deliberately unrealistic emissions scenarios to train climate emulators, which normally need six to eight full Earth system model runs that each take months. The method needs only one model run, and the emulator predicted unseen scenarios and local pollutant-control effects where traditional emulators failed. It was validated with a simple climate model rather than a full Earth system model, and the article reports no quantitative accuracy metrics.

3 Feb 2026MIT

First-principles neural network finds crystallization of fractional quantum Hall liquids

A self-attention neural-network wavefunction (MagNet), trained only by minimising the microscopic Hamiltonian of interacting electrons in a magnetic field, finds both topological liquid and electron-crystal ground states across a range of Landau-level mixing. The authors present it as a method that discovers correlated phases without assumptions. This is an arXiv preprint with computational results only, not yet checked experimentally or peer reviewed.

3 Oct 2025MIT

Enterololin: AI helps clarify an antibiotic and its mechanism

An AI screen of over 10,000 molecules identified enterololin, and DiffDock predicted its binding to LolCDE within minutes, confirmed by resistant E. coli mutants, RNA-seq, and CRISPR. The mechanism was clarified in about six months versus 18–24 with traditional methods. In mice, in Crohn's-like inflammation models, healing was faster than with vancomycin. Early stage; no human testing.

An autonomous platform speeds research on polymer mixtures

The platform tests up to 700 mixtures a day (96 at a time), guided by a genetic algorithm: hundreds of mixtures outperformed the individual components, and the best retained 73% enzyme activity, 18% more than the best component. Application is limited to enzyme thermal stability, replenishment is still manual; published in Matter.

20 Jan 2023MIT; Mass General Cancer Center and Chang Gung Memorial Hospital

Sybil: lung-cancer risk prediction up to six years from a low-dose CT

The model predicts risk from a single CT with a six-year C-index of 0.75 (NLST), 0.81 (MGH) and 0.80 (CGMH), and a one-year ROC area between 0.86 and 0.94. Retrospective validation on three independent datasets; trained only on smokers, with prospective testing and testing in nonsmokers as next steps.

A model trained on volunteer recordings recognized 98.5% of coughs associated with confirmed infections in the test reported by MIT. This is an early result on data collected online, with health status also self-reported; it is not a diagnosis or prospective clinical validation.
