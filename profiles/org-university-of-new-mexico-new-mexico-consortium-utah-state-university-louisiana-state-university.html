Simulated training data locates archaeological tar-kiln-like sites in Louisiana, field check finds WWII features

Machine learning models trained on simulated landscape data flagged 9 of 12 known targets (model 1) and all 12 (model 2, with 686 false positives among 709 remaining predictions) in Kisatchie National Forest, Louisiana; a model trained on real South Carolina tar kilns found 11 of 12. Field visits to 11 targets and augering of 2 found none of the expected tar-kiln features, and the authors concluded the structures more likely relate to a WWII military training site. Evidence caveat: only 12 known targets, and field validation is qualitative.

Technology: Machine learning classifiers trained on simulated archaeological sites (with a real tar-kiln-trained comparison model)
