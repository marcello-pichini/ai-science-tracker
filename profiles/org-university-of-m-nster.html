Deep-learning denoising reveals new short-period seismic observations of known ultra-low-velocity zones

A supervised deep-learning denoising autoencoder exposed previously undocumented short-period S-diffracted wave observations associated with several known ultra-low-velocity zones (ULVZs). It also revealed four Hawaiian-ULVZ S-diff post-cursor records at Japan F-net stations from East Pacific Rise earthquakes, providing ray paths with mean azimuth about 78° from earlier North American paths. These new cross-cutting observations add constraints on ULVZ geometry and frequency/azimuth-dependent waveforms, but the paper does not present a new ULVZ detection or a completed structural inversion.

Technology: Supervised deep-learning denoising autoencoder (DeepSeisDAE)

27 Mar 2024University of Münster; German Competence Network Multiple Sclerosis; Max Delbrück Center; Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin; collaborating German and international centers

Unsupervised clustering identifies three blood immune endophenotypes associated with distinct multiple-sclerosis trajectories

In two multicenter longitudinal cohorts of early multiple sclerosis (discovery n=309; validation n=232), unsupervised clustering of high-dimensional flow-cytometry and serum-proteomics profiles identified three peripheral-blood immune endophenotypes. Follow-up associated the groups with different trajectories of inflammation versus early structural damage. In the endophenotype-3 subgroup, patients subsequently treated with interferon beta had higher four-year progression and MRI activity than patients receiving other therapies.

Technology: Unsupervised clustering of high-dimensional flow-cytometry and serum-proteomics profiles (the reviewed abstract does not name the clustering algorithm)

  • Still unknown: The source reports cohort associations, not randomized treatment effects. The higher progression and MRI activity among endophenotype-3 patients treated with interferon beta may be affected by treatment selection and other confounding; it does not establish that interferon beta caused worse outcomes or that the signature can guide care. The validation cohort is an independent patient cohort within the study, not independent replication by another research group. The reviewed abstract does not provide the clustering algorithm name or establish prospective clinical utility.
  • AI role: The researchers combined flow-cytometry immune measurements and serum-protein profiles, then used unsupervised clustering to define three immune endophenotypes in the discovery cohort and assess them in an independent multicenter validation cohort. The clustering generated the patient groups whose longitudinal clinical and paraclinical trajectories were compared; the repository abstract does not specify the algorithm name.
