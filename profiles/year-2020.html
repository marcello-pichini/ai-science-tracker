23 Dec 2020Carnegie Mellon University

Autonomous machine-learning experiments identify a dual-anion aqueous sodium electrolyte with a wider stability window

A robotic electrochemistry stand coupled to Bayesian optimization tested 140 aqueous electrolyte formulas over 40 hours and selected a non-intuitive sodium mixture of NaClO4 and NaNO3. On platinum, the blend had a wider electrochemical stability window than the NaClO4 baseline despite lower total salt concentration; follow-up tests reported a 24 mV increase in the OER potential at the stated leakage-current criterion and 58% lower current density at 2 V. The result is a measured electrolyte formulation under the paper’s electrochemical protocols, not evidence of a commercial battery or field-ready cell.

Technology: Dragonfly Bayesian optimization software coupled to the Otto robotic electrochemistry test stand

6 Dec 2020Princeton University; DeepMind; New York University; Flatiron Institute; Carnegie Mellon University

AI-derived symbolic formula improves dark-matter halo overdensity estimates in a cosmological simulation

A graph neural network trained on a detailed N-body cosmological dark-matter simulation was distilled by symbolic regression into an analytic estimator for a halo’s overdensity from its mass and the masses, positions and velocities of nearby halos. On the analyzed simulation snapshot, the new expression reduced mean absolute error to 0.0882, compared with 0.121 for the authors’ existing hand-designed estimator. The formula’s mass-weighted neighborhood term decreases with separation and includes dependence on the central halo’s mass.

Technology: Sparse-representation Graph Neural Network followed by Eureqa genetic-programming symbolic regression

FermiNet: quantum physics of molecules from first principles

FermiNet approximates molecular ground-state electron wavefunctions from first principles. Against the coupled-cluster reference, it recovered 99.8% of the correlation energy beyond a single Slater determinant for the smallest molecules and at least 97% for bicyclobutane (30 electrons). The source page was updated on 22 August 2024 with a separate excited-state study; that later study's carbon-dimer error is not a 2020 result.
