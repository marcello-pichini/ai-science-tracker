Autonomous X-ray mapping reveals previously unseen block-copolymer morphologies, including a nanoscale ladder

An autonomous experiment planner selected synchrotron X-ray measurements across a combinatorial block-copolymer blend sample. The resulting map and targeted electron microscopy revealed previously unreported alternating, skew/bilayer, and ladder-like self-assembled morphologies, and the authors infer a general mechanism of local subdomain partitioning.

Technology: gpCAM autonomous experimentation framework for selecting high-value measurements
