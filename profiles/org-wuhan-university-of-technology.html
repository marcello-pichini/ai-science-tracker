13 Oct 2026Wuhan University of Technology; Institute of Seismology, China Earthquake Administration; collaborators

Dense-array seismicity analysis infers a buried fault beneath the Three Gorges Reservoir area

A two-phase dense-array catalog processed with the LOC-FLOW machine-learning workflow revealed microseismicity in three zones and shallow (0–6 km) and deep (6–15 km) layers near the northern Xiannvshan Fault Zone. A steeply dipping seismic band is interpreted as a previously hidden western extension of the Tianyangping Fault; the authors also propose fluid-permeable connections between depth layers. The enhanced catalog detects over an order of magnitude more events than the regional network catalog for the same period.

Technology: LOC-FLOW machine-learning seismic catalog workflow
