12 May 2026Southern University of Science and Technology; Institute of Geomechanics, Chinese Academy of Geological Sciences; ETH Zürich

Machine-learning earthquake catalog reveals scale-dependent fault controls at Gonghe geothermal site

A machine-learning workflow catalogued 7,346 microearthquakes (ML −2.0 to 2.18) recorded during 2021 stimulation and circulation tests at China’s Gonghe enhanced-geothermal-system site. The catalog resolves a ring-shaped distribution with four clusters and varying faulting styles. The authors infer that site-scale seismicity reactivated mapped, pre-existing natural faults; at borehole scale, the principal seismogenic zone coincides with strong SHmax rotation. Stress modeling estimates a low fault-friction coefficient near 0.23 (95% CI 0.19–0.48) and the low-to-moderate fluid overpressure needed for slip.

Technology: Machine-learning earthquake-detection and cataloging workflow (model details are not stated in the publisher abstract)

7 Jul 2025Southern University of Science and Technology; University of New Mexico; U.S. Geological Survey

PhaseNet catalog reveals a 2023 injection-linked earthquake cluster in the Raton Basin

Applying PhaseNet-based phase picking to eight years of recordings from eight broadband stations, the study built a roughly 96,000-event Raton Basin catalog (local magnitudes −1 to 4.3) and resolved a previously unreported 2023 cluster with a Mw 4.3 event. Nearby wells had elevated injection volumes for about a year before the cluster; analysis of relocated events and 90 full centroid moment tensors also supports basement-rooted normal faults and possible elevated pore pressure. The authors interpret injection as a likely influence, not a demonstrated sole cause.

Technology: PhaseNet deep neural-network seismic phase picker in the LOC-FLOW workflow
