4 Dec 2025Google DeepMind and Michigan State University

Heat-resistant crops: a hybrid photosynthesis enzyme

At Michigan State University, AlphaFold predicted the 3D structure of glycerate kinase in a crop and a heat-loving alga. Replacing three flexible loops with rigid loops from the alga produced a hybrid that remained stable up to 65 °C. The enzyme has not yet been tested in whole plants.

Technology: AlphaFold

25 Nov 2025Google DeepMind and University of Missouri

The structure of the LDL protein apoB100

At the University of Missouri, Zachary Berndsen and Keith Cassidy used AlphaFold to predict the structure of apoB100, the scaffold of LDL particles, and refined it using cryo-electron microscopy data. The result is a cage-like shell with a ribbon belt. The post gives no figures such as resolution and no clinical validation.

25 Nov 2025Google DeepMind and Norwegian University of Life Sciences

Healthier bees: AlphaFold predicts vitellogenin's structure

With the Norwegian University of Life Sciences, predicted structures of honeybee vitellogenin were confirmed by electron microscopy in July 2025; AlphaFold 3 helped study its interactions with other molecules. Early evidence suggests breeding times for resilient bees could fall from months to weeks, a still preliminary claim.

Technology: AlphaFold, AlphaFold 3

19 Jan 2023University of Toronto, Insilico Medicine and collaborators

A liver-cancer target and molecule identified with Pharma.AI

Pharma.AI combined PandaOmics, Chemistry42 and an AlphaFold-predicted structure to identify a hepatocellular-carcinoma target and produce a hit molecule within 30 days of target selection, synthesizing seven compounds. A second round produced a more potent hit; an early Chemical Science study, with no clinical efficacy demonstrated.

Technology: Pharma.AI, PandaOmics, Chemistry42 and AlphaFold

21 Sep 2022WEHI and Google DeepMind

AlphaFold helps interpret activation of PINK1, a Parkinson’s-linked protein

After experimentally determining different forms of PINK1, the WEHI group used AlphaFold to model the human protein and its dimer, obtaining structures consistent with experimental results. The predictions also helped interpret how mutations associated with early-onset Parkinson’s may affect dimerization; this does not amount to a validated therapy.

Technology: AlphaFold

28 Jul 2022Google DeepMind and EMBL-EBI

AlphaFold Database: over 200 million structures

The predicted-protein-structure archive, maintained with EMBL-EBI, grew from nearly 1 million to over 200 million structures, covering practically all cataloged proteins, with free access and bulk download from Google Cloud. More than 500,000 researchers in 190 countries had already used it. These are predictions, not experimental measurements.

10 Jun 2022EMBL

Model of the human nuclear pore with AlphaFold2 and electron microscopy

AlphaFold2 and ColabFold, combined with cryo-tomography and cryo-electron microscopy, produced a model of the human nuclear pore complex (about 30 proteins, about 1,000 copies, 120 MDa) covering over 90% of the complex, versus 46% for previous models. Flexible parts remain unresolved; a complete dynamic model is future work.

Technology: AlphaFold2 and ColabFold

22 Jul 2021Google DeepMind and EMBL-EBI

AlphaFold Protein Structure Database: first 350,000 models

Predicted structures for the human proteome (about 20,000 proteins) and 20 other organisms were published, over 350,000 models in total, hosted free by EMBL-EBI. DeepMind announced an expansion to over 100 million structures. These are predictions that complement experimental methods. Note: the page header says July 22, 2022, but the text calls CASP14 “last December” and the 2022 post refers to it as a 2021 release; date corrected to 2021.

30 Nov 2020Google DeepMind

AlphaFold wins CASP14 for protein folding

AlphaFold achieved a median GDT score of 92.4 across the target set (about 1.6 Å mean error) and 87.0 on the hardest targets, which organizers judged a solution to the problem of single-protein structures. DeepMind notes that protein complexes and interactions with other molecules remain open challenges.

Technology: AlphaFold 2

4 Mar 2020Google DeepMind

Predicted structures of SARS-CoV-2 proteins

DeepMind openly shared AlphaFold predictions, without waiting for review, for six poorly studied viral proteins (membrane, ORF3a, Nsp2, Nsp4, Nsp6, and the C-terminal domain of papain-like protease). They were not experimentally verified; an ORF3a experimental structure deposited in the PDB in June 2020 matched the second predicted model well.

Technology: AlphaFold
