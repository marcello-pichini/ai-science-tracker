Multicenter targeted metabolomics and machine learning identify a six-metabolite rheumatoid-arthritis diagnostic panel

Across seven cohorts and five medical centers, targeted metabolomics confirmed six metabolites—imidazoleacetic acid, ergothioneine, N-acetyl-L-methionine, 2-keto-3-deoxy-D-gluconic acid, 1-methylnicotinamide and dehydroepiandrosterone sulfate—as a panel for classifying rheumatoid arthritis against healthy controls and osteoarthritis. LASSO selected the six features, and logistic regression, random forest and XGBoost models were externally evaluated in geographically distinct cohorts; reported AUCs were 0.8375–0.9280 for RA versus healthy controls and 0.7340–0.8181 for RA versus osteoarthritis in three validation cohorts.

Technology: LASSO feature selection and logistic regression, random forest, support vector machine and XGBoost classifiers applied to untargeted and targeted metabolomics

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Across seven cohorts and five medical centers, targeted metabolomics confirmed six metabolites—imidazoleacetic acid, ergothioneine, N-acetyl-L-methionine, 2-keto-3-deoxy-D-gluconic acid, 1-methylnicotinamide and dehydroepiandrosterone sulfate—as a panel for classifying rheumatoid arthritis against healthy controls and osteoarthritis.
  • Still unknown: This is a cross-sectional case–control diagnostic study, not prospective screening or evidence of improved patient outcomes. The authors note that disease activity and treatment effects were not assessed, so prognostic and treatment-monitoring uses are not established. Performance varied across cohorts and was lower for RA-versus-OA than RA-versus-healthy-control classification; stronger platform-specific than sample-type effects were observed. Some mechanistic relevance of the six metabolites remains uncertain.
  • AI role: Machine-learning feature selection narrowed targeted-validated metabolites to a six-analyte panel, and supervised classifiers combined the measurements to distinguish RA from healthy controls and osteoarthritis in multiple independent cohorts. Targeted mass spectrometry provided absolute quantification; the models produced the reported cross-cohort diagnostic discrimination.
