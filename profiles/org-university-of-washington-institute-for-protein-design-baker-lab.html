3 Dec 2025University of Washington Institute for Protein Design (Baker lab)

RFdiffusion2 scaffolds enzyme active sites at atomic level

RFdiffusion2 scaffolded all 41 active sites in a diverse benchmark, compared with 16 for previous methods. Designs were tested experimentally, with fewer than 96 sequences needed per case to find active enzymes across three catalytic mechanisms. The journal abstract's three-mechanism count conflicts with a five-reaction claim in the Institute for Protein Design announcement, so the abstract should be treated as authoritative.

3 Dec 2025University of Washington Institute for Protein Design (Baker lab)

Computational design of zinc metallohydrolases with RFdiffusion2

RFdiffusion2 designed zinc metallohydrolases from quantum-chemistry-derived active-site geometries. The first round of 96 designs produced an enzyme with catalytic efficiency of 16,000 M-1 s-1, and a second round of 96 yielded three more highly active enzymes, with k/K up to 53,000 M-1 s-1. Crystal structures (PDB 9PYJ, 9PYL) were determined, but the evidence covers one designed enzyme family.

Technology: RFdiffusion2 with quantum-chemistry active-site models
