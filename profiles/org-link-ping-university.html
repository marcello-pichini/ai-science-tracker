Machine-learning mining uncovers hidden MXenes, including 11 new rare-earth MXenes

The journal abstract reports that machine-learning-assisted database mining identified 33 previously synthesized but unrecognized MXene candidates. Guided by that search, the researchers synthesized seven multilayer MXenes and realized 11 previously unexplored rare-earth-based MXenes. Experiments and modeling suggested semiconducting behavior and diverse magnetic states. Review is limited to the abstract and publication/affiliation metadata.

Technology: Machine-learning-assisted database mining for MXene candidates

30 Oct 2023Linköping University; University of Skövde; Karolinska Institutet and Karolinska University Hospital; collaborating Swedish institutions

Machine-learning models identify cerebrospinal-fluid protein signatures for multiple-sclerosis activity and disability progression

Using Olink proteomics of 1,463 proteins in CSF and plasma from two prospective Swedish cohorts, the study trained prediction models for multiple-sclerosis diagnosis, 2-year disease activity and long-term disability worsening. Lower CSF neurofilament light (NfL) predicted absence of disease activity at two years (replication AUC 0.77). A stepwise model using 11 CSF proteins (CXCL13, LTA, FCN2, ICAM3, LY9, SLAMF7, TYMP, CHI3L1, FYB1, TNFRSF1B and NfL) predicted normalized age-related MS severity; subgroup classification AUCs in the replication cohort ranged from 0.88 to 0.90.

Technology: Stepwise logistic regression for diagnosis and disease activity; stepwise linear regression for long-term disability severity

The plasma severity model had only 12 eligible replication samples and performed less convincingly. These are cohort-level results, not a clinically validated diagnostic assay or independent replication by another research group.
  • AI role: The study used statistical differential-expression testing to select disease-associated candidate proteins, then fitted logistic-regression models to predict MS diagnosis and 2-year disease activity and a linear-regression model to predict long-term normalized age-related MS severity. Stepwise feature selection produced the protein combinations; the frozen models were evaluated in a separate prospective cohort.
