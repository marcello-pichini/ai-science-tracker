25 Oct 2026Nanyang Technological University; Technical University of Darmstadt; Basis Research Institute; Harvard University; Cornell University

Successful human puzzle players voiced qualitative explanations more often than language agents

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In an ARC-AGI-3 trajectory study, qualitative descriptions appeared in 72 of 144 successful human runs and 17 of 119 successful runs from three agent systems. The human data came from think-aloud recordings and the model data from agent traces. The comparison measures recorded descriptions in successfully completed games.

Technology: GPT-5.6 Sol assisted analysis; ARC-AGI-3 agent traces

Family GPT-5: profile · models page

Editorial relevance 40 computer science PreprintPreprint Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
GPT-5.6 Sol implemented trigger detectors and annotated cognition from aligned transcripts and game traces. Learned agent trajectories supplied matched-task comparison material.
Test and result
The authors collected 144 human trajectories across 64 participants and 25 games, collected 250 publicly released model trajectories, and analyzed successful subsets. They spot-checked parts of the model-assisted annotation.
Limitation / still unknown
Differences in what humans say aloud and what agents write or log limit claims about underlying cognitive representations.

28 May 2024Nanyang Technological University

ML-guided mechanosynthesis yields a lead-free photocatalyst

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Machine-learning-guided synthesis and testing identified a lead-free metal-halide perovskite for oxidative alkene cleavage. The publisher reports an 80% benzoic-acid yield under the study’s conditions, compared with 6% for the initial Cs2AgBiBr6 benchmark. This is a formulation- and reaction-specific laboratory result, not universal photocatalytic performance or industrial deployment.

Technology: Machine-learning-assisted materials selection and iterative synthesis
