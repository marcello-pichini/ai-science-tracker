ML-guided mechanosynthesis yields a lead-free photocatalyst

Machine-learning-guided synthesis and testing identified a lead-free metal-halide perovskite for oxidative alkene cleavage. The publisher reports an 80% benzoic-acid yield under the study’s conditions, compared with 6% for the initial Cs2AgBiBr6 benchmark. This is a formulation- and reaction-specific laboratory result, not universal photocatalytic performance or industrial deployment.

Technology: Machine-learning-assisted materials selection and iterative synthesis
