25 Nov 2026Johns Hopkins University; Johns Hopkins School of Medicine

Deep learning predicts DNA damage response protein levels from routine slides

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Gated attention-based multiple-instance learning on frozen pathology foundation-model tile embeddings predicted reverse-phase protein array pathway scores from 1,673 TCGA haematoxylin-and-eosin whole-slide images covering 1,527 patients with breast, endometrial, cervical and ovarian cancer and uterine carcinosarcoma. For the 11-protein DNA damage composite the out-of-fold Spearman correlation was 0.43 against 0.31 for a cancer-type-mean baseline, about 0.29-0.30 within cancer type, and at most 0.00 with shuffled targets. Cross-validation was cancer-type-stratified and site-grouped. This is a moderate retrospective association in one public cohort: there is no external validation, no clinical or treatment-response outcome, and the authors list separating the signal from proliferation as future work, noting the cell-cycle score was predicted more strongly.

Technology: Gated attention-based multiple-instance learning over frozen UNI2-h and Virchow2 tile embeddings jointly regresses six RPPA pathway scores.

Machine learning prioritises KHDC4, tested as a colon cancer driver in cells and mice

Finding: Single-cell RNA-seq differential expression and univariate Cox screening on TCGA colon adenocarcinoma (428 patients) were followed by several machine learning methods (including LASSO, Boruta, random survival forests and XGBoost), whose intersection gave five prognostic genes; KHDC4 contributed across all algorithms. 25 Nov 2026University of Cambridge

Independent UK test of eight mammography AI algorithms as second reader and triage

Source check: Source checked (abstract only). Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Eight commercial deep learning breast cancer detection algorithms from five vendors were evaluated retrospectively on 22,778 mammograms from two UK screening sites (Cambridge, Norwich; 2017) with 3-year follow-up. Standalone AUCs ranged 0.82-0.91, with one algorithm inferior to all others. Paired with the first human reader, all but one were noninferior in sensitivity to double reading (64.7-67.4% vs 67.9%) with superior specificity (97.5% vs 97.1%); low-suspicion triage ruled out 37.4-65.7% of mammograms while missing at most 1.0% of screen-detected cancers. Gains came with more arbitration, and the study is retrospective; prospective workflow effects were not measured.

Technology: Commercial deep learning breast cancer detection algorithms scoring digital screening mammograms.

24 Nov 2026University Medical Center Utrecht; Meta VCI Map consortium; Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf; Research Centre Jülich

Learned lesion-network models identify shared and specific stroke cognition patterns

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A November 24, 2026 preprint analyzed 3,034 patients across 13 stroke cohorts using learned models of lesion anatomy, network connectivity and clinical characteristics. Structural lesion connectivity supplied most of the gain beyond clinical information and infarct size, with left anterior thalamic connectivity consistently informative across cognitive outcomes. Benefits persisted for several outcomes when entire cohorts were held out. The results support a bounded lesion-in-context account of cognitive impairment, without establishing causal circuitry or clinical readiness.

Technology: Stacked ridge classifiers and regularized logistic meta-learner; interpretable feature ablation and cross-cohort prediction
