Machine learning guides Fe–Mn–Ni catalysts for NOx reduction

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An artificial neural network trained on literature data and a genetic algorithm helped identify Fe–Mn–Ni catalyst compositions for ammonia selective catalytic reduction. Researchers synthesized and tested model-guided candidates and report good NOx conversion for Fe₀.₃₁₉Mn₀.₂₀₆Ni₀.₄₇₅ over the tested temperature range. The work combines model selection, human screening and controlled experiments; the ratio is not a complete oxide formula and does not demonstrate industrial exhaust performance. The article text differs on whether the workflow involved two or four iterations, so this summary avoids an iteration count.

Technology: Artificial neural network and genetic algorithm
