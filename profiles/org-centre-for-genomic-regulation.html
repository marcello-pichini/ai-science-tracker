AI-designed enhancers control gene expression in healthy mammalian cells

CRG researchers measured more than 64,000 synthetic enhancers across seven stages of blood-cell development and found that enhancer combinations can activate genes in one cell type but repress them in another. The AI model used those measurements to design previously unseen regulatory DNA sequences that drove the intended cell-selective reporter expression in mouse blood cells.

Technology: Machine-learning enhancer design model developed by Lars Velten's group
