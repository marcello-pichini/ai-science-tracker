NowcastNet: extreme-precipitation forecasts up to three hours ahead

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A model combining neural networks and physical constraints forecasts precipitation up to three hours ahead; 62 meteorologists preferred it in 71% of 2,400 evaluated events. Tsinghua reports it was already deployed on the National Meteorological Center’s operational SWAN 3.0 platform. Expert preference does not measure reduced damage or guarantee accuracy for every event.

AI contribution
A neural network combined with physics-based precipitation evolution forecasts extreme precipitation up to three hours ahead from radar data.
Test and result
62 forecasters from 23 meteorological stations ran retrospective and prospective checks on about 2,400 extreme-precipitation events and ranked NowcastNet first in 71% of cases against leading methods.
Limitation / still unknown
No limitations of NowcastNet are stated; the abstract reports no verification scores beyond expert preference, and the page says the team plans to extend the approach to other physics problems.
