Transformer-based SDSS search identifies two previously unreported optical–UV tidal disruption events

A PCA-enhanced Transformer classifier screened 298,195 SDSS DR7 spectra and selected 14 TDE-like cases for secondary review. The authors report two newly identified TDEs: SDSS J124225.39+642919.0, whose GALEX UV source was present by the spectrum date (before 11 February 2002), and SDSS J152459.70+045423.1, whose TDE-H+He spectrum coincided with a Catalina optical outburst in 2007–08. The first is reported as the earliest optical–UV TDE then known; one other model-selected source was a previously reported likely TDE.

Technology: PCA-enhanced Transformer TDE spectrum classifier
