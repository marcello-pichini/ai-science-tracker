Machine-learning screening identifies zeolite structure-directing agents

ZeoBind screened nearly 500 million zeolite–molecule pairs from a library of 2.3 million hypothetical, synthetically accessible organic structure-directing agents. The primary article reports two experimentally validated molecules that template ERI and CHA zeolites with novel compositions. The claim is limited to these tested framework/material outcomes; it does not establish exhaustive coverage of OSDA space or independent replication. Journal publication date is used because a prior preprint exists and its version chronology is not resolved here.
