Serine hydrolases designed from scratch

RFdiffusion backbones and the PLACER network for catalytic-cycle ensembles produced de novo serine hydrolases with kcat/Km up to 4.3e3 M^-1 s^-1 and crystal structures within 1 Å of the designs, in five folds not seen in this enzyme family. Design success rates not checked.

Technology: RFdiffusion and PLACER
