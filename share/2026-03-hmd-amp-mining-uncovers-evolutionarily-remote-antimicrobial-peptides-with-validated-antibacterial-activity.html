HMD-AMP mining uncovers evolutionarily remote antimicrobial peptides with validated antibacterial activity

A protein-language-model pipeline, HMD-AMP, screened host and gut-microorganism genomes from nine mammalian species and nominated antimicrobial peptides, including candidates with little similarity to known AMPs. Of 91 high-confidence peptides selected for experiments, 74 showed strong antibacterial activity and 48 were evolutionarily remote from known AMPs. Four tested peptides had broad-spectrum activity at low effective concentrations and low toxicity; the most potent reported candidate showed efficacy in a mouse peritoneal E. coli infection model.

Technology: HMD-AMP protein-language-model pipeline
