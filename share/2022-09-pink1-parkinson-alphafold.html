AlphaFold helps interpret activation of PINK1, a Parkinson’s-linked protein

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: After experimentally determining different forms of PINK1, the WEHI group used AlphaFold to model the human protein and its dimer, obtaining structures consistent with experimental results. The predictions also helped interpret how mutations associated with early-onset Parkinson’s may affect dimerization; this does not amount to a validated therapy.

Technology: AlphaFold

AI contribution
AlphaFold modelled human PINK1 and its dimer and the effect of mutations on dimerisation.
Test and result
The WEHI group compared an AlphaFold model of human PINK1 dimer with its own experimental structure work and used it to model disease mutations, which it says confirmed their suspicions.
Limitation / still unknown
The article describes no specific limitations of the predictions and says plenty of questions remain.
