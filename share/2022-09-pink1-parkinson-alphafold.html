AlphaFold helps interpret activation of PINK1, a Parkinson’s-linked protein

After experimentally determining different forms of PINK1, the WEHI group used AlphaFold to model the human protein and its dimer, obtaining structures consistent with experimental results. The predictions also helped interpret how mutations associated with early-onset Parkinson’s may affect dimerization; this does not amount to a validated therapy.
