Neural-network-guided autonomous experiments reveal an unreported TosMIC reaction

A reactivity-seeking neural network coupled to an autonomous reaction platform navigated unknown chemical mixtures using real-time analytical data. Among the reactions it identified, follow-up characterization revealed an unreported photochemical reaction and a new reactivity mode for p-toluenesulfonylmethyl isocyanide (TosMIC): six equivalents of TosMIC underwent a multistep single-substrate cascade yielding a trimeric product in 47% unoptimized yield and forming five C–C bonds.

Technology: Reactivity-seeking neural network and junction-tree neural network integrated with an autonomous robotic synthesis and analytics platform

The platform identified candidate reactions, while follow-up analyses and manual chemical investigation established the product and mechanism; no broad claim of fully autonomous mechanistic proof is made.
  • AI role: A convolutional neural network classified reaction signatures from online NMR/IR/analytical data and a junction-tree neural network encoded reagent structures to help prioritize new experiments. The closed-loop system surfaced reactions; chemists then manually investigated and characterized selected outcomes, including the TosMIC trimer-forming cascade.
