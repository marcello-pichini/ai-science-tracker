Iterative discovery of potent polymeric antibiotics via multi-stage and multi-task learning against antimicrobial resistance

PolyCLOVER integrated multi-stage learning, active learning and high-throughput experiments across a ~100,000-member poly(β-amino ester) space, identifying three lead nanoparticles with measured antibacterial activity and in-vivo mouse outcomes. Mouse groups were small; this does not establish human efficacy or safety.

Technology: Multi-stage self-supervised learning and active learning coupled to high-throughput experimentation
