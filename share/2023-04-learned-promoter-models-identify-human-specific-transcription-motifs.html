Learned promoter models identify human-specific transcription motifs

Models trained on promoter activity measurements helped characterize a human-specific downstream promoter variant and select synthetic motifs with species-specific activity. In-vitro transcription assays tested the predicted preferences with human and Drosophila extracts. These are gene-regulation and construct-assay findings, not demonstrated effects in organisms or disease.

Technology: HARPE-trained support-vector regression
