YOLO detector tracks endangered European hamster activity in camera traps

A YOLO object-detection model trained on 2023 camera-trap videos detected European hamsters with an F1-score of 0.93 and accuracy of 0.93. Its activity profile matched human annotation and showed peak activity at dusk and a secondary peak before sunrise. The evidence covers one season at one monitoring setup, so generalization to other sites is untested.

Technology: YOLO object detection (deep learning)
