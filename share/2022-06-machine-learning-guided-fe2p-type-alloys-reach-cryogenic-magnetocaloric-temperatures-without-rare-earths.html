Machine-learning-guided Fe₂P-type alloys reach cryogenic magnetocaloric temperatures without rare earths

An artificial neural network trained on reported experimental compositions guided optimization of Fe₂P-type Mn–Fe–P–Si alloys for sub-77 K magnetocaloric use. Experiments measured a 97 K transition for Mn₁.₇₀Fe₀.₃₀P₀.₆₃Si₀.₃₇ at 1 T; replacing a small fraction of Fe with Co lowered the transition to 73 K. The Co-containing composition showed a 7.5 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹ magnetic entropy change and a 1.4 K reversible adiabatic temperature change under a 0–5 T field.

Technology: Artificial neural network trained on a curated, literature-mined dataset of experimental Fe₂P-type magnetocaloric compounds
