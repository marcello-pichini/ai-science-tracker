ScreenDx identifies previously underdetected interstitial lung disease in COPDGene scans

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A retrospective COPDGene analysis used a learned CT model to detect cases subsequently relabeled as clinically significant interstitial lung disease. The original abstract reports 84.8% sensitivity and 98.0% specificity in a deliberately sampled cohort. This does not establish prospective screening benefit.

Technology: ScreenDx deep learning CT classifier

Editorial relevance 33 medicine Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A learned ScreenDx model evaluates CT features compatible with ILD; this is a retrospective test against subsequently assigned clinical reference labels.
Test and result
The source selected patients subsequently relabeled by COPDGene investigators as ILD-positive and sampled COPD/control negatives to target approximately 1–2% ILD prevalence. At a previously selected threshold, abstract-reported sensitivity was 84.8%(95% CI 68.1–94.9%) and specificity 98.0%(97.3–98.5%).
Limitation / still unknown
This review had the complete original abstract and primary bibliographic metadata, not full Methods. Exact sample counts, details of clinical adjudication, model-development overlap and threshold-lock timing were not authenticated. Positives had already been relabeled by investigators; these are not newly confirmed diagnoses made in a prospective AI screening program. Deliberate negative sampling limits prevalence-dependent interpretation. No improved patient outcomes or causal clinical benefit is established. The original journal history gives online publication November 26,2025, while PubMed records November 27; the represented date follows the original publisher.
