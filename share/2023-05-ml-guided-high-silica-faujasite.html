Machine learning guides direct synthesis of higher-silica faujasite

Machine-learning models learned relationships between crystallization composition/conditions and zeolite characteristics from prior synthesis records, guiding human selection of conditions for faujasite synthesis. The team reports directly synthesized, unseeded, organic-structure-directing-agent-free FAU with Si/Al 3.5, compared with the prior reported maximum 2.8. Following ion exchange, laboratory tests report improved propane cracking and dehydrogenation rates per proton site relative to the lower-silica comparator. The FAU framework was already known; this result concerns composition and route, not a new framework or industrial performance.

Technology: Geometric harmonics, neural networks and Gaussian-process regression
