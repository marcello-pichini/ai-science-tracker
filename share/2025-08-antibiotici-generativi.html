Antibiotics designed with generative AI against gonorrhea and MRSA

Over 36 million possible compounds were screened computationally: candidate NG1 killed resistant Neisseria gonorrhoeae in a dish and in a mouse model, while DN1 cleared a MRSA skin infection in mice. Only 2 of 80 fragment candidates could be synthesized; these are preclinical results.
