CleaveNet designs peptide substrates with MMP13 cleavage profiles

CleaveNet combines a generative model conditioned on protease cleavage profiles with a predictor that ranks candidate sequences. In an in-vitro screen of 95 peptide substrates against 12 matrix metalloproteinases, the authors report that designed substrates were cleaved by MMP13 and identify substrates with different efficiency/selectivity trade-offs. The panel includes training controls and baseline candidates; it is not 95 novel AI discoveries. These are laboratory substrates, not validated diagnostic or therapeutic products.

Technology: CleaveNet generator and predictor; deep learning
