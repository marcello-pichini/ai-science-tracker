FlowER predicts reaction mechanisms while conserving mass and electrons

A flow-matching generative model represents reactions as electron redistribution in bond-electron matrices, so predicted products conserve both mass and electrons. The authors report that it avoids hallucinated atoms, recovers mechanistic steps for unseen substrate scaffolds and generalizes to new reaction classes with little fine-tuning. Computational results on reaction datasets; no new reactions were run in the lab.

Technology: FlowER (flow matching on bond-electron matrices)
