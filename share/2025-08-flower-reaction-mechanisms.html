FlowER predicts ten-step pyrazole pathways consistent with known product selectivity

FlowER predicted two ten-step pathways to pyrazole products already reported in 2024 patents. Human researchers supplied acidic aqueous conditions omitted from the reaction database. Quantum-chemical calculations of intermediate energies favored the experimentally major product, consistent with the reported 8:2 product ratio; the kinetic interpretation assumes the Bell–Evans–Polanyi principle. This is a computational explanation of existing outcomes, with no new reaction experiment or claim of a previously unknown mechanism. Review covered the February 18, 2025 preprint results and the August 20 journal paper’s abstract, metadata and extended-data captions; the journal main text was not assessed.

Technology: FlowER (flow matching on bond-electron matrices)
