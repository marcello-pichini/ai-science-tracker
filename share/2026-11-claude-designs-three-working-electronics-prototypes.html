Claude designs three working electronics prototypes built and tested by an external manufacturer

Claude wrote the specifications, schematics, board layouts, enclosures, firmware and parts lists for three prototype devices built by Diode Computers, and the boards were assembled from the design files with no manufacturing revision rounds. Claude shortened a speakerphone microphone port from about 25 mm to 10 mm, moving its resonance from about 3.4 kHz to about 8.9 kHz. The authors state the devices have no electrical validation, antenna tuning, thermal testing or certification, and are not designed for mass production.
