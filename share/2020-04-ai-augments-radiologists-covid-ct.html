AI augmentation lifts radiologists in telling COVID-19 from other pneumonia

A CT model trained on 1,186 patients (521 COVID-19, 665 other pneumonia) reached 95% sensitivity and 96% specificity internally, and AUC 0.90 on an independent test set. Radiologist accuracy rose from 85% to 90% with AI assistance. The study is retrospective and the independent-test performance is lower than the internal result.

Technology: Deep-learning CT classifier used as an assistant for radiologists

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: A CT model was tested on an internal split and on an independent test set from separate hospitals, and six radiologists read cases unassisted and then with the model's probabilities.
  • Still unknown: The abstract states no limitations section; performance on independent data was lower (87% vs 96% accuracy) and the sensitivity confidence interval is wide.
  • AI role: The AI classified COVID-19 versus other pneumonia on CT slices and its probabilities were shown to radiologists as decision support.
