Hybrid machine learning guides experimental discovery of multicomponent metallic glasses

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Hybrid classification and regression models with physically motivated descriptors, trained on about 5,000 metallic-glass compositions, guided exploration of multicomponent alloy compositions. Researchers synthesized 23 selected compositions: 12 predicted glass-formers produced glass in bulk, ribbon or both, while 11 predicted negative comparisons did not form glass under the tested preparation conditions. Six of the positive designs formed bulk glass; six formed glassy ribbons only. These selected experiments are not an unbiased estimate of global predictive accuracy. Some authors disclosed inventorship on a filed patent related to the new bulk compositions; composition-level prior art and independent replication remain open.

Technology: Hybrid classification/regression models including adaptive boosting, SVM, KNN and rational-quadratic Gaussian-process regression
