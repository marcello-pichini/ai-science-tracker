AI-guided pure mathematics: knots and representation theory

With mathematicians Williamson (Sydney) and Lackenby and Juhász (Oxford), supervised-learning techniques revealed patterns leading to an unexpected link between a knot’s algebraic signature and its geometry (the ‘natural slope’), and to a new algorithm for the combinatorial invariance conjecture. The conjectures and proofs are the mathematicians’; the algorithm was checked on over 3 million examples, not proved in general.
