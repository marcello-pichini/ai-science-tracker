Catalysis AI Agent derives design rules for copper single-atom alloys for CO2 electroreduction

An LLM-based Catalysis AI Agent identified an energy descriptor for classifying copper-based single-atom alloys and a structural descriptor for predicting activation energy. The team reports experimental testing and that the approach extends to other metal dopants. Evidence caveat: the press release gives no quantitative activity or selectivity values and does not name the experimental group, so validation is limited.
