AI structural modeling helps map nearly 300 metabolite-binding interactions in E. coli

A cross-institutional study combined chemoproteomics with AI-driven structural modeling to identify nearly 300 small-molecule ligands and their binding sites on proteins in E. coli. The map adds experimentally grounded candidate links between bacterial metabolites and proteins involved in core cellular processes.

Technology: AI-driven structural modeling (Kozakov laboratory; specific model not named in institutional announcement)
