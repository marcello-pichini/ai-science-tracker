AlphaMissense: cataloging missense genetic mutations

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: It classified 89% of the 71 million possible missense variants: 32% as likely pathogenic and 57% as likely benign, with 90% precision on a database of known variants. Predictions are not intended for direct clinical use.

AI contribution
AlphaMissense, an AlphaFold adaptation fine-tuned on human and primate variant population frequency data, classified missense variants and released predictions for all possible human substitutions.
Test and result
The model was validated on genetic and experimental benchmarks, and Genomics England cross-referenced its predictions with previously aggregated human variant pathogenicity data and found them consistent. The reported comparison uses previously collected pathogenicity and variant data; an independent data source is distinct from a new independent experimental replication.
Limitation / still unknown
The model does not predict structural or stability effects, the blog says predictions are not designed for direct clinical use, and the abstract describes no clinical validation.
