Machine learning screened metal oxides for acetylene semihydrogenation

A neural-network model was used to predict metal-oxide catalyst performance for acetylene semi-hydrogenation. The authors report experimental validation of predictions for CuO, ZnO and V2O5, and report 99.6% conversion with 90% ethylene selectivity for CuO under the abstract-described conditions. The entry is limited to the publisher abstract; detailed model validation, candidate selection and full assay context were not reviewed.
