ChemCrow: a language-model agent with chemistry tools

An agent with 18 tools and GPT-4 planned and carried out the synthesis of DEET and three thiourea organocatalysts on a cloud platform, and guided discovery of a chromophore absorbing at 336 nm against a 369 nm target; evaluated on 14 cases by four expert chemists. Hallucinations, limited tasks and dual-use risks; 12 of 18 tools were released.

Technology: ChemCrow (GPT-4 with tools)
