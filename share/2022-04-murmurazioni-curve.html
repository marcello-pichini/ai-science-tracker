Murmurations: unexpected oscillations in elliptic-curve data

Classification experiments and analysis of elliptic-curve data show oscillations in the mean of Frobenius traces that vary with rank. A numerical observation, not proof of a general law; the 2022 preprint was later published in Experimental Mathematics in 2024.

Technology: Logistic regression and PCA
