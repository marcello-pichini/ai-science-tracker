OpenCRISPR-1: a genome editor designed by language models

Profluent built an atlas of 5.1 million CRISPR-Cas proteins and generated a Cas9-like editor with 403 mutations relative to SpCas9. Of 48 generated sequences, the best (OpenCRISPR-1) achieved 55.7% on-target editing versus 48.3% for SpCas9, and 0.32% off-target editing versus 6.1%. Human cells in the laboratory only; company data; the page does not indicate peer review.
