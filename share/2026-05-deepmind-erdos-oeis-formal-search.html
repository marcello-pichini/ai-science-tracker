AI-driven formal proof search resolves Erdős problems and OEIS conjectures

An agent that alternates LLM proof generation with Lean verification autonomously resolved 9 of 353 open Erdős problems and proved 44 of 492 OEIS conjectures, at a cost of a few hundred dollars per problem according to the abstract. Proofs are Lean-verified, so the checking is mechanical; the abstract does not name the system and the results are limited to the problem sets tested.

Technology: LLM-based agent with Lean-based proof verification (system unnamed in abstract)
