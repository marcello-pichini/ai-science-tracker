PlantRNA-FM identifies translation-associated RNA motifs and structure motifs in plants

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The PlantRNA-FM model, pretrained on sequence and predicted RNA structure from 1,124 plant transcriptomes, identified 5′-UTR motifs associated with translation efficiency across plant transcriptomes. Reporter assays in plants tested selected predictions: mutating a high-translation-associated RNA secondary-structure motif reduced measured translation efficiency by up to 5.3-fold, while disrupting a model-prioritized low-translation-associated RNA G-quadruplex motif increased it by up to 5.8-fold. The paper reports the rG4 motifs as translation-repressive, consistent with prior evidence on individual rG4s; the transcriptome-wide model prioritization and tested instances are the scoped findings here.

Technology: PlantRNA-FM, an interpretable RNA foundation model integrating RNA sequence and predicted structure

AI contribution
PlantRNA-FM was pretrained with plant RNA sequence and structure data, then fine-tuned on translation-efficiency data. An attention-contrast interpretation framework prioritized 5′-UTR sequence/structure motifs associated with translation; researchers tested selected motif predictions by mutating reporter constructs and measuring translation efficiency in plants.
Test and result
Reporter assays in plants tested selected predictions: mutating a high-translation-associated RNA secondary-structure motif reduced measured translation efficiency by up to 5.3-fold, while disrupting a model-prioritized low-translation-associated RNA G-quadruplex motif increased it by up to 5.8-fold.
Limitation / still unknown
Experimental reporter validation was limited to selected motifs and had n=3, as reported in the figure caption. The model’s transcriptome-wide associations are not individually causal validations. The repressive role of rG4 motifs was previously known for individual cases; the paper’s contribution includes model-prioritized plant 5′-UTR motifs and experimental effects for selected constructs, not first discovery of rG4 biology. Reporter assays do not establish whole-plant phenotype or crop benefit.
