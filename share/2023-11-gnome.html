GNoME: 2.2 million new predicted crystals

A graph neural network predicted 2.2 million new crystals, including 380,000 of the most stable, raising the number of known stable materials from about 48,000 to 421,000. External laboratories synthesized 736 predicted structures. Stability is calculated and does not guarantee a material can be produced or will be useful.

Technology: GNoME (Graph Networks for Materials Exploration)
