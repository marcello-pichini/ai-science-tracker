Deep clustering surfaces tremor-like episodes before the 2023 Türkiye earthquake

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An autoencoder and deep embedded clustering surfaced low-frequency tremor-like episodes in seismic records preceding the 2023 Türkiye earthquake. The authors then used source-location and particle-motion analyses to suggest an association with nearby cement-quarry activity. They explicitly state that evidence is insufficient to establish that industrial activity caused or influenced the earthquake; the paper is a retrospective signal interpretation, not an earthquake forecast or causal finding.

Technology: Autoencoder and deep embedded clustering
