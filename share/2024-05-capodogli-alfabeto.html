Sperm whales: a possible ‘phonetic alphabet’ in their calls

Analysis of nearly 9,000 codas from the eastern Caribbean clan, collected over more than ten years, proposes a combinatorial structure involving rhythm, tempo, rubato and ornamentation. Published in Nature Communications; communicative function remains unknown, data cover one clan, and the page does not specify machine-learning methods.
