Earth-abundant Ni3Mo alloy outperforms platinum for ethane dehydrogenation

A microkinetic and high-throughput screen of 1,998 bimetallic alloys, combined with machine-learning concepts, shortlisted noble-metal-free catalysts for selective ethane dehydrogenation. The predicted Ni3Mo alloy was synthesized and experimentally shown to outperform Pt in selective ethylene production. The machine-learning role is secondary to the descriptor-based workflow, and the evidence is a single catalyst test.

Technology: Descriptor-based microkinetic modeling with high-throughput DFT and machine learning
