CellTransformer maps previously uncatalogued regions of the mouse brain

A transformer model applied to spatial transcriptomics mapped fine-grained mouse-brain tissue domains and revealed hundreds of regions absent from existing anatomical atlases. The study evaluated the data-driven domains against the Allen Mouse Brain Common Coordinate Framework and reported strong alignment alongside the novel subdivisions.
