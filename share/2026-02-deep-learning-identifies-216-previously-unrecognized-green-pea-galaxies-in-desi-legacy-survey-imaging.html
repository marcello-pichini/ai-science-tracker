Deep learning identifies 216 previously unrecognized Green Pea galaxies in DESI Legacy Survey imaging

A MobileMamba-ECA image classifier searched DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys data and surfaced 775 Green Pea galaxy candidates. Cross-matching with DESI DR1 spectroscopy established 216 as previously unreported Green Pea galaxies: although spectra existed, the standard DESI pipeline had classified them only as generic galaxies. The study’s remaining 511 photometric-only objects are follow-up candidates and are not included in the confirmed count.

Technology: MobileMamba-ECA deep-learning classifier (Mamba state-space backbone with efficient channel attention)

  • AI role: Classified multiband DESI Legacy Survey images to prioritize compact Green Pea galaxy sources from an 843,592-object parent sample; this enabled spectral cross-identification and recognition of 216 previously unreported examples.
