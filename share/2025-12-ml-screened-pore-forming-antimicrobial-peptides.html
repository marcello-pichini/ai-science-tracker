Machine-learning screening identified experimentally active pore-forming peptides

Machine-learning models and membrane-related filters were used to prioritize natural peptide sequences for testing. The study reports seven experimentally active peptides; three showed broad-spectrum activity and pore-forming evidence in the reported membrane assays. Peptide 615 had already been disclosed in a Peking University patent published in March 2025, before the related preprint and journal article. This entry represents one study campaign and makes no first-disclosure or sequence-novelty claim. Assay results do not establish clinical safety or efficacy.

Technology: ProtBERT-BFD sequence embeddings with a support-vector classifier and membrane-interaction, secondary-structure and contact-map predictions
