D2ML guided prospective ROS1 potency and selectivity optimization

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Assay-aware models were used to select 84 new compounds for synthesis and prospective testing in a ROS1 kinase program, with TRKB as an anti-target. The most selective prospective compound had a measured ROS1 IC50 of 14.7 nM and 233-fold selectivity over TRKB, compared with 20.3 nM and 79-fold for the most selective training-set compound. The evidence is biochemical and does not establish therapeutic efficacy or safety.

Technology: D2ML multi-stage deep neural networks trained across direct-to-biology assay data

AI contribution
Assay-aware models using data from multiple direct-to-biology screening stages selected compounds for prospective synthesis and ROS1/TRKB testing.
Test and result
The authors prospectively synthesized and assayed 84 model-selected compounds. The most selective new compound had ROS1 IC50 14.7 nM and 233-fold selectivity over TRKB; the most selective training compound had 20.3 nM ROS1 IC50 and 79-fold selectivity.
Limitation / still unknown
This is an in-vitro biochemical discovery result. It does not establish cellular activity, therapeutic efficacy, safety or clinical benefit. The paper compares a historical analog but makes no claim of global chemical novelty.
