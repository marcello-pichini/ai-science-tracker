OPEX-guided experiments reveal bacterial cross-stress responses

Learned models selected bacterial stress-combination experiments and incorporated their measurements. The study reported cross-stress protection and vulnerability patterns, with follow-up supporting roles for stress-response machinery. This is a laboratory microbiology result, not a clinical resistance prediction or evidence of treatment efficacy.
