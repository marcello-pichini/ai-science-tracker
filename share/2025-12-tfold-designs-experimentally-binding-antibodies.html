tFold designs binding antibodies against four defined antigens

Tencent researchers generated antibodies against influenza hemagglutinin, PD-1, PD-L1 and SARS-CoV-2 RBD, followed by phage-display screening and SPR characterization. Anti-SC2RBD-H330 showed neutralization IC50 of 0.42 nM. Screening remained extensive, and some confident epitope predictions failed competition tests. The date represents initial journal publication, preceding the January 19, 2026 version of record; a tFold precursor was posted February 8, 2024.
