Machine learning and Mendelian randomization prioritize CA2 as a diabetic nephropathy biomarker

Across six kidney-expression datasets, researchers identified 314 differentially expressed genes in diabetic nephropathy and used LASSO, SVM-RFE and random forest to prioritize seven candidate genes. Mendelian-randomization integration further prioritized carbonic anhydrase II (CA2); its ROC AUC exceeded 0.878 across the analyzed datasets. The authors also reported qPCR validation of CA2 in clinical patient and control samples and an association with inferred immune-cell infiltration.

Technology: LASSO, support-vector-machine recursive feature elimination and random-forest feature selection
