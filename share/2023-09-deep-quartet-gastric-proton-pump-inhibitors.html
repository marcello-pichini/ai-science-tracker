Deep generative design with cryo-EM feedback yields a 47.6 nM gastric proton pump inhibitor

Source check: Needs source check. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Abe et al. designed candidate compounds in silico with deep generative models under pharmacophores taken from drug-bound gastric proton pump structures (workflow Deep Quartet), synthesized several, measured inhibition and solved binding poses by cryo-EM at up to 2.08 A. The best compound, DQ-18, has Ki 47.6 nM. Abstract-level review; comparison with non-AI design and preclinical development status not assessed.

Technology: Deep generative models (Deep Quartet workflow)
