Neural network ensemble for prenatal detection of complex congenital heart disease

An ensemble of neural networks trained on 107,823 fetal ultrasound images reached an AUC of 0.99, 95% sensitivity and 96% specificity in an internal test set of 4,108 fetal surveys with 0.9% CHD. Its sensitivity was comparable to that of clinicians and it stayed robust on outside-hospital and lower-quality images. Caveat: retrospective study with a low-prevalence internal test set.

Technology: Ensemble of convolutional neural networks with segmentation models

Evidence context (pilot; stated by the cited sources, not an editorial judgment):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Tested: Trained on 107,823 images from 1,326 retrospective studies; on 4,108 fetal surveys (0.9% CHD), AUC 0.99, 95% sensitivity, 96% specificity, 100% negative predictive value; comparable to clinicians and robust on outside-hospital images.
  • Still unknown: Prospective screening performance is not reported in the abstract.
  • AI role: An ensemble of neural networks identifies cardiac views and classifies normal versus complex congenital heart disease.
