ETopt-guided screening identifies thermophilic beta-agarases

ETopt combines sequence, structural and protein-language-model features in an ensemble XGBoost model to predict enzyme optimum temperatures. The authors report wet-lab screening that identified two novel thermophilic beta-agarases. The draft is limited to those source-identified enzyme findings; model benchmarks and other screened enzyme classes are not counted as separate discoveries. Industrial process performance and independent replication are not established.

Technology: ETopt multimodal model; ensemble XGBoost; ProtBERT and Prime embeddings
