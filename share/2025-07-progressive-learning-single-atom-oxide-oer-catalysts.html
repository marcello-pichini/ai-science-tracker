Progressive learning screened single-atom oxide catalysts for acidic oxygen evolution

A progressive-learning workflow combining density-functional theory and machine learning screened 261 single-atom-doped metal oxides and identified nine candidates with predicted OER overpotential below 0.5 V. The publisher abstract reports experimental evaluation confirming predicted activity and durability for Mn single atoms on RuO₂ under acidic conditions. Candidate-wide values are theoretical predictions; only the specified material’s validation is summarized as experimental.

Technology: Progressive learning with DFT-derived adsorption-energy features and machine learning
