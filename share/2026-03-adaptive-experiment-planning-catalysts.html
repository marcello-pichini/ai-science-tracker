Adaptive AI experiment planning finds multi-promoter catalysts in under 50 experiments

Fritz Haber Institute, with BASF and BasCat, used adaptive experiment planning in a self-driving lab to search over 10^13 promoter combinations with fewer than 50 experiments. The method was validated on propane-to-propylene conversion, and the authors report synergistic promoter effects that explain the catalyst's performance. The claim of outperforming the industry reference is not quantified in the release, so check the paper for numbers.

Technology: Adaptive experiment planning (Bayesian-style, gray-box) in a self-driving lab
