Knowledge-graph-guided discovery of antibiotic-resistance associations

KIDS combined an expert-curated knowledge graph with learned link-prediction models to rank gene–antibiotic resistance hypotheses for experiments. The authors report 93 validated associations involving 83 E. coli genes and 15 antibiotics, including six genes they describe as previously unknown resistance genes; five tested Salmonella homologs also showed resistance. These are laboratory gene–drug associations, not clinical validation. The article’s second-round percentage conflicts with its counts (29/90 is 32.2%, although the text states 28.8%). Count one study.

Technology: Knowledge graph with PRA, multilayer perceptron, AdaBoost-stacked model, TransE and TransD
