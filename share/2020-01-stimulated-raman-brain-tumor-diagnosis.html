Deep neural networks diagnose brain tumours in minutes from stimulated Raman histology

CNNs trained on over 2.5 million stimulated Raman histology images produced intraoperative brain tumour diagnoses in under 150 seconds, with 94.6% accuracy versus 93.9% for pathologists. The accuracy comparison comes from a prospective multicentre trial with 278 patients, but the abstract does not report sensitivity or specificity separately.

Technology: Deep neural networks on stimulated Raman histology images
