A new class of antibiotic candidates against MRSA

A deep-learning model trained on about 39,000 compounds screened about 12 million commercial compounds; of about 280 purchased and tested, two from the same class reduced MRSA burden tenfold in two mouse models, with very low toxicity to human cells. Results from plates and mice; toxicity was partly predicted computationally.

Technology: Deep learning with chemical-structure explainability
