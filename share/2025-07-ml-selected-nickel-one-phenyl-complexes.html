Machine learning helps select nickel(I)–phenyl complexes that insert CO₂ at room temperature

The researchers calculated descriptors for 132 bidentate phosphine ligands, used k-means clustering to identify 21 candidate ligands, then applied DFT barrier calculations and human selection before synthesis. They isolated and characterized Ni(I)–phenyl complexes, including an X-ray-confirmed i-Pr-Xantphos complex. The i-Pr-Xantphos and dtbpf complexes underwent CO₂ insertion at room temperature in the reported experiments; nickel carboxylation was also tested. The study does not establish CO₂ insertion or Ni(I) chemistry generally as new; its result concerns bounded ligand selection/speciation.

Technology: Unsupervised k-means clustering of DFT-derived ligand descriptors
