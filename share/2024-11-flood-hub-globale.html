Seven-day flood forecasts with reliability similar to earlier five-day forecasts

The new model, trained on 15,980 stations (up from 5,680), gives seven-day reliability comparable to the previous model's five-day forecast. Flood Hub covers over 100 countries and about 700 million people (up from 460 million). About 250,000 virtual points are unverified and intended for experts only; flash, urban, and coastal floods remain out of scope. The linked March 2024 Nature paper describes the predecessor model, also covered by the catalog's March entry; the November performance and coverage update is reported in this Google Research announcement.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Announcement only
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Gauged basins were checked with standard cross-validation, and ungauged locations were checked against Sentinel-1 radar inundation images; reliability was compared using Nash-Sutcliffe efficiency distributions across 3,079 basins.
  • Still unknown: Ungauged validation covers only a fraction of locations, flash, urban and coastal floods are out of scope, and virtual-gauge forecasts are unverified.
  • AI role: An LSTM network takes meteorological time series and static catchment attributes, adds input from DeepMind's medium-range forecast model, and outputs a probabilistic streamflow distribution.

Evidence sources: https://research.google/blog/a-flood-forecasting-ai-model-trained-and-evaluated-globally/
