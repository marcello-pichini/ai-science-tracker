Machine-learning-guided closed-loop synthesis experimentally realizes unreported dye molecules

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A closed-loop molecular discovery platform combined machine-learning property prediction, automated synthesis and characterization, and iterative model updates to explore dye-like molecules. In the published Science abstract, the team reports experimentally realizing 294 previously unreported molecules across three design–make–test–analyze cycles on four rarely reported scaffolds, while also identifying six top-performing molecules in a second case study using pre-existing chemical-space examples.

Technology: Autonomous molecular-discovery platform integrating generative and property-prediction machine-learning models, Bayesian optimization, and synthesis-planning software with automated chemical synthesis and characterization

Editorial relevance 60 chemistry Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Generative ML proposed candidate dye-like structures; property-prediction models ranked molecules against absorption, lipophilicity, and photo-oxidative-stability targets; automated multi-step synthesis and measurements generated training data for model retraining and the next cycles. The closed-loop process converted model-guided suggestions into synthesized and characterized compounds.
Test and result
In the published Science abstract, the team reports experimentally realizing 294 previously unreported molecules across three design–make–test–analyze cycles on four rarely reported scaffolds, while also identifying six top-performing molecules in a second case study using pre-existing chemical-space examples.
Limitation / still unknown
This is molecular discovery in dye-like chemical systems, not a validated drug or biological effect. The 294 number is from the published Science abstract; an earlier ChemRxiv preprint describes 312 unreported molecules, so version-specific counts differ and the published figure is used here. The measurements addressed absorption wavelength, lipophilicity and photo-oxidative stability; no environmental, toxicological or commercial benefit is claimed. Primary-source abstract and author-lab description checked; independent replication not assessed.
