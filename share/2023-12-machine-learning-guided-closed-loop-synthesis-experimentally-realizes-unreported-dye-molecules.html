Machine-learning-guided closed-loop synthesis experimentally realizes unreported dye molecules

A closed-loop molecular discovery platform combined machine-learning property prediction, automated synthesis and characterization, and iterative model updates to explore dye-like molecules. In the published Science abstract, the team reports experimentally realizing 294 previously unreported molecules across three design–make–test–analyze cycles on four rarely reported scaffolds, while also identifying six top-performing molecules in a second case study using pre-existing chemical-space examples.

Technology: Autonomous molecular-discovery platform integrating generative and property-prediction machine-learning models, Bayesian optimization, and synthesis-planning software with automated chemical synthesis and characterization
