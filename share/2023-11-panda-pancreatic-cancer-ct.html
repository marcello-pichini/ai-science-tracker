PANDA: pancreatic lesion detection on non-contrast CT with deep learning

PANDA was trained on non-contrast abdominal CT scans from 3,208 patients at a high-volume pancreatic cancer centre in Shanghai. In a retrospective evaluation of 20,530 consecutive patients across four clinical settings, the paper reports 92.9% sensitivity and 99.9% specificity for pancreatic lesion detection. The study also reports five cancers and 26 other pancreatic lesions missed by the initial standard of care. These results do not establish population-screening benefit; the authors call for prospective risk-benefit and cost-effectiveness studies and broader international validation.

Technology: PANDA (three-stage deep learning cascade on non-contrast CT)
