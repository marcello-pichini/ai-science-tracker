Machine-learning phase picks constrain the 2025 Drake Passage earthquake depth and activated fault geometry

For the Mw 7.4 Drake Passage earthquake of 2 May 2025, a trained classifier identified depth-sensitive pP–sP seismic phases used to refine focal depths under sparse station coverage. The authors infer a mainshock depth of about 25 km, consistent with the small tsunami, and report aftershocks on west-dipping faults southeast of the epicenter near the Shackleton Fracture Zone; the paper integrates this with seismic-reflection and previously collected imaging data.

Technology: Trained machine-learning classifier for depth-sensitive seismic phase identification
