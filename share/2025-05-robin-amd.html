Robin: ripasudil as a candidate for dry macular degeneration

FutureHouse's multi-agent system generated hypotheses, experiments, and analyses and identified ripasudil, already used for glaucoma, as a candidate against dry macular degeneration. Lab testing of ten candidates found that a ROCK inhibitor increases phagocytosis by retinal pigment epithelial cells; human researchers carried out the experiments. Announced May 20, 2025, and published in Nature May 19, 2026. Preclinical data.
