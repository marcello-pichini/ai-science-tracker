Short-term rainfall prediction with a generative model

Developed with the UK Met Office, the model forecasts precipitation up to two hours ahead; over 50 expert meteorologists chose it as their first choice in 89% of cases compared with the most widely used methods. The authors cite long-range accuracy and rare, intense events as limitations.

Technology: Deep generative radar model (DGMR)
