ProT-VAE designs synthetic enzymes with measured activity and high-temperature stability

The transformer–variational-autoencoder ProT-VAE generated synthetic enzyme sequences that were tested experimentally. Among 190 designed human phenylalanine-hydroxylase sequences, 69 had measurable activity and 19 exceeded wild-type activity, with the best at 2.5× wild type. A designed γ-carbonic anhydrase had a measured melting temperature of 116 °C (61 °C above the most thermostable sequence previously reported by the authors) and remained active in 23% methyl diethanolamine at pH 11.25 and 93 °C.

Technology: Protein Transformer Variational AutoEncoder (ProT-VAE), using ProtT5nv transformer representations and a family-specific variational autoencoder
