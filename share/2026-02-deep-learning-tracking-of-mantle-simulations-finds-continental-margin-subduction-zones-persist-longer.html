Deep-learning tracking of mantle simulations finds continental-margin subduction zones persist longer

A fully convolutional network segmented subduction zones in two-dimensional mantle-convection simulations using temperature, vertical velocity and fineness fields. Across the modeled runs, zones forming near continental margins were generally longer-lived and less sensitive to internal heating than oceanic zones, supporting the hypothesis that continental lithosphere can stabilize subduction during early-Earth-like conditions. This is a computational-model result rather than direct observation of ancient Earth.

Technology: Attention-based fully convolutional network with custom segmentation loss
