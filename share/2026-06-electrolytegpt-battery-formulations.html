ElectrolyteGPT can generate new formulations for battery development

University of Chicago researchers trained a GPT model with a new notation (fLine) to generate complete battery electrolyte formulations, including solvent ratios, salt concentrations and temperature. Several AI-proposed compositions were synthesized and, per the release, performed on par with the state of the art in lithium metal batteries. No cycling or capacity figures are given, the work is early, and generated candidates still need experimental verification.

Technology: ElectrolyteGPT (GPT with fLine notation)
