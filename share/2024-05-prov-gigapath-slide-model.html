Prov-GigaPath: a whole-slide foundation model for digital pathology

Archived from discovery counts: this paper introduces a foundation model and evaluates retrospective prediction benchmarks rather than reporting a separate biological finding or clinical field-study result. Prov-GigaPath was pretrained on 1.3 billion image tiles from 171,189 whole-slide images of more than 30,000 patients across 31 tissue types. It led on 25 of 26 evaluated tasks using both Providence and TCGA data, so evaluation was not confined to one health network. The historical entry is retained as method-development context, consistently with the archived UNI entry.

Technology: Prov-GigaPath (GigaPath vision transformer with LongNet dilated attention)
