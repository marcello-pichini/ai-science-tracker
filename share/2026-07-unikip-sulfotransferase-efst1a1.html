UniKP prioritizes a sulfotransferase for sulfotyrosine production

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: UniKP was used to rank sulfotransferase homologues by predicted kinetics. The study reports experimental testing of five prioritized candidates and identifies EfST1A1, with about 6.5-fold higher in-vitro activity than the initial hit under the reported assay. Later directed evolution is not attributed to the model. This is a bounded enzyme-discovery result, not proof of industrial production or broad enzyme generality.

Technology: UniKP kinetic prediction
