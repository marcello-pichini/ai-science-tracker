ForTy 2020: global map of forest types at 10 meters

A 10-meter map classifies each land area into six forest or land-use types (including primary forest, plantations, and tree crops), with an initial time series from 2017–2025. Validation across more than 9,000 sites identifies the correct type in 90% of cases and distinguishes forest from non-forest in 95%.

Technology: Satellite-image classification models

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: The map was validated at more than 9,000 random sites, where two trained experts checked high-resolution imagery to determine land type: correct forest type in 90% of cases and forest vs non-forest in 95%.
  • Still unknown: Per-class accuracy and regional breakdowns are not reported, the time series is early-release without separate validation, and results depend on the probability threshold chosen.
  • AI role: A custom remote-sensing model trained on about 1.7 million labeled locations, using AlphaEarth embeddings, outputs probabilities for six forest and land-use classes.
