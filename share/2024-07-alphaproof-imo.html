AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2: silver-medal level at the Math Olympiad

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The two systems solved 4 of 6 problems at IMO 2024, scoring 28 of 42 points, one point below the gold threshold. Problems were manually translated into formal language, and some required up to three days of computation instead of the official 4.5 hours.

Technology: AlphaProof (Lean) and AlphaGeometry 2

AI contribution
AlphaProof solved three problems with formal proofs, and AlphaGeometry 2 proved the geometry problem within 19 seconds of formalization.
Test and result
The systems solved official IMO 2024 problems, graded under IMO rules by Prof. Timothy Gowers and Dr Joseph Myers, reaching 28 of 42 points; problems were manually translated into formal language first.
Limitation / still unknown
Two combinatorics problems remained unsolved, translation was manual, and some solutions took up to three days.
