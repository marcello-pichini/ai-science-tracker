ECMWF puts AI forecasts (AIFS) into operation

The European center operationalized AIFS Single on February 25, 2025, alongside the physical IFS model. ECMWF reports gains of up to 20% on tropical-cyclone tracks and about 1,000 times lower energy use. It is deterministic, at 28 km versus 9 km for IFS.
