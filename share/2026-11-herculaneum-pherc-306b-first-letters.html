Writing seen in PHerc. 306B for the first time in 2000 years

Vesuvius Challenge awarded a $50K First Letters Prize for scroll PHerc. 306B, where the winning submission reached the 10-character target and the papyrology team agreed on a reading, tentatively a Greek phrase with parallels in Aristotle and Philodemus. Ink was detected by multiplying two models (TRACE v3B and a fine-tuned A-hn). Letters remain faint, the readable area is small, and the work relied on hundreds of hours of manual labeling.

Technology: ML ink-detection models (TRACE v3B, A-hn) with VC3D GrowPatch
