AlphaEvolve: an agent for designing algorithms

An evolutionary agent based on Gemini and automated evaluators. It recovered an average of 0.7% of Google's computing resources, sped up a Gemini training kernel by 23%, and found an algorithm using 48 multiplications for 4×4 complex matrices. Across more than 50 open math problems, it matched the state of the art in about 75% and improved it in about 20%.

Technology: AlphaEvolve (Gemini Flash and Pro)
