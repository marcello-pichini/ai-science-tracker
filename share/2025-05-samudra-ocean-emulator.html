Samudra: an AI emulator of the global ocean for climate simulations

Samudra, a UNet-style emulator trained on the ocean component of a state-of-the-art climate model (OM4 within CM4), reproduces sea surface height, velocities, temperature and salinity across the full ocean depth. It runs about 150 times faster than the original ocean model and remains stable for centuries. The authors note it struggles to reproduce the correct magnitude of forcing trends while staying stable, so it needs further work.

  • Publication status: Preprint

  • Tested: Samudra was compared with the original climate model's ocean component: no drift relative to the truth, reproduction of depth structure and interannual variability, stability for centuries, and about 150 times faster.
  • Still unknown: The authors state the emulator struggles to reproduce the correct magnitude of forcing trends while staying stable, and this needs further work.
  • AI role: A ConvNeXt-UNet-style emulator trained on the ocean component of a climate model to predict sea surface height, velocities, temperature and salinity.
