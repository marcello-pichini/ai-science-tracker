AI-assisted seismic mapping reveals segmented fault activity at the Discovery transform fault

A machine-learning earthquake-detection and relocation workflow applied to 2008 ocean-bottom seismometer records found that the western Discovery transform-fault strand may comprise seven patches with distinct seismicity. The authors also identify a seismicity-gap zone that they interpret as possibly linked to an active small off-ridge rift.

Technology: PhaseNet phase picker, GaMMA phase association, and machine-learning-enhanced seismic event workflow

  • Tested: A machine-learning earthquake-detection and relocation workflow applied to 2008 ocean-bottom seismometer records found that the western Discovery transform-fault strand may comprise seven patches with distinct seismicity.
  • Still unknown: The seven-patch segmentation and possible off-fault rift relationship are interpretations of one year of local 2008 seismic data, not a causal or universal transform-fault model.   • AI role: The AI workflow detected P/S arrivals from noisy ocean-bottom recordings and associated event phases; cross-correlation relocation and geological interpretation by the research team produced the fault-pattern interpretation.
