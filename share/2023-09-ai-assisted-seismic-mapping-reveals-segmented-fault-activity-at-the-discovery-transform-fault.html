AI-assisted seismic mapping reveals segmented fault activity at the Discovery transform fault

A machine-learning earthquake-detection and relocation workflow applied to 2008 ocean-bottom seismometer records found that the western Discovery transform-fault strand may comprise seven patches with distinct seismicity. The authors also identify a seismicity-gap zone that they interpret as possibly linked to an active small off-ridge rift.

Technology: PhaseNet phase picker, GaMMA phase association, and machine-learning-enhanced seismic event workflow
