Fusing CT images and EHR data with deep learning improves pulmonary embolism detection

A deep multimodal network combining CT imaging with electronic health record data reached AUROC 0.947 (95% CI 0.946-0.948) on a held-out test set for pulmonary embolism. The abstract reports no sensitivity, specificity, study size or external validation sites, so the evidence is limited to retrospective internal testing.

Technology: Multimodal deep neural network fusing CT and EHR data
