Interpretable machine learning discovers ultrapermeable polymer membranes for gas separation

Multitask machine-learning models trained on experimental gas permeability data screened over 9 million hypothetical polymers. They identified hundreds of never-before-seen ultrapermeable membranes, with molecular dynamics simulations confirming the predicted permeabilities of the top candidates. The validation is computational rather than fabricated membranes, so the candidates remain unproven experimentally.

Technology: Multitask machine learning with model interpretation, plus molecular dynamics
