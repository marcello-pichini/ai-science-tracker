Physics-informed retrieval-augmented language model guides synthesis of five previously unreported inorganic crystals

Researchers built PIRAG-LM, which combines a structured knowledge base of experimental synthesis routes, physics-based chemical/structural/thermodynamic similarity retrieval, and LLM route reasoning. In prospective tests, it guided synthesis of five compounds previously unreported in experimental literature: BaMo0.3In0.7O2.95, BaNb0.4In0.6O2.9, Hg[B(CN)4]2, CoCo(CN)6 and SrNb2Fe2(PO4)6. Powder X-ray diffraction patterns were consistent with target phases. This is one paper-level synthesis-planning result, not five separate entries.

Technology: PIRAG-LM, physics-informed retrieval-augmented language model, with DeepSeek-V4 used in the structured synthesis-knowledge-base extraction workflow
