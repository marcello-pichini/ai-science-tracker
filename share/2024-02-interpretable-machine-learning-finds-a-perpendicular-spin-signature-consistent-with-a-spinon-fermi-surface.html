Interpretable machine learning finds a perpendicular-spin signature consistent with a spinon Fermi surface

A correlator convolutional neural network trained on projective snapshots from numerical simulations of the Kitaev–Heisenberg model identified a characteristic correlation pattern in the intermediate gapless phase when analyzing spin snapshots perpendicular to the applied field. The authors interpret the learned pattern as Friedel oscillations of gapless spinons forming a Fermi surface, supporting earlier theoretical proposals for this phase and suggesting measurable correlations for future experiments.

Technology: Quantum-classical QuCl approach using a correlator convolutional neural network and regularization-path analysis
