EmbryoNet links statins to FGF-like zebrafish developmental effects

A learned embryo-image classifier flagged statin-treated zebrafish as resembling reduced FGF signaling. Follow-up morphology and pERK measurements supported the developmental-pathway association. The mechanism remains proposed, and the zebrafish assays do not establish effects in human pregnancy or clinical use.

Technology: EmbryoNet deep image classifier
