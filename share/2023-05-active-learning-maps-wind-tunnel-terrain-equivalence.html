Active learning maps wind-tunnel terrain equivalence

A learned surrogate selected automated wind-tunnel experiments that mapped terrain configurations with similar second-order wind statistics. Different terrain shapes could meet the study's equivalence criterion. This does not imply identical full turbulence fields or equal structural safety; higher-order statistics can differ. Estimated experiment savings are not a fully executed randomized comparison with alternative sampling.

Technology: Gaussian-process sequential active learning
