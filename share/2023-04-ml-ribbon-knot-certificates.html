Machine learning finds ribbon-knot certificates and rules out candidate pairs

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Bayesian optimization and reinforcement learning search for sequences of knot moves that certify a knot is ribbon. The primary v1 reports that both knots in 843 specified pairs were shown ribbon, eliminating those pairs as potential counterexamples in the studied strategy. It also recovers a separately independently established set of 1,705 ribbon knots; these are not claimed as novel. This does not resolve the smooth four-dimensional Poincaré conjecture.

Technology: Bayesian optimization; reinforcement learning
