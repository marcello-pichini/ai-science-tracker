Organ-specific learned networks prioritize tested seed-lipid regulators

Learned organ classification enabled regulatory-network inference that nominated seed-lipid regulators for genetic and physiological testing in Arabidopsis. The result was public in a June 2023 preprint before the April 2024 journal article. Candidate counts differ between versions, so the entry does not transfer final-version counts or mechanistic detail to the preprint. Crop-yield benefit was not established.

Technology: K-nearest-neighbor organ classification and regulatory-network inference
