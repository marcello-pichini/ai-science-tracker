Experimental feedback guides ESM-2 to brighter CreiLOV variants

Reinforcement Learning from eXperimental Feedback (RLXF) used a learned fluorescence reward model to align ESM-2 with measured CreiLOV sequence-function data. In the peer-reviewed study, the authors expressed 60 generated variants in E. coli and measured cellular fluorescence; six of ten aligned ESM-2 five-mutant designs exceeded CreiLOV fluorescence, and three aligned designs exceeded CreiLOV-T7S. The first bioRxiv version (posted 2025-05-08) already described experimentally enhanced fluorescence from RLXF-aligned designs; detailed measurements here are from the 2026 journal version. The earlier ICLR workshop comparison was computational and did not report new wet-lab fluorescence assays.

Technology: 650-million-parameter ESM-2 protein language model aligned by reinforcement learning from experimental feedback using a learned sequence-fluorescence reward model

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Preprint
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: The journal article reports 60 CreiLOV designs expressed in E. coli BL21(DE3) and measured for total cellular fluorescence. Six of ten aligned ESM-2 five-mutant designs exceeded CreiLOV fluorescence, and three aligned designs exceeded the prior CreiLOV-T7S control; the brightest reported design was 1.2-fold above T7S.
  • Still unknown: The measurements use total cellular fluorescence and are specific to tested designs/conditions; broader cellular contexts, protein function beyond fluorescence, and independent reproduction are not established. The qualitative enhanced-fluorescence result is present in the 2025 preprint abstract; detailed design-set metrics are verified from the later journal version.
  • AI role: A learned sequence-fluorescence reward model trained on measured CreiLOV variant data guides supervised fine-tuning and PPO alignment of ESM-2. The aligned model generates the candidate sequences that were expressed and assayed.
