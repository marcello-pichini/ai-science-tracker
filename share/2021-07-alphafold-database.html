AlphaFold Protein Structure Database: first 350,000 models

Predicted structures for the human proteome (about 20,000 proteins) and 20 other organisms were published, over 350,000 models in total, hosted free by EMBL-EBI. DeepMind announced an expansion to over 100 million structures. These are predictions that complement experimental methods. Note: the page header says July 22, 2022, but the text calls CASP14 “last December” and the 2022 post refers to it as a 2021 release; date corrected to 2021.
