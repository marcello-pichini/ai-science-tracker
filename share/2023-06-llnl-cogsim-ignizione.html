CogSim helps predict fusion ignition at the NIF

Neural networks, Bayesian inference and physics simulations helped assess, before the experiment, the NIF design that produced 3.15 MJ of fusion energy from 2.05 MJ of laser energy delivered to the target on December 5, 2022. Experts designed the experiment; AI estimated its outcome and uncertainties. This is not the energy balance of the entire facility; the study was later published in Science in 2025.

Technology: CogSim (neural networks, transfer learning and Bayesian inference)
