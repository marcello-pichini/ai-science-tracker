Machine-learning-guided synthesis confirms superconductivity in Mo20Re6Si4

Researchers used a deep-learning composition model to prioritize a sigma-phase ternary compound, synthesized Mo20Re6Si4, and measured bulk superconductivity with a critical temperature of 5.4 K. Magnetic susceptibility and a specific-heat anomaly support the bulk transition. The paper reports mixed model performance on its small test set and describes the model as guidance alongside chemical intuition.

Technology: Deep-learning neural network that classifies compositions as superconducting and predicts critical temperature
