Early experiments in accelerating science with GPT-5

A collection of case studies with universities and national labs in mathematics, physics, biology, computer science, astronomy, and materials. Examples: it helped complete a proof of an Erdős problem with Sawhney and Sellke, reconstructed the SL(2,R) symmetry of the Kerr wave equation, and proposed an immunology mechanism later confirmed by Unutmaz's lab. Reported by OpenAI; experts supervised the work.

Technology: GPT-5 and GPT-5 Pro

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Not reported
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: The OpenAI page lists case studies, for example a mannose rescue experiment in T cells whose results matched GPT-5 predictions, and Erdos Problem 848, where authors turned a GPT-5 density estimate into a full proof.
  • Still unknown: OpenAI says the cases are curated illustrations, not a systematic sample. GPT-5 can invent citations, mechanisms or proofs, and expert oversight is essential. One case reproduced an earlier published proof without citing it.
  • AI role: GPT-5 suggested ideas, mechanisms, literature links and experiments; scientists set questions, verified results and ran lab work. OpenAI says the model is not autonomous.
