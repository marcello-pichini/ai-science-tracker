Unsupervised learning guides experimental superalloy design

A gradient-boosted regressor and unsupervised sampling workflow guided fabrication and differential-scanning-calorimetry measurements of nine superalloys selected outside the initial training compositions. Three alloys had gamma-prime solvus temperatures about 52 °C above the relevant comparators. This is a phase-solvus result, not a blade service-temperature or engine-performance result.

Technology: Gradient-boosting regression; t-SNE sampling; SHAP analysis
