Latent diffusion designs antimicrobial peptides active against fungi and drug-resistant bacteria

A latent-diffusion and molecular-dynamics pipeline generated and filtered peptide sequences; 40 synthesized candidates were tested, and 25 met the study's MIC threshold for antibacterial or antifungal activity. AMP-29 showed activity against Candida glabrata and efficacy in a murine skin-infection model, while AMP-24 showed in-vitro Gram-negative activity and efficacy in murine Acinetobacter baumannii skin and lung infection models.

Technology: Latent diffusion model coupled to molecular dynamics and peptide filtering/ranking
