Deep-learning control links single-cell expression to growth responses

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A learned predictive controller enabled high-throughput measurements linking gene-expression patterns with functional outcomes in individual engineered bacterial cells. The study exposed heterogeneous growth responses under antibiotic challenge. The original result was public in the October 2022 preprint and later appeared in a 2024 journal article. This is a laboratory model-system study, not a clinical resistance result or validated intervention.

Technology: LSTM encoder-decoder predictive control
