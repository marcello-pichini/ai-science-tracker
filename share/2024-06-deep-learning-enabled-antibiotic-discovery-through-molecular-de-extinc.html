Deep-learning-enabled antibiotic discovery through molecular de-extinction

Source check: Needs source check. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Deep-learning-enabled antibiotic discovery through molecular de-extinction. Deep-learning ensembles scored 10,311,899 peptides from extinct-organism proteomes for antimicrobial activity and prioritized candidates for synthesis. 69 peptides were synthesized and tested against bacterial pathogens per the abstract; most killed bacteria by membrane depolarization. Evidence limits: In vivo and mechanistic details beyond the abstract were not assessed; clinical relevance is not established. Reviewed from Europe PMC abstract only.

Technology: Peptide-sequence encoder plus neural-network ensembles (APEX-type) mining extinct-organism proteomes
