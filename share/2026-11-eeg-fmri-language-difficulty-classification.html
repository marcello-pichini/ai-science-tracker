20 Nov 2026Beijing Technology and Business University; Beijing Wuzi University

EEG–fMRI feature fusion distinguishes CELF-defined language difficulty in a retrospective cohort

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Pairwise learned classifiers fused EEG microstate and fMRI-connectivity features to distinguish CELF-defined language difficulty from controls in 173 participants. The authors report 78.4% balanced accuracy under nested cross-validation with training-only feature selection. This is within-cohort discrimination, not a prospective screening result or evidence of a causal language mechanism. SHAP interpretation used an original non-nested model rather than the most frequent nested classifier. The main Methods body and complete fMRI preprocessing were unavailable in the reviewed publisher snapshot; subgroup counts do not fully describe the 173-person sample.

Technology: Random forest and Gaussian Naive Bayes feature fusion; nested classifier selection

Editorial relevance 40 medicine Published articlePublished article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Learned classifier pairs combine EEG and fMRI features; nested selection evaluates their ability to discriminate language-test-defined groups. Correlated features and SHAP attributions do not establish biological mechanisms.
Test and result
Complete primary abstract and Appendix C describe 173-person nested evaluation, training-only screening/FDR and inner selection across 4221 model/fusion configurations. Most frequent nested pair was RF+GaussianNB; full-sample balanced accuracy 78.4%.
Limitation / still unknown
Main Methods body, full fMRI preprocessing and hyperparameter grids not assessed. Listed age subgroups 5–17 sum to 165 versus 173 overall aged 5–21; exact remaining allocation not specified in the reviewed tables. External/prospective clinical performance, causal neural interpretation and benefit remain unestablished.
