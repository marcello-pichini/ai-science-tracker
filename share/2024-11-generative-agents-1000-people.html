Generative agents built from interview transcripts simulate 1,000 people

LLM agents built from participants' full interview transcripts reproduced their General Social Survey answers at about 85% of the rate at which participants reproduced their own answers two weeks later, a normalized score. They also matched personality and economic-game results (normalized correlations of about 0.80 and 0.66) and replicated four of five behavioural experiments. Caveat: arXiv preprint, not peer reviewed, and the results are normalized rather than raw accuracy.

Technology: Generative agent architecture with LLM and full interview transcripts

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Preprint
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Agents built from interview and survey data of 1,052 Americans were scored on held-out General Social Survey items against participants' own two-week test-retest consistency, and on personality, economic-game and experiment replications.
  • Still unknown: Preprint, not peer reviewed; there is no dedicated limitations section, and combining interviews and surveys gave only modest gains over either source.
  • AI role: An LLM was the engine of agents that answer questions on behalf of individuals, grounded in their self-report data.

Evidence sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10109
