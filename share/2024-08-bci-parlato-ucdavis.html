A speech neuroprosthesis reaches 97.5% accuracy after calibration

A man with ALS used a brain-computer interface with machine learning to turn attempted speech into text and synthetic voice. After further training, the system maintained 97.5% accuracy; the study reports more than 248 hours of spontaneous conversation over 32 weeks. Result from one participant with an invasive implant; still experimental.

Technology: Neural speech decoding with machine learning
