Vision transformer detects methane point-source plumes in Sentinel-2 imagery at global scale

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A vision transformer applied to multispectral Sentinel-2 data detects methane plumes as small as 0.01 km2, corresponding to sources of about 200 to 300 kg CH4 per hour, and is reported to improve on the prior state of the art by an order of magnitude. Detections were compared with airborne methane measurement campaigns; the claims rest on that comparison and the abstract gives no formal detection-rate metrics.

Technology: Vision transformer for multispectral methane plume detection (Sentinel-2)
