UMAP analysis of DESI spectra surfaces newly recognized inhomogeneous white dwarfs

A machine-learning-guided analysis of DESI multi-epoch spectra used UMAP to identify three systems with strongly varying spectral signatures that were proposed as new ‘double-faced’ white dwarfs with inhomogeneous surface composition. Follow-up phase-resolved time-series spectra verified the interpretation for one system; the other two remain candidates. This is one paper-level result and only the verified system is counted as confirmed.

Technology: UMAP dimensionality reduction applied to multi-epoch white-dwarf spectroscopy, alongside a neural-network spectral classifier
