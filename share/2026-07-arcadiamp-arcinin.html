Diffusion-guided peptide design yields antimicrobial candidate Arcinin

ARCADIAMP combines iterative discrete diffusion generation, ESM2-based activity classifiers and sequence novelty filtering. Eight of ten experimentally screened candidates met the paper’s MIC threshold; Arcinin showed activity against tested pathogens and reduced bacterial burden in a mouse wound infection model. These are selected preclinical findings, not evidence of human safety or treatment efficacy; formal resistance-evolution testing remains future work.
