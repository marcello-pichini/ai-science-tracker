Perch: an open bioacoustics model to protect endangered species

The updated Perch model, released openly, predicts bird species better than its predecessor and has nearly twice as much training data (birds, mammals, amphibians, and human noise). The post gives no numbers for the new model; results on Hawaiian honeycreeper detection are from the earlier model.
