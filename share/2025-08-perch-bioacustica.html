Perch: an open bioacoustics model to protect endangered species

Archived from discovery counts: the represented event updates a bioacoustics model; the application outcomes described concern its predecessor rather than a new result from this release. The updated Perch model, released openly, predicts bird species better than its predecessor and has nearly twice as much training data (birds, mammals, amphibians, and human noise). The post gives no numbers for the new model; results on Hawaiian honeycreeper detection are from the earlier model.
