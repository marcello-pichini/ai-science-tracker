Urania designs new gravitational-wave detectors

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The April 2025 Physical Review X article describes Urania, a computational search for gravitational-wave interferometer topologies. Its simulated solutions increase potentially observable volume by up to 50-fold under the study constraints; 50 designs are shared in a detector zoo. These are proposed designs rather than constructed detectors. The work first appeared as an arXiv preprint in December 2023.

Technology: Urania (continuous optimization with ML-inspired search)
