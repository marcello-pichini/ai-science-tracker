PepMLM designs experimentally binding target-conditioned peptide ligands, including peptides used for targeted protein degradation

PepMLM fine-tunes ESM-2 using masked-language modeling conditioned on a target protein sequence to generate de novo linear peptide binders without structural input. In the reported experiments, selected peptides showed sequence-specific binding to targets including NCAM1 and AMHR2; incorporation into ubiquitin-based antibody constructs enabled targeted degradation of disease-relevant proteins, including Huntington’s disease drivers and viral phosphoproteins.

Technology: PepMLM, ESM-2 fine-tuned with target-sequence-conditioned masked language modeling
