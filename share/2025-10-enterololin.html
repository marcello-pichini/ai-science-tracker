Enterololin: AI helps clarify an antibiotic and its mechanism

An AI screen of over 10,000 molecules identified enterololin, and DiffDock predicted its binding to LolCDE within minutes, confirmed by resistant E. coli mutants, RNA-seq, and CRISPR. The mechanism was clarified in about six months versus 18–24 with traditional methods. In mice, in Crohn's-like inflammation models, healing was faster than with vancomycin. Early stage; no human testing.

Technology: Machine-learning screening and DiffDock
