Protein-surface-designed molecules activate PPARs

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A generative model designed molecules from the three-dimensional surfaces of PPAR proteins: synthesized in the laboratory, they increased activity and were tested by Roche. They demonstrate the method and are not intended for commercial development; the tests do not establish safety or efficacy in humans.

Technology: Protein-surface-guided generative AI
