AI-assisted ultrasound for dating pregnancy

Prospective study of 385 pregnant women in Chicago and Nairobi: the model estimates gestational age from scans taken by minimally trained operators with a mean error of 4.18 days, non-inferior to the 4.48 days of expert sonographers. This is a non-inferiority result from only two sites, both relatively urban.

Technology: Ultrasound model for estimating gestational age

Evidence context (pilot; stated by the cited sources, not an editorial judgment):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: prospective or independent check
  • Tested: A prospective multi-center study of 385 pregnant women at two sites (Chicago and Nairobi) compared AI gestational-age estimates from blind sweep scans by minimally trained operators with expert sonographer biometry: mean error 4.18 vs 4.48 days.
  • Still unknown: The source does not state the non-inferiority margin or confidence intervals; only two relatively urban sites and one commercial probe were used, and clinical trust and confidence safeguards are not validated.
  • AI role: The model estimated gestational age from blind sweep ultrasound scans and ran live on mobile devices in clinics.
