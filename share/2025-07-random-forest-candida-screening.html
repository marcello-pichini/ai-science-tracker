Machine-learning screening selects compounds active against Candida

Random-forest prediction and chemical filters helped narrow commercial compounds for experimental testing. Of 17 selected compounds, the paper reports 11 with inhibitory activity against Candida albicans at tested concentrations. Compound 17 had an IC50 of 0.071 μM and MIC of 4.5 μM, compared with fluconazole values of 0.39 μM and 0.78 μM, respectively; its lower IC50 but higher MIC does not establish superiority. The reported selectivity-index definition appears inconsistent with tabulated values, so this draft omits that index. Cell assays do not establish clinical efficacy or safety.

Technology: Random-forest classifiers and regressors; chemical similarity filtering
