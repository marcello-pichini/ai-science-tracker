AI-designed protein family binds and senses small molecules, including cortisol

The study combines trRosetta-based scaffold hallucination and Rosetta design with ProteinMPNN/LigandMPNN sequence design and AlphaFold structural filtering to create NTF2-like binders for six small-molecule targets. An optimized cortisol binder had a measured dissociation constant of 68 nM. A purified-protein, split-luciferase cortisol sensor showed an estimated response EC50 of 72 nM; this is a sensor response measurement, not the binding-affinity value or a clinical detection limit. The sensor also responded to cortisone and 17-alpha-hydroxyprogesterone, so specificity needs improvement. The March 2026 peer-reviewed paper is the source of record.
