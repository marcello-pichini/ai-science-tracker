DinoTracker: AI classifies dinosaur footprints

An unsupervised model trained on nearly 2,000 fossil footprints and millions of simulated variations identifies eight features of variation and agrees with experts about 90% of the time. Some tracks over 200 million years old resemble bird feet, which could suggest bird origins tens of millions of years earlier. The interpretation is open: they could be early dinosaurs whose feet happened to look similar.

Technology: DinoTracker (unsupervised model)
