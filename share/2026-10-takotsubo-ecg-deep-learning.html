Deep learning ECG model separates Takotsubo syndrome from anterior STEMI, with a randomized reader study

A preprint reports external validation of an ECG-image ensemble for Takotsubo syndrome versus anterior STEMI in 95 participants: accuracy 0.914 and AUC 0.970. Among 70 readers, estimated diagnostic correctness rose from 0.632 unaided to 0.864 with AI. Only the fourth phase randomized assistance conditions; AI-assisted reader accuracy remained below model accuracy. This summary is limited to the abstract and version 3 preprint dated November 8, 2026; the first version was posted October 19.

Technology: Self-supervised deep learning ensemble with Grad-CAM explanations
