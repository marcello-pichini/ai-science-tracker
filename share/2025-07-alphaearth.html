AlphaEarth Foundations: a 10-meter map of Earth

A model combines optical, radar, 3D laser, and climate-simulation data into one representation for 10-meter squares, using 16 times less storage and with 24% lower average error than the compared models. The Google Earth Engine dataset has over 1.4 trillion embeddings per year and has been tried by more than 50 organizations. Benefits with LLM agents remain prospective.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Preprint
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Compared against traditional methods and other AI mapping systems on land-use identification and surface-property estimation; average error 24% lower than the models tested, storage 16 times lower. Over 50 organizations tested the dataset; no sample sizes given.
  • Still unknown: The page states no limitations; it says the team is still exploring time-based capabilities and that embeddings may be more useful combined with reasoning LLM agents.
  • AI role: The model turns satellite and Earth observation data into compact embeddings; scientists and partners use them to create maps and classify ecosystems.
