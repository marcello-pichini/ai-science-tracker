Machine learning and genetic search predict stronger refractory alloys

A random-forest model trained on published refractory high-entropy-alloy data was coupled to a stochastic genetic algorithm to optimize alloy compositions for yield strength at selected temperatures. For an AlMo0.5NbTa0.5TiZr base composition, the authors report modeled gains of 90 MPa at 25 °C and 13% at 1,000 °C for optimized compositions. Other base-alloy optimizations are also reported. The proposed alloys were not experimentally fabricated or mechanically measured in this study; these are computational design predictions.

Technology: Random-forest regression; stochastic genetic algorithm
