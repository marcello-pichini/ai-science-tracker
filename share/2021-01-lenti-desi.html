1,210 gravitational-lens candidates in the DESI Legacy Surveys

Applied to DR8 data from the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys, machine learning found 1,210 strong gravitational-lens candidates. The DESI collaboration says this is about twice the number of lenses known at the time; these are candidates, not objects individually confirmed in every case.

Technology: Residual neural network
