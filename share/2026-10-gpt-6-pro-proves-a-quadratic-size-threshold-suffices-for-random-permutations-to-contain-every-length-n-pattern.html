GPT-6 Pro proves a quadratic-size threshold suffices for random permutations to contain every length-n pattern

The paper proves that a uniformly random permutation of length 32n² contains every permutation of length n as a pattern with probability at least 1−exp(−c n/log n), for an absolute c>0 and sufficiently large n. This removes the prior log-log factor from the sufficient-length bound, resolves Alon’s open 1000n² problem, and establishes the correct Θ(n²) order.
