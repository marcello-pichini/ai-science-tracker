Deep-learning-guided cell-free screening identifies antimicrobial peptides

Generative and predictive deep-learning models proposed and prioritized peptide sequences for cell-free synthesis and screening. The authors report 30 functional antimicrobial peptides among 500 candidates tested across five design–build–test rounds. The assay tested peptides in unpurified cell-free mixtures, a limitation for interpreting activity and potency. The reported effects do not establish clinical efficacy or antibiotic approval.

Technology: Variational autoencoders; convolutional and recurrent neural-network regressors
