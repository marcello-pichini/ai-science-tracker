Random-forest selection and spectroscopy identify 20 new high-redshift quasars

A supervised random-forest search of Pan-STARRS1 and WISE photometry identified follow-up targets in the difficult redshift range around z≈5.5, where quasars resemble red and brown dwarfs. Spectroscopic observations of 37 candidates confirmed 20 previously unknown high-redshift quasars (18 at z=4.6–5.5 and two around z≈5.7), including a source outside common color-cut selections.

Technology: Supervised random-forest classification and regression applied to Pan-STARRS1 and WISE data
