AnomalyMatch finds over 1,300 unusual objects in the Hubble archive

The neural network scanned about 100 million cutouts from the Hubble Legacy Archive in roughly two and a half days: nearly 1,400 anomalies were flagged and over 1,300 confirmed, including more than 800 not previously documented in the literature (mergers, lenses, and jellyfish galaxies). AI only ranks candidates; the two counts are not explained, and no false-positive rate is given.

Technology: AnomalyMatch (neural network)
