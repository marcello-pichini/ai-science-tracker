Searching for antimicrobial peptides with Google Antigravity

An agentic workflow searched UniProt for antimicrobial candidates in Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae and found a 97-amino-acid bacteriocin with no homologues among manually reviewed Swiss-Prot proteins. This is a computational candidate now undergoing laboratory validation; no potency or toxicity data are available.

Technology: Google Antigravity (agentic platform)
