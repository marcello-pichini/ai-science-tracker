Machine-learning metabolomics identifies DHEAS and citrate signatures in anti-MDA5 dermatomyositis

In a single-centre cohort of 183 patients with idiopathic inflammatory myopathy and 104 healthy controls, seven machine-learning classifiers and LASSO feature selection were used with plasma and PBMC metabolomics to distinguish anti-MDA5-positive dermatomyositis. DHEAS and citrate were selected across the integrated analyses; plasma assays showed lower levels in anti-MDA5-positive cases, with concordant patterns reported in a separate 33-patient/33-control validation cohort. Within cases, DHEAS correlated inversely with rapidly progressive interstitial lung disease and citrate with rash.

Technology: Seven machine-learning classifiers (including random forest and XGBoost) with LASSO feature selection

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: In a single-centre cohort of 183 patients with idiopathic inflammatory myopathy and 104 healthy controls, seven machine-learning classifiers and LASSO feature selection were used with plasma and PBMC metabolomics to distinguish anti-MDA5-positive dermatomyositis.
  • Still unknown: Single-centre study with a relatively small validation group (33 anti-MDA5-positive cases and 33 controls). The metabolite associations with RP-ILD and rash are observational and do not establish causality. Reported discrimination is exploratory and does not establish a clinical diagnostic test or patient benefit; no treatment response was studied. DHEAS and citrate are known metabolites; the paper-specific result is their measured profile and candidate biomarker association in this disease cohort.
  • AI role: The models ranked discriminatory metabolites and selected features for disease classification; the team integrated these with pathway results to prioritize candidate biomarkers. DHEAS and citrate were then measured using ELISA and a biochemical assay, with patterns assessed in the validation cohort. Machine learning aided biomarker selection and classification; assays and clinical data supplied validation.
