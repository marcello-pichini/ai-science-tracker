Heat-resistant crops: a hybrid photosynthesis enzyme

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: At Michigan State University, AlphaFold predicted the 3D structure of glycerate kinase in a crop and a heat-loving alga. Replacing three flexible loops with rigid loops from the alga produced a hybrid that remained stable up to 65 °C. The enzyme has not yet been tested in whole plants.

Technology: AlphaFold

Family AlphaFold: profile · models page

Publication: Announcement only · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
AlphaFold predicted the enzyme's 3D structures, which were then used in molecular simulations to identify loops to modify.
Test and result
Hybrid glycerate kinase enzymes with three loops replaced by rigid loops from a heat-loving alga were tested in the lab, and one stayed stable up to 65 °C; whole-plant testing has not been done.
Limitation / still unknown
It is not yet known whether the hybrid enzyme improves heat resilience in a whole plant; the page does not say whether the AlphaFold-predicted structures were experimentally validated.
