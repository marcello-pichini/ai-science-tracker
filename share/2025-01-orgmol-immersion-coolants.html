Org-Mol prioritizes two experimental immersion-coolant candidates

A 3D transformer pretrained on molecular structures and fine-tuned for physical-property prediction screened millions of ester candidates. Researchers synthesized and characterized two selected candidates. One did not meet the preferred thermal-conductivity threshold. This is experimental candidate evaluation, not industrial deployment or environmental-safety validation.
