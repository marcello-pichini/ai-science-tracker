NeuralGCM: a hybrid physics-and-learning model for weather and climate

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: At 1.4° resolution, the model outperformed ECMWF-ENS in 95% of cases for forecasts from 2 to 15 days; at 2.8°, its mean 1980–2020 temperature error was 0.25 °C versus 0.75 °C for atmosphere-only models. It is more than 3,500 times faster than X-SHiELD on a TPU. It models only the atmosphere and is not yet a complete climate model.

Technology: NeuralGCM (differentiable dynamical core and neural networks)

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A neural network learns small-scale physics such as cloud formation, replacing hand-built parameterizations, coupled to a physics-based fluid dynamics solver.
Test and result
Evaluated with the WeatherBench 2 benchmark against ECMWF-ENS (better in 95% of 2-15 day cases at 1.4 degrees) and against AMIP climate baselines for 1980-2020 temperature (0.25 vs 0.75 degrees C error).
Limitation / still unknown
The model covers only the atmosphere and is not yet a complete climate model; oceans and the carbon cycle are future goals.
