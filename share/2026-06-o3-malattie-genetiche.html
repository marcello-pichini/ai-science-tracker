Genetic reanalysis assisted by o3 contributes to 18 diagnoses

Among 376 unresolved cases, o3 Deep Research proposed explanations reviewed by specialists: 18 diagnoses were clinically confirmed, seven of which had already been established elsewhere but were missing from local records. This was a retrospective study across heterogeneous cohorts, without a measure of time saved or effect on care; clinicians made the decisions.
