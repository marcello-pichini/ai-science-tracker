Zero-shot generative models design HER2 antibodies, with 71 low-nanomolar binders among 421 characterised

Source check: Needs source check. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Shanehsazzadeh et al. (Absci) described generative models for de novo antibody design against HER2 without further optimisation, combined with high-throughput screening of about 10^6 HCDR variants; binding rates were 10.6 percent (HCDR3) and 1.8 percent (HCDR123), 71 of 421 characterised binders had low-nanomolar affinity by SPR, 11 were functionally equivalent or better than trastuzumab, and one showed about 3x higher cell-based potency. Preprint, not peer-reviewed at this version; a later journal version should be checked. Abstract-level review.

Technology: Generative deep learning models for antibody design

Designed antibodies were expressed and measured by high-throughput screening and SPR, with comparison to trastuzumab (per abstract).
Limitation / still unknown
