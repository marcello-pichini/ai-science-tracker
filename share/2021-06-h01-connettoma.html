H01: reconstruction of one cubic millimeter of human cerebral cortex

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A sample of about 1 mm³ of human cortex was imaged at 4 nm across about 5,300 sections and automatically reconstructed: 1.4 petabytes, tens of thousands of neurons, 130 million annotated synapses, and 104 manually corrected cells. The sample is about one-millionth of a brain; reconstruction is imperfect and initial findings are preliminary.

Technology: Automated segmentation of electron microscopy images

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Automated machine-learning pipeline reconstructed neurons and synapses from electron microscopy images.
Test and result
The automated reconstruction was checked by manual proofreading of about 104 cells; the abstract says connections were validated.
Limitation / still unknown
Automated reconstruction is imperfect, the sample is about one-millionth of a brain, and findings are preliminary.
