WeatherNext 3: a global AI model for weather forecasting

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A model that learns from real-time geostationary satellite data and produces hourly forecasts, at resolutions down to 5 km for temperature and humidity (about five times finer than WeatherNext 2). For short-term precipitation, Google reports improvements of up to 60% over NASA's IMERG. These are ‘up to’ figures from Google's evaluations, with an independent ranking by Brightband.

AI contribution
The model ingests live geostationary satellite data and historical analyses, trains on station observations and produces gridded fields, cyclone tracks and station-level forecasts.
Test and result
Precipitation forecasts were scored with CRPS against baselines: gains of up to 60% over IMERG, 30% over MRMS and 10% over rain gauges for early lead times; independent live evaluations by Brightband ranked the model.
Limitation / still unknown
The 'up to' figures lack full test setup, longer-range gains are largest where forecasts are weak, and the atmosphere retains unpredictability.
