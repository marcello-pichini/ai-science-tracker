WeatherNext 3: a global AI model for weather forecasting

A model that learns from real-time geostationary satellite data and produces hourly forecasts, at resolutions down to 5 km for temperature and humidity (about five times finer than WeatherNext 2). For short-term precipitation, Google reports improvements of up to 60% over NASA's IMERG. These are ‘up to’ figures from Google's evaluations, with an independent ranking by Brightband.
