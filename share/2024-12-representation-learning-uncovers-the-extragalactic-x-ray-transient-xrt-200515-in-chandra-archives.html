Representation learning uncovers the extragalactic X-ray transient XRT 200515 in Chandra archives

A representation-learning pipeline searched archived Chandra event data and identified XRT 200515, a previously unnoticed extragalactic fast X-ray transient in the Large Magellanic Cloud. Its light curve has a sharp sub-10-second hard burst followed by an approximately 800-second oscillating tail with spectral softening. The paper discusses possible origins including a low-energy giant magnetar flare or a Type I burst from a faint low-mass X-ray binary; the source's physical nature is unresolved.

Technology: Unsupervised representation-learning pipeline using sparse autoencoders, dimensionality reduction and density-based clustering
