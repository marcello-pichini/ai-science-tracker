ModelAngelo: automated atomic model building in cryo-EM maps

A single graph neural network combining cryo-EM map density, protein sequence and local geometry builds protein models that the authors report are of similar quality to those built by human experts. Its sequence identification, using predicted amino acid probabilities in hidden Markov model searches, outperformed human experts on proteins of unknown sequence. Evidence is benchmark-based on the authors' own comparisons, with no new biological finding reported.

Technology: ModelAngelo graph neural network with hidden Markov model sequence search
