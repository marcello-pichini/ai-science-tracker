Spectroscopic confirmation of neural-network lens candidates from DESI imaging

A residual neural network selected strong gravitational lens candidates in the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys. VLT/MUSE spectroscopy of 76 candidates (223 spectra) confirmed 55 lenses with both lens and source redshifts, 15 with lens redshift only, and found 6 non-lenses. Caveat: the source is an arXiv preprint (submitted 22 September 2025) that does not name the network architecture or list institutions on its abstract page.

Technology: residual neural network (convolutional classifier for strong lens candidates)
