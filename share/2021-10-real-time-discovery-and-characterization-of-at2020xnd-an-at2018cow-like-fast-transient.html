Real-time discovery and characterization of AT2020xnd, an AT2018cow-like fast transient

ZTF observations identified AT2020xnd (ZTF20acigmel) at redshift 0.243 as the first unambiguous AT2018cow analogue found and confirmed in real time. Follow-up showed a rapid optical rise, persistently hot blue emission, luminous radio, millimetre and X-ray output, no classical radioactive-supernova peak, and a dwarf-galaxy host. The authors argue that these properties support a distinct fast-blue-optical-transient phenomenon, while its progenitor and central engine remain uncertain.

Technology: Zwicky Transient Facility real-time alert stream with a deep-learning real-bogus classifier and a custom contextual transient filter
