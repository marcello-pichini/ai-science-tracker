Learned cluster expansion guides an acidic oxygen-evolution catalyst

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A fitted energy model and quantum-inspired optimizer selected a mixed-oxide catalyst family for laboratory testing. The study reports a Ru-Cr-Mn-Sb oxide catalyst with higher ruthenium-normalized activity than its RuO2 comparator and sustained operation in the tested setup. This is one bounded materials result, not commercial electrolyzer validation or evidence that the optimizer itself is a learned model. The catalyst result already appears in the May 2022 preprint.

Technology: DFT-trained cluster expansion with quantum-inspired search
