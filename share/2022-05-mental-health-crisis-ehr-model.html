Machine learning model predicts mental health crises from health records

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A model using electronic health records to flag mental health crises over 28 days reached an AUROC of 0.797, with 58% sensitivity at 85% specificity. In a six-month prospective workflow study at one NHS trust, clinicians rated predictions useful for managing caseloads or mitigating crisis risk in 64% of cases. This measures clinician-reported usefulness, not a demonstrated reduction in crises; generalizability remains limited by the single-trust cohort, and precision was modest (AUPRC 0.159). The study includes authors affiliated with the developer Koa Health.
