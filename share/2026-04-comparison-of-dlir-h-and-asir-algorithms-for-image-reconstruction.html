Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The study compared deep-learning-based image reconstruction (DLIR-H) with ASIR on low-dose pediatric chest CT, reporting image-quality and diagnostic performance measures in children with Mycoplasma pneumonia; this is a specific imaging result.

Technology: Deep-learning reconstruction DLIR-H reconstructs low-dose pediatric chest CT images for comparison with adaptive statistical iterative reconstruction (ASIR).

Editorial relevance 47 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Deep-learning reconstruction DLIR-H reconstructs low-dose pediatric chest CT images for comparison with adaptive statistical iterative reconstruction (ASIR).
Test and result
In 142 children with Mycoplasma pneumoniae pneumonia, DLIR-H had lower background noise and higher lesion contrast-to-noise ratio than ASIR-80%; radiologists gave it the highest subjective image-quality score.
Limitation / still unknown
Retrospective single-vendor study; lung-parenchyma SNR was lower with DLIR-H, diagnostic accuracy was not assessed, and prospective multicenter validation is needed.
