LSTM-designed peptides show antibacterial activity and mixed efficacy in a mouse sepsis model

A generative long short-term-memory recurrent neural network produced 198 candidate antimicrobial peptides; five were synthesized. PEP-38 and PEP-137 inhibited carbapenem-resistant Klebsiella isolates in vitro, and PEP-137 also inhibited Pseudomonas aeruginosa. In a small mouse K. pneumoniae sepsis experiment, PEP-137 yielded 50% survival, PEP-38 did not improve survival, and the in-vitro-inactive PEP-36 control unexpectedly yielded 66.7% survival.

Technology: Generative long short-term memory recurrent neural network trained on antimicrobial peptide sequences
