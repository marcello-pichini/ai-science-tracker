ESM Metagenomic Atlas: over 600 million protein structures

Meta AI published predicted structures for almost the entire MGnify90 database (over 600 million), generated in about two weeks on roughly 2,000 GPUs with a 15-billion-parameter language model, up to 60 times faster than previous methods. These are predictions, and high-confidence structures are a much smaller subset.

Technology: ESMFold (15-billion-parameter language model)
