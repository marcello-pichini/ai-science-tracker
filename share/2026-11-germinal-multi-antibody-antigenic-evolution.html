Antibody design across observed and forecasted antigenic evolution

The Germinal Multi preprint abstract reports antibodies designed against several antigen variants at once. Experimental screens of tens of designs per campaign found binders to resistance-associated EGFR variants and neutralizers of historical SARS-CoV-2 variants, including variants withheld from design. Redesign of BD55-1205 restored neutralization of a forecast-guided engineered escape variant while retaining parental-variant activity. The escape result concerns an engineered variant, not a demonstrated future circulating strain. Review is limited to the abstract and original-server metadata.

Technology: Germinal Multi (computational antibody design framework); evolutionary forecasting model
