Conversations with an AI chatbot durably reduce conspiracy beliefs

Across experiments with more than 2,000 participants, three dialogues with GPT-4 reduced belief in the targeted conspiracy theory by about 20%, and the effect persisted for two months and spilled over to unrelated conspiracies. A professional fact-checker rated 99.2% of 128 sampled chatbot claims as true. Caveats: lab setting, the chatbot did not reduce belief in true conspiracies, and most participants still held the hoax belief.

Technology: GPT-4 (DebunkBot dialogue system)
