EnzySFC discovers nitrile-hydratase activity in nitrilases and evolves amide-selective variants

The AI-assisted EnzySFC workflow experimentally validated 10 previously uncharacterized nitrilases selected from 1,113 candidates. A wild-type Phototrophicales nitrilase showed exclusive nitrile hydratase activity; a separate Actinomycetia nitrilase was AI-evolved, with four tested top variants reaching 100% amide yield and complete substrate conversion.

Technology: EnzySFC AI-assisted enzyme discovery and functional-evolution platform
