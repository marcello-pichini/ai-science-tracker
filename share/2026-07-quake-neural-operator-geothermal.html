Quake Neural Operator builds microseismic catalogues without phase picking

The Quake Neural Operator detects and locates microseismic events directly from continuous data without explicit phase picking. Applied to the Geysers geothermal field, it finds nearly an order of magnitude more seismic detections than routine catalogues, checked against the Phase Neural Operator and visual inspection rather than an independent ground-truth catalogue.

Technology: Quake Neural Operator (QNO), multi-task neural operator for classification and regression
