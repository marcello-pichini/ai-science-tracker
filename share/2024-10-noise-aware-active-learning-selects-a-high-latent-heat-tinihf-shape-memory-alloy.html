Noise-aware active learning selects a high-latent-heat Ti–Ni–Hf shape-memory alloy

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A noise-aware Kriging model with active-learning acquisition selected candidate compositions from a million-composition NiTi-based alloy design space. After four experiments, researchers reported Ti₀.₂₆₆Ni₀.₄₈₄Hf₀.₂₅ with a measured latent heat of −36.08 J g⁻¹, 9.2% greater in magnitude than the best value in the initial training data (−33.04 J g⁻¹). The alloy also had an austenite-finish temperature of 481.71 °C and relatively small transformation hysteresis, making it a candidate for high-temperature thermal energy storage.

Technology: Noise-aware Kriging surrogate model with an active-learning acquisition strategy incorporating experimental-data noise

Editorial relevance 47 materials Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The model estimated the measurement-noise level from noisy experimental data, incorporated that level into Kriging predictions, and used a noise-augmented acquisition strategy to choose the next NiTi-based compositions. Four experiments in the closed loop identified Ti0.266Ni0.484Hf0.25 as a promising high-latent-heat alloy; researchers synthesized and measured the material properties.
Test and result
A noise-aware Kriging model with active-learning acquisition selected candidate compositions from a million-composition NiTi-based alloy design space.
Limitation / still unknown
The reported latent heat retains the source’s negative sign; the 9.2% improvement is a comparison of absolute magnitudes against the initial dataset’s best value. This is one material result from an active-learning search, not exhaustive exploration of the million-composition space. The reported temperature/hysteresis properties are laboratory characterizations; the paper does not demonstrate a deployed thermal-storage module or independent reproduction.
