AlphaGeometry: Olympiad geometry without human demonstrations

A language model proposing constructions and a symbolic engine deriving proofs solved 25 of 30 problems in the IMO-AG-30 benchmark (the previous method solved 10; the average gold medalist score was 25.9), trained on 100 million synthetic examples. It covers geometry only and a 30-problem benchmark.
