AlphaGeometry: Olympiad geometry without human demonstrations

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A language model proposing constructions and a symbolic engine deriving proofs solved 25 of 30 problems in the IMO-AG-30 benchmark (the previous method solved 10; the average gold medalist score was 25.9), trained on 100 million synthetic examples. It covers geometry only and a 30-problem benchmark.

Technology: AlphaGeometry (language model and symbolic engine)

AI contribution
A language model proposes auxiliary constructions and a symbolic engine derives proofs, looping until a proof is found.
Test and result
On the IMO-AG-30 benchmark of 30 Olympiad geometry problems, the system solved 25 versus 10 for the previous method, with each solution checked by computer.
Limitation / still unknown
It covers geometry only, roughly a third of Olympiad problems, and approaches but does not match gold-medalist performance.
