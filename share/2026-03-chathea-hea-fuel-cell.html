ChatHEA assistant guides high-entropy alloy catalysts for fuel cells

ChatHEA, a domain-specific LLM assistant for high-entropy alloy electrocatalysis, guided synthesis and screening of 100 five-element alloy catalysts. The FeCoCuPtIr catalyst reached a peak power density of 0.789 W cm-2 in a fuel cell and outperformed commercial Pt/C in electrochemical tests. These figures are reported by the institutional press release; its numeric activity values and DOE target are not given. The catalog date is the paper's 14 March 2026 publication date, stated in the 8 July press release; the full paper was not reviewed.

Technology: ChatHEA (LLM-based domain assistant)
