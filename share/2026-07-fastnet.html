FastNet: physically realistic AI forecasts from the Met Office

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Trained with a loss function that preserves energy across scales, it reaches a mean squared error equal to the Met Office global model and exceeds it on some metrics, with more realistic storm cores in Hurricane Ian and Storm Ciarán. The announcement gives no figures, covers only two extreme cases, and the system is not operational.

Technology: FastNet (spherical-harmonic loss)

Family FastNet: profile · models page

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A machine-learning weather model co-developed with the Alan Turing Institute, trained with a modified spherical-harmonic loss to keep energy across scales physically realistic.
Test and result
FastNet was compared with the Met Office global model on verification measures (RMSE and some other metrics, shown only in a figure) and qualitatively on Hurricane Ian and Storm Ciaran storm cores.
Limitation / still unknown
The model is not operational, the text gives no numerical results, only two extreme cases are described, and comparable skill does not establish the same validation as the Global Model.
