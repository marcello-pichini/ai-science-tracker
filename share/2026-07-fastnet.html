FastNet: physically realistic AI forecasts from the Met Office

Trained with a loss function that preserves energy across scales, it reaches a mean squared error equal to the Met Office global model and exceeds it on some metrics, with more realistic storm cores in Hurricane Ian and Storm Ciarán. The announcement gives no figures, covers only two extreme cases, and the system is not operational.

Technology: FastNet (spherical-harmonic loss)
