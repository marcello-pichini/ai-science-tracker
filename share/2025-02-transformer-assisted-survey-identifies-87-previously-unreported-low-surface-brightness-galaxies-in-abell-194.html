Transformer-assisted survey identifies 87 previously unreported low-surface-brightness galaxies in Abell 194

Transfer-learning transformer ensembles applied to deeper Hyper Suprime-Cam images of the Abell 194 cluster contributed to a vetted sample of 171 low-surface-brightness galaxies, including 87 reported as new discoveries. The models identified 159 sample members; Sérsic profile fitting and visual inspection were used to remove likely false positives, and the analysis characterized a subset of 28 ultra-diffuse galaxies. This is one paper-level survey result, not 87 separate catalog entries.

Technology: Ensembles of LSBG Detection Transformers (LSBG DETR) and LSBG Vision Transformers (LSBG ViT)

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Transfer-learning transformer ensembles applied to deeper Hyper Suprime-Cam images of the Abell 194 cluster contributed to a vetted sample of 171 low-surface-brightness galaxies, including 87 reported as new discoveries.
  • Still unknown: The paper reports photometrically defined LSBGs and does not provide spectroscopic confirmation for every new object. The 87 are a sample-level count, not individually confirmed galaxy memberships; 12 of the 171 sample objects were found by visual inspection rather than the transformer models, so AI is not attributed to every sample detection.
  • AI role: Applied models trained on Dark Energy Survey images to find low-surface-brightness structures in deeper HSC images from Abell 194 without fine-tuning; candidate masks were then assessed with Sérsic fits and human visual inspection.

Evidence sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03142
