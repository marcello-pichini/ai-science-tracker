Transformer-assisted survey identifies 87 previously unreported low-surface-brightness galaxies in Abell 194

Transfer-learning transformer ensembles applied to deeper Hyper Suprime-Cam images of the Abell 194 cluster contributed to a vetted sample of 171 low-surface-brightness galaxies, including 87 reported as new discoveries. The models identified 159 sample members; Sérsic profile fitting and visual inspection were used to remove likely false positives, and the analysis characterized a subset of 28 ultra-diffuse galaxies. This is one paper-level survey result, not 87 separate catalog entries.

Technology: Ensembles of LSBG Detection Transformers (LSBG DETR) and LSBG Vision Transformers (LSBG ViT)
