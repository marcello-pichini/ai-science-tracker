Machine learning and peptide-array experiments reveal new substrates for protein-modifying enzymes

A hybrid workflow coupled machine-learning models with enzyme-treated peptide arrays to predict previously unexplored protein post-translational-modification sites. Experimental validation confirmed 37–43% of proposed sites across SET8 and SIRT1–7; mass spectrometry confirmed dynamic methylation for several SET8 substrates and deacetylation at 64 unique SIRT2 sites. The work also found altered SET8 substrate networks associated with breast-cancer missense mutations.

Technology: Enzyme-specific ML-hybrid substrate prediction models trained from high-throughput peptide-array assays
