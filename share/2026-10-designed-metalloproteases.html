Deep-learning design of zinc metalloproteases

RFdiffusion2 for Molecular Interfaces designs zinc-dependent proteases for peptide-bond hydrolysis. The primary paper reports cleavage at the targeted bond in 36% of 135 designs and selected designs that cleave disease-associated proteins in vitro. These biochemical results do not establish therapeutic efficacy.

Technology: RFdiffusion2 for Molecular Interfaces (RFD2-MI); AlphaFold 3
