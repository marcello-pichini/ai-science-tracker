Novel AI-Based Algorithm for the Automated Computation of Coronal Parameters in Adolescent Idiopathic Scoliosis Patients: A Validation Study on 100 Preoperative Full Spine X-Rays

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An AI algorithm detected spinal structures and calculated coronal radiographic parameters in 100 preoperative adolescent idiopathic scoliosis images. Agreement with two physician raters was high for most measures (ICC 0.78–0.98), with weaker agreement for selected angles; this is retrospective measurement validation.

Technology: AI algorithm for spinal-structure detection and automated coronal radiographic measurement

Editorial relevance 53 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The AI algorithm detects spinal anatomy and automatically calculates coronal parameters from full-spine X-rays.
Test and result
In 100 images, algorithm-human ICCs ranged 0.78–0.98; clavicle angle and coronal balance agreement were among the highest reported.
Limitation / still unknown
Retrospective, single-center image comparison against physician measurements; no prospective workflow or patient-outcome evaluation.
