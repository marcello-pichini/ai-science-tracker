OCx24: experimental catalysts for evaluating prediction models

A dataset from Meta FAIR, the University of Toronto, and VSParticle covers over 600 mixed-metal catalysts made from 13 elements and tested under the same conditions, alongside analysis of over 19,000 materials. Hydrogen-evolution predictions flagged platinum-containing alloys as promising, although platinum was absent from training data. Fewer than 25% of produced catalysts matched the target; this was a pilot.

Technology: Catalyst prediction models (Open Catalyst)
