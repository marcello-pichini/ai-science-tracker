OCx24: experimental catalysts for evaluating prediction models

A dataset from Meta FAIR, the University of Toronto, and VSParticle covers over 600 mixed-metal catalysts made from 13 elements and tested under the same conditions, alongside analysis of over 19,000 materials. Hydrogen-evolution predictions flagged platinum-containing alloys as promising, although platinum was absent from training data. Fewer than 25% of produced catalysts matched the target; this was a pilot.

Technology: Catalyst prediction models (Open Catalyst)

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Preprint
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Computational predictions were compared with lab results: 441 gas diffusion electrodes tested in zero-gap electrolysis and 572 characterized samples, alongside DFT-verified adsorption energies on about 20,000 materials.
  • Still unknown: Platinum was ranked as a top hydrogen-evolution candidate but was not experimentally tested, and the abstract lists no explicit limitations.
  • AI role: AI-accelerated relaxations (685 million) fed a data-driven Sabatier volcano that flagged platinum-containing alloys as promising.

Evidence sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11783
