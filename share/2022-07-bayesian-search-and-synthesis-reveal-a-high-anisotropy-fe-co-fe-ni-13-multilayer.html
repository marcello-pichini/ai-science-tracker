Bayesian search and synthesis reveal a high-anisotropy [Fe/Co/Fe/Ni]₁₃ multilayer

A first-principles and Bayesian-optimization workflow selected [Fe/Co/Fe/Ni]₁₃ for experimental fabrication from four-element, four-layer-period magnetic multilayer candidates. The researchers grew the repeated monoatomic-layer structure and measured it with X-ray diffraction and SQUID magnetometry. They report a magnetic anisotropy constant of 374 kJ m⁻³ and saturation magnetization of 1313 emu cm⁻³, and identify the material as a new ferromagnetic multilayer with reported magnetocrystalline anisotropy exceeding L1₀ FeNi and FeCo benchmarks.

Technology: Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization with expected-improvement acquisition and a Matérn 5/2 kernel, coupled to first-principles magnetic-anisotropy calculations

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: A first-principles and Bayesian-optimization workflow selected [Fe/Co/Fe/Ni]₁₃ for experimental fabrication from four-element, four-layer-period magnetic multilayer candidates.
  • Still unknown: This is a proof-of-concept search over a restricted four-element, four-layer-period design space; the best candidate required 52 monoatomic layers in its repeated stack. The sample’s measured spontaneous easy axis remained in-plane and its shape-corrected effective anisotropy was negative, even though the authors report a large intrinsic magnetocrystalline-anisotropy value. Their proposed local perpendicular domains were a hypothesis requiring microscopy follow-up. The study does not establish a practical permanent magnet or device.
  • AI role: The model sequentially used first-principles magnetocrystalline-anisotropy calculations to rank discrete layer sequences and search for high-anisotropy multilayers. The top three candidates were fabricated and characterized; the resulting [Fe/Co/Fe/Ni]₁₃ experimental sample gave the paper’s highest measured anisotropy and saturation magnetization among the three. This ML-guided candidate selection and iterative calculation materially directed the new synthesis.
