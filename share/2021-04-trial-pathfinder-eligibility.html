Evaluating eligibility criteria of oncology trials using real-world data and AI

Emulating completed NSCLC trials on 61,094 patients showed many common exclusion criteria had minimal effect on hazard ratios; data-driven broadening more than doubled eligible patients on average and lowered hazard ratio by about 0.05.

Technology: Trial Pathfinder data-driven computational framework on real-world EHR data
