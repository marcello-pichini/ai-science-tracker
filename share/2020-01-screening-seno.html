An AI breast-cancer screening system evaluated in two countries

Among 25,856 women in the UK and 3,097 in the US, the system reduced false positives by 5.7% (US) and 1.2% (UK), and false negatives by 9.4% and 2.7% in absolute terms compared with radiologists. Trained only on UK data, it also outperformed specialists on US data. Retrospective evaluation (Nature): prospective studies are needed, and clinical deployment is unclear.

Technology: Neural network for mammograms
