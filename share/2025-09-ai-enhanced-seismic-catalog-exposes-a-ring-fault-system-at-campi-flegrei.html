AI-enhanced seismic catalog exposes a ring-fault system at Campi Flegrei

A retrained machine-learning workflow applied to continuous seismic records from 2022–2025 expanded the Campi Flegrei earthquake catalog from about 12,000 to more than 54,000 events. The more complete locations delineated a sharply defined, shallow caldera ring-fault system, illuminated previously unclear northern faults, and identified very shallow hybrid events likely associated with the hydrothermal system. The authors report no direct signature of upward magma migration in the analyzed data.

Technology: Retrained machine-learning seismic detection and phase-picking workflow developed at Stanford
