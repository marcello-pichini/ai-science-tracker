Text mining and machine learning guide Eu-activated phosphor wavelength prediction

A literature text-mining pipeline extracted phosphor/emission examples for model development; four Eu2+-activated candidates were synthesized and characterized. In the author manuscript, CaGd2S4:Eu2+ emitted at 440 nm versus 451 nm predicted under 365 nm excitation. Formula Li2SiN2 in one abstract conflicts with Li2SiN3 in results; this record does not claim that candidate formula.

Technology: Literature text-mining pipeline; genetic-algorithm feature selection; ML wavelength regression
