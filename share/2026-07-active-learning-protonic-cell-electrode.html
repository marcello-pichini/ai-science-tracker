Active learning selects an oxygen electrode for protonic ceramic cells

A supervised DFT-trained model and Gaussian-process experimental loop guided selection of BaCo0.8Nb0.1Zr0.1O3−δ. The publisher abstract reports 2.68 W cm−2 peak fuel-cell power at 600 °C and a separate 500-hour electrolysis test. These are specified laboratory cell tests; the Research Square v1 and journal versions differ in candidate counts and are not conflated here.

Technology: DFT-trained supervised model; Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization
