Active learning identifies a high-performing four-metal perovskite oxygen-evolution catalyst

A graph-based machine-learning model and closed-loop active-learning experiments identified a four-metal perovskite oxide with an intrinsic oxygen-evolution overpotential of 391 mV at 10 mA cm⁻²oxide, among the lowest reported for four-metal perovskite oxides. The study also reports recovery of several experimentally observed composition trends. This is an electrocatalyst material result; the abstract does not establish commercial electrolyzer performance.

Technology: Graph-based machine-learning model with active learning and closed-loop experimentation
