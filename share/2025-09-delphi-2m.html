Delphi-2M forecasts the risk of more than 1,000 diseases

A generative transformer trained on about 0.4 million UK Biobank participants and validated on 1.9 million people in Danish registries predicts rates for more than 1,000 diseases and simulates health trajectories up to 20 years ahead. Reported accuracy is comparable to single-disease models. Retrospective analysis: not a clinical tool and not causal.

Technology: Delphi-2M (GPT-style generative transformer)
