FermiNet: quantum physics of molecules from first principles

FermiNet approximates molecular ground-state electron wavefunctions from first principles. Against the coupled-cluster reference, it recovered 99.8% of the correlation energy beyond a single Slater determinant for the smallest molecules and at least 97% for bicyclobutane (30 electrons). It also modeled stretched nitrogen and hydrogen chains. These are computational results from the original work announced on 19 October 2020. The source page was updated on 22 August 2024 with a separate excited-state study; that later study's carbon-dimer error is not a 2020 result.
