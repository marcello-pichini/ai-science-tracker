Machine learning identifies alkaline laccases from Lepista nuda

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Machine-learning pH-optimum predictors screened metagenomic sequences, and two predicted Lepista nuda laccases were produced and profiled. The authors report LnLccA and LnLccB optimum activity at pH 10 and 9 for substrate 2,6-DMP, respectively, while both had pH 4 optima for ABTS. The substrate-dependent contrast is retained; it is not a general alkaline optimum or industrial demonstration.

Technology: Supervised, semi-supervised and unsupervised pH-optimum prediction models
