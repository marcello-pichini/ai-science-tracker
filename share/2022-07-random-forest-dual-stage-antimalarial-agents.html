Random forest prioritizes dual-stage antimalarial compounds

A random forest trained on parasite activity data screened commercial compounds; 100 predicted actives and 20 predicted inactives were tested. The primary manuscript reports 18 dose-response-validated compounds with liver- and blood-stage activity among 26 liver-stage hits. The selected sets are not population samples, and assays used P. berghei liver-stage and P. falciparum blood-stage parasites.

Technology: Random-forest classifier
