LLM-guided alloy design yields self-limiting oxidation behavior at 900 °C

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A knowledge-enhanced AI workflow led to Co22Cr24Fe20Ni25Mo5Mn4, a non-equiatomic high-entropy alloy with experimentally observed single-phase FCC structure and self-limiting oxidation kinetics at 900 °C. After about 50 hours, the study reports a steady-state oxidation-rate constant of 1.41 × 10⁻⁸ mgⁿ cm⁻²ⁿ h⁻¹ (reaction order n = 45.35) and a Cr₂O₃/(Mn,Fe)Cr₂O₄ bilayer oxide scale. The proposed barrier–buffer explanation is the authors’ mechanistic interpretation.

Technology: Physics-guided ML phase classifiers and LLM-assisted alloy-design workflow

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The authors used random-forest and support-vector classifiers trained on physics-based descriptors to prioritize single-phase alloy compositions. DeepSeek-R1-671B parsed literature processing descriptions into structured features. Separately, their LLM-agent workflow synthesized oxidation literature and mechanism knowledge to propose composition changes, including increasing Cr and adding about 4 at.% Mn; this guided selection of Co22Cr24Fe20Ni25Mo5Mn4 for synthesis and oxidation experiments. The paper does not clearly identify the model used for the reasoning-agent stage, so that identity is left unspecified.
Test and result
A knowledge-enhanced AI workflow led to Co22Cr24Fe20Ni25Mo5Mn4, a non-equiatomic high-entropy alloy with experimentally observed single-phase FCC structure and self-limiting oxidation kinetics at 900 °C.
Limitation / still unknown
The specific oxidation result is experimentally measured for one designed alloy under the paper’s 900 °C test conditions and time interval; it is not evidence of service lifetime or performance in a component. The bilayer-oxide barrier–buffer mechanism is the authors’ interpretation from characterization and kinetic behavior. The two additional non-equiatomic FCC/BCC alloys in the study validate phase predictions and remain bundled with the principal material result. The LLM reasoning-agent model is not identified clearly in the paper; DeepSeek-R1-671B is explicitly named for processing-text parsing.
