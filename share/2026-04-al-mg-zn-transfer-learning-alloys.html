CALPHAD-based transfer learning designs high-strength Al–Mg–Zn alloys

A CALPHAD-informed transfer-learning and multi-objective optimization workflow selected two laboratory alloys. The primary abstract reports tensile strengths of 472 ± 7 MPa and 569 ± 12 MPa, with elongations of 23.5 ± 0.5% and 14.9 ± 0.3%. These are measured alloy properties, not industrial qualification.

Technology: CALPHAD-informed transfer learning; ensemble regression; NSGA-II
