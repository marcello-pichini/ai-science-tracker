Cross-species machine learning identifies transcription factors that negatively regulate plant nitrogen-use efficiency

Evolutionarily informed XGBoost and GENIE3 analyses of conserved nitrogen-response expression patterns prioritized transcription factors associated with nitrogen-use efficiency in Arabidopsis and maize. Loss-of-function tests found higher NUE in Arabidopsis myb33 and tcp2 mutants under both low- and high-nitrogen conditions, and in six other tested TF mutants specifically under low nitrogen; a maize nfya3 mutant also showed higher nitrogen-use efficiency under sufficient nitrogen in field tests.

Technology: Evolutionarily informed XGBoost models and GENIE3 random-forest gene-regulatory-network inference
