Cross-linking mass spectrometry guided structure prediction of disordered Encephalitozoon hellem proteins

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Predicted a previously unreported Swp1b–EnP1 interaction/interface in the E. hellem spore wall; the restraint-guided model differs from low-confidence AlphaFold3 predictions and gives structural hypotheses for their interface. Three XL-MS runs supplied restraints; a fourth independent DSSO XL-MS run showed 51/68 links within 26 Å and 57/68 within 30 Å of the model. Authors also report that heterologous yeast-two-hybrid/co-IP attempts did not detect the interaction, no genetic perturbation system exists, and stoichiometry is assumed rather than established. Preprint abstract (PubMed PMID 42327317) dated 2026-06-12 already contains the same result; peer-reviewed journal version 2026-10-20. A preprint posted 2026-06-12 contained the result before the October 2026 journal version; attempted yeast-two-hybrid/co-immunoprecipitation did not detect the interaction, so the interface remains a model-supported hypothesis.

Technology: AlphaLink, a deep-learning/OpenFold-derived model, incorporates XL-MS distance restraints; MODELLER uses inter-protein restraints to dock Swp1b and EnP1.
