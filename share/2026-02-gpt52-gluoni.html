GPT-5.2 proposes a formula for nonzero gluon amplitudes

GPT-5.2 conjectured a formula for gluon scattering amplitudes with a single negative helicity in a special semi-collinear regime. An internal model produced a proof that the authors analytically verified; the announcement describes a preprint, not experimental confirmation.
