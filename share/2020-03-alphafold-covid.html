Predicted structures of SARS-CoV-2 proteins

DeepMind openly shared AlphaFold predictions, without waiting for review, for six poorly studied viral proteins (membrane, ORF3a, Nsp2, Nsp4, Nsp6, and the C-terminal domain of papain-like protease). They were not experimentally verified; an ORF3a experimental structure deposited in the PDB in June 2020 matched the second predicted model well.
