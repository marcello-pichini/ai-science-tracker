High-resolution machine-learning catalog outlines a potential unmapped fault in the Weiyuan shale-gas region

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A machine-learning event detector combined with waveform cross-correlation produced a high-resolution catalog for injection-induced seismicity in the Weiyuan area, identifying one-third more earthquakes than the routine catalog and improving magnitude completeness. Spatial migration patterns show a pore-pressure diffusion front; a cluster outlines a potential unmapped fault that may have hosted the region’s largest recorded event, the September 2019 Mw 5.2 earthquake.

Technology: Machine-learning earthquake detector combined with waveform cross-correlation (the abstract does not name a particular detector architecture)

Limitation / still unknown
The authors describe the fault as potential and state that it may have led to the September 2019 Mw 5.2 event; this is an interpretation, not a directly imaged fault or established causal proof. Results are from one field area and are limited by the station coverage.
