AlphaFold2-guided mining identifies context-independent cytidine deaminases for efficient base editing

Researchers used AlphaFold2 to predict structures for 1,483 cytidine deaminases, clustered the predictions and experimentally characterized representatives. The screen identified previously uncharacterized enzymes with high editing efficiency, and CD0208^P52A showed little sequence-context preference across AC/GC/TC/CC motifs, a narrow editing window and relatively low off-target activity. In mammalian cells, its base editor introduced stop codons in single- and multicopy genes without DNA double-strand breaks.

Technology: AlphaFold2 protein-structure prediction, followed by structural clustering of candidate cytidine deaminases
