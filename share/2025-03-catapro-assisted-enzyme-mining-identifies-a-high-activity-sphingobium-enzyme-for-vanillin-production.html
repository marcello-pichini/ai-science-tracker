CataPro-assisted enzyme mining identifies a high-activity Sphingobium enzyme for vanillin production

CataPro, a deep-learning model using pretrained protein-language representations and molecular fingerprints to predict enzyme kinetics, was combined with sequence and structure-based filtering to screen candidate enzymes for converting 4-vinylguaiacol to vanillin. The team experimentally identified Sphingobium sp. CSO (SsCSO), whose measured activity was 19.53 times that of the initial enzyme CSO2, and then used CataPro-guided engineering to increase SsCSO activity a further 3.34-fold.

Technology: CataPro
