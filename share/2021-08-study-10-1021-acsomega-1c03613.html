A Novel Scalarized Scaffold Hopping Algorithm with Graph-Based Variational Autoencoder for Discovery of JAK1 Inhibitors.

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: GraphGMVAE scaffold hopping from upadacitinib yielded seven synthesized JAK1 inhibitors from 25 prioritized candidates. In biochemical assays, separated Ten01/Ten02 had IC50 values of 5.0/16.8 nM; activity does not establish therapeutic efficacy.

Technology: GraphGMVAE graph variational autoencoder with message-passing encoders

Editorial relevance 47 medicine Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
GraphGMVAE, a graph variational autoencoder with Gaussian-mixture latent space, generated scaffold-hopped molecules from upadacitinib; docking and CNN affinity scoring helped prioritize compounds for synthesis.
Test and result
Seven synthesized compounds were tested in an ADP-Glo JAK1 kinase assay. Ten01 and Ten02 had IC50 values of 5.0 and 16.8 nM after chiral separation; the other isomeric mixtures ranged from 0.50 to 9.6 μM. The Ten01/Ten02 mixture had IC50 64 nM.
Limitation / still unknown
The assays establish biochemical JAK1 inhibition only. The campaign synthesized seven compounds from 25 prioritized molecules; some potency values followed chiral separation and other compounds were tested as isomeric mixtures.
