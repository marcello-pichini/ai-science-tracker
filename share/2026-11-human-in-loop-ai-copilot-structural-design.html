Human-in-the-loop AI copilot improves topology-optimized structure design

A machine-learning model trained on a synthetic dataset of probable human feedback flags regions of a topology-optimized design that a designer would likely change. The article reports a 39 percent improvement in manufacturability and linear buckling load, adding about 15 seconds of design time compared with all-algorithmic methods. It states the feedback training data is synthetic, editing is limited to thickening or thinning regions, and the approach complements rather than replaces AI-only methods.

Technology: Human-in-the-loop AI copilot with topology optimization (machine-learning region-flagging model)
