RFdiffusion: designing new proteins with a diffusion model

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A guided diffusion model generates tailored protein structures, such as proteins that bind tightly to another molecule, and outperforms previous methods across a wide range of problems. The post says only that ‘a useful fraction’ of generated designs work as intended, without figures; the software was released in March 2023.

Technology: RFdiffusion

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A generative diffusion model fine-tuned from RoseTTAFold generated protein backbones for monomer, binder, oligomer, enzyme scaffold and motif designs.
Test and result
Designs were characterized experimentally (symmetric assemblies, metal-binding proteins, protein binders), and a cryo-EM structure of a designed binder was nearly identical to the design model; the abstract reports no overall success rates.
Limitation / still unknown
The abstract states no explicit limitations; the press post gives no success rates, sample sizes or benchmark numbers and says only that a useful fraction of designs work.
