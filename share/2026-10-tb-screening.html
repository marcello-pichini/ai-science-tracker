Chest X-rays and AI for tuberculosis screening

Nexus AI says it has deployed Google Research's chest X-ray AI as a certified diagnostic tool at about 40 facilities in six countries: more than 25,000 X-rays assessed by over 90 clinicians, with response times under 45 seconds. These are author-reported deployment figures, with no sensitivity or patient outcome data; WHO and FDA approvals are still in progress.
