Computed structures of core eukaryotic protein complexes

Deep-learning structure modelling screened 8.3 million yeast protein pairs, identified 1505 likely interactions and built models for 106 previously unidentified assemblies and 806 not structurally characterised.

Technology: RoseTTAFold and AlphaFold applied to coevolution-paired alignments
