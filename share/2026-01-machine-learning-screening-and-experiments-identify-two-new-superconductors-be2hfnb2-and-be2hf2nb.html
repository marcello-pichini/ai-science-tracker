Machine-learning screening and experiments identify two new superconductors, Be₂HfNb₂ and Be₂Hf₂Nb

An AI-accelerated workflow screened more than 1.3 million material structures, combining a graph-neural-network model of superconducting properties with density-functional calculations and stability filters. The researchers experimentally synthesized and confirmed superconductivity in Be₂HfNb₂ and Be₂Hf₂Nb, reporting two previously unreported compounds among 741 computationally screened stable candidates. The two compounds were confirmed in the Be₂Nb₃ substitution series; other listed candidates remain computational predictions.

Technology: BEE-NET equivariant graph neural networks in an AI-accelerated high-throughput screening workflow
