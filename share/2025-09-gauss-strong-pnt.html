Gauss formalizes the Strong Prime Number Theorem in Lean

Math Inc reports that Gauss produced most statements and proofs for a Lean formalization of the strong Prime Number Theorem, with about 25,000 lines and 1,100 theorems/definitions in three weeks. Humans supplied mathematical scaffolding and reviewed strategies, and some files adapt prior work. The date follows the original project blueprint dated 11 September 2025, rather than an inferred announcement timestamp. This verifies a formalization of an existing theorem, not a new theorem.
