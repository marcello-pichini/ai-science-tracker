AI-guided autonomous experiments map phase domains in bismuth–titanium oxide films

A robotic active-learning campaign using SARA-H mapped processing regions that stabilize δ-Bi₂O₃ and Bi₂Ti₂O₇ in thin films and examined dwell-dependent phase behavior in the Bi–Ti–O system. The study also experimentally supported the prediction that bismuth substitution in TiO₂ inhibits the anatase-to-rutile transformation. This is a human-in-the-loop experimental materials result; the compounds/phases were not all newly discovered, while the mapped processing domains and comparative evidence are the study-specific contribution.

Technology: SARA-H (Scientific Autonomous Reasoning Agent with human input) and automated probabilistic phase labeling
