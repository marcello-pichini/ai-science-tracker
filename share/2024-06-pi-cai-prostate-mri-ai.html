PI-CAI: AI matches or beats radiologists in prostate cancer detection on MRI

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In a reader study with 62 radiologists on 400 cases, the AI detected about 6.8% more clinically significant cancers at the same specificity and produced about 50% fewer false positives. On 1,000 routine-practice cases, however, non-inferiority was not confirmed (specificity 68.9% vs 69.0% at 96.1% sensitivity). The authors call for prospective validation, and the system is not available for clinical use.

Technology: PI-CAI challenge super-algorithm (ensemble of top five submitted deep learning models)

AI contribution
A deep-learning system detected clinically significant prostate cancer on MRI.
Test and result
A multireader study with 62 radiologists on 400 paired cases compared AI and PI-RADS 2.1 reads, and 1,000 routine-practice cases were used to test non-inferiority against historical clinical readings.
Limitation / still unknown
Non-inferiority to routine practice was not confirmed (specificity 68.9% vs 69.0%); the authors state prospective validation is needed and the system is not available for clinical use.
