Bayesian optimization improves printed BiSbTe thermoelectrics

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Learned models guided ink formulation and printing settings for known BiSbTe-based material. The resulting printed samples reached a reported room-temperature zT of 1.3. This is an experimental processing result, rather than discovery of a new chemical composition or demonstrated deployed-device efficiency. The result was public in the July 2024 preprint before the journal version.

Technology: Gaussian-process regression and constrained Bayesian optimization
