EqGPT infers a previously unreported equation for steepening surface gravity waves

A knowledge-guided generative model trained on mathematical handbook equations generated candidate PDEs, then evaluated and optimized them against data. In a real-data case study of strongly nonlinear surface gravity waves approaching breaking, the authors report an equation not previously reported for that process.

Technology: EqGPT, a generative transformer using equation tokenization and a generation–evaluation–optimization loop
