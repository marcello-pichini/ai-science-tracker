Euclid and machine-learning selection identify 31 spectroscopically confirmed quasars at redshift 6.6–7.8

Using Euclid Wide Survey photometry over about 3,000 square degrees, the team applied several photometric selection methods, including XGBoost classifiers and probabilistic modeling, then obtained telescope spectra for candidates. Spectroscopy confirmed 31 new quasars at redshifts 6.6–7.8; 12 are at z≥7, and the paper reports EUCL J172902.75+641018.1 at z≈7.77 as the highest-redshift quasar known at publication. The sample extends quasar studies to fainter luminosities at z>7.

Technology: XGBoost machine-learning classifiers and Extreme-Deconvolution high-redshift quasar selection, alongside probabilistic spectral-energy-distribution methods
