AI screening identifies poly-PDO for recyclable barrier packaging

Machine-learning models screened approximately 7.4 million virtual ring-opening polymerization polymers for properties relevant to recyclable food packaging. Researchers experimentally evaluated existing poly(p-dioxanone), whose barrier performance had not previously been reported in the reviewed source, and reported water-barrier properties and 95% monomer recovery. The result is a specific material characterization and packaging candidate, not a newly invented polymer or demonstrated commercial package. The arXiv v1 already reports the measured outcome; later journal publication is the same study.
