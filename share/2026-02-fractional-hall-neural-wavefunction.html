First-principles neural network finds crystallization of fractional quantum Hall liquids

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A self-attention neural-network wavefunction (MagNet), trained only by minimising the microscopic Hamiltonian of interacting electrons in a magnetic field, finds both topological liquid and electron-crystal ground states across a range of Landau-level mixing. The authors present it as a method that discovers correlated phases without assumptions. This is an arXiv preprint with computational results only, not yet checked experimentally or peer reviewed.
