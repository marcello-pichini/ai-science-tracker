IBM designs two new antimicrobial peptides with generative models

Over 48 days, 20 candidates were designed, synthesized, and tested; two showed activity against several pathogens, including multidrug-resistant Klebsiella pneumoniae. In vitro and mouse tests only, with no demonstration of safety or efficacy in humans.

Technology: Generative autoencoder and CLaSS
