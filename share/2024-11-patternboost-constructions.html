PatternBoost: constructions in mathematics with a little help from AI

PatternBoost alternates a local search that generates candidate constructions with a transformer trained on the best ones. The authors report the best known solutions to several extremal combinatorics problems, including a counterexample to a conjecture that had been open for 30 years. Candidates are scored mechanically, so each construction is checked by computer; the abstract does not name the conjecture and the paper was not peer-reviewed at the time of posting.

Technology: PatternBoost (transformer trained on search outputs, combined with local search)
