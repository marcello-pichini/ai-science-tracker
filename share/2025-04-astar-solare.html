Seven organic-solar-cell molecules designed with generative models

Generative and predictive models, refined with experts and experiments, suggested new non-fullerene acceptors for organic solar cells. Seven molecules were synthesized and tested, with efficiencies close to predictions; the news item gives no efficiency values or advantage over the best existing materials.
