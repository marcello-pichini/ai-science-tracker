Machine learning nominates synergistic drug combinations in pancreatic cancer cells

Three research groups used machine-learning models trained on 496 measured PANC-1 drug pairs to nominate additional combinations. Of 88 nominated pairs tested, 51 met the study’s synergy threshold. The article also reports 307 synergistic combinations including the 256-pair training screen; the 307 total is not a count of new AI-nominated hits. The result is limited to cell assays.

Technology: Random forests; XGBoost; deep neural networks; graph convolutional networks
