DefensePredictor uncovers dozens of previously unknown bacterial anti-phage systems

DefensePredictor, a machine-learning classifier using protein-language-model embeddings, nominated candidate defense genes across diverse E. coli strains. Of 94 predicted gene clusters selected for testing, experiments validated 42 previously unknown systems; analyses of 1,000 prokaryotic genomes also revealed thousands of uncharacterized protein clusters without homology to known systems.

Technology: DefensePredictor, gradient-boosted classification over protein-language-model embeddings and genomic context
