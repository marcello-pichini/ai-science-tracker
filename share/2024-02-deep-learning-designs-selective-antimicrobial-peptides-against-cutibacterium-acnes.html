Deep learning designs selective antimicrobial peptides against Cutibacterium acnes

A deep-learning design pipeline combining sequence generation and classifiers, transfer learning, pretrained protein embeddings and C. acnes-specific training data produced 42 novel peptides for testing. Five showed high-potency, selective activity against C. acnes, with MICs of 2–4 µg/mL; additional in-vitro assays tested activity against other microbes, hemolysis and keratinocyte cytotoxicity.

Technology: Deep-learning peptide generators and activity/selectivity classifiers using transfer learning and pretrained protein embeddings
