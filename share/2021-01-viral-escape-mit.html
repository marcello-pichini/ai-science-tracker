Language models identify viral regions less prone to immune escape

Analysis of influenza, HIV, and coronavirus sequences identifies regions of surface proteins less likely to mutate to escape immune responses, including the S2 subunit of spike. These are computational leads for studying possible vaccine targets, not validated vaccines.

Technology: Protein-sequence language model
