LLM-based virtual screening identifies two NIR-II fluorophores tested in vitro and in vivo

Researchers trained a large-language-model framework to predict fluorophore emission wavelengths and used virtual screening to prioritize NIR-II imaging molecules. Two candidates were experimentally characterized and showed favorable imaging performance in vitro and in vivo, according to the abstract. The abstract supplies no quantitative imaging effect sizes. This summary is limited to the abstract and October 15, 2026 publication metadata; the candidates are imaging probes.

Technology: Large language model for structure-property prediction, with virtual screening
