Generative models forecast retinal structure and function from patient histories

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The first public version reported forecasts of OCT scans and logMAR visual acuity on OLIVES data (SSIM 0.9264, FID 11.9, PSNR 38.1 dB; logMAR MAE 0.052). The later journal article revised the generative cohort and evaluation design. Both evaluations are retrospective dataset analyses, not external clinical validation or evidence of treatment benefit.

Technology: Multimodal StyleGAN-inspired GAN, DeepShallow LSTM and EfficientNet

Editorial relevance 47 medicine PreprintScientific article Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A multimodal generative model synthesized future OCT scans, an LSTM forecast visual acuity, and an image classifier predicted retinal biomarkers from longitudinal patient data.
