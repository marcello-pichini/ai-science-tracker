Claude optimizes more than 30 open models for biology

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In just under four weeks, Claude optimized over 30 models for structure, protein design, and genomics. They were on average four times faster with a small accuracy loss, and nearly twice as fast with identical outputs. It also created a low-memory mode for systems over 10,000 tokens on a single GPU node. Design results are in silico only, and comparisons are internal to Anthropic.

AI contribution
Claude wrote custom FlashPairformer kernels, optimized more than 30 models, built a low-memory Big mode, and ran autonomous protein binder design.
Test and result
Accelerated models were checked against their downstream tasks (for example structure prediction) and FlashPairformer kernels were compared with cuEquivariance (2.7-2.9x faster on triangle attention); design results are in silico with internal comparisons.
Limitation / still unknown
Design results have no wet-lab validation, large viral capsid predictions were incorrect, ColabFold kernels were not benchmarked, and the model is not reproducible.
