Machine-learning search of archived spectra reveals vinyl–NHC coupling products in palladium catalysis

MEDUSA Search mined archived high-resolution mass spectra and uncovered previously unreported vinyl–N-heterocyclic-carbene (NHC) coupling products in Pd/NHC-catalyzed Mizoroki–Heck reactions. Researchers retrieved the corresponding reaction records and independently tested the proposed transformation with multiple palladium–NHC complexes; ultrahigh-resolution mass spectrometry and MS/MS supported the product formulas and structures. The work also found an ethyl–NHC product across tested reaction mixtures, suggesting additional catalyst-transformation pathways.

Technology: MEDUSA Search, an isotope-distribution-driven HRMS search engine with machine-learning ion-threshold regression and false-positive classification

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Tested: MEDUSA Search mined archived high-resolution mass spectra and uncovered previously unreported vinyl–N-heterocyclic-carbene (NHC) coupling products in Pd/NHC-catalyzed Mizoroki–Heck reactions.
  • Still unknown: The claimed novelty is specific to vinyl–NHC coupling products in the studied Pd/NHC catalytic context. HRMS formulas alone cannot distinguish structural isomers, so the paper's MS/MS, ultrahigh-resolution measurements, reaction notebooks and independent experiments are essential evidence. The authors state that most other detected ions could not be associated confidently with reaction conditions because of incomplete metadata; those are not counted here.
  • AI role: Researchers generated candidate product-ion formulas from reaction fragments and catalysts; MEDUSA Search screened archived spectra using isotope-pattern matching, with two ML models estimating ion-presence thresholds and filtering likely false positives. It surfaced previously overlooked ions in old spectra; follow-up experiments with different NHC ligands and MS/MS tests checked the proposed vinyl–NHC products.
