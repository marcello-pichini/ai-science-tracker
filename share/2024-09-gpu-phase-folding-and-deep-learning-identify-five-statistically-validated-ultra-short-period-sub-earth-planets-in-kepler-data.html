GPU phase folding and deep learning identify five statistically validated ultra-short-period sub-Earth planets in Kepler data

GPU phase folding and deep learning identify five statistically validated ultra-short-period sub-Earth planets in Kepler data

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A GPU-based transit search paired with a convolutional neural network identified five sub-Earth-size ultra-short-period transit signals in Kepler light curves: Kepler-158d, Kepler-963c, Kepler-879c, Kepler-1489c, and KOI-4978.02. The authors applied transit vetting, false-alarm tests, and statistical false-positive probabilities below 1% to each, and statistically validated the five signals as planets. The validation is photometric/statistical rather than independent radial-velocity or spectroscopic confirmation; KOI-4978.02 remains designated as a KOI object in the source.

Technology: GPFC algorithm: GPU phase folding with convolutional neural network transit scoring
