Deep learning-guided synthesis yields fluid ferroelectric molecules

A graph variational autoencoder proposes molecules while learned classifiers and regressors, retrosynthesis and stock-inventory constraints guide selection. The journal version reports 11 first-generation materials characterized through mixture-based extrapolation and two additional materials selected in a second iteration that exhibited the ferroelectric-nematic phase in their neat state. The first arXiv version (2025-12-18) contained the first-generation mixture-based work, not the later neat-state confirmation; this draft uses the journal date for that result. Predictions have material errors, and the work does not establish device performance.

Technology: Graph variational autoencoder; graph neural-network ensembles; XGBoost
