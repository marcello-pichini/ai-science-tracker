Bayesian search identifies organic metallophotocatalyst formulations

Gaussian-process models and chemist decisions guided synthesis and testing of organic photocatalysts for a specified cross-coupling reaction. A catalyst search reached 67% yield before reaction-condition optimization. This establishes a bounded formulation result for the studied reaction, not discovery of the reaction or general superiority to iridium catalysts.

Technology: Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization
