Prospective machine-learning screen identifies six previously unreported OATP1B1 inhibitors

Researchers trained and compared seven machine-learning classifiers for OATP1B1 transporter inhibition. In prospective CHO-cell testing, 15 of 19 compounds predicted active by SVC models inhibited the transporter; six (abamectin, asiaticoside, berbamine, doramectin, mobocertinib and umbralisib) were described as previously unreported OATP1B1 inhibitors.

Technology: Seven molecular-fingerprint machine-learning classifiers, with prospective selection by support vector classifier (SVC)
