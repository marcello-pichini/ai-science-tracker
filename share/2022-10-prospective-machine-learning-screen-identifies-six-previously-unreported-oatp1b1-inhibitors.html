Prospective machine-learning screen identifies six previously unreported OATP1B1 inhibitors

Researchers trained and compared seven machine-learning classifiers for OATP1B1 transporter inhibition. In prospective CHO-cell testing, 15 of 19 compounds predicted active by SVC models inhibited the transporter; six (abamectin, asiaticoside, berbamine, doramectin, mobocertinib and umbralisib) were described as previously unreported OATP1B1 inhibitors.

Technology: Seven molecular-fingerprint machine-learning classifiers, with prospective selection by support vector classifier (SVC)

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: In prospective CHO-cell testing, 15 of 19 compounds predicted active by SVC models inhibited the transporter; six (abamectin, asiaticoside, berbamine, doramectin, mobocertinib and umbralisib) were described as previously unreported OATP1B1 inhibitors. The new candidate screen used contracted Eurofins assays; external execution is not a separate replication study.
  • Still unknown: The 84% refers to a small prospective test set of 19 compounds, and transporter inhibition was measured in vitro in engineered CHO cells. These findings flag possible drug–drug interaction liability; they do not show clinical interaction or patient outcome. The authors qualify six inhibitors as appearing novel at publication.
  • AI role: Models trained on in-vitro OATP1B1 inhibition data ranked compounds for prospective testing. Researchers tested 19 model-selected compounds in transporter-expressing CHO cells and observed inhibition for 15, including six compounds the authors described as novel OATP1B1 inhibitors.
