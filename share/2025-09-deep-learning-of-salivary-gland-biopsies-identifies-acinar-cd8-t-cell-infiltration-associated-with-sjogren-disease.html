Deep learning of salivary-gland biopsies identifies acinar CD8 T-cell infiltration associated with Sjögren disease

Deep learning of salivary-gland biopsies identifies acinar CD8 T-cell infiltration associated with Sjögren disease

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A deep-learning model trained on digitized minor salivary-gland biopsy slides from five expert centres and externally validated at a sixth classified focus score and Sjögren disease in a retrospective cohort of 545 participants. Explainable-model analysis identified a histological pattern of CD8-positive T cells around acinar epithelial cells associated with Sjögren disease. External-validation AUROC was 0.89 for disease classification and 0.92 among patients negative for anti-SSA antibodies.

Technology: Deep-learning histopathology classifier with Shapley-value interpretation

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The authors trained the deep-learning model on digitalized biopsy slides to classify focus score and Sjögren disease, tested generalization at a held-out centre, and used Shapley-value explanations to identify histological patterns associated with model decisions. The study reports acinar CD8-positive T-cell infiltration as a disease-associated pattern.
Test and result
A deep-learning model trained on digitized minor salivary-gland biopsy slides from five expert centres and externally validated at a sixth classified focus score and Sjögren disease in a retrospective cohort of 545 participants.
Limitation / still unknown
Retrospective multicentre cohort; external validation used one held-out expert centre. The authors state further validation is needed. The CD8 acinar-infiltration pattern was associated with disease classification and is not established as causal, pathognomonic, or clinically actionable. The study does not establish improved patient outcomes or replace expert pathology.
