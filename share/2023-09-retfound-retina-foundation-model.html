RETFound: a foundation model for disease detection from retinal images

RETFound is self-pretrained on unlabelled retinal images, reportedly about 1.6 million from Moorfields Eye Hospital, and then adapted to disease tasks. The authors report better diagnosis of sight-threatening eye diseases than comparison models, better prediction of systemic conditions such as heart failure and myocardial infarction, and strong performance with fewer labels. The evaluation is retrospective, with no clinical deployment study, and the model was released openly.

Technology: RETFound (masked-autoencoder foundation model for retinal images)
