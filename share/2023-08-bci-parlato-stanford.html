A neuroprosthesis translates attempted speech at 62 words per minute

For a participant with ALS, intracortical electrodes and an AI algorithm converted attempted speech into text at 62 words per minute. With a 125,000-word vocabulary, the error rate was 23.8%, versus 9.1% for only 50 words. Proof of concept in one person, with an invasive implant and experimental device.

Technology: Neural network for phoneme decoding and language model
