Machine learning prioritizes microbiome metabolites that stimulate interleukin-8 secretion

Researchers trained ML models on public drug-development data to prioritize biological properties of microbiome metabolites, then tested predictions prospectively. In vitro experiments identified previously unreported IL-8 secretion stimulation by spermine and spermidine, metabolites previously regarded as anti-inflammatory. The paper reports average accuracy of 92.86% in prospective experiments, while cautioning that sample sizes were small.

Technology: Machine-learning property prediction models implemented in KNIME and trained on public drug-development data
