Autonomous robotic system maps Bi2O3 synthesis phase diagram with active learning

The SARA agent combined robotic laser spike annealing, optical characterization and nested active learning to map synthesis phase boundaries of Bi2O3. It reached orders-of-magnitude faster establishment of the phase diagram and identified conditions that stabilize metastable delta-Bi2O3. Validation is experimental but restricted to one oxide system.

Technology: Scientific Autonomous Reasoning Agent (SARA) with hierarchical active learning and uncertainty quantification
