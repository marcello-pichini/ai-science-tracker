Polybot: an autonomous lab finds processing recipes for conductive polymer films

Polybot used importance-guided Bayesian optimization to select and execute experiments across seven film-processing variables. The study reports recipes for transparent conductive polymer films with mean conductivity above 4,500 S/cm; morphology and feature-importance analyses identified influential processing factors. These are laboratory fabrication results, not demonstrated industrial-scale performance.

Technology: Importance-guided Bayesian optimization with automated synthesis and characterization
