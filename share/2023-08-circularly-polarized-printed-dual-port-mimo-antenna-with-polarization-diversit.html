Circularly polarized printed dual port MIMO antenna with polarization diversity optimized by machine learning approach for 5G NR n77/n78 frequency band applications

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding / tested result: Scientific Reports full text identifies the DOI/title and first publication date (2023-08-26); Methods describe CATBoost/ANN optimization of defected-ground shifts from simulation-derived axial-ratio data (with an 80:20/90:10 split), and Results report fabricated-prototype VNA measurements, measured 3.17–4.55 GHz resonance and 2.66–3.92 GHz circular-polarization bands, gain/radiation measurements, and polarization diversity. The finding is a bounded experimentally checked antenna design, not a claim of deployment or network performance.

Technology: CATBoost and ANN map right/left shifts of the defected ground structure to simulated axial-ratio outcomes and select an optimized geometry; this operation is directly tied to the antenna design.
