DolphinGemma: a model for finding patterns in dolphin sounds

A model with about 400 million parameters, trained on decades of recordings of Atlantic spotted dolphins from the Wild Dolphin Project, predicts subsequent sounds to identify recurring patterns; it runs on Pixel phones used in the field. The patterns have not been confirmed to carry meaning.

Technology: DolphinGemma (audio model with about 400M parameters)
