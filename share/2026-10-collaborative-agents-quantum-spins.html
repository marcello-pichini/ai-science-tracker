Collaborating LLM agents propose and test a calibration rule for diamond quantum spins

Mutually reviewing LLM agents selected a question about the minimum number of calibration settings needed to predict responses to microwave pulse sequences in diamond spin ensembles, derived the theory and tested it experimentally. The first measurement was inconclusive because of signal drift, and after the protocol was revised the result matched the predictions under unchanged acceptance criteria. The abstract reports no numerical values, and this is an unrefereed arXiv preprint with an authors-reported validation.

Technology: Collaborative LLM agents that review each other's work
