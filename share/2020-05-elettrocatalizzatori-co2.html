Cu–Al electrocatalysts for CO2 found with active learning

DFT and active machine learning identified Cu–Al catalysts that reduce CO2 to ethylene with over 80% Faradaic efficiency at 400 mA/cm² (about 66% for pure copper) and 55% half-cell energy efficiency at 150 mA/cm². Synthesized and tested in the lab; the abstract does not state how many candidates were screened.

Technology: Active machine learning and DFT
