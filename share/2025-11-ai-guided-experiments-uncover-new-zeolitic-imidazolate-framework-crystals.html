AI-guided experiments uncover new zeolitic imidazolate framework crystals

AIRES combined probabilistic machine-learning predictions with robotic synthesis, optical crystal screening and single-crystal X-ray diffraction to explore zinc zeolitic imidazolate frameworks. The campaign crystallized new structures using 10 of 48 previously untested linkers, doubling the discovery rate of random exploration (700 versus 1,400 experiments) and increasing the single-linker Zn-ZIF library by about one-third. The paper reports structure-resolved products, including eleven previously unreported crystals across its discovery campaign.

Technology: Algorithmic Iterative Reticular Synthesis (AIRES)

  • AI role: AIRES used probabilistic random-forest and Gaussian-process crystallization models to guide experiment selection across linker chemistries and reaction conditions. The AI-guided campaign found structures for 10 of 48 new linkers, with twice the distinct-crystal discovery rate of random exploration.
