Machine-learning-guided photoredox design experimentally enables three transformations

A workflow combining a variational-autoencoder latent space of 11,069 literature photoredox reactions with random-forest catalyst/reagent recommendations was used to propose three transformations. Experiments synthesized a previously inaccessible substituted dihydroquinolinone 43 using fac-Ir(ppy)3, a related oxidative cross-coupling product 46 in 64% yield, and heterocycle product 49 in 74% yield. The paper presents these as experimentally validated transformations identified through model-assisted precedent retrieval and condition selection.

Technology: Variational autoencoder for reaction-space navigation and random-forest multilabel catalyst/reagent recommendation
