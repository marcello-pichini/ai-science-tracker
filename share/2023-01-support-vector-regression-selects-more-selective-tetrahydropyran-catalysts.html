Support-vector regression selects more selective tetrahydropyran catalysts

Models of catalyst fragments and reaction settings selected catalysts for asymmetric tetrahydropyran synthesis. A tested catalyst reached an enantiomeric ratio of 91.5:8.5, above the training examples' maximum of 82:18. The result improves a known catalyst class for a focused reaction; it does not establish invention of the reaction or broad generality.

Technology: Support-vector regression with tunable fragment descriptors
