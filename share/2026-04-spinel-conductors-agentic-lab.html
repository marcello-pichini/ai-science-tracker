An LLM agent in an air-free robotic lab maps lithium halide spinel conductors

An agent-guided self-driving lab ran 352 air-sensitive synthesis samples, realizing 72% of the 171 pairwise combinations of 19 metals. The share of samples meeting both ionic conductivity above 0.05 mS/cm and high spinel phase purity rose from 1.33% in the first 75 agent-proposed samples to 5.33% in the last 75. This is an arXiv preprint; characterization methods and new-compound counts are not confirmed from the abstract page.

Technology: A-Lab GPSS (LLM agent integrated into a robotic synthesis platform)
