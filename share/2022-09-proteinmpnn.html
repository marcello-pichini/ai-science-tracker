ProteinMPNN: protein sequences designed in about one second

A neural network generates the amino-acid sequence for a given protein shape in about one second, over 200 times faster than the best previous software, without specialist tuning. A related study shows that proteins designed with ProteinMPNN fold correctly much more often, including nanoscale rings and complex assemblies. Researchers describe it as the beginning of machine learning in protein design.
