popEVE: diagnosing rare developmental disorders

Applied to about 30,000 patients with severe undiagnosed developmental disorders, it led to a diagnosis in about one-third of cases and identified variants in 123 previously unknown genes, 25 of which were independently confirmed by other labs. This is preliminary clinical use; further checks are needed before adoption, and the page gives no detailed metrics.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: prospective or independent check
  • Tested: popEVE was tested on documented variants and case studies, and applied to about 30,000 undiagnosed patients, leading to a diagnosis in about one-third; 123 novel gene candidates were identified, and 25 were independently confirmed by other labs.
  • Still unknown: The press article says popEVE will need further verification for safety and accuracy before wide clinical adoption, and gives no accuracy metrics; it is not yet in routine clinical use.
  • AI role: A deep generative model extending EVE with a protein language model, producing a calibrated disease-likelihood score for each variant.
