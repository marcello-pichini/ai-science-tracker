REDMOD: pancreatic cancer signals in CT scans taken before diagnosis

Among nearly 2,000 abdominal CT scans originally considered normal, an AI radiomics model identified 73% of prediagnostic pancreatic cancers, with a median lead time of about 16 months. Validation included images from multiple institutions and protocols. This analysis used scans taken before diagnosis; clinical benefit and false positives in practice still need prospective evaluation.

Technology: REDMOD (Radiomics-based Early Detection Model)

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: REDMOD was trained on 969 patients from a multi-institutional cohort and tested on an independent set of 493 patients (63 pre-diagnostic), with external specificity checks on further datasets and a comparison with radiologists; AUC 0.82 and 73.0% sensitivity, versus 38.9% for radiologists.
  • Still unknown: The article states that prospective validation in high-risk cohorts is the necessary next step; no explicit limitations section.
  • AI role: A fully automated radiomics framework with AI segmentation and an ensemble classifier on a 40-feature signature from standard-of-care CT scans.
