An AI system predicts progression of macular degeneration

The system analyzes OCT scans to predict whether the less affected eye of patients with macular degeneration will develop the wet form, outperforming specialists in the comparison described. The announcement gives no numerical metrics; preventive clinical studies were still to be planned.

Technology: Deep learning on OCT images

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Retrospective prediction of whether the second eye converts to wet AMD within 6 months: 80% sensitivity at 55% specificity, and better than five of six experts in the comparison described.
  • Still unknown: Preventive use needs prospective trials that were only being considered; 56% of individual eyes were false positives at the high-sensitivity setting; dataset size and generalizability not stated.
  • AI role: Models built on 3D OCT scans and automatically generated tissue maps to predict conversion to wet AMD.
