Sequential model-based optimization discovers two previously unreported chemical reactions

A programmable robotic synthesis platform used inline HPLC, Raman or NMR feedback and closed-loop optimization to explore selected reaction spaces. In a tosMIC campaign, sequential model-based optimization with a Gaussian-process surrogate identified a reaction between toluenesulphonylmethyl isocyanide and benzylidenemalononitrile; subsequent optimization increased yield from the 27% initial condition to 49% (22 percentage points) over 50 reactions. The article also reports a second previously unreported reaction and an exploratory trifluoromethylation campaign that discovered new molecules.

Technology: ChemputationOptimizer, a sensor-integrated reaction engine with sequential model-based optimization (Gaussian-process surrogate); the platform also exposes random search, design-of-experiments, Bayesian-optimization and genetic-algorithm routines
