MACE-guided screening yields three corundum high-entropy oxides and a cation-ordered oxide

MACE-MP-0b2 interatomic-potential calculations screened 462 four- and five-cation trivalent-oxide compositions and selected 16 for synthesis. Experiments produced three phase-pure corundum high-entropy oxides—(Al,Cr,Fe,Ga)₂O₃, (Al,Cr,Fe,Rh,Sc)₂O₃ and (Cr,Fe,Ga,Rh,Sc)₂O₃—and a separate phase-pure cation-ordered (Al,Fe,Ga,Sc)₂O₃ oxide. The ordered compound crystallized in orthorhombic Pba2; its precise cation-site occupancies were not resolved conclusively by powder diffraction.

Technology: MACE-MP-0b2 machine-learning interatomic potential (MLIP)

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Preprint
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: MACE-MP-0b2 interatomic-potential calculations screened 462 four- and five-cation trivalent-oxide compositions and selected 16 for synthesis.
  • Still unknown: This is an unrefereed arXiv preprint. Only three of 16 tested compositions formed phase-pure corundum high-entropy oxides; most attempts produced competing phases, and rhodium-containing samples showed redox instability. Synthesis outcomes depended strongly on route: the three corundum HEOs were phase-pure only with one of solid-state or combustion synthesis. The ordered oxide’s structure is supported by powder diffraction/Rietveld refinement, but exact site preferences could not be conclusively determined from pXRD. The screening supports candidate ranking, not reliable prediction of phase purity or synthesis conditions for arbitrary HEOs.
  • AI role: The MLIP relaxed approximately 1,000-atom disordered supercells and estimated mixing enthalpy and atom-energy-distribution descriptors for the 462-composition search. The authors combined these with a stability metric to rank 16 synthesis candidates. Experimental powder-X-ray diffraction and Rietveld refinement then assessed the predicted materials, linking model-guided selection to the new synthesized compositions.
