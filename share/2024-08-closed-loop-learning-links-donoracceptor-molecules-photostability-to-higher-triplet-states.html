Closed-loop learning links donor–acceptor molecules’ photostability to higher triplet states

A closed-loop synthesis and learning campaign found that solution photostability across a donor–bridge–acceptor molecular series correlated with the high-energy portion of the triplet excited-state manifold, beyond the commonly considered lowest triplet state. Researchers tested the model-derived hypothesis on an experimental set and changed solvent triplet energy to test the proposed Dexter triplet-energy-transfer degradation pathway. The study used Bayesian optimization and interpretable supervised learning to extract the chemical hypothesis from measured photostability data.

Technology: Closed-loop transfer (CLT), combining Gryffin Bayesian optimization with physics-feature supervised learning
