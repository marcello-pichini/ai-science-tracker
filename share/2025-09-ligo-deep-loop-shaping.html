Deep Loop Shaping reduces LIGO control noise

The method, described in Science and tested at the Livingston site, reduced noise 30–100-fold in the observatory's most unstable feedback loop, in collaboration with LIGO, Caltech, and GSSI. The result covers one control loop; extending it to all loops and adding hundreds of events per year are projections.

Technology: Deep Loop Shaping (reinforcement-learning control)
