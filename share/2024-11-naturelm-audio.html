NatureLM-audio: an audio-language model for bioacoustics

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Archived from discovery counts: the November 2024 preprint evaluates a bioacoustics model on benchmark tasks rather than reporting a new biological finding or a field-study result. NatureLM-audio achieved 19.6% accuracy for scientific-name classification of unseen species and 38.3% accuracy for counting zebra finch individuals in recordings. These are evaluations on BEANS-Zero, not discovery of new species or interpretation of animal communication; the historical entry is retained as method-development context.
