NatureLM-audio: an audio-language model for bioacoustics

Based on an audio encoder and LLaMA 3.1-8B, the model gives the correct scientific name about 20% of the time across 200 unseen species and counts zebra finch calls at 38.3% versus a 25% baseline; it is state of the art on most BEANS-Zero benchmark tasks. Early, modest results on the authors' own benchmark; preprint.
