Regional deep-learning phase picking reveals 24 additional earthquakes in a Gorda Ridge swarm

A long-window regional phase picker and phase-association workflow detected 34 earthquakes in a 29 February 2024 swarm near the Gorda Ridge, offshore northern California: all 10 reported by the USGS plus 24 additional events. The detections were possible from stations roughly 300 km from the swarm, beyond the distance range represented by standard local-event pickers. This is one paper-level catalog result, not 24 separate discoveries.

Technology: SKYNET deep-learning P/S phase picker with GENIE graph-neural-network phase association
