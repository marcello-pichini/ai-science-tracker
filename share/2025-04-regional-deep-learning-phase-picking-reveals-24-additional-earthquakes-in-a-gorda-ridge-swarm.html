Regional deep-learning phase picking reveals 24 additional earthquakes in a Gorda Ridge swarm

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A long-window regional phase picker and phase-association workflow detected 34 earthquakes in a 29 February 2024 swarm near the Gorda Ridge, offshore northern California: all 10 reported by the USGS plus 24 additional events. The detections were possible from stations roughly 300 km from the swarm, beyond the distance range represented by standard local-event pickers. This is one paper-level catalog result, not 24 separate discoveries.

Technology: SKYNET deep-learning P/S phase picker with GENIE graph-neural-network phase association

AI contribution
SKYNET picked long-duration regional seismic waveforms and GENIE associated the picks into events; their combined workflow supplied the new event detections in sparsely instrumented offshore seismicity.
Test and result
A long-window regional phase picker and phase-association workflow detected 34 earthquakes in a 29 February 2024 swarm near the Gorda Ridge, offshore northern California: all 10 reported by the USGS plus 24 additional events.
Limitation / still unknown
The application covers one swarm day and a specific offshore region. The paper reports 24 additional algorithmically detected events and estimates local magnitudes, but does not claim every event had independent analyst confirmation. SKYNET is designed to complement local pickers; its near-source performance is weaker than PhaseNet, and the result is not a new tectonic mechanism.
