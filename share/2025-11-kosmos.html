Kosmos: an AI scientist for autonomous discovery

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Each run reads about 1,500 papers and executes 42,000 lines of analysis code; outside scientists judged 79.4% of its conclusions accurate. Of seven discoveries, three reproduce results already obtained by humans and four are new. Limitations include spurious correlations, a cost of about $200 per run, and a six-month work gain estimated by beta users.

Technology: Kosmos (agents with a structured world model)

AI contribution
An AI scientist runs about 12-hour cycles of data analysis and literature search (about 1,500 papers and 42,000 lines of code per run), linked by a structured world model, and generates traceable reports; humans set the objectives.
Test and result
Independent scientists judged 79.4% of Kosmos's conclusions accurate; three discoveries reproduced findings from preprints or unpublished manuscripts that Kosmos did not access at runtime; the four new discoveries are wet-lab validation in progress, with one checked in an independent scRNA-seq dataset.
Limitation / still unknown
The authors state that Kosmos chases statistically significant but irrelevant findings, that about one in five statements is not accurate, and that the human time-saving estimates are intrinsically suspect.
