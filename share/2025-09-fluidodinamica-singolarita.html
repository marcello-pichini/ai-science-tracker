New families of unstable singularities in fluid equations

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Physics-informed neural networks trained to near machine precision revealed a new family of unstable singularities (blow-ups) in three fluid-dynamics equations, with a clear pattern between blow-up speed and instability order for the incompressible porous-medium and Boussinesq equations. Work with Brown, NYU, and Stanford: a step toward computer-assisted proofs, not a proof; the Navier–Stokes problem remains open.

Technology: Physics-informed neural networks

AI contribution
Neural networks trained on the equation residuals, with a high-precision optimizer, located new families of unstable singularities, with humans supplying the mathematical insight.
Test and result
Physics-informed neural networks trained to near machine precision found unstable singularities, and the accuracy was assessed numerically against the governing equations.
Limitation / still unknown
The source states the initial conditions must be tuned with extreme precision, the scaling formula is empirical, Navier-Stokes remains open, and supplementary information is not yet available.
