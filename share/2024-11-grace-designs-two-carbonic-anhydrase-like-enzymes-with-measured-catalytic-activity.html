GRACE designs two carbonic-anhydrase-like enzymes with measured catalytic activity

The GRACE workflow combined RFdiffusion structure generation, ProteinMPNN sequence design, CLEAN enzyme classification and solubility/structure filtering to select two carbonic-anhydrase-like sequences from 10,000 candidates. The authors report experimental expression and carbonic anhydrase activity: dCA12_2 at 400 WAU/mL and dCA23_1 at 260 WAU/mL in the reported assay.

Technology: GRACE (RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN and CLEAN, with computational solubility/structural filters)
