Chematica: computational synthesis planning for natural products

The system plans multistep syntheses of complex natural products, with routes in a Turing-test study largely indistinguishable from human routes; three computer-designed syntheses were carried out in the lab: dauricine, tacamonidine, and lamellodysidine A. Based on over 100,000 rules plus AI, it is not modern generative AI.
