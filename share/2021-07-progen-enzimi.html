ProGen generates artificial proteins with enzyme activity

A protein language model generated synthetic lysozymes: two of the five characterized in cells degraded bacterial cell walls with activity comparable to egg-white lysozyme. Laboratory tests and crystallography support the result, but do not demonstrate clinical applications. The preprint dates to July 18, 2021; the Nature Biotechnology publication and UCSF announcement are from January 2023.
