A seven-metabolite AI model detected renal-cell carcinoma across six validation cohorts

An SVM-based screen of untargeted plasma metabolomics identified candidate renal-cell-carcinoma metabolites, which were confirmed by targeted analysis and used in the RCAID AI diagnostic model. In 1,680 participants across five hospitals, seven plasma metabolites supported AUROCs of 0.977, 0.911, 0.945 and 0.972 in internal, external, multicenter and temporal validation cohorts, respectively; validation also included late-stage and non-clear-cell RCC. The authors report six associated dysregulated pathways from multi-omic analyses.

Technology: Support-vector-machine metabolite selection and the Renal Cell Carcinoma Artificial Intelligence Detector (RCAID)
