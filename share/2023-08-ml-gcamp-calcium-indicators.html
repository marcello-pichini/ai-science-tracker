Machine learning guides engineering of fluorescent calcium indicators

An ensemble of three regression models trained on experimentally derived GCaMP mutation libraries screened 1,423 uncharacterized variants. The study reports eGCaMP and eGCaMP+ with faster kinetics and larger fluorescence responses than previously published fast variants, and eGCaMP2+ with greater dynamic range than the tested sixth-, seventh- and eighth-generation indicators. The preprint reports cell and neuron assays. These are laboratory sensor results, not human or clinical validation. This is one study; the journal article is a later version.
