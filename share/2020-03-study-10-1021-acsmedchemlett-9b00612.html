Accelerated Discovery of Novel Ponatinib Analogs with Improved Properties for the Treatment of Parkinson's Disease.

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Paired Naive Bayes models prospectively prioritized ponatinib analogs for c-Abl potency and reduced hERG liability. Of six new compounds tested, two combined sub-10 nM c-Abl activity with improved hERG assay profiles; this is biochemical optimization, not evidence of Parkinson’s efficacy or cardiac safety.

Technology: Paired Naïve Bayes networks

Editorial relevance 47 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A single Naive Bayes network predicted c-Abl potency; two networks in concert prioritized c-Abl potency and predicted hERG IC50 greater than 1 μM, and the workflows were prospectively tested.
Test and result
Six novel compounds were tested. Table 1 reports compounds 4 and 5 with c-Abl IC50 below 10 nM and hERG IC50 5.02 and 12.1 μM; nilotinib/ponatinib hERG values are 0.45/0.77 μM. The correction swaps compounds 4/5 in the separate kinase-panel Table 2, not these Table 1 values.
Limitation / still unknown
The enzyme/channel and cell-toxicity assays do not establish Parkinson’s efficacy, in-vivo exposure, or clinical cardiac safety. The June 24 correction swaps compounds 4 and 5 in kinase-panel Table 2.
