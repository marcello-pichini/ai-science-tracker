CNN-assisted search confirms quadruply lensed quasar PS J2107−1611

A convolutional neural network trained on simulated lens images screened 27,760 preselected quasar candidates with multiple nearby Pan-STARRS detections. Researchers visually inspected the top 2,000 scores and selected follow-up targets; spectroscopy and multi-survey imaging established PS J2107−1611 as a quadruply imaged quasar at redshift 2.673, with a 4.3-arcsecond image separation and a lensed host-galaxy arc. Its fold-image optical fluxes are roughly 15 times fainter than a smooth lens-mass model predicts, while radio flux ratios are consistent with that model. The date is the original preprint posting; the journal version appeared in November 2023.

Technology: Convolutional neural network trained on simulated quadruply lensed quasars, applied to Pan-STARRS gri image cutouts

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: A convolutional neural network trained on simulated lens images screened 27,760 preselected quasar candidates with multiple nearby Pan-STARRS detections.
  • Still unknown: This is a single confirmed system from a prefiltered candidate set; the authors visually reviewed high-scoring candidates and performed follow-up. Spectroscopy confirms the source is a quasar at z=2.673, while the four-image lens interpretation is supported by high-resolution imaging, lens modeling, a host-galaxy arc, and wavelength-dependent flux ratios. The ∼15-fold optical fold-pair anomaly is relative to the smooth macromodel; the cause is not resolved in this letter. Detailed JWST/VLT/EVN follow-up was left for future work.
  • AI role: The CNN ranked image cutouts of 27,760 quasar catalog entries that already had multiple nearby Pan-STARRS detections. The team visually examined the top 2,000, selected 131 promising systems, and followed targets with imaging/spectroscopy; the model therefore enabled prioritization but did not itself confirm lensing. Human follow-up established the quasar redshift and lens configuration.
