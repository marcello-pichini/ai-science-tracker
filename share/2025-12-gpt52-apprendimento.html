GPT-5.2 Pro and an open problem about learning curves

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: GPT-5.2 Pro solved an open problem in statistical learning theory (learning curves are monotonic in the Gaussian case with known mean and unknown standard deviation), then extended it to higher dimensions. The authors checked the proof, including with outside experts. Reported by OpenAI.

Technology: GPT-5.2 Pro

AI contribution
All results were derived by variants of GPT-5.2 Pro, with humans prompting for further results and verifying the proofs.
Test and result
The authors verified and transcribed the model's proofs of monotonicity of the maximum likelihood estimator's KL divergence for Gaussian and Gamma families, with no outside expert review described.
Limitation / still unknown
The guarantees cover only the listed well-specified parametric settings, and the reverse-KL result relies on a folklore trick; the page shows only the abstract.
