GPT-5.2 Pro and an open problem about learning curves

GPT-5.2 Pro solved an open problem in statistical learning theory (learning curves are monotonic in the Gaussian case with known mean and unknown standard deviation), then extended it to higher dimensions. The authors checked the proof, including with outside experts. Reported by OpenAI.
