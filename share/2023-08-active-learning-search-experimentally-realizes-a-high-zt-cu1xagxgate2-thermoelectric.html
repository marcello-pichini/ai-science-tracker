Active-learning search experimentally realizes a high-zT Cu₁₋ₓAgₓGaTe₂ thermoelectric

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Compressed-sensing symbolic regression and active learning screened the Cu₁₋ₓAgₓGaTe₂ thermoelectric series and guided synthesis of selected compositions. The published study reports a p-type Cu₀.₄₅Ag₀.₅₅GaTe₂ sample with a measured thermoelectric figure of merit zT of about 1.90 at 770 K, using measured heat capacity in the analysis. This is one paper-level materials result from a targeted search, not a claim that every composition in the series is new.

Technology: SISSO compressed-sensing symbolic regression within an active-learning workflow

Editorial relevance 53 materials Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
The authors used SISSO to derive interpretable descriptors from their experimental thermoelectric dataset, then used active learning to rank prospective compositions for synthesis and measurement. The model guided candidate selection; the researchers synthesized and characterized the samples and measured the properties.
Test and result
Compressed-sensing symbolic regression and active learning screened the Cu₁₋ₓAgₓGaTe₂ thermoelectric series and guided synthesis of selected compositions.
Limitation / still unknown
The zT value is the final peer-reviewed paper’s reported result using measured heat capacity; an earlier preprint version of the same study reported a different higher estimate (about 2.8 at 827 K), so that preprint value is not substituted here or counted as a separate finding. The reported result is a laboratory thermoelectric sample at elevated temperature, not a device or commercial-performance demonstration. Active learning searched a defined composition series and does not establish that every searched composition was novel.
