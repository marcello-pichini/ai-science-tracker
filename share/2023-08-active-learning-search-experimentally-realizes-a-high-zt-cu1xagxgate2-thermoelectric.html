Active-learning search experimentally realizes a high-zT Cu₁₋ₓAgₓGaTe₂ thermoelectric

Compressed-sensing symbolic regression and active learning screened the Cu₁₋ₓAgₓGaTe₂ thermoelectric series and guided synthesis of selected compositions. The published study reports a p-type Cu₀.₄₅Ag₀.₅₅GaTe₂ sample with a measured thermoelectric figure of merit zT of about 1.90 at 770 K, using measured heat capacity in the analysis. This is one paper-level materials result from a targeted search, not a claim that every composition in the series is new.

Technology: SISSO compressed-sensing symbolic regression within an active-learning workflow

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Compressed-sensing symbolic regression and active learning screened the Cu₁₋ₓAgₓGaTe₂ thermoelectric series and guided synthesis of selected compositions.
  • Still unknown: The zT value is the final peer-reviewed paper’s reported result using measured heat capacity; an earlier preprint version of the same study reported a different higher estimate (about 2.8 at 827 K), so that preprint value is not substituted here or counted as a separate finding. The reported result is a laboratory thermoelectric sample at elevated temperature, not a device or commercial-performance demonstration. Active learning searched a defined composition series and does not establish that every searched composition was novel.
  • AI role: The authors used SISSO to derive interpretable descriptors from their experimental thermoelectric dataset, then used active learning to rank prospective compositions for synthesis and measurement. The model guided candidate selection; the researchers synthesized and characterized the samples and measured the properties.
