ML ranking identifies active sesquiterpene-synthase variants

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A composite score combines Boltz2-predicted binding and DLKcat-predicted turnover to rank variants in a focused Agr5 library. Of 15 top-ranked variants tested, four produced more viridiflorol than wild-type Agr5; the paper reports 11.05-fold higher production for Y374F and a 952.79 mg/L titer in its yeast assay. The initial V314G design involved mechanism-guided human analysis, and the measured values are assay-specific.

Technology: DeEnzyme_score combining Boltz2 and DLKcat predictions
