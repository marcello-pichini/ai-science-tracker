An AI system improves a Chan–Lam coupling reaction

The system identified TEMPO as a useful additive for coupling primary sulfonamides; chemists reproduced yield improvements in 11 of 14 substrate pairs at bench scale. The work retains human supervision and specialized infrastructure; independent reproducibility and generalization remain to be checked.
