Adaptive learning finds higher-activity Fe–N–C oxygen-reduction catalysts

An adaptive-learning design-of-experiments loop guided robotic high-throughput synthesis of Fe–N–C oxygen-reduction electrocatalysts across a six-dimensional process/composition space. Starting from 36 characterized samples, the team synthesized 14 additional catalyst formulations from a nominal search space of roughly 10 billion combinations. Four outperformed the best sample in the initial dataset; the best showed about 33% higher ORR activity, reported as 16.3 mA mg⁻¹ at 0.8 V in rotating-disk measurements.

Technology: Adaptive learning framework with machine-learning surrogates, statistical inference, uncertainty quantification, and global optimization, paired with robotic high-throughput synthesis
