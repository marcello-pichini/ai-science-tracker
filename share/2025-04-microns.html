MICrONS: a map of one cubic millimeter of mouse visual cortex

A map with over half a billion connections and about 75,000 reconstructed neurons from roughly 28,000 sections, produced over nine years by more than 150 researchers. AI traced every neuron outline, and the result requires human checking, much of which is already done. This is about one-thousandth of a mouse connectome, and cell counts vary within the text.

Technology: AI neuron segmentation
