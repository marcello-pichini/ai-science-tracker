RoseTTAFold: accessible prediction of protein structures

A three-track neural network (sequence, residue interactions, and 3D structure) computes a structure in about ten minutes on a single gaming computer. Baker’s group predicted hundreds of new structures, including little-known proteins in the human genome. The web server received over 4,500 requests in the previous month.
