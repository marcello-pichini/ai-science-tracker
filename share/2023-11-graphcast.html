GraphCast: global 10-day weather forecasts

It outperforms ECMWF’s HRES system on over 90% of 1,380 variables and test forecast lead times, and produces a 10-day forecast in under a minute on a single TPU. Comparison is limited to deterministic HRES; training uses ERA5 data.
