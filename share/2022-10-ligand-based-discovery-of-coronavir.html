Ligand-based discovery of coronavirus main protease inhibitors using MACAW molecular embeddings

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: MACAW molecular embeddings and ligand-based models prioritized purchased compounds for assays. The paper reports inhibition of recombinant coronavirus main protease for eight of ten selected molecules; compound 7 also reduced hCoV-229E infectivity in a cell assay. This is not a clinical COVID-19 result or proof of SARS-CoV-2 patient benefit.

Technology: MACAW molecular embeddings used in ligand-based machine learning virtual screening
