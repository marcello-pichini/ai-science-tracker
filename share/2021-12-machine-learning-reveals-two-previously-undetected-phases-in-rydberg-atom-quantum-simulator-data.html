Machine learning reveals two previously undetected phases in Rydberg-atom quantum-simulator data

An interpretable hybrid unsupervised/supervised learning analysis of experimental snapshots from a programmable Rydberg-atom array mapped phase regions on a square lattice and identified signatures of two previously undetected phases: boundary-ordered and rhombic. The rhombic pattern had been predicted theoretically, so the result is its experimental-data signature rather than a wholly unanticipated theoretical phase; the boundary-ordered phase was revealed from edge correlations.

Technology: Hybrid-correlation convolutional neural network (Hybrid-CCNN) with PCA and clustering
