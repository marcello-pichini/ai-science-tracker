WeatherNext and Hurricane Melissa: earlier warning in Jamaica

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: WeatherNext forecast a Category 5 landfall in Jamaica five days ahead with 80% probability, rising to nearly 100% at three days. According to the post, it was the best single model for track and intensity in the National Hurricane Center's 2025 verification report, which used it alongside its HAFS model. The post gives no numerical comparisons; rapid intensification remains very hard to predict.

AI contribution
WeatherNext, an AI weather model with 50-member ensembles, forecast the Hurricane Melissa landfall alongside physics-based HAFS and was used by NHC forecasters.
Test and result
The National Hurricane Center's 2025 annual verification report found WeatherNext the top-performing individual model for track and intensity; the Melissa forecast is presented as a single case.
Limitation / still unknown
The page notes that predicting rapid intensification is very difficult and that Weather Lab is experimental; no dedicated limitations section.
