Bayesian optimization and graph learning identify two synthesized ultra-incompressible hard materials

A Bayesian optimization workflow using symmetry-constrained crystal relaxation and the MEGNet graph neural network screened 399,960 transition-metal boride and carbide compositions. The study identified MoWC₂ (P6₃/mmc) and ReWB (Pca2₁), then synthesized both by in-situ reactive spark-plasma sintering; the measured hardness was reported to agree with model and DFT predictions. The result is limited to these two synthesized compounds and the authors’ tested synthesis conditions.

Technology: BOWSR (Bayesian Optimization With Symmetry Relaxation) with the MEGNet graph neural network energy model
