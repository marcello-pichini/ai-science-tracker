BindCraft designs protein binders in one shot

An open AlphaFold2-based hallucination pipeline designs binders with reported experimental success rates of 10-100% across 12 targets, with affinities predominantly in the nanomolar range. The authors also demonstrated effects in cell assays and patient-derived samples; these are experimental results, not established clinical treatments. This entry uses the Nature publication date (27 August 2025); the preprint appeared in 2024.
