AI-assisted transfer-matrix analysis yields an exact solution for the 1D frustrated q-state Potts model

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The study exactly solves the one-dimensional J₁–J₂ q-state Potts model for arbitrary q by reducing its q²×q² transfer matrix to a 2×2 maximally symmetric subspace and mapping it to an effective q-state Potts model in a field. OpenAI’s o3-mini-high reasoning model helped derive the q=3 case; the author then generalized the result to arbitrary q and checked the formulas numerically. The solution nontrivially extends an older mapping known for q=2/Ising and yields q-dependent phase and residual-entropy behavior.

Technology: OpenAI o3-mini-high reasoning model

AI contribution

Limitation / still unknown
The AI contribution was to the q=3 transfer-matrix solution; the general-q theorem and full paper are the human author’s derivation, so this is an AI-assisted paper-level theoretical discovery, not an autonomous AI solution for arbitrary q. The exact result concerns the one-dimensional model and does not itself demonstrate a material realization.
