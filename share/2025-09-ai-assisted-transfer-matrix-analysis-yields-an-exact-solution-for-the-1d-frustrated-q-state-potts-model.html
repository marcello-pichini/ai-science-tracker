AI-assisted transfer-matrix analysis yields an exact solution for the 1D frustrated q-state Potts model

The study exactly solves the one-dimensional J₁–J₂ q-state Potts model for arbitrary q by reducing its q²×q² transfer matrix to a 2×2 maximally symmetric subspace and mapping it to an effective q-state Potts model in a field. OpenAI’s o3-mini-high reasoning model helped derive the q=3 case; the author then generalized the result to arbitrary q and checked the formulas numerically. The solution nontrivially extends an older mapping known for q=2/Ising and yields q-dependent phase and residual-entropy behavior.

Technology: OpenAI o3-mini-high reasoning model
