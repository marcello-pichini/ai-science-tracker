LCLS magnet tuning with machine learning

A physics-informed Gaussian process using historical data tunes the accelerator’s 24 LCLS quadrupole magnets. The authors estimate tuning is about three to five times faster, with beams tending to be more intense; this is a result on the real machine, limited to this part of the tuning process.

Technology: Gaussian-process optimization

Evidence context (pilot; stated by the cited sources, not an editorial judgment):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Applied to the LCLS X-ray free-electron laser; the abstract reports the Bayesian optimizer as sample-efficient and outperforming existing optimizers, and the press release estimates tuning three to five times faster.
  • Still unknown: Speed and beam-quality gains are estimates; the method covers only part of the tuning process; results from three other accelerators are not reported.
  • AI role: Gaussian process model predicted the effect of magnet adjustments, chose which to try, and was trained on past machine data.
