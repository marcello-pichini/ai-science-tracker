trRosetta releases over 6,300 predicted protein-family structures

Over 6,300 protein structures predicted with deep learning are available in Pfam to explore families without a known structure. These are computational models with estimated quality, not newly experimentally determined structures; this result is distinct from RoseTTAFold and the AlphaFold Database.
