Claude designs binders against five scorpion neurotoxins

In a mostly autonomous 64-hour campaign, Claude designed minibinders against five neurotoxins. External labs tested 211 designs, 17 of which met the criteria; the best (Cn2-B1 and Cn2-B2) had affinities in the low nanomolar range. Only recombinant toxins were tested, with no neutralization tests in cells or animals; Anthropic does not claim to have produced an antivenom.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Announcement only
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: External labs tested 211 designs by surface plasmon resonance: Adaptyv Bio in cell-free expression and Twist Bioscience in human kidney cells; a binder needed KD of 1 µM or tighter in every replicate at both labs and no CLEC12A signal, giving 17 binders. The two collaborating external laboratories tested candidates from the discovery project; this establishes direct testing rather than a separately established independent follow-up replication.
  • Still unknown: Only recombinant toxins were tested, with no neutralization in cells or animals; the authors do not know whether the binders neutralize venom and do not claim an antivenom; multitoxin designs failed.
  • AI role: Claude selected toxin structures and hotspots, generated 318,484 designs, filtered and co-folded them, ran about 24 hours autonomously, then produced 180 selected designs after human follow-ups.

Evidence sources: https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-scorpion-toxin-binder-design
