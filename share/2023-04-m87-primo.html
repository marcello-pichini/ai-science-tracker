PRIMO: a sharper image of the M87 black hole

A machine-learning method trained on more than 30,000 simulated black-hole images reconstructed M87* from 2017 Event Horizon Telescope data, using the array’s full resolution: the ring is about half as wide as in the 2019 image. It fills gaps with patterns learned from simulations, without quantified uncertainty.

Technology: PRIMO (dictionary learning)
