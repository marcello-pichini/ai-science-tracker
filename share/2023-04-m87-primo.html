PRIMO: a sharper image of the M87 black hole

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A machine-learning method trained on more than 30,000 simulated black-hole images reconstructed M87* from 2017 Event Horizon Telescope data, using the array’s full resolution: the ring is about half as wide as in the 2019 image. It fills gaps with patterns learned from simulations, without quantified uncertainty.

Technology: PRIMO (dictionary learning)

AI contribution
A machine-learning method trained on simulated images filled gaps in the 2017 EHT data to reconstruct the M87* image.
Test and result
The PRIMO reconstruction, built from over 30,000 simulated black-hole images, was checked for consistency with the 2017 EHT data and the 2019 image; no held-out tests are described.
Limitation / still unknown
The article states that generating the image requires assuming an appropriate form of the missing information and describes no quantified uncertainties; implications for the black hole are framed as future possibilities.
