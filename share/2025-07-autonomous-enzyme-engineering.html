A closed-loop platform engineers enzymes with minimal human input

Machine learning and language models choosing variants, with a biofoundry running build and test, raised Arabidopsis halide methyltransferase substrate preference 90-fold (16-fold ethyltransferase activity) and Yersinia mollaretii phytase activity 26-fold at neutral pH, in four rounds over four weeks and under 500 variants per enzyme. Two enzymes only.

Technology: Machine learning and LLM-guided variant design with biofoundry automation
