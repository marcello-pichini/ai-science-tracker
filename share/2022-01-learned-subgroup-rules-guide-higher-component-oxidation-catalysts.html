Learned subgroup rules guide higher-component oxidation catalysts

Rules learned from high-throughput catalyst measurements guided tests of more complex four- and five-component materials for propylene oxidation. Selected materials reached approximately 60% oxygenate yield in laboratory tests. The learned descriptor ranges are associative design guides, not proven atomic mechanisms or universal industrial performance rules.

Technology: Subgroup-discovery rule learning
