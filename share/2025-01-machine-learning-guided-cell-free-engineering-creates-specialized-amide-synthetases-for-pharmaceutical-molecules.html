Machine learning-guided cell-free engineering creates specialized amide synthetases for pharmaceutical molecules

A cell-free design-build-test-learn platform generated sequence-function data from 1,217 McbA variants across 10,953 reactions. Ridge-regression ML guided enzyme variants that produced nine small-molecule pharmaceuticals with activity 1.6- to 42-fold above the parent; initial substrate-promiscuity assays found McbA could make 11 pharmaceutical compounds plus hybrid molecules. This is experimental enzyme engineering, not evidence of therapeutic efficacy of the products.

Technology: Augmented ridge-regression ML models within a cell-free sequence-function mapping and enzyme-engineering workflow

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: A cell-free design-build-test-learn platform generated sequence-function data from 1,217 McbA variants across 10,953 reactions.
  • Still unknown: The platform optimizes in-vitro enzyme production of molecules and does not establish drug efficacy, manufacturing readiness, or broad transferability beyond tested reactions. The nine-product gains are relative to the parent enzyme under the reported assay conditions.
  • AI role: Used measured variant fitness data to predict and select higher-activity McbA variants for distinct amide-forming reactions; selected variants were synthesized and assayed.
