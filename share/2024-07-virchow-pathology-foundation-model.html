Virchow: a pathology foundation model for clinical-grade cancer detection

Virchow was trained on about 1.5 million H&E whole-slide images from roughly 100,000 patients at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. Its pan-cancer detector reached a 0.95 specimen-level AUC and matched or exceeded tissue-specific clinical-grade models on some rare cancers. Evaluation is retrospective, and the study is co-authored by the company Paige, whose press materials carry the headline claims.

Technology: Virchow (vision transformer foundation model for pathology)
