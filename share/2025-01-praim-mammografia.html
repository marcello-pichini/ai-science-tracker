PRAIM: AI finds more breast cancers in German screening

In a prospective study of over 460,000 women at twelve German sites, AI-assisted reading detected 6.7 cancers per thousand exams versus 5.7 without AI. Recall rates for further assessment were 37.4 versus 38.3 per thousand. This observational study does not show a survival benefit, and estimates of workload reduction with automated reading come from simulations.
