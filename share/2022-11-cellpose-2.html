Cellpose 2.0: human-in-the-loop training for cell segmentation

Archived from discovery counts: Cellpose 2.0 develops and evaluates cell-segmentation and annotation tools on existing Cellpose, TissueNet and LiveCell images. Fine-tuning used 500-1,000 annotated regions, or roughly 100-200 with human-in-the-loop annotation. The paper investigates segmentation accuracy, annotation styles and annotation effort; it does not report a distinct biological finding from those images. This is method-development evidence, retained as historical context consistently with the archived UNI entry.

Technology: Cellpose 2.0 (deep learning cell segmentation, human-in-the-loop fine-tuning)
