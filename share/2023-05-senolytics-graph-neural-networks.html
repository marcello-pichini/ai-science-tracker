Graph neural networks trained on a 2,352-compound screen find three selective senolytic compounds

Source check: Needs source check. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Wong et al. screened 2,352 compounds for senolytic activity in etoposide-induced senescence, trained graph neural networks on the results and scored more than 800,000 molecules. Three drug-like hits selectively killed senescent cells across senescence models with selectivity comparable to ABT-737; docking and TR-FRET suggest partial Bcl-2 inhibition. One compound, BRD-K56819078, reduced senescent-cell burden and senescence-associated gene expression in kidneys of aged mice. Evidence is from the abstract only; effect sizes, mouse numbers and the absence of efficacy or safety data in humans are not assessed here.

Technology: Chemprop message-passing graph neural networks
