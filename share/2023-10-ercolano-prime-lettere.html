Vesuvius Challenge: first letters read from an unopened Herculaneum scroll

For the first time, a complete word (‘porphyras’, purple) was read from inside a never-opened carbonized scroll; a model trained on crack patterns yielded 13 letters independently and unanimously annotated by papyrologists. The letters are faint and lack context; other readings are not ruled out.

Technology: Machine-learning models for ink detection
