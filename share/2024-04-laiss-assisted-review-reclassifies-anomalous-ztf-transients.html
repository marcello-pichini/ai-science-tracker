LAISS-assisted review reclassifies anomalous ZTF transients

Expert review of 455 previously observed transients changed 16 normal labels to anomalous; the random-forest model flagged 11 of these. Spectra, light curves and host context supported the reclassifications. This is a retrospective study-level result rather than a count of newly observed events or newly established physical classes. Human review and similarity searches also contributed, so the entire retrospective sample is not attributed to the classifier.

Technology: LAISS random-forest anomaly classifier and similarity search
