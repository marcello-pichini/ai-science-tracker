PENet detects pulmonary embolism on volumetric CT angiography

PENet, a 3D deep-learning model for pulmonary embolism on CT angiography, reached AUROC 0.84 on an internal hold-out test and 0.85 on data from one unnamed external institution. The abstract does not report sensitivity, specificity or study size, and the external validation is limited to a single site.

Technology: PENet, 3D deep convolutional network on volumetric CT
