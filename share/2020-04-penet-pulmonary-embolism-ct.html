PENet detects pulmonary embolism on volumetric CT angiography

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: PENet, a 3D deep-learning model for pulmonary embolism on CT angiography, reached AUROC 0.84 on an internal hold-out test and 0.85 on data from one unnamed external institution. The abstract does not report sensitivity, specificity or study size, and the external validation is limited to a single site.

Technology: PENet, 3D deep convolutional network on volumetric CT

Editorial relevance 53 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
A 77-layer 3D CNN, pretrained on Kinetics-600, detected pulmonary embolism on CT angiography and is proposed as a triage tool.
Test and result
PENet, a 3D CNN, was tested on a hold-out set from the training institution (AUROC 0.84) and on an external institution (AUROC 0.85), and was compared with other state-of-the-art 3D CNN models.
Limitation / still unknown
The abstract states no limitations; external validation covers one institution and sensitivity, specificity and study size are not reported.
