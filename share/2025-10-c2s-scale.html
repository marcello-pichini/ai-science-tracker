C2S-Scale: a model-generated cancer hypothesis confirmed in cells

The 27-billion-parameter single-cell model, built on Gemma with Yale, predicted that silmitasertib would increase antigen presentation only in an immune-positive context. In human neuroendocrine cell models with low-dose interferon, the increase was about 50%. Validation is in cells only; the authors call this ‘a first step.’

Technology: C2S-Scale 27B (Gemma)
