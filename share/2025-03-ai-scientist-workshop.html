The AI Scientist-v2: a fully generated paper passes a workshop review

A paper produced end to end by the system received a mean score of 6.33 at the ICBINB workshop at ICLR 2025, above the average acceptance threshold. Humans only supplied the topic and selected three papers to submit, and the accepted one was withdrawn. Only one of three cleared the threshold; it was a workshop, not the main conference, and there was no meta-review.
