Physics-based differentiable imaging detects 51 Eridani b in a JWST band where standard processing did not

A physics-based coronagraph model optimized with automatic differentiation and gradient descent recovered 51 Eridani b in JWST/NIRCam F430M images at 4.3 μm, where the published standard-processing result was a non-detection. The paper also reports two- to fifteen-fold sensitivity gains across its test observations.

Technology: FLIP (Physics-based Learning for Imaging Planets), a differentiable instrument model optimized with automatic differentiation
