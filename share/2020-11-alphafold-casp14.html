AlphaFold wins CASP14 for protein folding

AlphaFold achieved a median GDT score of 92.4 across the target set (about 1.6 Å mean error) and 87.0 on the hardest targets, which organizers judged a solution to the problem of single-protein structures. DeepMind notes that protein complexes and interactions with other molecules remain open challenges.
