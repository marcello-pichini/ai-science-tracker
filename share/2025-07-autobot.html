AutoBot: robotic optimization of halide perovskites

A closed loop with robotics and Bayesian optimization explored a four-parameter synthesis space of over 5,000 combinations, and the model made predictions after sampling less than 1%; humidity during deposition was the most influential factor. This is process optimization, not a new material; the page gives no accuracy figure.

Technology: Bayesian optimization with robotics
