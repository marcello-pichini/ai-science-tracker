Machine-learning screening identifies bacterial-selective membrane-active compounds

The June 2026 bioRxiv version describes an initial machine-learning-guided screen of ZINC20 compounds balancing predicted antibacterial activity, mammalian-cell toxicity, and mechanism-of-action novelty. Among 61 purchased candidates, six showed partial growth inhibition; 49 were non-toxic to HepG2 cells at 50 μM. Flow cytometry indicated membrane disruption for four of the six initial active hits and membrane depolarization for two. A September version reports additional screening and revised models; its expanded results are not attributed to the June version here. The study does not report a novel protein target or clinical antibiotic.

Technology: Graph-convolutional neural networks for antibacterial activity/toxicity with XGBoost mechanism-of-action classification and novelty filtering
