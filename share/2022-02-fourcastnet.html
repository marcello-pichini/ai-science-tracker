FourCastNet: high-resolution global forecasts with neural networks

The preprint presents global forecasts at 0.25° resolution and a one-week forecast in under two seconds. Comparison with IFS is favorable at short lead times for large-scale variables and some finer-scale variables; this does not mean general superiority or operational use.

Technology: Adaptive Fourier Neural Operators
