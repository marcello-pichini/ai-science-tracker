AI-designed brain-penetrant HER2 inhibitor IAM1363 shows durable responses in Phase 1/1b

Iambic reported updated results from an open-label Phase 1/1b study of its AI-designed HER2 inhibitor IAM1363. At the August 31, 2026 cutoff, 122 participants had received treatment across four study parts; the company reported antitumor and intracranial responses and that more than half of participants treated at 960 mg remained on treatment. This summary is limited to the company release of October 26, describing an October 23 conference poster. These uncontrolled early results do not establish comparative efficacy.
