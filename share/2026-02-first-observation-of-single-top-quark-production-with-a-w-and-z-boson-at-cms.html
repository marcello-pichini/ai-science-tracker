First observation of single-top-quark production with a W and Z boson at CMS

CMS reports the first observation of single-top-quark production in association with a W and Z boson in proton-proton collisions. A Particle Transformer classifier and improved reconstruction separated the rare tWZ signal from major backgrounds in three- and four-lepton events. Combining 13 and 13.6 TeV data (200 fb⁻¹), the measured signal has 5.8σ observed significance (3.5σ expected) and cross sections of 248 ± 52 fb at 13 TeV and 242 ± 77 fb at 13.6 TeV. The result agrees with the Standard Model within 2.3σ.

Technology: Particle Transformer (ParT) multiclass classifier

Evidence context (pilot; stated by the cited sources, not an editorial judgment):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: CMS reports the first observation of single-top-quark production in association with a W and Z boson in proton-proton collisions.
  • Still unknown: This is the first observation of the Standard-Model tWZ production process, not evidence for physics beyond the Standard Model. A 2024 CMS analysis had reported earlier evidence at 3.4σ; the 2026 paper adds 13.6 TeV Run 3 data and improved reconstruction/ML analysis to reach observation. The 5.8σ result combines 13 and 13.6 TeV datasets. Cross sections and signal strength are consistent with the Standard Model within the reported uncertainties.
  • AI role: CMS trained four ParT models, split by three- versus four-lepton signal region and 13 versus 13.6 TeV samples. In the three-lepton region, a four-node classifier distinguished tWZ, ttZ and other backgrounds; in the four-lepton region, a binary classifier separated tWZ from backgrounds. Its classifier-score categories entered the statistical signal extraction. The paper reports a substantial sensitivity gain versus the previous analysis: expected significance on the reused 13 TeV data rose from 1.4σ to 3.0σ, contributing to the first observation when combined with 13.6 TeV data.
