Machine learning designs bulk metallic glasses with about 2,450 HV hardness

Max Planck Institute for Sustainable Materials used VIBANN, an attention-based variational network trained on only 673 compositions, to design hard multicomponent bulk metallic glasses. Five alloys were synthesised as fully amorphous 2 mm rods, reaching about 2,450 HV, with high-energy X-ray scattering and molecular dynamics explaining the origin. The abstract gives inconsistent hardness values (about 2,450 vs above 2,050 HV), so verify the figure in the paper.

Technology: VIBANN (variational information-bottleneck attention network)
