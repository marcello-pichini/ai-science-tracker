ML prioritization identifies two antimicrobial peptide candidates

CalcAMP ranked 16,384 14-residue sequences in a researcher-defined LL-37-derived design space bounded by patent WO2015088344. Researchers selected GDST-038 and GDST-045 for laboratory tests; the paper reports activity against tested bacteria and elimination of detectable adherent S. aureus in a 3D human epidermal infection model at concentrations of at least 15 µM. Retro-inverso variants were prepared and tested after selection. These results do not establish human safety or clinical efficacy, and exact sequence-level patent priority remains open.
