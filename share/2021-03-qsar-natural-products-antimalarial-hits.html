QSAR screening identifies antimalarial sesquiterpene lactones

QSAR virtual screening of a natural-product database prioritized compounds for in-vitro testing against chloroquine-sensitive and multidrug-resistant P. falciparum. The paper reports two sesquiterpene lactones, LDT-597 and LDT-598, with potent and selective activity. ADME/PBPK predictions are not measured pharmacokinetics, and the assays do not establish clinical efficacy.

Technology: QSAR activity models; QSPR ADME and PBPK predictors
