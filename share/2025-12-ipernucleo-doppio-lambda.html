A double-Lambda hypernucleus of boron-13 found with deep learning

Neural networks selected images from the J-PARC E07 emulsion plates; microscopy and kinematic analysis then confirmed an event as a double-Lambda hypernucleus of boron-13. The discovery comes from analysis of 0.2% of the emulsions and is based on a single event; published in Nature Communications.

Technology: Deep learning for nuclear-emulsion analysis
