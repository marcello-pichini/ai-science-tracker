A double-Lambda hypernucleus of boron-13 found with deep learning

Neural networks selected images from the J-PARC E07 emulsion plates; microscopy and kinematic analysis then confirmed an event as a double-Lambda hypernucleus of boron-13. The discovery comes from analysis of 0.2% of the emulsions and is based on a single event; published in Nature Communications.

Technology: Deep learning for nuclear-emulsion analysis

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Neural networks pre-selected candidate images from 0.2% of the J-PARC E07 emulsion data; microscopy and detailed kinematic analysis then confirmed one event as a boron-13 double-Lambda hypernucleus.
  • Still unknown: Only one event is confirmed; the estimate of more than 2,000 events in the full dataset is an extrapolation from the detection rate, and the article has no limitations section.
  • AI role: Deep learning networks trained to recognize double-strangeness signatures selected candidate images for microscopic examination.
