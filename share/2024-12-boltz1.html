Boltz-1: an open model for biomolecular structures

A fully open MIT model predicts 3D structures of proteins and other biomolecules and, according to its developers, matches AlphaFold3's accuracy; unlike AlphaFold3, its training pipeline is also released. The comparison is developer-reported; the group calls it a starting point for the community.
