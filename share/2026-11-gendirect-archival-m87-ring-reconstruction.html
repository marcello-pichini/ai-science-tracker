GenDIReCT recovers the M87* ring from archival telescope data

GenDIReCT used diffusion-based reconstruction conditioned on calibration-independent closure invariants to analyze EHT observations from 2017, 2018 and 2021. The authors recover ring morphology and estimate a diameter of 42.2 ± 1.5 microarcseconds; geometric modeling gives 42.3 ± 1.1. This is a new analysis of existing observations, overlapping the target and some data of the cataloged 2023 PRIMO reconstruction. It is not a new observation or the first black-hole-shadow discovery.

Technology: GenDIReCT latent diffusion
