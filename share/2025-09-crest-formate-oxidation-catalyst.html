CRESt discovers a multielement formate-oxidation catalyst

CRESt combines multimodal models, knowledge-assisted Bayesian optimization and robotic synthesis and testing. Across more than 900 chemistries and 3,500 electrochemical tests, it identified an eight-element catalyst with a reported 9.3-fold improvement in cost-specific formate-oxidation performance. The metric is cost-specific, not a universal activity comparison.

Technology: CRESt multimodal model; knowledge-assisted Bayesian optimization
