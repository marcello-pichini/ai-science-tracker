Deep learning forecasts aftershock rates in seconds, comparable to ETAS

Neural networks trained on earthquake catalogues from California, New Zealand, Italy, Japan and Greece forecast the number of aftershocks in the following 24 hours after magnitude 4+ earthquakes. Their quality is reported as comparable to the operational ETAS model, while producing results in seconds rather than hours or days. The abstract reports no numeric skill scores, so the comparison rests on the full paper.

Technology: Deep neural networks for spatiotemporal seismicity rate forecasting, benchmarked against ETAS
