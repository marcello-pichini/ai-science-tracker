Deep learning resolves subtle seismic-wave changes across the 2016 Norcia earthquake cycle

A seven-layer convolutional neural network classified raw seismic waveforms recorded near Italy’s 2016 Mw 6.5 Norcia earthquake as pre- or post-mainshock and separated time-to-failure intervals with over 90% test accuracy. The authors interpret the model’s performance, frequency dependence and null tests as evidence that local waveforms encode evolving attenuation and elastic properties consistent with progressive fault damage before rupture and postseismic recovery.

Technology: Seven-layer convolutional neural network trained on local seismic waveforms
