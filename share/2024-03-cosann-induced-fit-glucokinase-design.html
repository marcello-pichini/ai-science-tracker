CoSaNN designs and tests an induced-fit carbohydrate kinase

CoSaNN combined neural structure prediction, learned sequence optimization and solubility ranking to design variants of an induced-fit ROK carbohydrate kinase. The authors measured expression, thermal stability and activity in vitro. The paper reports measurable expression for 189 of 348 designs in one table, while the strategy-level counts do not sum to that total; no success-rate claim is made here.

Technology: CoSaNN: AlphaFold2-based conformation modeling, ProteinMPNN sequence design and SolvIT graph neural network ranking

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: The authors applied CoSaNN to redesign the Arthrobacter polyphosphate glucokinase ROK kinase and performed expression, thermal-stability and enzyme-activity assays on designed variants. The primary Results table reports 189 designs with detectable soluble expression among 348 listed designs; its strategy counts (119+95+94 plus 40 in another strategy; rows in the prose/table are internally inconsistent) do not reconcile cleanly, so the record does not use a single total as a success-rate denominator.
  • Still unknown: The study tests a company-designed enzyme set in vitro; it does not establish industrial deployment. The primary paper’s count inconsistency remains explicitly disclosed.
  • AI role: CoSaNN uses deep-learning structure prediction (AlphaFold2), sequence optimization with ProteinMPNN and a learned solubility classifier (SolvIT) to rank enzyme designs for experimental characterization.

Evidence sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10945820/
