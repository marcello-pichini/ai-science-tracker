Knowledge-guided graph learning designed a printable nickel superalloy

A knowledge-informed graph attention model with uncertainty quantification selected the KG-AMS nickel superalloy composition and processing conditions for laser directed-energy deposition. The study reports experimental validation with defect area fractions of 0.037%–0.133% and hardness of 391–417 HV across tested parameter sets; it also reports room-temperature tensile strength of 1307 MPa and elongation of 22.2%. These measurements apply to the tested alloy and processing window, and larger builds remain untested. This entry covers KG-AMS only; the paper’s separate aluminum alloy KG-AMAA had already been reported by the same group in 2025.

Technology: Knowledge-informed graph attention network with uncertainty quantification (UQ-KGAT)
