GPT-6 Astra finds a counting proof of the Erdős–Sós tree-embedding conjecture

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A counting argument proves that every finite graph with average degree greater than t−2 contains every tree on t vertices, resolving the Erdős–Sós conjecture. The proof counts adjacency-marked vertex orderings and uses an induction on the tree to bound those not supporting a rooted copy. The exposition states the sharp classical theorem and records its proof was discovered by GPT-6 Astra; a Lean repository checks the benchmark formalization of the result. Related expository papers of the same proof are aliases, not separate discoveries.
