Machine learning prioritizes compounds active against heterogeneous glioblastoma stem-cell models

Researchers trained cell-survival predictors on image-based screening data from six patient-derived glioblastoma stem-cell lines and screened more than 12,000 compounds. Experimental follow-up identified three compounds with activity across the GBM phenotype; HSP90 inhibitor XL-888 eliminated all six tested lines at nanomolar IC50 values, with analyses consistent with HSP90 inhibition and downstream client-protein disruption.

Technology: Machine-learning cell-survival predictors trained on high-throughput phenotypic screening

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Experimental follow-up identified three compounds with activity across the GBM phenotype; HSP90 inhibitor XL-888 eliminated all six tested lines at nanomolar IC50 values, with analyses consistent with HSP90 inhibition and downstream client-protein disruption.
  • Still unknown: This is an in-vitro lead-discovery study across six patient-derived cell lines, not clinical efficacy. XL-888 is an existing HSP90 inhibitor and its proposed development as a GBM lead needs medicinal-chemistry optimization, including improved blood–brain-barrier penetration; no patient outcomes are reported.
  • AI role: ML models trained on in-house imaging/compound-response data predicted cell survival and prioritized compounds from a >12,000-compound screen; experimental assays across patient-derived GBM stem-cell models validated three leads and tested mechanisms. Researchers performed the wet-lab work and mechanism analyses.
