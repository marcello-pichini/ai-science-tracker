ExoGRU-guided experiments support RBM24 involvement in small-RNA secretion

A recurrent model of RNA-sequence features helped nominate RNA-binding proteins involved in secretion. Perturbation and binding experiments supported RBM24 involvement in the tested cell system, alongside previously implicated factors. This mechanism study was public in an April 2023 preprint before the 2024 journal version. It does not establish a universal secretion mechanism, in-vivo benefit or a therapeutic result.
