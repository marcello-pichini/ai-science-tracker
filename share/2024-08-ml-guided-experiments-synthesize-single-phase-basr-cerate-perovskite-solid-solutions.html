ML-guided experiments synthesize single-phase Ba–Sr cerate perovskite solid solutions

Machine-learning screening and expert down-selection identified Ba–Sr cerate oxide compositions for synthesis. The researchers used an automated rapid-sintering and dielectric-characterization platform and reported X-ray diffraction evidence of single-phase solid-solution specimens with refined orthorhombic Pnma structures. The finding is an experimental validation of selected ML-predicted compositions and a process-structure result; it does not establish device performance or broad predictive success.

Technology: Gradient-boosting perovskite classifier; VAE and composition-embedding screening

The text is inconsistent about the Sr fraction for one reported specimen (abstract/table/figure versus one results sentence), so this entry avoids assigning that composition. The paper validates selected members of a prior predicted set rather than claiming a universal synthesis predictor. Dielectric analysis is reported, but no application/device performance is established.
  • AI role: A gradient-boosting classifier trained on experimental perovskite/non-perovskite structures screened roughly 0.6 million possible compositions, assigning high perovskite likelihood to a candidate set. Variational-autoencoder and element-composition embeddings, plus human literature and synthesis-feasibility review, down-selected compositions that were then synthesized and characterized.
