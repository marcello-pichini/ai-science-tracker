DeepMet anticipates and helps identify dozens of previously unrecognized mammalian metabolites

DeepMet, an LSTM chemical language model trained on known human metabolites, generated a ranked space of metabolite-like structures and was integrated with mass spectrometry data. Researchers report identifying several dozen previously unrecognized mammalian metabolites across standards, human biofluids, mouse tissues and repository data. Some standard-supported assignments still have isomer ambiguity; the study also found known metabolites missing or misannotated in HMDB.

Technology: DeepMet chemical language model (three-layer LSTM over SMILES), integrated with MS/MS-spectrum prediction and meta-learning
