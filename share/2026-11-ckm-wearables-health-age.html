Wearable-based health age models for cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic risk profiling and coaching

Google Research reported a wearable-based health-age model associated with subsequent hypertension, cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes in the All of Us cohort (22,748 participants). A companion world model simulated 12-week trajectories. In 490 blinded paired reviews, coaches preferred simulation-grounded plans in 71.4% of evaluations; physicians rated 97.6% of physiological claims accurate. This summary is limited to the November 11, 2026 institutional blog. The authors state all evaluations were offline and retrospective; clinical effectiveness and durable habit change require prospective trials.

Technology: Coachable health-age model, SensorWM action-conditioned world model, feasibility-constrained optimizer with a language model agent, multimodal CKM deviation model
