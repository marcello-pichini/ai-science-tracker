WDNet-guided DESI follow-up confirms 75 new white dwarfs and 7 cataclysmic variables

WDNet detected candidates in DESI Legacy Surveys DR9 images. The researchers cross-matched 18,242 photometric candidates with DESI DR1 spectra, obtaining 719 candidate spectra; manual visual inspection of those spectra identified 75 new white dwarfs and 7 new cataclysmic variables. These spectroscopic classifications, not the much larger image-candidate list, are the reported verified sample.

Technology: WDNet, a deep-learning object-detection network based on YOLOv8 with multi-scale feature fusion

Evidence context (pilot; stated by the cited sources, not an editorial judgment):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Tested: WDNet detected candidates in DESI Legacy Surveys DR9 images.
  • Still unknown: The authors confirmed 75 new WDs and 7 new CVs among the 719 candidates with DESI spectra, and explicitly note substantial contamination among remaining candidates and selection effects in the spectroscopic subsample. No atmosphere-model fitting was done for all identifications; the authors recommend further high-S/N spectroscopic modeling. The article's abstract/results/conclusion give inconsistent counts for the full detected/candidate catalog (18,242, 61,624, and 63,214 are all reported in different sections); this entry relies only on the consistent 719 spectral matches and 75+7 inspected confirmations. The full photometric catalog is not counted as confirmed discoveries.
  • AI role: WDNet detected and localized white-dwarf candidates in DESI Legacy Surveys photometric images, thereby prioritizing objects for DESI spectral cross-match and manual spectral inspection. Human inspection of DESI spectra supplied the classification confirmation; the neural network did not itself confirm the new stellar classes.
