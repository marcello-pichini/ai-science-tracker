New zirconium-indium-nickel superconductor identified with machine learning

Predictive models and experts guided fabrication of candidates and identified a zirconium, indium and nickel alloy with a superconducting transition around 9 K. The press release reports a three-month discovery; this is not a room-temperature superconductor or a demonstration of industrial use.
