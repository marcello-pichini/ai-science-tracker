FlyWire: the complete map of neurons in the Drosophila brain

Connectome of the adult Drosophila brain: nearly 140,000 neurons, over 15 million synaptic connections, and over 8,000 cell types. The page says deep neural networks segmented images and extracted neurons, but automated segmentation had millions of errors and required about 33 years of human correction across over 50 labs and citizen scientists.

Technology: Deep neural networks for segmentation
