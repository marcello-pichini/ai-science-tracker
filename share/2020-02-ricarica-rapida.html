Closed-loop optimization of fast-charging battery protocols

A model estimating cycle life from the first 100 cycles, paired with Bayesian optimization, assessed 224 fast-charging protocols in 16 days; the authors estimate exhaustive search without early prediction would take over 500 days. It optimizes protocols, not new materials; only the abstract was reviewed.

Technology: Bayesian optimization with early prediction

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: 224 fast-charging protocols tested experimentally by measuring battery cycle life; high-cycle-life protocols identified in 16 days, versus an estimated 500+ days for exhaustive search without early prediction.
  • Still unknown: The abstract lists no explicit limitations; generalization to other problems is suggested but not discussed; only the abstract was read.
  • AI role: An early-prediction model estimated final cycle life from the first cycles, and Bayesian optimization chose which protocols to test next.
