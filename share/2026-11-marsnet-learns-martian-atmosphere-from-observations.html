MarsNet learns the Martian atmosphere from observations alone

Archived as a model-development and atmospheric-reanalysis benchmark. The November 9, 2026 MarsNet preprint evaluates temperature and pressure estimates against held-out Martian observations. The reviewed abstract and discussion describe improved near-surface estimation and possible future uses as a discovery tool, rather than a separate atmospheric finding. The authors note poorer performance aloft and that much temperature skill precedes neural training. The historical ID and source are retained outside discovery counts.

Technology: MarsNet, a learned forecast-and-update model with a fixed physical structure and bounded Kalman update
