AlphaFold structure analysis helps reveal PHGDH transcriptional activity linked to Alzheimer’s amyloid pathology

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: AlphaFold-based structural analysis highlighted a helix-helix-turn-helix subdomain in PHGDH that resembles a TALE homeodomain, prompting tests of a previously unrecognized DNA-binding and transcriptional function. Experiments supported PHGDH binding to promoters and activation of IKKα and HMGB1 in astrocytes, independently of its metabolic enzyme activity. In mouse models and human brain organoids, PHGDH expression promoted amyloid pathology; a blood-brain-barrier-permeable inhibitor of its transcriptional function reduced amyloid pathology and improved measured behavioral deficits in mice.

Technology: AlphaFold structure prediction, followed by structural comparison of the predicted PHGDH fold

AI contribution
The researchers inspected AlphaFold-predicted PHGDH structure and identified a helix-helix-turn-helix subdomain resembling a TALE homeodomain, which motivated the hypothesis and targeted mutation tests for DNA binding. AlphaFold did not establish PHGDH function or disease causality; the transcriptional activity and pathology effects were tested through biochemical, cellular, organoid and mouse experiments.
Test and result
AlphaFold-based structural analysis highlighted a helix-helix-turn-helix subdomain in PHGDH that resembles a TALE homeodomain, prompting tests of a previously unrecognized DNA-binding and transcriptional function.
Limitation / still unknown
The AlphaFold-derived structural resemblance generated the mechanistic hypothesis; experiments establish the reported PHGDH transcriptional and pathology effects in the tested models. The inhibitor and behavioral effects were tested in mice, not people. Human brain organoid findings are in vitro and do not establish clinical disease causation or treatment efficacy. PHGDH overexpression alone did not reproduce all Alzheimer’s disease features, and no human therapeutic outcome is reported.
