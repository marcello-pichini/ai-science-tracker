SMDET-selected ultracool-dwarf candidates include two objects with spectroscopically confirmed T-dwarf types

The SMDET image-level neural network identified 118 previously uncatalogued high-proper-motion ultracool-dwarf candidates in WISE time-series images. Follow-up near-infrared spectra for two selected objects yielded T5 and T3 classifications, confirming their estimated spectral types within one subtype.

Technology: SMDET, a convolutional neural network for detecting faint moving sources in WISE time-series coadds
