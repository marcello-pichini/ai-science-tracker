Evo genomic language model designs functional toxin-antitoxin and anti-CRISPR systems

Evo used genomic context to generate functional bacterial defence-system components. The paper reports an AI-generated toxin and low-sequence-similarity antitoxins that neutralized it in growth assays, including antitoxins that also acted against multiple natural toxins; it also reports five generated anti-CRISPR proteins that protected bacteria against Cas9 activity in cell and phage assays. Two generated anti-CRISPR proteins lacked significant sequence similarity to known proteins. These experiments demonstrate function-guided genomic design beyond simple copying of natural sequences.

Technology: Evo 1.5, a long-context generative genomic language model trained on prokaryotic DNA sequences

AI generated candidates; the biological activity was established experimentally.
