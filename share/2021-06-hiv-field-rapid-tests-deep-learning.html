Deep learning reads HIV rapid tests photographed in rural South Africa

Deep learning models trained on 11,374 smartphone-tablet images of HIV lateral-flow tests, captured by 60 fieldworkers, classified results with 97.8% sensitivity and 100% specificity in a pilot field study compared with human visual reading. The approach also reduced false positives and false negatives relative to human readers. Caveat: a pilot deployment in one rural setting, so wider field validation is still needed.

Technology: Deep learning image classifier on mobile-captured rapid test images
