Deep learning helps prioritize potent MAGL inhibitor candidates

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Deep graph neural networks trained on 13,490 Minisci reaction outcomes predicted reaction feasibility for a virtual library of 26,375 compounds derived from moderate MAGL inhibitors. Combined with physicochemical and structure-based scoring, this workflow prioritized 212 candidates; 14 were synthesized and showed subnanomolar MAGL inhibition, with reported potency improvements up to 4,500-fold over the starting hit. This is an AI-assisted, human-selected laboratory optimization result, not an autonomous workflow or a therapeutic outcome.

Technology: Deep graph neural networks for reaction outcome prediction; multi-objective molecular optimization
