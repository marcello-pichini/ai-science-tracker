Deep-learning CADe colonoscopy raises adenoma detection in a double-blind trial

In a double-blind randomised trial of 962 patients in China (484 analysed with CADe, 478 with a sham system), the deep-learning system raised adenoma detection to 34% (165/484) from 28% (132/478), OR 1.36 (95% CI 1.03-1.79). The primary analysis was per protocol, the trial was single-centre, and the abstract reports no ITT analysis.

Technology: Deep-learning computer-aided detection (CADe) system for polyps during colonoscopy
