Earth AI for public health: geospatial models across five use cases

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The PDFM model increased the explained variance in MMR vaccination coverage from 16% to 22% across 146 counties on the US–Canada border, and improved weekly correct cholera-risk-area rankings in the DRC from 1.78 to 2.10. Gains for cardiovascular mortality and postpartum depression were small or not significant.

Technology: Population Dynamics Foundation Model (PDFM), TimesFM

Editorial relevance 47 medicine Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
PDFM combines search trends, mobility, built-environment and environmental data into location embeddings used without task fine-tuning; for dengue it was paired with TimesFM 2.0.
Test and result
Five partner-led evaluations compared PDFM embeddings with baselines: MMR explained variance rose from 16% to 22% across 146 border counties and cholera Precision@5 improved from 1.78 to 2.10; cardiovascular gains were not significant.
Limitation / still unknown
Several results were non-significant or small, cholera gains were not significant at one or two weeks, and the authors cite static embeddings as a limitation.
