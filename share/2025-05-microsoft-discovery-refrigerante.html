Microsoft Discovery: a PFAS-free refrigerant in about 200 hours

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Microsoft researchers used AI models and high-performance simulations to identify a prototype PFAS-free immersion coolant for data centers in about 200 hours, then synthesized it in under four months; initial tests of key properties match predictions. Microsoft describes it as an experiment still under analysis.
