Microsoft Discovery: a PFAS-free refrigerant in about 200 hours

Microsoft researchers used AI models and high-performance simulations to identify a prototype PFAS-free immersion coolant for data centers in about 200 hours, then synthesized it in under four months; initial tests of key properties match predictions. Microsoft describes it as an experiment still under analysis.

Technology: Microsoft Discovery (agentic orchestration and high-performance computing)
