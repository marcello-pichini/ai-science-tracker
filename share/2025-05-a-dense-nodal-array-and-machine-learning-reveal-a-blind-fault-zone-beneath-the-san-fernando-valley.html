A dense nodal array and machine learning reveal a blind fault zone beneath the San Fernando Valley

A machine-learning workflow analyzed one month of recordings from a 140-station nodal array and located 62 earthquakes, including 36 not in the regional catalog. Their distribution exposes a previously unidentified 5.3 × 4 km blind fault zone about 5 km beneath the southern San Fernando Valley; the small-event cluster is rare in the regional catalog despite a 2014 Mb 4.4 earthquake in the zone.

Technology: Previously developed machine-learning seismic phase picker, fine-tuned on similar waveforms, with two-step event association
