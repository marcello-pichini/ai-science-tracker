Evo: a foundation model for DNA, from molecules to genomes

Published in Science, Evo (Arc Institute, Stanford, and UC Berkeley) designed EvoCas9-1, a CRISPR system sharing only about 73% of its sequence with CRISPR-Cas9 but with comparable activity after eleven designs, and new IS200/IS605 transposons that cut and paste DNA. The team calls this the beginning and conducted an ethics and safety study.
