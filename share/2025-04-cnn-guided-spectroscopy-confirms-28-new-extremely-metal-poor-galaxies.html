CNN-guided spectroscopy confirms 28 new extremely metal-poor galaxies

A three-stage convolutional-neural-network pipeline screened more than 7.7 million SDSS DR17 imaging sources lacking SDSS spectra, then selected targets for observation. Follow-up spectra of 45 candidates confirmed all as metal-poor galaxies, including 28 newly discovered extremely metal-poor systems; 18 have weak or undetected [N II] lines and oxygen-abundance upper limits.

Technology: Three-stage CNN characterization pipeline (metal-poor galaxy classifier, XMP classifier, and N2-index predictor)

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: A three-stage convolutional-neural-network pipeline screened more than 7.7 million SDSS DR17 imaging sources lacking SDSS spectra, then selected targets for observation.
  • Still unknown: The 28 new XMP classifications derive from follow-up of 45 targeted candidates, not the full 7.7-million-source imaging search. For 18 galaxies with weak or absent [N II], oxygen abundance is reported as a 2-sigma upper limit; the paper also reports four possible low-metallicity AGN candidates, which are not counted as confirmed here.
  • AI role: The CNN pipeline ranked imaging-only sources using predicted spectral line ratios to nominate telescope targets; subsequent INT and SOAR spectroscopy established their metallicity classifications and confirmed 28 as new XMP galaxies.
