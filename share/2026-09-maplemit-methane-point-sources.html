Global methane point sources mapped from EMIT imaging spectroscopy with a vision transformer

The MAPL-EMIT vision transformer uses full EMIT radiance spectra to jointly retrieve methane enhancements, plume outlines and source locations across global scenes, trained on 3.6 million synthetic plumes. The paper reports high recall and precision and better detection of weak plumes than matched-filter methods, but the abstract shows only synthetic evaluation; real-plume validation is not confirmed from the abstract.

Technology: MAPL-EMIT (end-to-end vision transformer on EMIT hyperspectral radiance)
