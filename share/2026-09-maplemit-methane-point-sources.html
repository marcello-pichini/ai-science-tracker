Global methane point sources mapped from EMIT imaging spectroscopy with a vision transformer

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: MAPL-EMIT used a vision transformer trained on 3.6 million synthetic plumes embedded in EMIT radiance data to map methane sources. Beyond synthetic tests, the paper abstract reports recovery of 84% of known annotated plume complexes across 1,084 EMIT granules. Comparisons with airborne data, landfills and controlled releases supported previously uncaptured sources. This is source-reported validation, not an independent emissions audit.

Technology: MAPL-EMIT (end-to-end vision transformer on EMIT hyperspectral radiance)
