WenHai: a deep neural network forecasts the eddying ocean

The WenHai data-driven global ocean forecast system outperforms a state-of-the-art eddy-resolving numerical model and an AI-based ocean forecast system for temperature and salinity profiles, sea surface temperature, sea level anomaly and near-surface currents at lead times from 1 day to at least 10 days. Skill beyond 10 days is not reported in the abstract, and the computational speed-up claimed in secondary coverage was not confirmed in the paper abstract.
