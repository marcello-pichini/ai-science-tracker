MOFGen: agentic AI generates MOF linkers and five AI-designed MOFs are synthesized

MOFGen, a multi-agent system combining language models, diffusion models and quantum-mechanical checks, generated hundreds of thousands of novel MOF structures and synthesizable linkers. The authors report that five AI-designed MOFs were synthesized through high-throughput experiments. Evidence caveat: the abstract describes the validation only generally, the structure counts are computational, and this is an arXiv preprint not confirmed as peer-reviewed.

Technology: MOFGen (multi-agent LLM system with diffusion model)
