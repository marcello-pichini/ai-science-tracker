RFdiffusion2 scaffolds enzyme active sites at atomic level

RFdiffusion2 scaffolded all 41 active sites in a diverse benchmark, compared with 16 for previous methods. Designs were tested experimentally, with fewer than 96 sequences needed per case to find active enzymes across three catalytic mechanisms. The journal abstract's three-mechanism count conflicts with a five-reaction claim in the Institute for Protein Design announcement, so the abstract should be treated as authoritative.

Evidence context (pilot; stated by the cited sources, not an editorial judgment):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Designed enzymes were expressed and screened experimentally for retroaldolase, cysteine hydrolase and zinc hydrolase activity, with kinetic constants measured for the best variants.
  • Still unknown: Authors state the designs are not as active as native enzymes, the theozymes may miss interactions needed for high activity, and scaffolding catalytic atoms does not guarantee proficient enzymes.
  • AI role: RFdiffusion2 generated scaffolds from atom-level active-site descriptions, LigandMPNN assigned sequences, and structure predictors filtered designs before testing.
