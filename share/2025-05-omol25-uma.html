OMol25 and UMA: quantum-chemistry data and a universal model for atoms

Meta released Open Molecules 2025, which it calls the largest and most varied set of high-accuracy quantum calculations for biomolecules, metal complexes, and electrolytes (about six billion compute hours), and UMA, an interatomic potential trained on over 30 billion atoms. The post gives no numerical accuracy values.
