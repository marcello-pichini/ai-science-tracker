Active-learning experiments reveal distinct on-field and off-field polarization-switching behavior in ferroelectrics

An active-learning workflow paired with automated piezoresponse-force microscopy discovered relationships between local ferroelectric domain structure and polarization-switching hysteresis. The experimentally selected sampling paths and points differed substantially between on-field and off-field hysteresis loops, indicating that the two measurement modes are dominated by different mechanisms. This is a bounded result from the reported ferroelectric sample and spectroscopy workflow, not a universal mechanism established for all ferroelectrics.

Technology: Machine-learning active-learning framework for automated piezoresponse force microscopy and spectroscopy
