From 32 million candidates to a new battery electrolyte

With the PNNL laboratory, Microsoft screened 32.6 million materials using AI models and high-performance computing, narrowing them to 500,000, then 800, 150, and finally an electrolyte replacing some lithium with sodium (about 70% less lithium). PNNL synthesized it and built a working solid-state battery. Validation and optimization are ongoing; the post is by a Microsoft executive.

Technology: Azure Quantum Elements (AI and DFT simulations)
