Autonomous machine-learning experiments identify a dual-anion aqueous sodium electrolyte with a wider stability window

A robotic electrochemistry stand coupled to Bayesian optimization tested 140 aqueous electrolyte formulas over 40 hours and selected a non-intuitive sodium mixture of NaClO4 and NaNO3. On platinum, the blend had a wider electrochemical stability window than the NaClO4 baseline despite lower total salt concentration; follow-up tests reported a 24 mV increase in the OER potential at the stated leakage-current criterion and 58% lower current density at 2 V. The result is a measured electrolyte formulation under the paper’s electrochemical protocols, not evidence of a commercial battery or field-ready cell.

Technology: Dragonfly Bayesian optimization software coupled to the Otto robotic electrochemistry test stand
