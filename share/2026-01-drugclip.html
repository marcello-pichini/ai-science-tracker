DrugCLIP screens about 10,000 human proteins against 500 million compounds

The January 2026 Science abstract reports DrugCLIP screening roughly 10,000 human proteins against 500 million compounds. Experimental hits included 15% for the norepinephrine transporter, with structures of two inhibitor complexes, and 17.5% for TRIP12 using AlphaFold2 structures. Most database results remain computational. The work also has a bioRxiv preprint first indexed in September 2024.

Technology: DrugCLIP (deep contrastive learning)
