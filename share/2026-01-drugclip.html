DrugCLIP screens about 10,000 human proteins against 500 million compounds

Contrastive learning, reported up to 10 million times faster than docking, gave wet-lab hit rates of 15% for the norepinephrine transporter and 17.5% for thyroid hormone receptor interactor 12, with two inhibitor structures solved. Hit rates cover a small set of targets; most of the genome-wide screen is computational.

Technology: DrugCLIP (deep contrastive learning)
