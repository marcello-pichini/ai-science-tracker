Neural operators reveal nonreciprocal interactions behind microfluidic nonlinear waves

Interpreting a microfluidic droplet experiment movie with a Fourier neural operator and sparse regression yielded a KdV–Burgers-type continuum model containing a dispersive term absent from the prior Burgers description. The authors’ simulations attribute the nonlinear-wave propagation and stabilization to nonreciprocal hydrodynamic interactions. The work analyzes existing experimental data and does not report a new experiment or a general proof for all nonequilibrium crystals.

Technology: Fourier neural operator, interpretable neural-network pipeline and sparse regression
