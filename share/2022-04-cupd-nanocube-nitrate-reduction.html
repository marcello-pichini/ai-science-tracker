Machine-learned design of CuPd nanocubes for electrocatalytic nitrate reduction to ammonia

Interpretable machine learning identified ordered B2 CuPd intermetallics whose adsorption energies break the usual scaling relations. The team synthesized monodisperse CuPd nanocubes that reached a 92.5% Faradaic efficiency at -0.5 V RHE for nitrate reduction to ammonia, with a yield of 6.25 mol h-1 g-1 at -0.6 V. Performance was measured in the authors' own electrochemical tests, and the design rules come from a limited set of predicted intermetallics.

Technology: Interpretable machine learning on d-state Pauli repulsion descriptors (DFT-based)
