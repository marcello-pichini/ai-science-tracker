Satellite map of industrial activity at sea

A model applied to about 2 million GB of Sentinel-1 radar imagery (2017–2021) maps vessels and infrastructure: about 75% of industrial fishing vessels are not publicly tracked, over 25% of shipping and energy activity is missing from AIS data, wind turbines more than doubled, and global fishing fell 12% during COVID. Published in Nature; ‘fishing’ classification is inferred; source is the organization’s own release.

Technology: Deep learning on radar imagery
