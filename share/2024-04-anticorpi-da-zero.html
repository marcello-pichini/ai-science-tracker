Antibodies designed from scratch with RFdiffusion

Baker’s group showed RFdiffusion can be adapted to generate antibodies: in laboratory tests, designs bound influenza virus and other targets as intended. Proof-of-concept study, first circulated as a preprint in March 2024; experts quoted by Nature call it promising but an early step; no affinity values reported.
