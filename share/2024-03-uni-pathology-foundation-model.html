UNI: a general-purpose foundation model for computational pathology

Archived from discovery counts: this entry documents retrospective method benchmarks rather than a qualifying scientific discovery. UNI was pretrained on more than 100 million image patches from over 100,000 H&E whole-slide images across 20 major tissue types. Across 34 pathology tasks, including cancer classification and transplant-rejection assessment, it outperformed established pathology AI models. Evaluation is retrospective, and the weights are gated under a non-commercial academic licence.

Technology: UNI (self-supervised vision transformer foundation model for pathology)
