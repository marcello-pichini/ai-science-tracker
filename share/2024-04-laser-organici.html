Closed-loop, distributed discovery of organic laser emitters

In a two-month campaign, more than 1,000 candidates were synthesized and tested at six laboratories across five sites on three continents, identifying at least 21 high-performance candidates as gain materials from a space of more than 150,000 experiments. These are gain candidates, not confirmed lasers; the page gives no laser metrics; source is a consortium press release.
