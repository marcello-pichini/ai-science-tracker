Generative model identifies high-conductivity liquid electrolyte formulations

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A predictive model and permutation-invariant generative approach were used to design liquid electrolyte mixtures. The primary v3 preprint reports three experimentally identified formulations with high ionic conductivity and anion-concentrated solvation structure. The later journal abstract additionally mentions cycling stability; that claim is excluded here because its version-specific test was not reviewed.
