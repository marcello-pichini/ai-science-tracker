Machine learning guides a proposed UCd3 intermetallic phase assignment

A support-vector classifier using composition-derived elemental features predicted a Cu₃Au-type structure for UCd₃ and guided synthesis. Powder X-ray diffraction from the second of two samples showed peaks the authors fit to a UCd₃ phase with a = 4.648(2) Å; the sample also contained uranium, uranium oxide and cadmium, while the first sample differed. This is the authors’ proposed phase assignment from a multiphase powder, not phase-pure material or independent structural confirmation.

Technology: Support-vector machine discriminant analysis; Gaussian-process regression for feature completion
