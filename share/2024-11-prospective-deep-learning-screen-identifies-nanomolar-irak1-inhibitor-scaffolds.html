Prospective deep-learning screen identifies nanomolar IRAK1 inhibitor scaffolds

Ro5's prospective HydraScreen structure-based deep-learning screen ranked compounds for IRAK1 and was evaluated against a 47,000-compound robotic high-throughput screen. The study reports that 23.8% of IRAK1 hits were in HydraScreen's top 1% and identifies three nanomolar scaffolds, two described in the abstract as novel IRAK1 candidates.

Technology: HydraScreen structure-based deep-learning virtual screening, with SpectraView knowledge-graph target evaluation
