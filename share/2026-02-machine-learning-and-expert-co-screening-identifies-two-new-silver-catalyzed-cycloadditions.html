Machine-learning and expert co-screening identifies two new silver-catalyzed cycloadditions

Researchers at the Zelinsky Institute used unsupervised machine learning to filter and cluster candidate atom-economic cycloaddition reactions generated from quantum-chemical data, then used a digital co-expert and chemist review to prioritize experiments. The team experimentally confirmed two previously undescribed silver-catalyzed cycloaddition reactions yielding new products within about one week.

Technology: Machine-learning candidate filtering and clustering combined with the digital co-expert reaction-prioritization workflow
