Co-training identifies a high-efficiency Cr-activated NIR phosphor

Semi-supervised co-training with kernel ridge regression and support-vector regression expanded the labeled phosphor dataset and selected Lu3Y2Ga3O12:Cr3+. The study reports emission near 750 nm with internal and external quantum efficiencies of 97.1% and 38.8%, respectively, and a fabricated laboratory phosphor-converted LED. The abstract’s values are author-reported laboratory measurements, not commercial product performance.

Technology: Semi-supervised KRR/SVR co-training
