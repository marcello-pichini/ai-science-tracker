African elephants address one another with calls resembling names

About 470 calls from 101 callers to 117 recipients in Kenya: a model trained only on acoustic structure identifies the intended recipient, and playback experiments show elephants respond more to calls addressed to them. The model confirms a previously suspected pattern; more data are needed to isolate the ‘names’.

Technology: Acoustic classification model
