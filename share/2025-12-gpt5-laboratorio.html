GPT-5 and molecular cloning: 79-fold greater efficiency

In a cycle with Red Queen Bio, GPT-5 optimized a cloning protocol, introducing RecA and gp32 as a new mechanism: 2.6-fold improvement with the enzymatic reaction, 36-fold with transformation, and 79-fold when combined. This applies to the model system used, with scientists carrying out the protocols; reported by OpenAI.
