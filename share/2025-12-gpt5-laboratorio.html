GPT-5 and molecular cloning: 79-fold greater efficiency

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In a cycle with Red Queen Bio, GPT-5 optimized a cloning protocol, introducing RecA and gp32 as a new mechanism: 2.6-fold improvement with the enzymatic reaction, 36-fold with transformation, and 79-fold when combined. This applies to the model system used, with scientists carrying out the protocols; reported by OpenAI.

Technology: GPT-5

AI contribution
GPT-5 reasoned about the protocol and proposed modifications, including a RecA and gp32 mechanism; humans only ran the protocol and uploaded data.
Test and result
Scientists carried out GPT-5-modified cloning protocols over several rounds; the final protocol recovered 79 times more sequence-verified clones for a fixed amount of input DNA than the baseline.
Limitation / still unknown
The work used a benign experimental system with a limited task scope.
