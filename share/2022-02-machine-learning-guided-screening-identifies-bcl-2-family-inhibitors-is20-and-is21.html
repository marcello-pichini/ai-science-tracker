Machine-learning-guided screening identifies Bcl-2-family inhibitors IS20 and IS21

Machine-learning-guided virtual screening followed by surface-plasmon-resonance and biological assays identified IS20 and IS21 as compounds binding anti-apoptotic Bcl-2, Bcl-xL and Mcl-1 proteins. Both affected cancer-cell viability and induced apoptosis/autophagic flux in tested cell models; IS21 also reduced tumor growth in leukemia and melanoma mouse models.
