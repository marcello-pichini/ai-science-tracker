A mobile robotic chemist

A mobile robot ran 688 experiments in eight days in a ten-variable space, guided by a batch Bayesian algorithm, to search for photocatalysts that produce hydrogen from water; the best mixtures were six times more active than the initial formulations. This optimizes a formulation, not a new structure; only the abstract was reviewed.

Technology: Batch Bayesian optimization and mobile robot
