Federated learning model predicts oxygen needs in COVID-19 patients

The EXAM federated model, trained on data from 20 institutes without sharing patient data, achieved an average AUC above 0.92 for predicting oxygen requirements at 24 and 72 hours. At the largest independent test site it reached sensitivity 0.950 and specificity 0.882, with a 16% average AUC gain over single-site models. Caveat: retrospective data from the pandemic period and unharmonized sites.

Technology: EXAM federated learning model (EMR data and chest X-rays)
