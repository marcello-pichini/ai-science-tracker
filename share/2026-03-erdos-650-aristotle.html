Erdős Problem #650 solved with ChatGPT strategy and Aristotle Lean proof

The authors prove f(m) = min(m, ceil(2 sqrt(m))) for all m, resolving Erdős Problem #650 on Bloom's site, where f(m) counts disjoint divisibility pairs in an interval for an m-element set. The detailed argument was formally verified in Lean by Harmonic's Aristotle, following a strategy proposed by ChatGPT. Caveat: arXiv preprint; the AI contribution is the strategy and formalization, not an independently checked autonomous solution.

Technology: ChatGPT (strategy) and Aristotle (Lean formalization)
