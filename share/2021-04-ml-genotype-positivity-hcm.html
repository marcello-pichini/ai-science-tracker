23 Apr 2021Columbia University Irving Medical Center

Machine learning predicts genotype positivity in hypertrophic cardiomyopathy

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Three machine learning models were built on clinical and cardiac imaging data from 102 Columbia patients and tested on 76 patients from Massachusetts General Hospital; 63 of 178 patients (35%) were genotype positive. The random forest model reached an AUROC of 0.92 (95% CI 0.85-0.99) in the test set, above the Toronto HCM Genotype Score (0.77, 95% CI 0.65-0.90). Cohorts are small and retrospective, so the result is a prediction benchmark, not evidence about screening outcomes.

Technology: Random forest and gradient boosted decision tree models on clinical and cardiac imaging data
