Active learning selected antimicrobial polymer P7

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: An automated Design-Build-Test-Learn campaign applied machine learning and high-throughput synthesis/characterization to a library of 13,728 polymer combinations. Across four active-learning rounds, the authors selected seven candidates for detailed testing. The leading polymer P7 had a reported MIC of 2 μg/mL and IC20 of 256 μg/mL, and was tested in in-vitro and animal models. These laboratory and preclinical results do not establish clinical efficacy or safety.

Technology: Graph Transformer model with active-learning selection
