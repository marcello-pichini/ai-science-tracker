Paper2Agent integrates perturbation datasets to prioritize GPR137 at a psoriasis risk locus

Paper2Agent-generated agents used AlphaGenome to prioritize GPR137 as the top predicted gene affected by psoriasis-associated variant rs887314 in CD4+ T cells, then integrated that prediction with existing MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq data. A cross-screen correlation of gene-expression signatures supported GPR137 as the probable causal gene under stimulated conditions (Spearman rho 0.613 at 8 h and 0.630 at 48 h; FDR < 0.05), but not at rest. The paper presents this as activation-dependent computational evidence.

Technology: Paper2Agent-generated AlphaGenome, MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq agents, coordinated through an AI co-scientist

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: retrospective or benchmark
  • Tested: Paper2Agent-generated agents used AlphaGenome to prioritize GPR137 as the top predicted gene affected by psoriasis-associated variant rs887314 in CD4+ T cells, then integrated that prediction with existing MPRA-coupled scCRISPRi and CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq data.
  • Still unknown: The analysis reuses existing published datasets and does not report new wet-lab experiments for this psoriasis case. AlphaGenome supplied a model prediction; cross-dataset signature correlation supports, but does not establish, causal biology. The GPR137 concordance was significant only after stimulation, and the authors describe it as probable/activation-dependent. A human researcher selected which AI-proposed strategy to run. Primary paper source checked; no independent replication assessed.
  • AI role: The AlphaGenome paper agent scored and ranked candidate genes at rs887314. Under human-in-the-loop supervision, the AI co-scientist inspected two other paper agents' datasets, proposed ten validation strategies, and the researcher selected signature-correlation analysis. The resulting cross-screen, cross-modality analysis found significant concordance for GPR137 only under stimulated conditions.
