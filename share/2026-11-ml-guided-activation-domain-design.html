Machine learning guided design of strong transcriptional activation domains

The preprint abstract reports computational generation of 500,000 candidate transcriptional activation domains and selection of 18,750 high-scoring sequences for yeast experiments using sequence-to-function models including three neural networks. More than 90% of 16,770 well-measured sequences functioned as activation domains. Some designed sequences retained activity in fungi, plants and human cells. Model agreement motivated the MADRIGAL consensus predictor. Review is limited to the abstract and original-server metadata.

Technology: Neural-network sequence-to-function models (three networks); consensus predictor MADRIGAL
