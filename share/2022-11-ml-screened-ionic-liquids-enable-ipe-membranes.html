Machine-learning screening guides ionic-liquid polymer electrolytes for lithium-metal batteries

A machine-learning and quantum-chemistry workflow screened ionic liquids for conductivity and electrochemical-window properties, then guided selection for rigid-rod polyelectrolyte membranes. The 2022 preprint reports ~50 μm membranes and lithium-cell testing, including symmetric-cell cycling up to 800 hours at room temperature and reversible operation at 6 mA cm−2 at 80°C. Its preprint abstract reports 146 mAh g−1 at 5C/80°C; the later 2023 journal abstract lists 3C, so those version-specific figures are not conflated.

Technology: Graph-convolutional neural network and ensemble SVM/random-forest/XGBoost screening with Psi4 quantum-chemistry descriptors
