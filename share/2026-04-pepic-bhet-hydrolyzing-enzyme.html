PEPIC identifies a BHET-hydrolyzing enzyme candidate

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: PEPIC is a sequence-based convolutional model that predicts PET-hydrolyzing potential. The study reports prioritizing W2061_PET9 and experimentally showing that purified protein converts soluble BHET into MHET, confirmed by HPLC and mass spectrometry. The experiment used a soluble PET intermediate; it does not establish degradation of polymer PET. Homology screening and human selection also contributed.

Technology: PEPIC sequence convolutional neural network

Editorial relevance 60 biology Original sourcePaper Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
PEPIC scores sequences for predicted PET-hydrolytic activity to prioritize a candidate after genomic/homology selection; direct assay validates soluble BHET turnover.
Test and result
Purified W2061_PET9 converted BHET to MHET in an in-vitro assay confirmed chromatographically and by mass spectrometry.
Limitation / still unknown
Polymer PET degradation, exact sequence-level prior characterization and independent replication are not established.
