AI detects Parkinson's disease from nocturnal breathing signals

An attention-based AI model extracting breathing from radio waves during sleep detected Parkinson's disease with an AUC of 0.90 on held-out data and 0.85 on external sets, across 7,671 individuals. Its severity estimates correlated with MDS-UPDRS scores (R = 0.94), and it worked for at-home assessment. Caveat: largely retrospective data and no prospective diagnostic trial reported.

Technology: Attention-based deep learning on contactless radio-wave breathing signals
