Few-shot protein-language-model design yields high-output bicyclogermacrene synthase variants

After experimentally screening activity-enhancing single mutants of LdTPS5, the researchers used PIFS-PLM, a few-shot model that selects features from protein-language-model embeddings using active-site and conserved-residue information, to propose mutation combinations. All 10 tested model-predicted combinations were active; nine produced more than 100 mg/L bicyclogermacrene in yeast, and the top variant produced 213.59 mg/L versus 2.93 mg/L for wild type (about 73-fold higher yield). A related selectivity-optimized combination increased bicyclogermacrene selectivity by 15%.

Technology: PIFS-PLM (Physics-Inspired Feature Selection of Protein Language Models), using ESM-2 embeddings with a few-shot mutation-combination classifier

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: After experimentally screening activity-enhancing single mutants of LdTPS5, the researchers used PIFS-PLM, a few-shot model that selects features from protein-language-model embeddings using active-site and conserved-residue information, to propose mutation combinations.
  • Still unknown: The 73-fold result is product yield in the reported yeast system, reflecting effects of enzyme activity and expression; it is not a 73-fold increase in catalytic rate. The selectivity improvement was 15%. The model relies on a defined active site/conserved-site set and a small, enzyme-specific experimental dataset. Product profiles differed between cell-free extracts and yeast; these results do not establish universal performance across enzymes or production settings.
  • AI role: After single substitutions were screened and activity-enhancing mutations experimentally validated, PIFS-PLM used 60–100 measured combinations to learn from selected ESM-2 features at active-site and conserved residues and predict beneficial combinations. Ten predicted LdTPS5 variants were tested; all were active, and the top variants substantially increased measured yeast product yield. The model also guided a selectivity optimization test. Initial single-mutation nomination used MM/GBSA or sequence conservation analysis, separate from PIFS-PLM combinatorial predictions.

Evidence sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13502649/
