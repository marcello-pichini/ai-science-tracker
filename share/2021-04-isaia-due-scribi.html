Two scribes in the Great Isaiah Scroll: new evidence from handwriting

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Independent analyses of character shapes distinguish two groups across the scroll’s 54 columns, consistent with two scribes alternating around the manuscript’s midpoint. This is statistical evidence from one artifact; the writers’ identities are unknown, and other explanations for the differences are not entirely ruled out.

Technology: Pattern recognition and Kohonen maps
