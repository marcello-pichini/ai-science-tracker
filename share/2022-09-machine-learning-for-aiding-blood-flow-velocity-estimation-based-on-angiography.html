Machine Learning for Aiding Blood Flow Velocity Estimation Based on Angiography

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: MLP, CNN and LSTM models estimated blood-flow velocity fields from angiographic projection images and were compared with optical-flow/PIV measurements in an in-vitro dye-flow rig (5,071 points). The MLP reported lower mean absolute error than LASSO; this controlled proof-of-concept does not establish patient diagnosis or clinical utility.

Technology: MLP, CNN and LSTM regress blood-flow velocity fields from angiographic projection images.

AI contribution
MLP, CNN and LSTM regress blood-flow velocity fields from angiographic projection images.
Test and result
The MLP, CNN and LSTM velocity estimates were compared with 5,071 PIV/optical-flow points; MLP mean absolute error was reported as 5×10⁻⁴ versus 3×10⁻³ for LASSO.
Limitation / still unknown
Evidence is limited to the controlled in-vitro rig; patient diagnosis and clinical utility were not tested. The publisher-hosted PDF denied access (HTTP 403), and Crossref listed no affiliation, so the record states that the affiliation was not provided.
