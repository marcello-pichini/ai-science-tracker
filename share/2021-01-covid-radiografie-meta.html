Sequential X-rays to predict COVID-19 deterioration

Models trained in part on 26,838 X-rays from 4,914 patients predict deterioration up to 96 hours ahead, outperforming specialists in some retrospective comparisons. This research is for hospital resource planning, not a clinical product or evidence of better patient outcomes.
