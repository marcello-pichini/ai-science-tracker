An attention-based neural network generates functional secretion signal peptides

An attention-based Transformer trained on signal-peptide data from Swiss-Prot generated candidate secretion sequences. Experimental testing in an industrial Bacillus subtilis strain found that model-generated signal peptides directed secreted enzyme activity competitive with industrially used signal peptides. The result establishes functional candidates in the reported host and assay; it does not establish broad performance across proteins or production hosts. The primary institutional record gives the article publication date as 2020-07-10.
