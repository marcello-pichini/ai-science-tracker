AlphaFold Database: over 200 million structures

The predicted-protein-structure archive, maintained with EMBL-EBI, grew from nearly 1 million to over 200 million structures, covering practically all cataloged proteins, with free access and bulk download from Google Cloud. More than 500,000 researchers in 190 countries had already used it. These are predictions, not experimental measurements.
