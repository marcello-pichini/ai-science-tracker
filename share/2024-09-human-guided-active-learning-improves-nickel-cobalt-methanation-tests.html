Human-guided active learning improves nickel-cobalt methanation tests

A human-in-the-loop model selected nickel-cobalt catalysts and reaction settings for a 48-test laboratory campaign. Further selected experiments improved methane space-time yield by nearly 50% over the previous maximum in that dataset. Extrapolation remained harder than interpolation. This is a bounded catalyst-and-condition result, not evidence of industrial deployment or lifecycle climate benefit.

Technology: Gaussian-process active learning with batch acquisition
