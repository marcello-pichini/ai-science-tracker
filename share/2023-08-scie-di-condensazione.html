Avoiding aircraft contrails with AI forecasts

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: In a trial with American Airlines and Breakthrough Energy, pilots flew 70 flights over six months using AI forecasts to avoid forming contrails, reducing them by 54% compared with flights without the forecasts. Flights that avoided contrails used 2% more fuel; across an airline’s flights, the impact could fall to 0.3% if only a small share need rerouting.

Technology: AI contrail-formation forecasts and satellite imagery
