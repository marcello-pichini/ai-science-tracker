Machine learning helps identify 61 new cataclysmic variables in SDSS-V spectroscopy

A convolutional neural network shortlisted cataclysmic-variable candidates from more than two million SDSS-V spectra, reducing the spectra requiring visual inspection to about 2% of the total. Human review and follow-up analysis of the resulting 504-object sample reported 61 previously unreported cataclysmic variables, spectroscopically confirmed 248 candidates and yielded 82 new or improved orbital periods; the article discusses selection biases and the CNN missed 27 spectra later found by human review.

Technology: Convolutional neural network classifier for SDSS-V/BOSS spectra
