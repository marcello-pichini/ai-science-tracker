Machine-learning-guided Co–Mn diatomic oxygen-reduction catalyst

A machine-learning-derived theoretical activity map guided a nitrogen-bridged Co–Mn catalyst proposal. The study reports synthesized Co-N-Mn/NC with a 0.90 V half-wave potential and 271 mW cm−2 peak power density in its alkaline oxygen-reduction and zinc–air tests. These are study-specific laboratory results, not commercial battery performance.

Technology: Machine-learning activity map; theoretical descriptor modeling
