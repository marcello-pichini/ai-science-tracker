XunZi prioritizes CHK2 as a Parkinson’s disease mechanism and therapeutic target

The XunZi AI-biologist integrated literature reasoning with multimodal molecular data and nominated CHK2 and IRAK4 kinase activation in Parkinson’s disease models. Follow-up experiments found that pharmacological or genetic Chk2 inhibition rescued dopaminergic-neuron loss and motor deficits in PD mouse models, supporting CHK2 as a disease-associated mediator in the tested systems.
