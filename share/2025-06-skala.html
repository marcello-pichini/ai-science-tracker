Skala: a deep-learning exchange-correlation functional

It comes close to the 1 kcal/mol threshold on the W4-17 benchmark (0.85 kcal/mol on the single-reference subset) and is competitive with leading hybrid functionals on GMTKN55 at lower cost. It was trained on about 150,000 high-accuracy energy differences, roughly 100 times the previous dataset size. Accuracy is shown only within the training data's chemical space; it was not yet public at the time.

Technology: Skala (neural network for DFT)
