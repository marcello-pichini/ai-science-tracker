Crystal-graph learning and DFT identify 19,512 additional thermodynamically stable materials

The authors trained crystal-graph attention networks on an expanded, composition- and symmetry-balanced dataset and used them to search 2,500 binary and ternary structure prototypes covering about one billion inorganic compounds. Density-functional-theory validation identified 19,512 additional compounds on the calculated thermodynamic convex hull and about 150,000 more within 50 meV/atom of it; the paper also analyzed predicted superconducting and superhard materials and compounds with large gap deformation potentials.

Technology: Crystal-graph attention neural networks trained on expanded density-functional-theory materials data
