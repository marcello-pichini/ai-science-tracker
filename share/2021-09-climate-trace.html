Climate TRACE: first independent database of global emissions

Using machine learning on data from over 300 satellites and 11,100 sensors (over 59 trillion bytes), emissions from oil and gas in major countries were estimated at about twice UNFCCC reports (about 1 billion tonnes more), while rice emissions in India were nearly three times the official 2016 inventory. No peer review; the page gives both September 14 and September 16, 2021.

Technology: Machine learning on satellite and sensor data
