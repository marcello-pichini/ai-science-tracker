AI-built Baihetan reservoir catalog reveals fault-controlled and depth-dependent seismicity

LOC-FLOW analysis of 55 stations and continuous waveforms from January 2021 to July 2022 identified 53,838 events around China’s Baihetan Reservoir, about four times the manual catalog, with reported relative location errors of tens of metres and recall of about 99% of manual events. The authors infer that post-impoundment seismicity is concentrated along regional structures and fractures, differs across the Jinsha River, and includes migration away from the inundation zone and with depth. They report depth-dependent b-value and water-depth/event-rate patterns consistent with different shallow and deep controls.

Technology: LOC-FLOW automated earthquake-detection and location workflow using PhaseNet deep-learning phase picking
