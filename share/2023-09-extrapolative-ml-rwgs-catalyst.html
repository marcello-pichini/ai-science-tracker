Extrapolative machine learning identifies a multielement RWGS catalyst

In a closed loop combining machine-learning prediction with experiments, researchers tested 300 reverse water-gas shift catalyst formulations over 44 cycles, starting from 45 data points. They report Pt(3)/Rb(1)-Ba(1)-Mo(0.6)-Nb(0.2)/TiO₂ as the optimal tested composition; Nb was absent from the initial dataset. The result is specific to the study’s catalyst tests and conditions, not commercial CO₂ conversion. The earlier ChemRxiv version is not used to claim priority here.
