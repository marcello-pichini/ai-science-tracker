Machine learning guided multi-metric optimization of photocatalytic CO2 reduction

Bayesian optimization guided experiments on a five-component self-assembled photocatalytic micelle system for CO₂-to-CO reduction. The authors report that optimized conditions increased measured CO yield from 1.48 ± 0.24 to 5.72 ± 0.12 μmol h⁻¹, with improved turnover and quantum-yield metrics while maintaining CO selectivity. The article’s narrative fold-change (4.2×) is inconsistent with the displayed means (about 3.9×), so this record gives the means and omits the fold claim. The findings apply to the tested aqueous micelle system and conditions.

Technology: Bayesian optimization for experimental selection; random-forest models and SHAP for later analysis

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: The article reports CO yield means of 5.72±0.12 and 1.48±0.24 μmol h−1 for optimized vs initial conditions, along with turnover and quantum-yield metrics and maintained selectivity.
