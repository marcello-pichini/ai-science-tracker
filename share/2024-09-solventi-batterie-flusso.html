Solvent mixtures for flow batteries found through an AI–experiment cycle

PNNL and Argonne identified solvent mixtures that dissolve three times more than a compound proposed for flow batteries, exploring less than 10% of about 2,000 combinations. The model receives experimental data and guides subsequent experiments; the result concerns solubility, without demonstrating performance of a complete battery.

Technology: Machine learning in a loop with high-throughput experiments
