Catalysts without iridium or ruthenium for acidic oxygen evolution

An AI-guided, human-supervised platform assessed 2,942 catalysts and identified complex palladium oxides. InMnPdOx maintained an overpotential below 0.5 V for more than 1,000 hours in sulfuric acid at 10 mA/cm². This is a preprint and laboratory result, without validation in a PEM electrolyzer.

Technology: Sequential learning, composition–property models, and LLMs

Relevance 73 chemistry Original source Suggest a correction

Publication: Preprint · Test context: direct test. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Preprint
  • Test type: direct test
  • Tested: Lead catalysts were tested in 1 M H2SO4 at 10 mA/cm2: InMnPdOx kept overpotential below 0.5 V for over 1,000 hours; a retrospective benchmark compared the agent with Bayesian optimization and in-context language-model selection.
  • Still unknown: Testing in a full PEM electrolyzer is not reported, and 'stable' means overpotential under 0.5 V rather than a broader durability assessment.
  • AI role: A sequential-learning agent with ML composition models, adaptive optimization and LLM reasoning ran a human-supervised closed loop with over 90% automation.

Evidence sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30133
