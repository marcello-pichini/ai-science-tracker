An autonomous platform speeds research on polymer mixtures

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The platform tests up to 700 mixtures a day (96 at a time), guided by a genetic algorithm: hundreds of mixtures outperformed the individual components, and the best retained 73% enzyme activity, 18% more than the best component. Application is limited to enzyme thermal stability, replenishment is still manual; published in Matter.

Technology: Genetic algorithm with robotics
