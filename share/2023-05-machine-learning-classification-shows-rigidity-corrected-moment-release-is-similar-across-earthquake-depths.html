Machine-learning classification shows rigidity-corrected moment release is similar across earthquake depths

Random-forest classification of source-time-function characteristics for 3,675 global Mw > 5.5 earthquakes showed that depth-dependent rigidity explains much of the differences in rupture duration, length and stress drop between shallow, intermediate and deep earthquakes. Once medium rigidity is corrected, the analyzed events exhibit similar moment-release processes, supporting a constant-strain-drop interpretation across depths and host-rock types.

Technology: Random-forest machine-learning classification and feature-importance analysis
