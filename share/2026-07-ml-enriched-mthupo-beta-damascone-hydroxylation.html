Machine learning enriched MthUPO variants for beta-damascone hydroxylation

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Researchers combined experimental activity measurements with machine learning to enrich a second MthUPO peroxygenase library for substrate-specific activity. Across 506 distinct variants screened and sequenced, 97 of 108 variants in the ML-enriched library outperformed wild type in the screening assay, compared with 14% in the initial htFuncLib library. The authors report an 11.8-fold turnover number for the best variant producing 4-hydroxy-beta-damascone, with greater than 99% regioisomeric excess. Separate 5–12-fold enrichment and recovery figures in the paper are simulation benchmarks and are not treated as experimental outcomes.

Technology: Machine-learning enrichment of experimental enzyme-activity data following htFuncLib design
