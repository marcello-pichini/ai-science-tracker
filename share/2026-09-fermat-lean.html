Claude formalizes Fermat's theorem in Lean

A proof of Fermat's Last Theorem fully machine-verified in Lean: Claude produced most of it independently over 11 days, with about 13 million lines and 29,500 intermediate theorems, and a comparator confirms that its statement matches Mathlib's. It follows a simplified version of Wiles's proof, uses Mathlib and parts of an Imperial College project, and is probably longer than necessary.

Result, AI contribution and limits

Evidence context (descriptions follow the cited sources; test type is a catalog classification):

  • Publication status: Announcement only
  • Test type: prospective or independent check
  • Tested: The proof was checked by Lean using only standard axioms; a comparator tool confirmed the theorem statement matches Mathlib's, and mathematician Kevin Buzzard reviewed it.
  • Still unknown: The proof is about 13 million lines and likely much longer than needed; it follows a simplified version of Wiles's proof and is presented as verification, not new mathematics.
  • AI role: Dozens of agents worked for 11 days on the Prove2Me platform, producing about 30,300 computer-verified theorems; humans gave occasional high-level instructions.
