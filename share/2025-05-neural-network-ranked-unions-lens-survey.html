Neural-network-ranked survey found candidate lenses and spectroscopically followed seven

Two residual neural networks ranked multiband UNIONS galaxy images for strong-lens candidates; researchers visually graded the candidates. The paper lists 1,346 candidates and reports independent Keck/Gemini spectroscopy for seven selected high-quality systems, with background-source redshifts above 2.1. The authors report overlaps with other lens surveys. This entry represents one AI-assisted survey and its selected spectroscopic follow-up, not 1,346 confirmed lenses or seven uniquely first-discovered objects; object-level priority and complete cross-survey overlap remain unresolved.

Technology: Two residual neural networks trained on real lens images and applied to multiband UNIONS imaging
