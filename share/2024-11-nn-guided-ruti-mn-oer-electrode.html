Neural network guides a Ru–Ti–Mn oxygen-evolution electrode

A backpropagation neural network fitted composition/activity and deactivation data and suggested a Ru:Ti:Mn ratio of 0.26:0.26:0.48. The synthesized electrode had measured composition near 0.24:0.28:0.48 and reported 165.2 mV overpotential at 10 mA cm−2 in the acidic assay. Model optimum and measured composition are distinct; reported long-duration tests apply only under the study’s specific conditions.
