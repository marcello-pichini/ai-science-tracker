Immune and inflammatory features associated with pyroptosis- and myocardial fibrosis-related genes in HFpEF: a machine learning study

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Machine-learning feature selection from transcriptomic data proposed a three-gene HFpEF signature; a small clinical cohort linked one marker with HFpEF status and clinical measurements. Results are exploratory: the signature was unstable across resampling, cross-species expression directions differed, and the evidence does not establish causal mechanisms or clinical biomarkers.

Technology: Machine-learning feature selection prioritized candidate genes and fit an exploratory transcriptomic classifier.

Editorial relevance 53 medicine Published article Suggest a correction

Publication: Peer-reviewed paper · Test context: retrospective or benchmark. Catalog categories, not an evidence grade.

AI contribution
Machine-learning feature selection generated candidate signature findings from transcriptomic cohorts.
Test and result
A three-gene signature was proposed; lower whole-blood PTPN11 was associated with HFpEF status and higher NT-proBNP/CRP in a small clinical cohort.
Limitation / still unknown
The exact gene set showed limited resampling stability; the clinical cohort was small and unmatched, with no causal or clinical diagnostic evidence.
