Riff-Diff: computational enzyme design by catalytic motif scaffolding

Riff-Diff places catalytic motifs around an active centre and uses RFdiffusion to generate the full protein; 35 tested sequences yielded active enzymes for several reaction types, reported as significantly faster than earlier computational designs. Angstrom-level accuracy was checked against experimentally determined high-resolution structures, and almost all designs kept their fold up to about 90 degrees Celsius. Figures come from university press coverage; the Nature paper itself was not read directly.

Technology: Riff-Diff (RFdiffusion with rotamer-based motif placement)
