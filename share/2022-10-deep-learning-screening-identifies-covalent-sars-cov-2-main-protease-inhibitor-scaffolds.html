Deep-learning screening identifies covalent SARS-CoV-2 main-protease inhibitor scaffolds

A two-stage deep-learning screen selected cysteine-directed compounds for testing against SARS-CoV-2 3CL protease. Of 32 top-ranked compounds experimentally assayed, 12 inhibited the enzyme; six had IC₅₀ values below 12 μM, with the strongest reported at 1.4 μM. Five of those six exhibited time-dependent inhibition consistent with covalent binding.

Technology: Deep-learning COVCL and NOVCL classifiers integrating a directed message-passing neural network and feed-forward network

  • AI role: The COVCL model first prioritized cysteine-targeted covalent candidates, then NOVCL ranked those candidates for predicted 3CL-protease inhibition. Researchers tested 32 selected molecules in enzyme assays and performed follow-up tests of time-dependent inhibition for active hits.
