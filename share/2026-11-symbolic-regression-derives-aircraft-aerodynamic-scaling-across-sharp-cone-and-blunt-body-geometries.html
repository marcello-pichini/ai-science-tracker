Symbolic regression derives aircraft aerodynamic scaling across sharp-cone and blunt-body geometries

A Chain of Symbolic Regression (CoSR) analysis of hypersonic simulation data derived an interpretable non-power-product scaling relation for axial-force coefficient across sharp-cone and blunt-body configurations. It combines angle of attack, Mach number and Reynolds number through a dominant term and a viscous correction; the relation has a shared functional form but geometry-specific fitted constants. The authors interpret the constants as reflecting differences in shock/base drag, friction drag and pressure drag. This is a new model-derived scaling result from simulations, not a wind-tunnel or flight measurement.

Technology: Chain of Symbolic Regression (CoSR), a progressive symbolic-regression framework for dimensionless physical relations
