CNN distinguishes pancreatic cancer on contrast CT with cross-racial validation

A CNN trained on contrast-enhanced CT from a Taiwanese centre reached 98.3% sensitivity on combined local test sets versus 92.9% for radiologists (difference 5.4 points, p=0.014). On a US external test set, sensitivity fell to 79.0% with specificity 97.6% (AUC 0.920), and sensitivity for tumours under 2 cm was 63.1% in that set. The study is retrospective and the authors describe it as supplementary to radiologists.

Technology: Convolutional neural network applied to CT image patches
