Machine learning guides a near-infrared Mo/Sb halide phosphor

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A regression model trained on experimental and theoretical data guided Mo/Sb/Br co-doping for a lead-free near-infrared phosphor. The authors report synthesizing Cs₂Zr(Cl₀.₄₆Br₀.₅₄)₆:12%Mo⁴⁺/3.6%Sb³⁺, with emission near 920 nm, internal quantum efficiency of 92.4%, and external quantum efficiency of 65.9% under the reported conditions. Its measured intensity was about 20% higher than the authors’ orthogonal-design comparator. A separate diffusion model was used for image reconstruction demonstrations; this does not establish clinical diagnostic performance. Earlier composition-level prior art and independent replication remain open.

Technology: Transformer-based regression model
