Discovery of Pyrazolo[3,4-d]pyridazinone Derivatives as Selective DDR1 Inhibitors via Deep Learning Based Design, Synthesis, and Biological Evaluation.

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A scaffold-design workflow incorporating a deep generative model, kinase screening and docking produced compound 2, with DDR1 IC50 10.6 ± 1.9 nM and selectivity tested against 430 kinases. Cell and mouse-colitis results are preclinical; sample sizes and comparators were not assessed from the abstract.

Technology: Deep generative molecular model

AI contribution
The publisher abstract identifies a deep generative model within a scaffold-design workflow with kinase selectivity screening and docking; its specific operation was not assessable from the abstract.
Test and result
The publisher abstract reports DDR1 IC50 10.6 ± 1.9 nM, selectivity testing against 430 kinases, cell cytokine/autophosphorylation assays and a DSS-induced mouse colitis model. Sample sizes and comparator details were not available in the reviewed abstract.
Limitation / still unknown
Only the publisher abstract and metadata were reviewed. Animal sample size, comparator regimen, detailed assay design and the generator's specific operation were not assessed; mouse results do not establish clinical efficacy.
