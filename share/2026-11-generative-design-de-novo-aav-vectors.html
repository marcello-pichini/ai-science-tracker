Generative design of de novo AAV vectors with unique serological profiles

The preprint abstract reports a variational autoencoder trained on natural AAV sequences to generate capsids. More than 255 designs were synthesized and tested in vitro. Seven transduced human cells at over 50% efficiency; four of those avoided neutralization by every AAV-reactive human serum sample tested, at the reported NT50 threshold below 1:50. This establishes in-vitro capsid properties, not in-vivo delivery or clinical efficacy. Review is limited to the abstract and original-server metadata.

Technology: Variational autoencoder (generative model of AAV capsid sequences)
