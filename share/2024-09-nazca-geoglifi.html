Nazca: AI helps discover 303 new figurative geoglyphs

An IBM model flagged 1,309 candidates; about a quarter were ground-checked over roughly six months, finding 303 new figurative geoglyphs among 430 already known and increasing the discovery rate 16-fold. Another 968 candidates remain to be checked; the study had been accepted by PNAS at the time of the announcement.

Technology: Neural network for aerial images
