Active learning identifies 28 new DFT-validated pressure-induced phase transitions

Across 13 active-learning cycles, a graph neural network screened phase pairs for pressure-induced transitions and selected cases for density-functional-theory (DFT) calculations. DFT confirmed 28 previously unreported transformations; 14 high-pressure phases were dynamically stable at transition pressure, including 10 also predicted dynamically stable at zero pressure. The study also identifies a previously unrecorded dynamically stable monoclinic P2₁/c phase of Cr₂O₃.

Technology: MEGNet graph neural network with five-model ensemble and iterative active learning

  • Tested: Across 13 active-learning cycles, a graph neural network screened phase pairs for pressure-induced transitions and selected cases for density-functional-theory (DFT) calculations.
  • Still unknown: These are computational predictions corroborated by DFT, not experimentally synthesized or observed phases. Of 28 transitions, 14 high-pressure structures were dynamically stable at transition pressure; only 10 were also predicted stable at zero pressure. Two selected phase-pair calculations remained difficult to converge. The paper's discovery count excludes 18 rediscoveries, and individual stability remains subject to DFT approximations.
  • AI role: The GNN screened 7,677 phase pairs for consensus transition-pressure predictions. Active learning selected high-confidence pairs for DFT equation-of-state calculations, and the resulting DFT outcomes were added to training data for subsequent cycles. The AI search was the discovery and prioritization mechanism; DFT calculations supplied the physical verification of the transitions.
