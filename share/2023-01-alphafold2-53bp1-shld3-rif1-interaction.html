AlphaFold2-Multimer screen of the 53BP1 pathway predicts a RIF1-SHLD3 interface that is confirmed in cells

Source check: Needs source check. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: Sifri et al. used AlphaFold2-Multimer to predict all pairwise interactions in the 53BP1-RIF1-shieldin-CST pathway, recovered seven known interfaces and predicted a new one between the RIF1 HEAT-repeat domain and the SHLD3 eIF4E-like domain. Pulldown and cellular assays supported the model and showed the interaction is needed for shieldin recruitment, class switch recombination and PARP inhibitor sensitivity. First public version: bioRxiv 2023-01-12; journal version 2023-06-12. Abstract-level review only.
