Co-Scientist and molecular switches in severe infectious diseases

Archived from discovery counts: the represented announcement describes hypotheses and cell lines still being prepared to test them, not a completed biological result. Clare Bryant (Cambridge) used Co-Scientist, which ranked hypotheses and pointed to a protein she had not considered down to individual amino acids, to study molecular switches behind diseases such as sepsis. The lab expects to build cell lines with those mutations in about six months instead of two or three years; the hypotheses are not yet validated.
