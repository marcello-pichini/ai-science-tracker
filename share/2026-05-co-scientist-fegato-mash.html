Co-Scientist and mechanisms of metabolic steatohepatitis (MASH)

The tool proposed the NLRP3 inflammasome as a link between inflammation and metabolism, and the post says the hypothesis was later tested experimentally. No numbers are given; the drug combinations were suggested for testing, not demonstrated.
