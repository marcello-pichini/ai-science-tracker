Prospective multicentre study combines EV protein biomarkers and machine learning for preclinical oesophageal cancer detection

The BarFlare assay and an interpretable multi-criteria decision-making classification-fusion machine-learning framework combined extracellular-vesicle SCC and MMP13 biomarkers with clinical factors. The model was evaluated in prospective multicentre diagnostic cohorts and two population cohorts; the longitudinal cohort contained baseline samples from 30 people who later developed oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma, with a reported median lead time of 34.9 months and AUC 0.864. The paper also reports external diagnostic validation in two cohorts.

Technology: Multi-criteria decision-making Classification Fusion (MCF) machine-learning framework; 289 candidate feature-selector/classifier pairs were compared and three base models fused

  • Publication status: Peer-reviewed paper
  • Tested: The BarFlare assay and an interpretable multi-criteria decision-making classification-fusion machine-learning framework combined extracellular-vesicle SCC and MMP13 biomarkers with clinical factors.
  • Still unknown: This is a prospective observational diagnostic study, not an intervention trial or proof that screening improves outcomes. The longitudinal preclinical cohort had 30 incident cases and matched controls; performance estimates require external replication in larger populations. The source reports an association and lead time, not guaranteed individual prediction. Primary publisher full text and abstract checked; no independent replication assessed.
  • AI role: The MCF framework integrated newly identified EV biomarkers with clinical variables, selected and fused machine-learning classifiers, and produced the risk score evaluated in diagnostic and prospective population cohorts. BarFlare is the high-sensitivity EV assay platform, not the AI model.
