Gemini Deep Think on research problems in mathematics, physics, and computer science

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: The Aletheia mathematical agent scored up to 90% on IMO-ProofBench Advanced and independently solved four open Erdős questions; work with experts covered 18 research problems, including a disproof of a submodular optimization conjecture. Its score on doctoral-level problems falls to about 38–46%; no result is claimed as a breakthrough, and the papers were submitted to journals.

Technology: Gemini Deep Think, Aletheia agent

AI contribution
Aletheia, a math research agent built on Gemini Deep Think, generated, verified and revised proofs and used search, with varying autonomy across projects.
Test and result
Aletheia was evaluated on IMO-ProofBench Advanced (about 90%, human experts graded), an internal PhD-level benchmark (about 46%), a semi-autonomous run over 700 open Erdős problems (four autonomous solutions), and expert collaborations; Level 2 papers were submitted to journals.
Limitation / still unknown
The article states that no Level 3 or Level 4 results are claimed; the abstract states no limitations, and the PhD-level score falls to about 38-46%.
