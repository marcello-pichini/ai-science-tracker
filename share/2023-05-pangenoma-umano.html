First draft of the human pangenome reference

Source check: Source checked. Not independent scientific confirmation.

Finding: A consortium of 119 scientists from 60 institutions published in Nature a pangenome combining assembled genomes from 47 people of diverse ancestries, rather than one person. Google adapted DeepVariant to identify variants and DeepConsensus to correct sequencing errors; a long-read assembler achieved over 99.999% final accuracy. This is a draft: benefits for diagnosis and treatment remain prospective.

Technology: DeepVariant and DeepConsensus

AI contribution
Google adapted DeepVariant to call variants and DeepConsensus to correct sequencing errors in the pangenome assembly.
Test and result
The draft pangenome from 47 assemblies was evaluated for accuracy at structural and base-pair levels, and short-read analysis was compared with GRCh38-based workflows (34% fewer small-variant errors).
Limitation / still unknown
The abstract states no explicit limitations; the blog says this is a draft and benefits for diagnosis and treatment remain prospective.
