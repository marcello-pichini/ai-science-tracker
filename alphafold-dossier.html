What has AlphaFold helped scientists discover?

A study using AlphaFold-Multimer reported experimental support for six previously unreported protein interfaces. CASP14 and the database expansion provide context for the accuracy and availability of structure predictions.

Editorial review 18 Nov 2026. Three catalog examples illustrate different kinds of evidence.

Experiments supported six previously unreported protein interfaces

A study of 62 selected human protein interactions used AlphaFold-Multimer to predict where proteins might contact one another. Follow-up experiments corroborated six previously unreported interfaces. The authors also reported limits in prediction specificity. The experimental support applies to the interfaces tested in this study. Read the protein-interface study.

CASP14 tested prediction accuracy

In the blind CASP14 assessment, AlphaFold 2 scored a median 92.4 on the Global Distance Test across targets, according to Google DeepMind. Organizers compared predictions with experimentally determined reference structures. That score describes benchmark performance on the assessed targets. Read the CASP14 announcement.

The AlphaFold Database expanded access to predictions

In July 2022, Google DeepMind and EMBL-EBI made more than 200 million predicted structures available. The release greatly expanded the pool of structural models researchers could consult. The number counts predicted structures released in that expansion. Read the 2022 database announcement.

How to interpret the evidence

What the CASP14 score measures

CASP14 was a blind community assessment. Participants submitted a prediction before seeing the reference structure used for judging that target. Global Distance Test measures agreement in the positions of parts of that structure. A median summarizes the assessed targets and can hide differences between easy and difficult regions or examples.

A useful comparison starts with the target list and the scoring rule. CASP14 is strong evidence about geometric accuracy on those benchmark targets. A question about a specific protein in a particular cellular state needs evidence that covers that protein and state.

How to read confidence in the AlphaFold Database

The database shows model confidence alongside the structure. The pLDDT score is local and can vary widely along the same chain. Predicted Aligned Error describes how confidently the model places one region relative to another. Two regions can look individually well determined even when their relative arrangement is uncertain.

The July 2022 release number describes the scale of available predictions. A database entry can help identify a region worth examining, and a linked experimental structure can provide a separate comparison. Keep the estimate of confidence attached to the visual when citing a predicted structure.

What experimental support adds for an interface

The interface study asked where two proteins might meet. Its experiments support specific contact hypotheses, and the authors also reported limited specificity in their benchmarks. That distinction matters: a predictor can recover plausible contacts while still producing candidates that need to be ruled out.

The six experimentally supported interfaces belong to one study of 62 selected interactions. That study set and the validation subset should both accompany the result. Estimating performance on arbitrary protein pairs would require a defined sampling scheme and systematic assessment across positive and negative cases.
